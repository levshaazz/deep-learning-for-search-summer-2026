## Thesis
Easy negatives teach nothing (no gradient); the hardest are often **impostors** — unlabelled positives
— that corrupt the blade; effectiveness lives in a narrow *hard-but-honest* band. Two orthogonal axes:
*geometric hardness* `cos(q, d⁻)` and *collateral danger* `cos(d⁻, d⁺)`. A false negative is dangerous
on the **second** axis, invisible to a query-only view.

11. **static-ladder** — random → in-batch → BM25; static negatives are model-independent and go stale.
12. **ance-dynamic** — mine the model's own ANN index.
13. **sparring-ghosts** — staleness between refreshes; STAR/ADORE go per-step.

18. **cures-false-negatives** — filter (RocketQA) · sample (SimANS) · co-train (AR2).
19. **turn-teacher** — distillation; the teacher's margin unmasks the impostor (Margin-MSE).

22. **experiment-honesty** — the toy is *constructed* to be false-negative-dense; it reproduces the ordering, not the magnitudes.
23. **payoff** — forged by worthy opponents; bridge to *The Curved Map*.
