## Spine position
The spine **returns to *Measure*** for the generative era. For ten lectures *Measure* meant grading a **ranked list**
against a known relevant set — precision, recall, MAP, nDCG — all of which assume a **gold answer** to diff against.
L10 gave the Ship a voice (the *Generate* leg); L11 asks the question that voice forces: **how do you measure a
free-text answer when there is no gold key?** The lecture builds the courtroom in three climbs — **RAGAS**
(reference-free metrics: faithfulness, answer relevance, context precision/recall), the **LLM-as-judge** (a model
grading a model, audited for its measured biases), and the **agentic loop** (ReAct / Self-RAG / CRAG / Adaptive-RAG)
that moves the judge *inside* the pipeline so the Ship grades and repairs its own retrievals mid-flight. It is
cumulative: the retriever (L3–L9), the Oracle's RAG pipeline (L10), and the self-correcting loop (L10) are all
upstream — L11 puts a number on whether they actually worked. Because *Measure* is the spine's recurring fight,
**Goodhart gets his definitive scene here**: the moment a judge becomes a target, gaming begins (position/verbosity
bias) — the climax of the villain arc opened in L1.

## Creatures & callbacks
- **Goodhart the Trickster** (L1 → here) gets his **strongest, definitive return** — the *Measure*-territory villain's
  climax. He games the LLM-judge by padding answers and exploiting position/verbosity bias; the lecture names him
  ("Goodhart's Law"), audits the judge with the mitigations he forces, and **walks him out of the courtroom** on the
  payoff. Bare-headed, course-blue, clickbait fishing-hook — no green (P9).
- **RAGdoll the Oracle** (callback L10) is **on trial**: it answers fluently whether right or wrong, gets graded by
  RAGAS and the judge, then — via the agentic loop — learns to **grade and correct itself** (Self-RAG reflection
  tokens, CRAG retrieval grading). Never on a precision climb's arithmetic.
- **Confabulous** (callback L10) is the **unseen cause**: the confident-fabrication phantom introduced at L10's catch.
  L11 names the symptom and finally **measures** it — faithfulness is the metric that catches the Harvey-style
  hallucination directly.
- **Callbacks:** L1 (Goodhart + "when a measure becomes a target") · L9/L1 (the classic IR metrics that *don't* fit
  generation) · L10 (the Oracle, the RAG pipeline, the self-correcting loop, Confabulous, the deferred
  faithfulness-vs-relevance axes — now measured).
