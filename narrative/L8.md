## Thesis
One machine, three families. Attention gives every token direct access to every other
(softmax(QKᵀ/√dₖ)V); the block wraps it in PE + FFN + residual/LayerNorm; stacking it with a **full
mask** gives BERT (read the whole cloth → understanding/retrieval), with a **causal mask** gives GPT
(write forward → generation), with **cross-attention** gives T5/BART. Decoding chooses the next
thread; FlashAttention pays the O(n²) bill in tiles; RLHF aligns behavior, not knowledge — that last
stretch lies beyond the border of search, kept for general culture.
