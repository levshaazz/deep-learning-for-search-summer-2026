## Thesis
Chunk-then-embed severs global context: a chunk whose subject is a pronoun loses its referent. Late
chunking embeds the WHOLE document first (full-document attention resolves the coreference), then
pools per chunk — one vector per chunk, free at query time. It is not late interaction (L8): the two
"lates" have mirror-image bills (index-time vs query-time). A small win, paid for with nothing.
