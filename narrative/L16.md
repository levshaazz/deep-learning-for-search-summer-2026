## Thesis
Chunk-then-embed severs global context: a chunk whose subject is a pronoun loses its referent. Late
chunking embeds the WHOLE document first (bidirectional full-document attention resolves the
coreference), then pools per chunk — one vector per chunk, free at query time. It is not late
interaction (L8): the two "lates" have mirror-image bills (index-time vs query-time). A small win,
paid for with nothing — **on the condition** that the encoder is bidirectional, long-context and
mean-pooling, and that the neighbouring text is actually relevant. Outside that fence the outcome
runs from zero to a collapse.
