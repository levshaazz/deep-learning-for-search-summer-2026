## Thesis
Russian carries its grammar *inside* its words (fusional morphology), which sends three bills against a
pipeline built on English assumptions: a **token tax** (BPE fragments Russian into many more sub-words),
a **surface-form miss** (BM25 shares zero strings between query and answer), and an **embedder mismatch**
(an English-only encoder never saw the endings). One principle answers all three: **normalize
symmetrically** — fold the query and the corpus to a common form, whether that form is a lemma (lexical)
or a shared embedding space (dense). Measured payoff on the toy: the gold answer's BM25 goes 0.0 → 1.3884,
rank 2 → 1, the moment the forms match.

## Beats
1. **hook-torn-forms** — the answer *котёнок играет* is in the corpus; search walks past *котята играют*.
2. **fusional-language** — one ending fuses several values; 6 cases × 2 numbers; the root hides.
3. **token-tax** — *widget-free image*: BPE trained on English shreds *запроса* into 7; 1.0 vs 5.8 tok/word.
4. **surface-form-miss** — BM25 sums only over `t ∈ q ∩ d`; no shared string → score 0.0, wrong doc wins.
5. **lemma-vs-stem** — stemming (chop, cheap) vs lemmatization (dictionary form, homonymy); apply symmetrically.
6. **bm25-worked** — the inversion, measured: gold 0.0 (rank 2) → 1.3884 (rank 1); distractor 1.3608.
7. **normalization-stack** — the analyzer: index-time chain + the *same* analyzer at query time (ES `russian`).
8. **dense-speaks-russian** — bi-encoder matches by cosine; sub-words + contrastive training absorb morphology.
9. **multilingual-vs-ru** — multilingual-E5 / BGE-M3 / LaBSE vs ru-specific; shortlist then evaluate on your data.
10. **benchmarks-translationese** — MIRACL (native) vs mMARCO (translationese) vs ruMTEB; the L4 Goodhart lesson.
11. **small-cuts** — ё/е, case, transliteration, keyboard-layout (ghbdtn→привет); each one line, symmetric.
12. **when-to-reach** — BM25+lemma · multilingual dense · ru-specific · hybrid (RRF) as the production baseline.
13. **payoff** — morphology strikes back, loses to the same move three times; not a new model, the tuned pipeline.
