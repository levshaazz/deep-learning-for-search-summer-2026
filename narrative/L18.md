## Thesis
Embedding spaces are distorted maps. Contextual vectors crowd into a narrow cone (**anisotropy** —
random words look 0.99-similar; the tallest directions secretly encode *frequency*, not meaning), and
a few points sit in everyone's neighbour list (**hubness** — a curse-of-dimensionality effect, the
Wraith's cousin from L2). Cosine lies on this map. Two repairs: re-project (center → whiten →
contrastive isotropy) and tax the crowded hubs at query time (CSLS).
