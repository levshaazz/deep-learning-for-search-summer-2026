# L7.md — *Scouts and Judges* (beat sheet + build design)

> **Lecture:** Bi-encoders (DPR, SBERT) · Cross-encoders & reranking · multi-stage pipelines.
> **Mode:** LOTR/quest. **Spine:** Rank (the Bridge). **Creatures:** **Sir Cosine** (scores the Scouts).
> **Slides:** `/slides/10` (EN, to build, depth between L6 and L7). **Book:** `/{lang}/book/10` (trilingual en/ru/tt).
> **Open:** *"Two kinds of crew: scouts who sweep fast, judges who deliberate slow."* (callback → L1 funnel, now neural)
> **Close:** scouts + judges win — but the scouts still sweep **linearly**; too many to judge at scale (bridge → ColBERT/hybrid L12, ANN L13).

## Spine position
Makes the **L1 cascade neural** (the Bridge): fast bi-encoder retrieval (Scouts) → accurate cross-encoder
reranking (Judges). The core architecture of modern neural search. L6 gave words *coordinates*; L7 gave the
encoder a *contrastively-trained space* (InfoNCE) and *attention*; **L10 puts that encoder to work two ways** —
encode-separately (cheap, cacheable) vs encode-jointly (accurate, uncacheable) — and stages them into a funnel.
