## Frontier & lab (TurboQuant / turbovec)
- **Quantization frontier — fold into beat 5 (`climb-pq`):** after PQ, introduce **TurboQuant**
  (Zandieh, Daliri, Hadian & Mirrokni 2025, arXiv:2504.19874, Google Research) — data-oblivious,
  *online* (no codebook training): random rotation → Beta-concentrated coordinates → per-coordinate
  optimal scalar quantizer → bit-packing → length-renormalized scoring; beats FAISS IndexPQFastScan.
  Cite the **paper** as canonical; keep FAISS the baseline. Strong candidate for the L9 paper-essay
  list (hero figure: distortion-rate / Beta-concentration curves).
- **L9 lab — use `turbovec`** (github.com/RyanCodrai/turbovec, Python bindings): build a quantized ANN
  index, measure **recall@k vs memory vs latency**, compare FAISS Flat / IVF-PQ vs turbovec. It's a
  *community* implementation (not Google's) — a hands-on demo, not an authority; verify maintenance
  before assigning. **Out of scope:** TurboQuant's KV-cache/LLM-inference use (a serving tangent → at
  most a one-line nod in L12 efficiency, not L9).
