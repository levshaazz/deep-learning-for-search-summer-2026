## Spine position
The **frontier of the whole spine, and its last territory.** Territories I–III answered *Get Data → Measure → Rank*;
L10 added the fourth leg — *Generate* — and built the Oracle (retrieve → augment → generate); L11 learned to *judge*
that generation (faithfulness vs. relevance, the confident-vs-correct gap). **L12 takes *Rank → Generate* to its
edge** along three frontiers that all sit *on top of* the retrieval stack, never replacing it:
1. **Reason across records (GraphRAG).** Naive RAG is a **flat bag of chunks** with no edges; a multi-hop question
   is a *path* (company → founder → field) that flat top-*k* retrieval cannot walk. Build a **graph** of entities and
   relations, traverse it (local search), and **cluster + pre-summarise** it for corpus-wide questions (global search).
2. **See beyond text (multimodal).** When the record is an image or a scanned page, search in a **shared cross-modal
   space**: CLIP aligns image and caption by **contrastive** training so retrieval is a cosine ranking — *Sir Cosine's*
   angle, now between modalities (a direct callback to L6 contrastive learning); ColPali matches a query to page
   *patches* with late interaction, no OCR.
3. **Answer responsibly (ethics & safety).** Once the Ship can answer the deepest questions, a fluent reply that is
   wrong, biased, leaks a secret, or cites nothing is not a feature — it is a **harm**. Four risks, four mitigations,
   one principle: **ground it, cite it, and let it say "I don't know."** The captain answers for the Ship.

It is **cumulative**: the index (L3), embeddings (L5/L6), the cascade (L7/L8), the ANN lanes (L9), the Oracle (L10),
and the judge (L11) are all *upstream* — the frontier consumes them. Because this is the **final** territory chapter,
its payoff is **arc-level**: the spine slide lights every stop, the bestiary takes a bow, and Séréga hands the helm to
the student — the bridge into the **Defense** ("You Take the Helm") that resolves the L0 send-off.
