## Thesis
The user's words are almost never the archive's words. Three gaps — **vocabulary**, **specificity**,
**compositionality** — and a family of rewrites that close them: PRF/RM3 (the echo with a ceiling),
HyDE/query2doc (embed a *hypothetical answer*, not the question), step-back (climb UP to the
principle), multi-query + RRF (consensus beats a single favourite), decomposition (pay the p^n tax per
hop), and the trainable rewriter. Every technique is a tax — diagnose the gap, then pick the move.
