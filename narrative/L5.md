training objective that earns them, and the tools to *see* a 300-D space. The first half of the neural turn:
L3 gave words *weights*; L5 gives them *coordinates*.

## Widgets & data (NEW)
- **`embedding-space`** ← `data/l5-embeddings.json` — ~10–14 real word vectors (a small verified GloVe/word2vec
  subset OR an LSA-on-curated-corpus embedding where the analogy holds), the `king−man+woman` analogy with
  computed cosine to `queen` + runner-ups, pairwise cosines for a few words. Steps: plot words → draw the
  analogy parallelogram → show the cosine ranking.
- **`dimred-projection`** ← `data/l5-dimred.json` — a set of high-D word vectors, PCA components + per-component
  explained-variance, the 2-D PCA coords, AND a t-SNE/UMAP 2-D layout (precomputed). Steps: 300-D blur →
  PCA axes → 2-D projection (variance %) → the t-SNE neighbor plot.
- Data gen: `_research/gen_l5.py` (reproducible). Reuse the sklearn `TruncatedSVD`/PCA pattern from
  `embedding_demo.py`/`gen_l3.py`. The analogy is the one piece needing real word vectors — resolve with a
  small committed GloVe-50d subset (verified) or a curated mini-corpus; numbers become facts-gate-checkable.
