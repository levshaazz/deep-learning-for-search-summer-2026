> Dimensionality Wraith**. **Slides:** `/slides/02` (EN, exists, 71). **Book:** `/{lang}/book/02`.
> **Open:** *"There's an alien on my screen and I can't tell if two things it says mean the same."*

## Spine position
Two territories in one mission: **Get Data** (turn alien text into computable units) → **Measure**
(tell when two meanings are close). Builds the tokenizer (Archives) and the first Instrument (cosine).
