One-liner: BPE merges by frequency; WordPiece merges by likelihood gain; Unigram prunes by likelihood loss.

The crispest summary of Part 2's machinery. All three end up with a bounded subword vocabulary; they differ in how they choose the inventory and how they mark continuations. If you remember one thing: frequency vs likelihood-gain vs likelihood-loss.
