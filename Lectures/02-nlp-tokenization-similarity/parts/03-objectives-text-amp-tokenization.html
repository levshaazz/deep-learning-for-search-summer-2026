Objectives · text & tokenization
  • ✓Explain why raw text is hard for computers — discreteness, variable length, ambiguity, and the Zipf/Heaps argument that a fixed word vocabulary can never cover real text.

  • ✓Trace a BPE and a WordPiece run by hand, and contrast BPE / WordPiece / Unigram.
These are measurable outcomes — by the end you should be able to do each one on paper. Part 1: why text is hard, the tokenization pipeline, and tracing BPE/WordPiece by hand. The corner cases and the similarity objectives are on the next slide.
