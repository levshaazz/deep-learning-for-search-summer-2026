overview
Fills the gap the old deck skipped (it cut Minkowski/Chebyshev for time). Euclidean P(3,2),Q(4,1): √((1)²+(−1)²)=√2≈1.414. Manhattan P(2,9),Q(3,5): 1+4=5. Minkowski unifies: p=1 L1, p=2 L2, p→∞ max. L1 is more robust to outliers (linear not quadratic penalty) and, per Aggarwal 2001, preserves contrast better in high dimensions — a teaser for the concentration slide. Fractional p<1 is NOT a metric.
