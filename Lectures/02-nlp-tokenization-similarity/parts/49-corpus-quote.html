The reassuring close to Part 3: most corner cases soften when the tokenizer trains on a big, diverse, well-balanced corpus — the digit, code, and rare-word problems shrink, though the multilingual tax and the letter-counting limit are structural and persist. The frontier response is to drop tokenization entirely: token-free, byte-to-byte models like ByT5 are robust to any language and any input, at the cost of much longer sequences. Not yet the industrial standard, but a live research direction.
