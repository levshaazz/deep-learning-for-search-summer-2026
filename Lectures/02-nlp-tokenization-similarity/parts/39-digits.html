Числа: артефакт частот, а не логикаNumbers: a frequency artifact, not logic

почему LLM плохи в арифметикеwhy LLMs are bad at arithmetic

Обучи байтовый BPE на 20 Newsgroups — и буквальное разбиение меняется с размером словаря: при словаре 1k «327» → 3 | 2 | 7 (3 токена); при 4k и 16k «327» → 32 | 7 (2 токена). Разряды перестают выстраиваться — и арифметика страдает.Train a byte-level BPE on 20 Newsgroups and the literal split shifts with vocab size: at 1k vocab "327" → 3 | 2 | 7 (3 tokens); at 4k and 16k, "327" → 32 | 7 (2 tokens). Place values stop lining up — and arithmetic suffers.

This is one of the most counter-intuitive results for students: LLMs are bad at arithmetic partly because of TOKENIZATION, not just reasoning. The split shown is not invented — it is the literal output of `_research/data/bpe_merges.py`, which trains a huggingface byte-level BPE on sklearn's 20 Newsgroups at three vocab sizes and records how "327" gets sliced. LLaMA and PaLM force single-digit tokens to fix this; Singh et al. 2024 show right-to-left comma grouping helps, and multiplication collapses to ~0 accuracy by about six digits.
