Закон Ципфа: несколько слов делают всю работуZipf's law: a few words do all the work

Препятствие 3 · длинный хвостObstacle 3 · the long tail

Частота \(r\)-го по распространённости слова \(\propto r^{-1}\). На 20 Newsgroups (~3.7 M токенов) эмпирический наклон в log–log равен −1.02: топ‑10 слов покрывают 20.6% корпуса, топ‑100 — 47.3%, а топ‑1000 — 71.4% — оставляя огромный хвост слов, встреченных единожды (hapax legomena).The frequency of the \(r\)-th most common word is \(\propto r^{-1}\). On 20 Newsgroups (~3.7 M tokens), the empirical log–log slope is −1.02: the top 10 words cover 20.6% of the corpus, the top 100 cover 47.3%, and the top 1000 cover 71.4% — leaving a vast tail of words seen once (hapax legomena).

Zipf is steep. "the", "of", "and" eat enormous probability mass; then a power-law tail of rare words, most of which appear only once in a given corpus. The slope and coverage numbers cited here are not invented — they are computed by `_research/data/zipf.py` on sklearn's 20 Newsgroups corpus (public domain, headers/footers/quotes stripped). Empirical slope on the top-1000 is −1.018, which is textbook Zipf within rounding. Practical consequence: you can never collect enough text to see every word "enough" times.
