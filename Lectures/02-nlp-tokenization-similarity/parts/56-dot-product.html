Когда его НЕ использовать? Когда длина мешает (например, сырой TF-IDF, где длинные документы получают высокий балл просто за длину) — тогда нормализуй и используй косинус.When NOT to use it? When magnitude is a nuisance (e.g. raw TF-IDF where long documents score high just for being long) — then normalize and use cosine.

The dot product is cosine WITHOUT the normalization, so it rewards both alignment and length. Worked: u=2i−3j, v=4i+2j → 2·4 + (−3)·2 = 8 − 6 = 2. Max-inner-product search (MIPS) is argmax aᵀb and underlies trained dual-encoder retrieval (DPR), where the model deliberately learns meaningful norms. The caveat: don't use raw dot product when length is an artifact rather than signal.
