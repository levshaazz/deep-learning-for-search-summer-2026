Английский ≈ 4 символа/токен.Real vocab sizes: GPT-2 50,257; cl100k (GPT-3.5/4) ~100k; o200k (GPT-4o) ~200k; BERT 30,522; LLaMA / T5 (SentencePiece) 32k. English ≈ 4 chars/token.

You cannot win on all three corners. Push toward characters: vocabulary shrinks to ~256 bytes and OOV vanishes, but sequences blow up ~4–5×, which is brutal under the O(n²) attention cost we define in the transformers lecture (L7). Push toward words: sequences are short, but the vocabulary is unbounded (Heaps) and OOV is severe. Subword sits in the interior — a bounded vocab of 30k–200k, moderate sequence length, near-zero OOV. Bigger vocab → fewer tokens per text → shorter sequences and lower cost, but a bigger embedding matrix and softmax.
