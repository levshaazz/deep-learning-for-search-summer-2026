The reference table — seven rows, keep it on screen while talking. Two facts students miss: (1) cosine and dot DISTANCE are not metrics (cosine fails the triangle inequality; the angular distance arccos(cos)/π is a metric); (2) Pearson is literally cosine after mean-centering, which is why it removes per-user rating bias in recommendation. Hamming and Mahalanobis are honorable mentions (binary codes; correlated/whitened features).
