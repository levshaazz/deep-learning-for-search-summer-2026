Trailing-space sensitivity

" the" and "the" are different tokens. A prompt ending in a trailing space lands off-distribution and degrades the completion.

!
Watch your prompt endings

Trim trailing whitespace unless you specifically want the "continuation" token.

Why spelling is hard

"How many R's in strawberry?" and "reverse this string" are hard because the model never sees individual letters — only opaque token chunks — under cl100k strawberry is str + aw + berry.

Character-level operations require the model to have memorized the letter content of each token. It is not looking at letters at run time.

Both quirks come from the same fact: the model's atoms are tokens, not characters. " the" ≠ "the" because the leading-space token is a distinct id (byte-level BPE design from earlier). And counting letters in "strawberry" fails because the model sees "str"+"aw"+"berry" (cl100k), not s-t-r-a-w-... — it would have to have learned the spelling of each token. This is why letter-counting and string-reversal trip up otherwise strong models.
