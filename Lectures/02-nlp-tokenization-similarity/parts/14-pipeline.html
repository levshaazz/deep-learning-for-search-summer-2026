Today we go deep on the two starred boxes: tokenization, and the similarity that vectors finally make possible.

This is the whole map (after Raschka's "LLMs from scratch"). Normalization is housekeeping; tokenization turns strings into reusable units; numericalization just looks up integer ids; embeddings turn ids into vectors (next weeks). Once we are in vector space, "relevant" becomes "close" — and that is Part 4. Concretely: "This is an example." might become ids like [40134, 2052, 133, 389, 12].
