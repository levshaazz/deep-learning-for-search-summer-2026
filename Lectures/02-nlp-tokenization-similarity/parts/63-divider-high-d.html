When high dimensions
break intuition

Concentration · hubness · anisotropy — why naive nearest-neighbour can mislead.

A short, sharp coda. The measures are clean in 2D; in the hundreds-to-thousands of dimensions where real embeddings live, three effects quietly degrade them. The knights' nemesis appears here — the Curse-of-Dimensionality Wraith, who flattens every distance until far and near look the same. Knowing these three effects separates someone who can plug in cosine from someone who can debug a retrieval system.
