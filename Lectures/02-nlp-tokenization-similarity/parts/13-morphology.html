«из ваших домов» — одно написанное слово, четыре морфемы. Словарю слов потребовалась бы отдельная запись для каждой такой комбинации."from your houses" — one written word, four morphemes. A word vocabulary would need a separate entry for every such combination.

  • ·Даже английский: «New York», «can't», URL-адреса, #хештеги.Even English: "New York", "can't", URLs, #hashtags.
Most of the planet does not put a space between every meaning-unit. Agglutinative languages glue morphemes; CJK and Thai have no spaces at all. So "tokenize = split on whitespace" is parochial. This pushes us toward a method that learns its units from data and works on any script — which is exactly what subword tokenizers (especially SentencePiece) do.
