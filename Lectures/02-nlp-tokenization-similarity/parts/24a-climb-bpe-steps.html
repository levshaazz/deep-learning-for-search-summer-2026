Цикл BPE своими руками: посчитай — слей — повториThe BPE loop by hand: count — merge — repeat

шаг (←/→): пять слияний подряд; читай счётчик пар до того, как выбиратьstep (←/→): five merges end to end; read the pair tally before you pick

Определение с прошлого слайда — теперь как живой цикл: посчитай пары, слей самую частую, перетокенизируй и повтори. Смотри, как суффикс собирает себя сам, кусок за куском, без единого правила, написанного руками. К последнему шагу незнакомое слово разложится на уже готовые куски — без всякого [UNK].The definition from the previous slide, now run as a live loop: count the pairs, merge the most frequent one, re-tokenize, repeat. Watch a suffix assemble itself piece by piece, with not a single hand-written rule. By the last step an unseen word falls apart into already-learned pieces — no [UNK] anywhere.

Act 2’s main climb: the full BPE training loop on a tiny corpus, five merges end to end, read from data/l2-bpe-steps.json — every tally and every merge is precomputed there, the browser only replays it. The rhythm matters more than any single step: always READ the count table aloud before revealing the pick, so students see the pick CAUSED by the counts. Stumbling points: the end-of-word marker (why it exists — so suffixes can bind to the boundary), and ties (broken by rule order, which is also why trained BPE is deterministic but not unique — fore-echoes the misconception slide later in this act). After this loop, the compression-intuition slide and the real-corpus slide that follow land almost for free.
