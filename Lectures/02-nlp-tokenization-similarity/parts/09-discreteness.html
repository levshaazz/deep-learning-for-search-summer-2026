Пиксели интерполируются; символы — нетPixels interpolate; symbols do not

Препятствие 1 · дискретностьObstacle 1 · discreteness

Серый ровно между чёрным и белым осмыслен; «слово посередине между cat и dog» — нет. У символов нет собственной метрики — в этом и есть главная мотивация обучения представлений.A grey halfway between black and white is meaningful; a "word halfway between cat and dog" is not. Symbols have no inherent metric — that is the core motivation for representation learning.

This is the deepest obstacle. Pixel space is continuous and already has a metric; you can interpolate and the result is valid. Symbol space is discrete: "cat" and "dog" share no letters with their meanings, and there is no valid point "between" them. So we cannot read distance off the strings — we must manufacture a vector space where similar meanings sit close. Hold that thought; it is exactly Part 4.
