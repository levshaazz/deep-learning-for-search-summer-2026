Range \([-1,1]\) in general; \([0,1]\) for non-negative vectors (e.g. word counts). Cosine is the cosine of the angle between vectors. Because it divides out both norms, it cares only about direction — a long document and a short one on the same topic can score 1.0. That magnitude-invariance is its defining feature and the source of the misconception later. The three-angle strip: aligned → 1, orthogonal → 0, opposite → −1.
