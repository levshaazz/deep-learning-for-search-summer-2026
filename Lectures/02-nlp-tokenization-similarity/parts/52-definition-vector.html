Vector representation · embeddingembedding

Represent a piece of text as a vector in \(\mathbb{R}^{d}\), such that similar meanings map to nearby vectors.

How to learn good embeddings is the subject of coming weeks. Today we assume we already have vectors, and ask: how do we compare them?
