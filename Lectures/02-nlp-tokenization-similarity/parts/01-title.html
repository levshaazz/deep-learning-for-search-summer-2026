First contact. Séréga, the doodle guide from the briefing, is back — and this time he wakes up alone, light-years from home, with another mind beside him but no shared words, no alphabet, no grammar. That is exactly the problem a machine faces with human language. Today we take the first two steps toward making a machine "understand": cut messy text into computable units (tokenization), and measure how close two meanings are (similarity).
