Схема WinogradWinograd schema

«The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big». — it = кубок (the trophy)."The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big." — it = the trophy.

Поменяй одно слово: «…because it was too small». — теперь it = чемодан (the suitcase).Flip one word: "…because it was too small." — now it = the suitcase.

Строка почти не изменилась; смысл местоимения перевернулся. Чтобы это разрешить, нужны знания о мире, а не только символы.The string barely changed; the meaning of the pronoun flipped. Resolving this requires world knowledge, not just the symbols.

Ambiguity is not an edge case; it is the normal state of language. The Winograd schema is the cleanest demo: a single adjective flip moves the referent. No purely lexical system can solve it — you need a model of the world (big things don't fit in small containers). This is why "understanding" is hard and why context-sensitive embeddings matter later.
