$$a=(1,2,2),\quad b=(2,0,1)$$

Two tiny vectors in \(\mathbb{R}^{3}\). Press → to compute \(\cos\theta\).

$$a\cdot b=\sum_i a_i b_i$$

Componentwise product, then sum.

$$\lVert a\rVert=\sqrt{\textstyle\sum_i a_i^2},\quad \lVert b\rVert=\sqrt{\textstyle\sum_i b_i^2}$$

Length of each vector.

$$\cos\theta=\dfrac{a\cdot b}{\lVert a\rVert\,\lVert b\rVert}$$

Divide.

$$\cos\theta\in[-1,1]$$

\(1\) same, \(0\) orthogonal, \(-1\) opposite.

Numbers

↓ Concrete arithmetic at each step.

\(a\cdot b = 1\cdot2 + 2\cdot0 + 2\cdot1 = 4\)
\(\lVert a\rVert=\sqrt{1+4+4}=\sqrt{9}=3\)
\(\lVert b\rVert=\sqrt{4+0+1}=\sqrt{5}\approx2.236\)
\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{4}{3\sqrt{5}}=\dfrac{4}{6.708}\approx0.596\)
\(\cos\theta\approx 0.60\)

Moderately similar (angle \(\approx 53^\circ\)).
