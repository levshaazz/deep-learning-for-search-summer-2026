Byte-Pair EncodingBPE

Начни с отдельных символов; многократно сливай самую частую соседнюю пару в новый символ, записывая каждое правило слияния, пока словарь не достигнет целевого размера.Start from single characters; repeatedly merge the most frequent adjacent pair into a new symbol, recording each merge rule, until the vocabulary reaches a target size.

Происхождение: Philip Gage, «A New Algorithm for Data Compression» (1994). В NLP принесли Sennrich, Haddow & Birch (2016) для машинного перевода. Жадный, частотно-управляемый, детерминированный.Origin: Philip Gage, "A New Algorithm for Data Compression" (1994). Brought to NLP by Sennrich, Haddow & Birch (2016) for machine translation. Greedy, frequency-driven, deterministic.

BPE began as a compression trick: replace the most common byte pair with an unused byte, repeat. Sennrich realized the same loop gives an open-vocabulary tokenizer for NMT. Two phases: TRAIN (learn an ordered list of merges from a corpus), and ENCODE (apply those merges in order to any new text). The next two slides are worked examples of the training loop. Picture Tokenosaurus — the friendly dino who snips long words into reusable chunks; BPE is just his rule for deciding where to bite.
