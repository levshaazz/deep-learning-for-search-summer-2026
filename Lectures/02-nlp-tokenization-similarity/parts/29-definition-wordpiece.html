WordPieceBERT's tokenizer

Как BPE, но каждое слияние выбирается по оценке правдоподобия, а не по сырой частоте, и внутрисловные куски помечаются префиксом-продолжением ##.Like BPE, but it chooses each merge by a likelihood score rather than raw frequency, and marks word-internal pieces with a ## continuation prefix.

Schuster & Nakajima (2012), для голосового поиска на японском/корейском; принят в BERT. «playing» → play + ##ing; «tokenization» → token + ##ization.Schuster & Nakajima (2012), for Japanese/Korean voice search; adopted by BERT. "playing" → play + ##ing; "tokenization" → token + ##ization.

WordPiece is BPE's cousin. The single conceptual difference: instead of merging the most FREQUENT pair, it merges the pair that most increases the likelihood of the corpus under a unigram model — i.e. pairs that co-occur more than chance. The ## marker tells the model "this piece continues the previous word", which preserves word boundaries for tasks like NER. Next slide: the exact score.
