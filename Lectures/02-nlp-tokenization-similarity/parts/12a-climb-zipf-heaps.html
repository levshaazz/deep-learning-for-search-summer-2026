Оба закона с двух предыдущих слайдов — теперь на одном настоящем корпусе. Прощёлкай шаги и убедись: частоты ложатся на прямую в лог-лог осях, а словарь так и не насыщается, сколько текста ни подкладывай. Это не курьёз статистики, а причина, по которой во второй части лекции появятся подслова.Both laws from the previous two slides — now on one real corpus. Step through and see it: the frequencies fall on a straight line in log-log axes, and the vocabulary never saturates no matter how much text you feed it. That is not a statistical curiosity — it is the reason sub-words appear in part two of this lecture.

Act 1’s climb, right after the two law slides. Every point is read from data/l2-corpus-stats.json (a real 20 Newsgroups count) — nothing is fitted in the browser. Steps: (0) raw counts — a handful of words does all the work; (1) the log-log straight line; (2) the slope and how much the top words swallow; (3) Heaps’ curve — DWELL here and ask “so when does the vocabulary stop growing?”; the answer (never) is the bridge to tokenization in act 2. Students stumble on reading log-log axes — spend a moment at step 1 explaining why a power law becomes a line.
