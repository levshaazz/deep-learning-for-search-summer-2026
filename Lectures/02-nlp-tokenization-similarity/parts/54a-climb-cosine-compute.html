Косинус по шагам: длина сокращаетсяCosine, stepped: the length cancels out

шаг (←/→): два вектора → скалярное произведение → длины → их произведение → делениеstep (←/→): two vectors → dot product → the norms → their product → the division

Формула с прошлого слайда, прожитая по одному действию за шаг. Два вектора смотрят в одну сторону, но один во много раз длиннее — и знаменатель съедает ровно эту разницу длин. Остаётся чистое направление: то самое свойство, за которое поиск и выбирает косинус.The formula from the previous slide, lived one operation per step. The two vectors point the same way, but one is many times longer — and the denominator eats exactly that difference in length. What survives is pure direction: the very property search picks cosine for.

Act 4’s first climb, wedged between the formula and the by-hand walkthrough that follows. The two vectors and every intermediate value come from data/l2-cosine.json. Steps: (0) the two count vectors — same direction, wildly different loudness; (1) the dot product; (2) the two norms — ugly radicals on purpose; (3) their product — the radicals cancel into a clean number, this is the PUNCHLINE, slow down here; (4) the division — magnitude gone, direction left. Students stumble on why we bother with the denominator at all; the loud-vs-quiet framing at step 0 sets that up.
