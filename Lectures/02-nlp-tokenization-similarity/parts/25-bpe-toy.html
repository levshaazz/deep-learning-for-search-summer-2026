Gage's original compression example, exactly. aaabdaaabac (11) → Zabd Zabac (aa→Z) → ZYd ZYac (ab→Y) → XdXac (ZY→X), 5 symbols. The loop is trivial: count adjacent pairs, merge the top one, repeat. The same loop, run on a word corpus with frequencies, gives the subword tokenizer on the next slide.
