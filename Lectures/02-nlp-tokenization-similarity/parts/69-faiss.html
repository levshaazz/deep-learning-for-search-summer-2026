Численные тонкостиNumerics

Косинус не определён для нулевого вектора (добавь \(\varepsilon\)); KL требует сглаживания нулевых бинов; нормируй один раз заранее и храни единичные векторы.Cosine is undefined for a zero vector (add \(\varepsilon\)); KL needs smoothing for zero bins; pre-normalize once and store unit vectors.

The deployment reality. FAISS returns squared L2 (cheaper, same ordering) and has no cosine metric — you L2-normalize and use inner product. The single most common production bug is forgetting to normalize, which silently turns your "cosine" search into dot-product search that rewards long vectors. And always match the index metric to the model's training objective; a cosine-trained sentence model behaves oddly under raw dot. Numerics: guard the zero vector with ε, smooth KL's zero bins.
