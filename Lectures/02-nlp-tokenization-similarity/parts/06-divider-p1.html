Teaching a machine
to read

Два разума, ни одного общего символа. До смысла, до грамматики — нам нужны единицы.Two minds, no shared symbols. Before meaning, before grammar — we need units.

Séréga is stranded with an alien engineer. Neither knows a word of the other's language. The breakthrough is mechanical, not magical: pin a symbol to a thing, then build up. A machine reading text starts in the same place — it sees a stream of bytes, not words.
