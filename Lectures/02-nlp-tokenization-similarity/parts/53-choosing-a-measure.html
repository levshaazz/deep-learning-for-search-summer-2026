Which measure? A decision guide

match the measure to the data
If your data is……andthen use
Text / embeddings (non-numerical)direction matters, length should notCosine
Continuous featureson the same scaleEuclidean \(L_2\)
Continuous featuresdifferent scales / outliersManhattan \(L_1\)
Categorical / setsoverlap of membersJaccard
Ordinal (ranks)monotonic agreementSpearman
Multivariatecorrelated, mean-centredPearson

A rough but useful map (ported from Lecture 1). It is heuristic, not law — the real choice depends on how your embeddings were trained (next slides). But it anchors the toolbox: cosine for direction/text, L2 for scaled continuous features, L1 when scales differ or outliers bite, Jaccard for sets, Spearman/Pearson for ranks and correlated multivariate data.
