How close are
two meanings?

Measures of similarity — a ruler for meaning.

We can now turn text into tokens, ids, and vectors. But search needs more than vectors — it needs to RANK, and to rank you must COMPARE. This is where the LOTR arc opens: Sir Cosine and the Knights of the Unit Sphere set out to measure the angle between meanings. This part builds the ruler, then stress-tests it in high dimensions where intuition fails.
