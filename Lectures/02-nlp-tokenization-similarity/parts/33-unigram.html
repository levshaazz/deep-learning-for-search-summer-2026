Two ideas that are often conflated. Unigram is an ALGORITHM (prune a big vocab by likelihood loss). SentencePiece is a TOOLKIT that can run either BPE or Unigram and, crucially, treats the space as a normal symbol (▁), which removes the English-centric "split on spaces" assumption from Part 1. That reversibility is why SentencePiece dominates multilingual models.
