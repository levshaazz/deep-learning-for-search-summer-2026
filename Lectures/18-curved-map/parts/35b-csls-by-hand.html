In distance form CSLS_d = 2d − r̄(q) − r̄(y) with lower better: 0.8979 for the hub against −0.4631 for #58, so the rank flips with a margin of 1.3611 — computed on unrounded values, which is why subtracting the four-place figures printed on the slide differs in the last digit. The arithmetic is visible AND the doubling is now printed (late review §3): the distance term carries a factor 2, so the hub's 0.2609 raw-distance edge counts as 2 × 0.2609 = 0.5218 against a 1.8829 density penalty, and 1.8829 − 0.5218 = 1.3611. Without the 2 a student subtracts 1.8829 − 0.2609 and gets 1.6220, which matches nothing on the slide.
