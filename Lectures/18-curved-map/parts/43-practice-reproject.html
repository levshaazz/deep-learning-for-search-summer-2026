The practitioner's checklist, step 1 — re-project the space: center+whiten (Su 2021, free on frozen embeddings) or contrastive-train (SimCSE, isotropy built in), so the wedge opens, axes de-correlate and cosine is meaningful again. This fixes the CONE (global shape) only; the neighbour graph is a separate failure treated by step 2 (CSLS). The two steps are complementary, not either/or.
