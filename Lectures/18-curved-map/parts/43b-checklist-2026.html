Одной строкой: всё, чему учит эта лекция, верно для сырых представлений языковой модели — и требует проверки на твоём эмбеддере. «Случайные пары ≈ 0,99» — про GPT-2; у BERT около \(0{,}51\). «Хабность — болезнь» верна на евклиде и лишь наполовину — в графе ANN.In one line: everything this lecture teaches is true of raw language-model representations — and needs checking on your embedder. "Random pairs ≈ 0.99" is about GPT-2; BERT is around \(0.51\). "Hubness is a disease" holds under Euclidean distance and only half-holds inside an ANN graph.

Plan NEW-32 — the takeaway slide, and the one that keeps the lecture from ageing badly. The five rules are the operational residue of the 2024–2026 literature: (1) the dose-response curve says remove the mean DIRECTION, not the covariance; (2) full PCA whitening is negative on all five modern embedders measured; (3) mean random-pair cosine fails 4 of IsoScore's 6 requirements, so skew of N_k is the better instrument; (4) hubness corrections pay off only above S_{N₁₀} ≈ 1.4 (Feldbauer & Flexer 2019), and inside an NSW graph hubs are load-bearing; (5) QB-Norm's foreign-bank row, 13.4 against a 14.9 baseline. The framing sentence matters as much as the list: the lecture's conclusions are sound for RAW LM representations, weakened for contrastive embedders and locally inverted for whitening — say it out loud, because the alternative is students applying 2021 advice to a 2026 encoder.
