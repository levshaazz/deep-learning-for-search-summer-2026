Mu & Viswanath 2018, the "why" half: the dominant principal axes encode word frequency, not semantics, so removing them de-biases the space. Toy (our measurement): dropping the dominant direction keeps random-pair cosine flat at −0.0352 ≈ 0 (cf. centeredCos −0.0323). Cheaper but coarser than whitening — it only kills the axes you delete by hand, while whitening rescales all axes at once.
