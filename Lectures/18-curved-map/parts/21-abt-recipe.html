Честная строка: ремонт не бесплатен. У GloVe на RG65 качество падает, \(76{,}96 \rightarrow 74{,}36\) — верхние оси несут не только частоту. Это самая дешёвая из трёх операций, но и самая грубая: она убивает ровно те оси, которые ты назвал руками. Отбеливание трогает все сразу — и потому рискует сильнее.The honest line: the repair is not free. GloVe on RG65 goes down, \(76.96 \rightarrow 74.36\) — the top axes do not carry only frequency. It is the cheapest of the three operations and the bluntest: it kills exactly the axes you name by hand. Whitening touches all of them at once — and so risks more.

Mu & Viswanath 2018 "all-but-the-top", recipe + why merged (plan N2, the 21+22 salami pair; the "top PCs = frequency" claim is already made on slide 13, so it is not repeated here — only its number). Recipe: subtract the common mean, project out the top ≈ d/100 principal components. Our toy value is now the HONEST one: −0.0313, from projecting out the top PC of the RAW (un-centred) cloud, with |cos(PC1, 1_d)| = 0.9997 showing why it works. The previous −0.0352 came from zeroing coordinate 0 of an already-centred vector, which is neither the top PC nor the raw cloud (plan F1). Real D values: 3 for word2vec and 2 for GloVe at d = 300; Timkey uses 7 at d = 768. And the honest counter-cell: GloVe RG65 drops 76.96 → 74.36 — evidence that dominant axes are not purely frequency, which the next act's "isotropy is not the goal" slide develops.
