Практическая кривая (nomic-embed-text-v1.5, MTEB): \(768 \rightarrow 62{,}28\) · \(256 \rightarrow 61{,}04\) · \(64 \rightarrow 56{,}10\) — то есть \(3\times\) сжатия стоит \(1{,}2\) пункта, а \(12\times\) уже \(6{,}2\). Kusupati et al., NeurIPS 2022: at 8 dimensions MRL scores 62.19 on 1-NN against FF+SVD 19.14 and a JL projection 0.11 (Table 2); on linear probing 66.63 against random-LP 4.56 and SVD 2.34 (Table 1). The pedagogical point is the reconciliation: slide 13 says the top PRINCIPAL axes encode frequency, MRL says the first coordinates carry the most meaning — both true, because PCA orders by variance while MRL orders by a training objective. Two footnotes worth knowing. The famous "14×" is a cascade number (§4.2: 36.26 expected dimensions ≈ FF-512); Appendix D.1's total-dimension accounting gives 62, i.e. 8.2×. And OpenAI publishes no per-dimension table for text-embedding-3, so the curve here is nomic-embed-text-v1.5's published one. Sharpest counterpoint (Tsukagoshi & Sasano 2025): naively truncating a NON-MRL model also works well for classification/clustering — gte-Qwen2 hits 76.34 on classification at 2 dimensions — so MRL's real value concentrates in retrieval and STS.
