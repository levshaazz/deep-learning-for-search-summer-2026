Соблазнительное убеждение The tempting belief

«Конус образуют редкие подслова: зипфов хвост толкает их в общий угол — значит, лечим частотный перекос». “The cone is made of rare subwords: the Zipfian tail pushes them into one corner — so we treat a frequency bias.”

Анизотропия есть у моделей без зипфова хвоста подслов — символьных и байтовых (ByT5, CANINE, MANTa) — и в каждом слое каждой речевой модели. Даже необученный блок трансформера уже поднимает косинус. Вывод Godey 2024: анизотропия — цена острого внимания, а не побочный эффект словаря. Anisotropy appears in models with no Zipfian subword tail — character- and byte-level (ByT5, CANINE, MANTa) — and in every layer of every speech model. Even an untrained transformer block already raises the cosine. Godey 2024's conclusion: anisotropy is the price of sharp attention, not a vocabulary artefact.

И механизм проще, чем конус. Timkey 2021: косинусом правят 1–5 «изгойных» координат. У XLNet L11 одна координата даёт 99,6 % ожидаемого косинуса: её средняя активация 180,0 против \(-0{,}084\) (\(\sigma = 0{,}77\)) у всех остальных. Убери 5 координат — и \(\hat{A}\) не превышает \(0{,}25\) ни в одном слое ни одной модели. А в GPT-2 и RoBERTa два токена на позиции 0 дают \(> 0{,}99\) против \(0{,}623\) и \(0{,}564\) в остальных позициях: знаменитое «0,99» во многом позиционный артефакт. Рецепт меняется: убрать несколько координат \(\ne\) отбелить ковариацию. And the mechanism is simpler than a cone. Timkey 2021: cosine is dominated by 1–5 rogue dimensions. In XLNet layer 11 one coordinate supplies 99.6% of the expected cosine: its mean activation is 180.0 against \(-0.084\) (\(\sigma = 0.77\)) for every other. Drop 5 dimensions and \(\hat{A}\) never exceeds \(0.25\) in any layer of any model. And in GPT-2 and RoBERTa two tokens at position 0 read \(> 0.99\) against \(0.623\) and \(0.564\) elsewhere: the famous "0.99" is largely a positional artefact. The recipe changes: dropping a few coordinates \(\ne\) whitening a covariance.

The competing explanation act 01 used to be missing (plan F0-f, R2). Two independent lines. (a) Godey, de la Clergerie & Sagot, EACL 2024: anisotropy exists in character/byte models (ByT5, CANINE, MANTa) that have no rare-subword tail at all, and in every layer of every speech model (mean cosine ≥ 0.2); an untrained transformer block already raises it. That kills the Zipfian explanation as THE cause and reframes anisotropy as the price of sharp attention. (b) Timkey & van Schijndel, EMNLP 2021: 1–5 "rogue dimensions" dominate cosine; XLNet L11 has one dimension (667) contributing 99.6% of the expected cosine, with mean activation 180.0 against −0.084 (σ=0.77) elsewhere; removing 5 dimensions caps at 0.25 across all layers and models; and position-0 tokens read >0.99 vs 0.623/0.564. This is not a footnote: it changes the prescription — projecting out a handful of coordinates is not the same operation as whitening a covariance. Values in data/l18-bench.json → anisotropy.rogueDims.
