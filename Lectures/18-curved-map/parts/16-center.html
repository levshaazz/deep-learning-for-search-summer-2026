Центрирование — самая дешёвая половина ремонта. Но оно лишь двигает карту: перекос между осями (корреляции) остаётся — его снимет отбеливание.Centering is the cheapest half of the repair. But it only slides the map: the skew between axes (correlations) stays — whitening removes that.

Step 1 of the re-projection: subtract the mean μ. The cone points away from the origin, inflating cosine to every point; centering puts the origin inside the crowd. Toy (our measurement, d=10, n=80): random-pair cosine 0.8985 → −0.0323, i.e. ≈0.90 → ≈0, isotropy recovered on the diagonal. Centering is necessary but incomplete — it does not touch cross-axis correlation, which whitening (next) fixes.
