Одна линейная перепроекция поднимает STS-B с \(59{,}04\) до \(71{,}34\) — обучать нечего. А вариант whitening-256 ещё и режет вектор с 768 до 256 измерений: карта ровнее и легче.One linear re-projection lifts STS-B from \(59.04\) to \(71.34\) — nothing is trained. And whitening-256 also cuts the vector from 768 to 256 dims: the map is flatter and lighter.

Su 2021 BERT-whitening, reported STS-B Spearman ρ×100: raw BERT cosine 59.04 → full whitening 71.34, achieved by a training-free linear re-projection to identity covariance. The whitening-256 variant keeps a comparable score while shrinking the embedding from 768 to 256 dimensions — flatter map plus a smaller/faster index. Numbers are reported (Su 2021), not our toy.
