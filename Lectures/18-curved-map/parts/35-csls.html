\(r(x)=\tfrac{1}{K}\sum_{y'\in\mathcal{N}_K(x)}\cos(x,y')\), \(K\!=\!10\). Вычитаем её — точка в плотной толпе (хаб, большое \(r\)) штрафуется, а анти-хаб (малое \(r\)) поднимается. \(r(x)=\tfrac{1}{K}\sum_{y'\in\mathcal{N}_K(x)}\cos(x,y')\), \(K\!=\!10\). Subtract it: a point in a dense crowd (a hub, large \(r\)) is penalized, an anti-hub (small \(r\)) is boosted.

CSLS(x,y) = 2·cos(x,y) − r_T(x) − r_S(y), where r(x) is the mean cosine to x's K=10 nearest neighbours — its local density. Conneau et al. 2018. The 2× keeps the raw similarity comparable to the two density penalties. Subtracting each side's crowding demotes hubs (large r) and promotes anti-hubs (small r): a symmetric, retrieval-time correction, no retraining.
