Контрастивное обучение уплощает сингулярный спектр: изотропия не залатана постфактум, а выучена вместе с картой. Без учителя \(76{,}3\), с учителем \(81{,}6\) — против прежнего потолка \(72{,}05\).Contrastive training flattens the singular spectrum: isotropy is learned with the map, not patched on afterward. Unsup \(76.3\), sup \(81.6\) — versus the prior ceiling of \(72.05\).

Gao 2021 SimCSE, reported STS average Spearman ρ×100: prev SOTA 72.05 → SimCSE unsup 76.3 (dropout as the only augmentation) → SimCSE sup 81.6 (NLI entailment pairs as positives). Mechanism: the contrastive objective flattens the singular-value spectrum of the representation matrix, so isotropy is trained IN rather than restored by post-hoc whitening. All numbers reported (Gao 2021), not our toy.
