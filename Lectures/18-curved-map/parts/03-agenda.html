Five acts tracing one distorted map from defect to cure. (1) Anisotropy: contextual embeddings live in a narrow cone (Ethayarajh 2019, random words cos≈0.99), so cosine loses resolution and measures string overlap (Li 2020). (2) Re-project: center → whiten (Su 2021, STS-B 59.04→71.34) → contrastive isotropy (SimCSE, Gao 2021). (3) Hubness: N_k skew rises with dimension (Radovanović 2010), hubs sit near the centroid. (4) The crowding tax CSLS = 2cos − r_T − r_S (Conneau 2018, word-translation P@1 42.6→66.1). (5) Why retrieval breaks: false neighbours + Romes/ghosts hit L7/L9/L13; whitening and CSLS are complementary. Anchors approximate.
