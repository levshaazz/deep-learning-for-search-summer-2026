Worked example W1 (plan §4). Four cities (9,7) (7,9) (3,5) (5,3), μ = (6,6). Stage 1, raw: the six pairwise cosines are 0.9692 · 0.9326 · 0.9927 · 0.9927 · 0.9326 · 0.8824, mean 0.9504 — even the loosest pair reads 0.88, which is a cone. Stage 2, centred: 0.60 · −1 · −0.60 · −0.60 · −1 · 0.60, mean −0.3333 — the antipodes are finally truthful but the STRANGERS still read 0.60. Stage 3, the reason: Σ = [[5,3],[3,5]], trace 10, determinant 16 ⇒ λ₁ = 8, λ₂ = 2, condition number 4; the off-diagonal 3 is the residual shear.
