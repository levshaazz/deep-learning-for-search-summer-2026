Коррекция хабности оценивается по распределению запросов. Ошибись с ним — и потеряешь \(1{,}5\) пункта против «не делать ничего», хотя приём «работает». В той же статье продакшн-форма CSLS через банк запросов даёт R@1 \(29{,}6 \rightarrow 33{,}3\) (MSR-VTT), \(21{,}6 \rightarrow 24{,}2\) (DiDeMo), \(30{,}3 \rightarrow 34{,}8\) (MSCoCo t\(\rightarrow\)i) — но всего \(64{,}4 \rightarrow 64{,}8\) на image\(\rightarrow\)image CUB-200, где хабов почти нет. И механизм подтверждён напрямую: скос \(k\)-вхождений \(0{,}939 \rightarrow 0{,}509\).A hubness correction is estimated from the query distribution. Get that wrong and you lose \(1.5\) points against doing nothing, even though the technique "works". In the same paper the production form of CSLS via a query bank gives R@1 \(29.6 \rightarrow 33.3\) (MSR-VTT), \(21.6 \rightarrow 24.2\) (DiDeMo), \(30.3 \rightarrow 34.8\) (MSCoCo t\(\rightarrow\)i) — but only \(64.4 \rightarrow 64.8\) on image\(\rightarrow\)image CUB-200, where there are barely any hubs. And the mechanism is confirmed directly: the \(k\)-occurrence skew falls \(0.939 \rightarrow 0.509\).

Plan R3, NEW-29 + NEW-30 merged — the best boundaries slide available anywhere in this literature. Bogolin et al., QB-Norm (CVPR 2022) Table 2 on MSR-VTT/TT-CE+: no normalisation 14.9 → CSLS 16.8 → inverted softmax 17.1, but with a query bank drawn from a FOREIGN domain CSLS collapses to 13.4 and IS to 11.6, i.e. BELOW the do-nothing baseline. The lesson generalises past CSLS: any correction estimated from a distribution fails when that distribution is wrong. Note it is not transduction — their Table 1 shows a bank drawn from the training set captures ~97% of the oracle gain. The gains half (same paper): MSR-VTT 1k-A 29.6 → 33.3, DiDeMo 21.6 → 24.2, MSCoCo 5k t→i CLIP ViT-B/32 30.3 → 34.8, and the honest near-null case, image→image CUB-200 64.4 → 64.8. Mechanism confirmed by their Table 3: k-occurrence skew 0.939 → 0.509 on MSR-VTT and 1.21 → 0.39 on DiDeMo — the same quantity our own toy reports, on real embeddings.
