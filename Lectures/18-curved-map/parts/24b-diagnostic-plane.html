Теперь главное. В §5 той же статьи прямо сказано: BERT-flow и BERT-whitening уплощают сингулярный спектр ЕЩЁ сильнее, чем SimCSE — и всё равно проигрывают ему десяток пунктов. Значит, отбеливание проиграло не по изотропии. Оно покупает равномерность за счёт выравненности, а STS меряет именно выравненность. Изотропия — диагностика, а не цель.Now the point. §5 of the same paper says it outright: BERT-flow and BERT-whitening flatten the singular spectrum EVEN HARDER than SimCSE does — and still lose to it by ten points. So whitening did not lose on isotropy. It buys uniformity by spending alignment, and STS measures alignment. Isotropy is a diagnostic, not a goal.

The single most valuable idea in the act, and it was absent (plan R6, NEW-12+13 merged). Gao 2021 Fig. 3 plots every method on the alignment/uniformity plane with its STS average attached: Avg. BERT 56.7 · BERT-flow 66.6 · BERT-whitening 66.3 · SBERT 74.9 · unsup SimCSE 76.3 · SBERT-whitening 77.0 · sup SimCSE 81.6. The authors' own four readings: the pretrained model has good alignment and poor uniformity; flow and whitening greatly improve uniformity but DEGRADE alignment; unsupervised SimCSE improves uniformity while PRESERVING alignment; supervision adds alignment. Then §5 and Fig. F.1: the InfoNCE denominator minimises an upper bound on Sum(WWᵀ), flattening the top singular value — but "BERT-flow or BERT-whitening flatten the curve EVEN MORE". Conclusion: whitening did not lose the isotropy race, it won it and still lost the task, because isotropy is not what STS rewards.
