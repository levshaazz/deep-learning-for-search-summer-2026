Все шесть — одна и та же поломка: смысл чанка держится на словах за его границей. Лекции обычно знают ровно первый вид; чинятся все шесть сразу. All six are one and the same break: the chunk’s meaning rests on words outside its boundary. Most write-ups know only the first; all six are repaired by the same move.

Taxonomy slide — widens the problem beyond the pronoun the deck has been showing. Six kinds of severed dependency: anaphora, definite description, an acronym expanded once, a unit that lives in a table header, ellipsis, and a negation/condition carried over from the previous paragraph. Constructed examples in the style of the paper's Table 1/Table 4. The point: the coreference gap is a special case of "the chunk's meaning lives outside its boundary", and late chunking repairs the whole class, not just pronouns.
