The practical takeaway (part 2 of 2 — the swap). The only change is that per-chunk pooling happens after full-document attention, at index time — no LLM call, no extra store, no retraining, training-free. Reranker, generator, and ANN index all stay as-is. In contrast to ColBERT (which shifts cost onto the query), here nothing at query time gets more expensive.
