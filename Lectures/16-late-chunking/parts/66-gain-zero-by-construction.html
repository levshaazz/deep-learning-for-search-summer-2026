Quora: три одинаковых числа — побайтово. Документ короче одного чанка, у него нет ни соседей, ни границ — восстанавливать нечего. Это не «приём слабо помог», это тождество: при \(g = 0\) закон разрыва даёт ноль сирот, и все три конвейера — один и тот же конвейер. Quora: three identical numbers, digit for digit. The document is shorter than one chunk, so it has neither neighbours nor boundaries — there is nothing to restore. This is not “the trick helped a little”, it is an identity: at \(g = 0\) the gap law gives zero orphans, and all three pipelines are the same pipeline.

Вторая крайность неудобнее. На двух наборах из пяти «не чанковать вовсе» обходит позднее чанкование. Колонка «No Chunking» есть в v1 и в README репозитория — и исчезла из v2 и v3. Смотри на первую версию статьи: там чаще всего лежит то, что потом убрали. The second extreme is more awkward. On two of five datasets “do not chunk at all” beats late chunking. The “No Chunking” column exists in v1 and in the repository README — and disappeared from v2 and v3. Look at a paper’s first version: that is usually where what was later removed still lives.

Two boundary cases where the arithmetic settles the question before any experiment. Quora: documents average ~62 characters, so naive = late = no-chunking = 87.19, identical to the digit — at g = 0 the gap law returns zero orphans and the three pipelines coincide. Then the uncomfortable one: on TRECCOVID (65.18 vs 64.70) and NFCorpus (30.40 vs 29.98) simply NOT chunking beats late chunking. That column is present in arXiv v1 and in the repository README and absent from v2 and v3 — worth saying out loud both as a fact and as a research habit: read v1, it often contains what was later dropped.
