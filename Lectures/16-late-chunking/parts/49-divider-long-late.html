Весь приём держится на одной фразе: «прочитай весь документ». А если документ длиннее окна модели? Тогда нужен второй алгоритм — и он не про качество, а про то, как честно расплатиться за перекрытие. The whole trick rests on one phrase: “read the whole document”. And if the document is longer than the model’s window? Then you need a second algorithm — and it is not about quality, it is about paying honestly for the overlap.

Серёга — «стол у меня один; держу открытыми три ширины стола внахлёст и скрепляю швы». Séréga — “I have one desk; I keep three overlapping desk-widths open and clip the seams”.

Divider P5 — a NEW act, lifted out of the evidence act where Long Late Chunking used to sit (it is a METHOD, not evidence, and it was breaking the evidential thread). The act answers: what happens when the document exceeds l_max? Truncate loses the tail; naive macro-cuts re-sever the dependency; long late chunking overlaps by ω and then DISCARDS the overlap embeddings. Then the bill, the three-way comparison, and the upper bound of the whole method (length-induced embedding collapse).
