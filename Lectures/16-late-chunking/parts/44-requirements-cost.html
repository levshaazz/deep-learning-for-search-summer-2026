На индексации — дороже: внимание по всему документу ~\(O(n^2)\), и нужно держать в памяти токен-эмбеддинги всего документа перед пулингом.Index-time — higher: whole-doc attention is ~\(O(n^2)\), and you must hold the document’s token embeddings in memory before pooling.

На запросе — бесплатно: хранится по-прежнему один вектор на чанк — стоимость поиска идентична наивной.Query-time — free: you still store one vector per chunk — search cost is identical to naive.

Requirements: a long-context encoder (so attention spans the whole doc), mean pooling (CLS gives nothing to re-pool), and token-level outputs (need the per-token ϑ_j before pooling — a pooled black-box API can't do it). Cost: index-time is higher (~O(n²) attention over the whole doc, hold whole-doc token embeddings), but query-time is unchanged — still one vector per chunk, same as naive.
