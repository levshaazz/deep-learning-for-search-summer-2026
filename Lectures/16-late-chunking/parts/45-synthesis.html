Тезис часа The thesis of the hour
Меняй, когда ты пулишь, а не саму модель. Change when you pool, not the model.

Кодируй весь документ один раз, затем усредняй по границам чанков. Внимание разрешает «Его»/«город» → Берлин в векторы токенов до пулинга — корефренция бесплатно и без дообучения. Прибавка скромная (BeIR nDCG@10 52.4 → 54.3, reported, Günther et al. 2024), но даром.Encode the whole document once, then mean-pool at the chunk boundaries. Attention resolves “Its”/“the city” → Berlin into the token vectors before pooling — coreference for free, no fine-tuning. The lift is modest (BeIR nDCG@10 52.4 → 54.3, reported, Günther et al. 2024) but free.

Synthesis in one view. The two lates: late-interaction (ColBERT) keeps a vector per token and pays at query time; late-chunking pools back to one vector per chunk and pays once at index time, free at query. The thesis: change WHEN you pool, not the model — full-doc attention gives free, training-free coreference; the BeIR lift is modest (sentence-chunk avg 52.4→54.3, reported Günther et al. 2024) but free.
