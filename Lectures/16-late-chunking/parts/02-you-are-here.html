This supplementary lecture deepens L10 (RAG chunking, Chunk Norris) by fixing a hidden defect of chunk-then-embed — the coreference gap — and contrasts L8 (ColBERT, late interaction) with today's late chunking. Same "late" prefix, opposite cost profiles: L8 pays at query time (vector per token), late chunking pays once at index time (vector per chunk). The whole method is just reordering {cut, attend}, no new model layer.
