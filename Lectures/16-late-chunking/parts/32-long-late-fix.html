Платишь за перекрытие лишь на стыках, а не за весь документ разом. You pay for overlap only at the seams, not the whole document at once.

Long Late Chunking (Algorithm 2), part 2 of 2 — the fix. Split the document into macro-chunks of l_max tokens (each fits the window), with an ω-token overlap between neighbours so context stitches across seams; stride = l_max − ω. Inside each macro-chunk, do ordinary late pooling over the small-chunk boundaries. Overlap is paid only at the seams, not for the whole document at once.
