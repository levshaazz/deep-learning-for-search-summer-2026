Late chunking помогает ровно тогда, когда соседи несут недостающий референт («Его 3.85 млн жителей» → Берлин). Когда соседи — просто шум, полный контекст лишь вредит.Late chunking helps exactly when the neighbours carry the missing referent (“Its 3.85 M inhabitants” → Berlin). The governing rule (part 2 of 2). Late chunking is conditional: it helps exactly when the neighbours supply the missing referent (coreference — "Its 3.85 M inhabitants" → Berlin), and hurts when the neighbours are noise. The diagnostic is simple: is the surrounding context relevant, or is it filler?
