What multimodal retrieval buys — and what it costs

one shared space is powerful, and only as good as its training pairs
Applicability · the shared space has edges too
CLIP unlocks records text never could — but it only knows the concepts it was trained on, and inherits its image–caption biases.
  • ✓Reach for it when — the corpus is visual or you need text↔image search; ColPali when layout matters.
  • ×Mind the limits — fine detail (counting, exact text, rare concepts) is weak; real matrices noisier than our 3/3.
  • ×Modality gap — image and text embeddings sit in offset regions of the shared space, so cross-modal cosines run lower and less calibrated than within-modality ones; tune the retrieval cutoff per modality, don't reuse a text-search threshold (it's why the toy's 0.9944 matched cosines look unrealistically high).
  • ·The catch ahead — a model that sees and answers can also confabulate; its pairs carry bias. Power → responsibility.
