Multi-hop in the wild — HotpotQA

a benchmark built so that one passage is never enough
Benchmark · HotpotQA (Yang et al., EMNLP 2018)
Questions deliberately need two or more documents, with supporting facts annotated — find the bridge, not one passage.
  • ·Bridge questions — our anchor shape: an entity in doc A answers from doc B. Single-hop cannot solve them.
  • ·Explainable — labelled supporting sentences grade the evidence chain, not just the answer.
  • ·Why it matters — real questions whose answers live in the links — what GraphRAG makes walkable.
HotpotQA. data/l12-bench.json: Yang et al., "HotpotQA", EMNLP 2018, arXiv:1809.09600. Diverse, explainable multi-hop QA — questions need 2+ docs, supporting facts annotated. Bridge questions = our anchor shape. The empirical motivation for multi-hop / GraphRAG.
