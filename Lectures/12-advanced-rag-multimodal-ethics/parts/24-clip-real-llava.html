Real llava run. data/l12-clip.json real block: llava:7b, top1Accuracy 1.0, top1Correct 5, n 5, on TEXT-FREE shapes (so it's vision, not OCR). IMPORTANT mechanism: this is NOT cosine retrieval. llava:7b is a generative VLM — for each image it was shown the five candidate captions and prompted "Which ONE caption best describes it? Reply with only the number" (see _research/exp_l12_ollama.py, run_clip), then we parsed the digit. So it GENERATES a forced multiple-choice answer grounded in the pixels; it does not produce a CLIP-style image embedding or an argmax over cosine. Reserve the cosine/argmax-diagonal framing for the toy CLIP matrix (slides 21–22) and CLIP proper. The takeaway that survives: a real vision model genuinely grounds its answer in the picture (5/5 on text-free shapes), which is why the toy diagonal was not just hand-picked vectors. n=5 is an illustrative existence-proof, not a benchmark.
