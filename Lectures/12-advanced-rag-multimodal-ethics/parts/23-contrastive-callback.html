An image and its caption are trained to be neighbours in the shared space; every other image–caption pairing is pushed apart.
  • ·Same trick as L6 — the in-batch contrastive loss pulls matched pairs together and repels the rest. CLIP just makes one side an image and the other its caption.
  • ·That is the gap — training drives matched cosine high (0.9944) and mismatched low (0.3791); the 0.6153 gap is the contrastive objective, made visible.
  • ·Sir Cosine returns — the angle he measured on the unit sphere in L2/L6 is the very same score; he simply now compares a photograph to a sentence.
