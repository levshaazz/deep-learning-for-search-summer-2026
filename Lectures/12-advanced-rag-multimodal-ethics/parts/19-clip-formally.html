CLIP, formally — one shared space, two encoders

cross-modal retrieval is argmax cosine
Definition · contrastive image–text pretraining (Radford et al., 2021)
$$ \text{retrieve}(\text{image}) = \operatorname*{arg\,max}_{j}\ \cos\!\big(\mathbf{e}_{\text{img}},\, \mathbf{e}_{\text{txt}}^{(j)}\big) $$
  • ·Two encoders, one space — an image encoder \(f_{\text{img}}\) and a text encoder \(f_{\text{txt}}\) map into the same coordinate system, so an image and a caption are comparable by cosine.
  • ·Retrieval = argmax cosine — embed both, take the nearest neighbour: exactly L9's NN search, now cross-modal.
  • ·One figure says it all — a cosine matrix of images vs captions; the diagonal should win every row. Let's compute it.
