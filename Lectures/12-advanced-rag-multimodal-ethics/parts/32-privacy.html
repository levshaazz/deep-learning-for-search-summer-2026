Risk 2 — privacy / PII

the index is an attack surface
Risk · the index can leak
Indexed documents may leak personal or secret data — once a passage is retrievable, the generator can surface it to anyone who asks the right question.
  • ·Where it enters — PII or confidential text swept into the corpus becomes retrievable; a well-phrased query is now an exfiltration path. Multi-tenant systems can leak across tenants.
  • ·Grounding cuts both ways — the very thing that makes RAG trustworthy (it answers from the index) is what makes a leaky index dangerous.
  • ✓Mitigation — PII scrubbing; access control on the index; per-tenant isolation. Treat the index with the same care as the source database.
