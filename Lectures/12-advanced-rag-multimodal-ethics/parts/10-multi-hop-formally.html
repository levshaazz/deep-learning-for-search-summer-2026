Multi-hop retrieval, formally

the answer is a path, not a passage
Definition · multi-hop over an entity graph
$$ \mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{E},\,\mathcal{R}), \quad \text{answer} = \text{walk}\big(e_0 \xrightarrow{r_1} \cdots \xrightarrow{r_h} e_h\big) $$
  • ·Nodes \(\mathcal{E}\) are entities, edges \(\mathcal{R}\) relations — each a triple (head, relation, tail) from a document.
  • ·\(h\) hops from start \(e_0\) to answer \(e_h\). Our anchor: \(h=2\), Acme Corp → Dana Reyes → computer science.
  • ·On top of L10's \(R_k\) — retrieval finds documents; the graph's edges chain facts across them.
