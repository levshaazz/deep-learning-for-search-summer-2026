Risk 1 — bias

retrieval and generation both inherit it
Risk · bias enters at two stages
Retrieval + generation inherit the biases of the corpus and the model — what gets indexed, what ranks highly, and what the LLM was trained to say.
  • ·Where it enters — a skewed corpus surfaces skewed passages (the recall floor from L10, now ethical); a biased model amplifies them in the answer. Both stages compound.
  • ·Multimodal makes it sharper — CLIP's shared space is only as fair as its image–caption training pairs (the limit we just flagged).
  • ✓Mitigation — audit the corpora; measure subgroup performance; diversify sources. Bias you don't measure, you ship.
