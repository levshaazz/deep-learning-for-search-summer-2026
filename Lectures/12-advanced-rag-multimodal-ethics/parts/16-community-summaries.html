Two ways to query a graph: local walk vs global summary

GraphRAG clusters the graph and pre-summarises each community
Definition · local & global search (Edge et al., 2024)
Cluster the graph into communities, pre-summarise each, then route a query to a local walk or a global map-reduce.
  • ·Local search — walk the query entities' neighbourhood (our 2-hop traversal). Best for specific questions.
  • ·Global search — map: each community summary yields a partial answer plus a self-rated relevance score; reduce: drop the low-scored partials, combine the rest. That per-community LLM pass is the cost global search pays for corpus-wide “main themes?” coverage.
  • ·From Local to Global — the paper's title is the idea: the close walk and the bird's-eye view.
