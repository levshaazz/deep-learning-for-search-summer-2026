Part 1 · Reason across records

The constellation map

Stop treating the corpus as a flat bag of passages. Extract the entities and the relations between them and link them into a graph — suddenly the scattered records resolve into a constellation. Now an answer that no single document holds can be walked to, one hop at a time. Multi-hop reasoning · GraphRAG.

Divider · GraphRAG. The constellation metaphor: flat documents → a navigable graph of entities + relations. Edge (Edge et al., Microsoft, arXiv:2404.16130) for GraphRAG; HotpotQA (arXiv:1809.09600) for multi-hop QA. Walk the graph to reach answers no one document holds.
