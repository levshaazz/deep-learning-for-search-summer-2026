The responsibility framework — four risks, four mitigations

risk · what goes wrong · the mitigation (the centerpiece)
risk what goes wrong the mitigation
biasretrieval + generation inherit corpus and model biasesaudit corpora; measure subgroup performance; diversify sources
hallucination harmfluent but unsupported claims mislead usersgrounding + citations + abstention; faithfulness checks (L11)
privacy / PIIindexed documents may leak personal or secret dataPII scrubbing; access control on the index; per-tenant isolation
attributionanswers without sources cannot be verified or contestedcite the retrieved passages; show provenance; let users inspect

Every mitigation is a control you build, not a hope you hold. They share one spine: ground the answer, cite its sources, and let it abstain. Faithfulness — whether each claim is supported — is the metric from L11 turned into an ethical obligation.
