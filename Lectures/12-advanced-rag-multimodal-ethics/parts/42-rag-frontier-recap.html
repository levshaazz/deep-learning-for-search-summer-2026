Recap · the advancing edge of retrieval-augmented generation
Every frontier answers a different failure of naive RAG — better structure, better indexing, better self-correction, and better modalities.
  • ·Structure — GraphRAG (Edge et al., 2024) walks an entity graph for multi-hop; RAPTOR (Sarthi et al., ICLR 2024) builds a tree of recursive summaries to retrieve at the right level of abstraction.
  • ·Self-correction — Self-RAG (Asai et al., ICLR 2024) bakes reflection tokens in to gate and critique retrieval — the faithfulness instinct, learned.
  • ·Modalities — CLIP (Radford et al., 2021) and ColPali (Faysse et al., 2024) extend retrieval beyond text into one shared space. The frontier is wide, and still moving.
