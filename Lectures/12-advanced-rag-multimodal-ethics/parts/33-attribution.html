Risk 3 — attribution

an unsourced answer cannot be checked
Risk · no source, no recourse
Answers without sources cannot be verified or contested — the user has no way to tell a grounded claim from a confident guess, and no way to push back.
  • ·Where it enters — the generator can paraphrase the context without pointing to it. Fluency hides the seam between “the passage said” and “the model decided.”
  • ·This is the L10 citation discipline made ethical — we wrote grounded prompts with citations; attribution is why that was non-negotiable.
  • ✓Mitigation — cite the retrieved passages; show provenance; let users inspect. A claim a user can trace is a claim a user can challenge.
