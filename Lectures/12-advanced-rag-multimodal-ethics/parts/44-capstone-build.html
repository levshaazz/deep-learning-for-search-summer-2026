What you can now build

twelve lectures, one capability

The full stack, end to end

  • Ingest & tokenize a corpus; embed it; build an ANN index with the right metric (L2–L9).
  • Retrieve with a bi-/cross-encoder cascade and generate a grounded, cited answer (L7, L10).
  • Judge it — faithfulness and relevance, the agentic loop (L11).
  • Reason across records with a graph, search beyond text with a shared space, and answer responsibly (today).

The judgement to wield it

  • You can diagnose a failure mode — chunk boundary, vocabulary gap, multi-hop, wrong modality, hallucination — and reach for the one fix it calls for.
  • You know not to over-engineer: start naive, measure, earn each stage.
  • And you carry the standard: the captain answers for the Ship.
  • That is a search engineer. The rest is practice — which begins at the helm.
