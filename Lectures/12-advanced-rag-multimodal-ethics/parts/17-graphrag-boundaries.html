When GraphRAG earns its cost — and when it doesn't

a graph is powerful, and expensive to build & keep current
Applicability · don't reach for a graph by default
GraphRAG pays off on relational, multi-hop answers — at the price of an extraction-and-indexing pipeline.
  • ✓Reach for it when — facts chain across documents (multi-hop), or corpus-wide theme questions.
  • ×Skip it when — the answer is in one passage. Naive RAG (L10) is cheaper.
  • ×Extraction is noisy — relations get named inconsistently, edges missed or hallucinated. Graph quality, not just freshness, gates GraphRAG.
  • ·The costs — corpus-wide extraction, graph storage, staleness. Start flat; earn the graph.
