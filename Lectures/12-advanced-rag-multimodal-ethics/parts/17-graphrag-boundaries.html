When GraphRAG earns its cost — and when it doesn't

a graph is powerful, and expensive to build & keep current
Applicability · don't reach for a graph by default
GraphRAG pays off on relational, multi-hop answers — at the price of an extraction-and-indexing pipeline kept current as the corpus changes.
  • ✓Reach for it when — facts chain across documents (multi-hop), or corpus-wide “main themes?” questions.
  • ×Skip it when — the answer is in one passage. Naive RAG (L10) is cheaper and already correct.
  • ×Extraction is noisy — relations get named inconsistently (our real run emitted both “studied at” and “studied”), edges are missed or hallucinated, and entity resolution can split or merge the wrong nodes; a broken edge breaks the walk. Graph quality, not just freshness, gates GraphRAG.
  • ·The costs — corpus-wide extraction (LLM calls), graph storage, staleness on updates. Start flat; earn the graph.
GraphRAG boundaries. Reach for it on relational/multi-hop and global theme questions; skip when the answer is single-passage (naive RAG cheaper).
