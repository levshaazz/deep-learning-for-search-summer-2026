“If the model hallucinated, leaked a record, or answered with bias, that's the model's fault — the system designer just wired it up.”

You chose the corpus, the retriever, the prompt, and whether the system is allowed to say “I don't know.” Every harm in the framework is a design decision, not an act of God: a leaky index, an unaudited corpus, an answer with no citation, a model never given permission to abstain. The captain answers for the Ship. Ground it, cite it, and let it refuse — and when it errs, the responsibility is yours, because the controls were yours to build.

This is the principle the whole act reduces to — and the standard you carry when you take the helm.
