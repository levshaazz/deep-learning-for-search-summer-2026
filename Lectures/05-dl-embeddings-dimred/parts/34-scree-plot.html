Локоть после PC2 говорит: двух осей хватит для первого взгляда.The first two components (blue) dominate: \(19.6\% + 18.1\% = 37.7\%\). Then a fast decay (9.2%, 8.4%, …). The elbow after PC2 says two axes suffice for a first look.

This is the scree plot for our data, the bar heights taken directly from the explained-variance-ratio array in the dimred file: nineteen point six, eighteen point one, then nine point two, eight point four, seven point zero, and on down to two point seven five for the tenth component. Two readings. The two blue bars are the axes we keep, summing to thirty-seven point seven percent. And the elbow — the sharp drop after the second bar — is the standard heuristic for how many components are worth keeping; here it cleanly says two for a first visualisation. The long thin tail is the structure PCA discards.
