The Same Trick Everywhere

«Слова, которые встречаются вместе, близки» — это не про слова.“Things that occur together sit near” — this was never about words. Дай любой области последовательность токенов — и skip-gram строит карту.Give any field a sequence of tokens and skip-gram draws its map.

A short interlude before the payoff, and the headline transfer of the whole lecture. Nothing in word2vec knew that its tokens were words. The only assumption was a sequence in which co-occurrence carries meaning. The moment any field can produce such a sequence — nodes visited on a graph walk, products bought in a session, amino acids in a protein, time-windows of a brain signal — the exact same skip-gram objective learns an embedding whose geometry is searchable.
