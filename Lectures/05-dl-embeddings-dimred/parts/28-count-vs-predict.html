Predict and count are two faces of one coin.

A compact trade-off table. word2vec uses local windows, streams through data with a tiny memory footprint, and is naturally online. GloVe uses the global co-occurrence matrix, trains in one weighted least-squares pass, but must hold or stream that large matrix. Both deliver working analogies and good cosine geometry. The deep result, from Levy and Goldberg in 2014, is that they are not really rivals: skip-gram with negative sampling is implicitly factorising a shifted pointwise-mutual-information matrix, which is exactly the kind of object GloVe factorises explicitly. Predict and count are two faces of the same coin.
