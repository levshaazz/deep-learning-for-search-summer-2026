overview
The flagship climb, with real numbers. We start at the king vector. Subtract the man vector — intuitively that strips away maleness and leaves a royalty direction. Add the woman vector to put femaleness back. The result is a point in space that is not exactly any word, so we ask which real word is nearest by cosine. The answer is queen, with cosine zero point eight six one — these are the exact figures from the L5 embeddings file. The runner-ups, prince and throne, both sit around zero point seven six, so queen wins clearly. Meaning has become arithmetic.
