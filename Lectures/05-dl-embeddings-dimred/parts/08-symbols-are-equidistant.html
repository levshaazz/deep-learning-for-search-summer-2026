one-hot · ортогональные осиone-hot · orthogonal axes

В one-hot (и в BM25 на термах) разные слова ортогональны: косинус любой пары различных слов равен 0. Геометрии смысла нет — это домашняя территория Лексического Гремлина.In one-hot (and in term-based BM25) distinct words are orthogonal: the cosine of any two different words is 0. There is no geometry of meaning — the Lexical Gremlin's home turf.

State the problem cleanly. In a one-hot encoding each word is its own axis, so any two distinct words are orthogonal and their cosine is exactly zero. Cat is no closer to dog than to airplane — the representation knows nothing about meaning. This is the same lexical gap that beat BM25 back in Lecture 3: a string matcher sees couch and sofa as orthogonal too. The Gremlin lives here, in the zero off-diagonal of the identity matrix. To get nearness we need vectors that are dense, not one-hot.
