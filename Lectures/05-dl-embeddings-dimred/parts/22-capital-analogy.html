Направление «страна→столица» — отдельная ось, параллельная для разных стран. Та же машина, что и для пола: вычитаешь страну, добавляешь другую — получаешь её столицу.The country→capital direction is its own axis, roughly parallel across countries. Same machine as the gender one: subtract a country, add another, land on its capital.

A second analogy proves the first was not a fluke. paris minus france plus italy lands nearest to rome, cosine zero point eight three eight, exactly the figure in the data file. The runner-ups are other capitals — madrid, berlin — which makes sense because they all share the capital-city direction; spain, a country rather than a city, ranks lower. There is a whole family of these linear directions: gender, plural, comparative, country-to-capital. The space has learned consistent, reusable axes of relation, purely from predicting neighbours.
