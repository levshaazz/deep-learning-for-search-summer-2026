UMAP made concrete, not abstract: a real umap-learn run on the forty-four GloVe-fifty word vectors, stepped through its actual epoch snapshots. Step zero is the spectral initialisation — crucially, UMAP does not start from random noise like a naive t-SNE run, it starts from the eigenvectors of the neighbour graph, so coarse global structure is already visible. Step one, by epoch eighty, the fuzzy-graph optimisation is pulling each meaning group together and widening the gaps. Step two, epoch three hundred, the seven clusters — animals, royalty, family, capitals, countries, tech, transport — are nearly separated. Step three, converged at epoch five hundred: seven crisp clusters, the within-over-between tightness ratio fallen from zero point one four seven to zero point zero six one, so clusters end about two-point-four times tighter relative to the gaps. The dials are n_neighbors, ten — the perplexity analogue — and min_dist, zero point one. Step four spells out how UMAP differs from t-SNE: it builds a fuzzy simplicial set, a weighted k-nearest-neighbour graph of fuzzy membership strengths, and minimises the cross-entropy between that high-dimensional graph and a low-dimensional one, rather than t-SNE's KL divergence on Gaussian-versus-Student-t affinities; it preserves more global structure, is markedly faster, scales to large n, and can even transform new points. But the same caveat holds — absolute UMAP distances and cluster sizes are not literal.
