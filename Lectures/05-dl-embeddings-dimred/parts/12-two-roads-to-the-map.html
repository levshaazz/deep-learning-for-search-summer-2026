Обе дороги ведут в одно место: пространство, где близость = смысл. Сначала пройдём предсказательную (word2vec), затем вернёмся к счётной (GloVe).Both roads arrive at the same place: a space where nearness = meaning. We walk the predictive road (word2vec) first, then revisit the count road (GloVe).

Preview the two families so the structure is clear. The predictive route, word2vec, slides a window over raw text and trains a small network to predict context words; it is local, online, and streams through a corpus. The count route, GloVe, instead builds the global word-by-word co-occurrence matrix once and factorises it. They look different but converge on the same kind of geometry — both are cashing in the distributional hypothesis. We do word2vec in depth next because its objective is the cleanest window into how the coordinates are earned.
