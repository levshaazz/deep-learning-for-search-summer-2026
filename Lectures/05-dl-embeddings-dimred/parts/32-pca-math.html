PCA каузален: ковариация — из центрированных \(X\); собственные векторы — из \(C\); проекция — на эти векторы. На практике то же даёт SVD матрицы \(X\) (устойчивее). На наших словах две оси держат 37.7% дисперсии.PCA is causal: covariance from the centred \(X\); eigenvectors from \(C\); projection onto those vectors. In practice the SVD of \(X\) gives the same axes more stably. On our words the top two axes keep 37.7% of the variance.

PCA shown as a causal chain, one object built from the last, rather than three facts at once. Step zero: centre the data so the origin sits at the mean of the cloud. Step one: form the covariance matrix C, one over n times X transpose X — the covariance OF the centred data. Step two: diagonalise — the eigenvectors of C are the principal axes and each eigenvalue lambda sub k is the variance along that axis; watch u sub k and lambda sub k appear OF the covariance. Step three: project each point onto the top d eigenvectors, z equals U sub d transpose x. Step four: the fraction of variance each axis keeps is its eigenvalue over the sum of all eigenvalues — on our corpus principal component one keeps nineteen point six percent and component two eighteen point one, thirty-seven point seven percent together, the number the scree plot will confirm. In practice nobody forms C explicitly; the SVD of X gives the same components more stably. Step through with the arrows.
Step 0 — centre X (subtract the mean).
Step 1 — covariance C of the centred X.
Step 2 — eigenvectors u_k of C are the principal axes; λ_k is the variance.
Step 3 — project onto the top-d eigenvectors.
Step 4 — variance kept = λ_k / Σλ_j → 19.6% + 18.1% = 37.7%.
