Урок.Lesson. Аналогии — красивое свидетельство структуры, но не доказательство «понимания». Косинус \(0.861\) реален; магии — нет.Analogies are beautiful evidence of structure, not proof of “understanding”. The \(0.861\) cosine is real; the magic is not.

A quick honesty beat so we do not oversell. The analogy is a linear regularity in the geometry, not comprehension. Two technical caveats: the standard evaluation explicitly removes the three query words from the candidate set, because without that the nearest vector to king minus man plus woman is frequently king itself — the arithmetic barely moves. And accuracy is much lower on rare words and on relations that are not nicely linear. The cosine of zero point eight six one is a genuine, reproducible fact about the space; treating it as evidence of structure is right, treating it as understanding is not.
