Каждой паре точек в высокой размерности приписывается вероятность быть соседями. t-SNE ищет 2-D раскладку, в которой эти вероятности совпадают — близкое остаётся близким.Each pair of high-D points gets a probability of being neighbours. t-SNE finds a 2-D layout whose neighbour probabilities match — near stays near.

Ключевой рычаг — perplexity: сколько соседей считать «близкими». В наших данных perplexity \(= 14\). Тяжёлые хвосты \(t\)-распределения в 2-D разводят кластеры, борясь со скучиванием.The key dial is perplexity: how many neighbours count as “near”. In our data perplexity \(= 14\). The heavy-tailed \(t\)-distribution in 2-D spreads clusters apart, fighting crowding.

t-SNE, from van der Maaten and Hinton in 2008, takes the neighbour-preserving route. In the high-dimensional space it converts distances into probabilities: how likely is point j to be picked as a neighbour of point i. Then it lays points out in two dimensions and adjusts them until the low-dimensional neighbour probabilities match the high-dimensional ones, minimising a Kullback-Leibler divergence. Two knobs matter. Perplexity sets the effective number of neighbours each point pays attention to — fourteen in our run. And using a heavy-tailed Student-t distribution in the plane gives faraway points room, which is what produces those clean, well-separated clusters.
