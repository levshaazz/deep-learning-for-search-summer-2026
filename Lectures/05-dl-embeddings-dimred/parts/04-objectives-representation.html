These are measurable outcomes — by the end you should be able to do each one on paper. First: state precisely why a symbol-based representation cannot encode meaning — every distinct one-hot vector is the same distance from every other. Second: write the word2vec skip-gram objective and explain the negative-sampling shortcut that makes the softmax affordable. Third: compute cosine similarity and use it to rank the analogy result. The remaining objectives — dimensionality reduction and the t-SNE caveat — are on the next slide.
