position and verbosity are not the only ones

More known biases

  • Self-enhancement — a judge rates answers from its own model family higher. Never let a model grade itself.
  • Authority / formatting — confident tone, headings, bullet lists and fake citations read as “quality” regardless of truth.
  • Sycophancy — an answer that agrees with the prompt's framing scores higher than one that corrects it.

The one bias the judge dodged

It is not all bad news. On our planted hallucination — an answer with one invented fact — the same frozen judge caught it and marked the answer down on grounding.
