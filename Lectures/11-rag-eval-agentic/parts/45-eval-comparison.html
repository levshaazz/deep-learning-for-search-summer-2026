Use them as a ladder: RAGAS for cheap, continuous diagnosis; the LLM judge for fast comparisons (audited for the measured biases); humans on a small held-out sample to calibrate the two cheap proxies. The expensive method isn't the everyday one — it's the anchor that keeps the cheap ones honest.
