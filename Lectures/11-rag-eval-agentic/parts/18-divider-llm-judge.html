Act 2 · Hire a judge

LLM-as-a-judge

If grading free text needs judgment, use a model that has it. Hand a strong LLM a rubric and ask it to score answers — or to pick the better of two. It is fast, cheap, and correlates surprisingly well with human raters (Zheng et al., NeurIPS 2023). It is also the moment we hand Goodhart the verdict pen: the judge is a metric, and metrics can be gamed.

turn-llm-judge. LLM-as-judge (Zheng et al., MT-Bench / Chatbot Arena, arXiv:2306.05685): a model scores or compares answers via a rubric. Strong human correlation — but it is itself a metric, which sets up Goodhart in Act 3.
