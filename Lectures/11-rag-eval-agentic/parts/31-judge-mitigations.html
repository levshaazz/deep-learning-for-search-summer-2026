How to keep a judge honest

each measured bias has a cheap defence

self-enhancementown-family answers scored higheruse a different model family as judge; never self-grade

drift / overfitscore climbs, quality doesn't (Goodhart)calibrate against human labels on a held-out sample

The judge stays useful — if you treat it like an instrument that needs calibration, not an oracle. Swap the order, normalise length, mix the panel, and anchor to humans. The numbers are real (0.6667, 1.0), so the defences must be too.
