We froze a real judge (llama3.1:8b, temp 0, seed 42) and fed it genuine ties. It anchored to whichever answer sat in slot 1 two times out of three (0.6667) — a coin that lands heads because of where the answer was printed, not what it said. The same judge is perfect (1.0) when one answer is clearly better. The bias hides exactly where it matters most: the close calls.
