Judging the Oracle

The Oracle learned to speak — now it stands trial. A correct answer and a fabricated one sound identical: both fluent, both confident. How do you grade an Oracle that sounds confident whether it is right or wrong? With no gold answer in hand, we must build the courtroom from scratch — and an old trickster is already waiting in the gallery.
