three criteria, a 1–5 scale, one number out
Setup · the rubric we hand the judge
Score each answer on three criteria — relevance, grounding, completeness — on a 1–5 scale, then average to one pointwise score.
  • ·relevance — does it answer the actual question? grounding — is each claim supported by the context? completeness — does it cover what matters?
  • ·The rubric mirrors RAGAS in spirit — relevance ≈ answer relevance, grounding ≈ faithfulness — but here a model reads the answer and assigns the numbers, no fractions to compute.
  • ·A clear rubric is what makes the score reproducible and explainable: the judge must justify each criterion, not emit a vibe. The next slide steps two answers through it.
