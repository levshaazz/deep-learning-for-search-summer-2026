Context precision, by hand

are the relevant chunks ranked high? · precision@k over relevant ranks
$$ \text{context precision} = \frac{1}{\#\text{relevant}} \sum_{k:\,\text{rel}_k} \text{precision@}k $$ $$ \text{ranks: } \underbrace{c_1}_{\text{rel}},\ \underbrace{c_2}_{\text{noise}},\ \underbrace{c_3}_{\text{rel}},\ \underbrace{c_4}_{\text{noise}} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{p@1}=\tfrac{1}{1}=1,\ \ \text{p@3}=\tfrac{2}{3} $$ $$ \text{context precision} = \frac{1 + \tfrac{2}{3}}{2} = \mathbf{0.8333} $$

Walk the retrieved list; at each relevant rank, take precision@k (how clean the top-k is so far), then average over the relevant items. The first relevant chunk sits at rank 1 (perfect), but the second is buried under noise at rank 3 (only 2/3 clean). Relevant-but-low-ranked passages cost precision — this grades the retriever's ordering. (Here #relevant = 2 = the relevant chunks that actually appear in the retrieved list; chunks never retrieved are recall's problem, not precision's.)

context-precision by-hand. l11-ragas.precisionAtK: relevant at ranks 1 (p@1=1) and 3 (p@3=0.6667), 2 relevant total → (1 + 2/3)/2 = 0.8333 (gated contextPrecision). Noise at rank 2 between the relevants is what costs precision. Ranking-aware, like MAP from L1.
