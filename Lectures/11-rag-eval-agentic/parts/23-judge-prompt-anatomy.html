Anatomy of a judge prompt

what you actually send the model

The four parts

  • Role + rubric — “You are an expert grader. Score relevance, grounding, completeness 1–5.”
  • The inputs — the question, the retrieved context, and the answer(s) to grade.
  • Reason then score — require a short justification before the number (chain-of-thought lifts agreement).
  • Structured output — JSON per criterion, so scores are parseable and auditable.

Design rules that buy reliability

Make the scale concrete (anchor each point with a description, not just a digit). Demand evidence — “quote the supporting span.” Force a verdict (no “both are good”). And keep the judge model separate from the model being judged, to dodge self-enhancement. Every one of these is a defence against the biases on the next three slides.
