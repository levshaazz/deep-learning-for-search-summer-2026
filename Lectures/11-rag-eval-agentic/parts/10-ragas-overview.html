The RAGAS scorecard

four metrics, two about generation, two about retrieval
Definition · RAGAS (arXiv:2309.15217)
Four numbers in \([0,1]\), each from the question, retrieved context, and generated answer — no gold reference.
  • ·Faithfulness & answer relevance grade the generation: is every claim supported, and does it address the question?
  • ·Context precision & recall grade the retrieval: are relevant chunks ranked high, and were the needed facts retrieved at all?
  • ·A low number says which stage failed. We compute all four on one example: “How does the heart pump blood?”
