Definition · the two independent axes of a RAG answer
Faithfulness: is every claim supported by the context?  ·  Relevance: does the answer address the question?
  • ·They are independent: an answer can be faithful but irrelevant (true but off-topic) or relevant but unfaithful (on-topic but invented — our 0.75 faithfulness, with the Harvey claim).
  • ·RAGAS gives each a number; Self-RAG gives each a token (IsSup = faithfulness, IsUse = usefulness). Grade them separately, report them separately.
  • ·The trap: a confident answer can be high on one axis and zero on the other. Collapse them into one score and you can't tell which way it failed.
