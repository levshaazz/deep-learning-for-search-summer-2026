Two answers, one voice

  • Answer A — every claim traces to a retrieved passage. True.
  • Answer B — one sentence is invented, the rest borrowed. False.
  • From the outside — same fluency, same confidence, same citations-shaped prose. Indistinguishable.

The judgment problem

Confidence is not a truth signal — the Oracle has no internal “I'm unsure” light. So the question is not does it sound right? but can we measure whether it is right? Without a measurement, every confident answer is a coin flip we cannot see.

Hook payoff. A fluent answer reveals nothing about truth — A (true) and B (one hallucinated sentence) are indistinguishable from the outside. Sets up the need for measurement, not vibes. Confabulous (L10's phantom) is the unseen cause; here we name the symptom.
