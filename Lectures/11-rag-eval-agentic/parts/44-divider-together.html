Choosing how to judge

Three ways to grade, two axes to grade on, one rule to remember. RAGAS, the LLM judge, and human raters each trade off cost, speed, and trust. Faithfulness and relevance pull apart into independent axes. And every score is a proxy — useful, never sovereign. Let's assemble the evaluation toolkit and the map of when to reach for each.
