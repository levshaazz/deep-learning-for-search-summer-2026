The judge is not the ground truth

the one rule that survives this whole lecture

“Now that we can auto-grade RAG with RAGAS and an LLM-judge, we don't need humans — the metrics tell us what's true.”

Every number we computed today — the 0.75 faithfulness, the 0.7033 relevance, the judge's 4.0 — is a proxy produced by a model, and models are biased (position 0.6667 on ties, verbosity 1.0). The metrics are indispensable: they let you catch regressions cheaply, diagnose which stage failed, and let the Ship grade itself in the loop. But they are instruments, not the territory. Keep a small human-labelled set as the anchor, report axes separately, and audit any score you optimise against. Used that way, you get the speed of automation and the trust of human judgment — and Goodhart goes home empty-handed.

Measurement made the Oracle accountable — but only if we remember the map is not the territory. That humility is the engineering discipline.
