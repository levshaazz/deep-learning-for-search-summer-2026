Definition · Self-RAG (arXiv:2310.11511)
Train the LM to emit reflection tokens — Retrieve · IsRel · IsSup · IsUse — that gate retrieval and let it grade its own output as it generates.
  • ·Retrieve — decide per segment whether retrieval is even needed (skip what the model already knows).
  • ·IsRel / IsSup — is the passage relevant, and is each claim supported by it? This is the RAGAS faithfulness check, now a token the model emits.
  • ·IsUse — is the response useful (1–5)? The judge from Act 2 is now inside the decoder — Asai et al. (ICLR 2024).
self-rag definition (callback L10). Cite Asai et al., "Self-RAG", ICLR 2024 (arXiv:2310.11511). Reflection tokens Retrieve/IsRel/IsSup/IsUse. IsSup = faithfulness as a token; IsUse = the judge inside the decoder. Contrast with CRAG on the next slide (bolt-on vs baked-in).
