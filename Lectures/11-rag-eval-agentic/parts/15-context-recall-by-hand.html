Context recall, by hand

did retrieval bring back the facts the answer needed?
$$ \text{context recall} = \frac{\#\{\text{ground-truth claims present in the retrieved context}\}}{\#\{\text{ground-truth claims}\}} $$ $$ \text{3 truths: pumps blood }\checkmark,\ \text{systole ejects }\checkmark,\ \text{diastole refills }\times $$ $$ \text{context recall} = \frac{2}{3} = \mathbf{0.6667} $$

The mirror of precision: take each ground-truth claim and check whether any retrieved chunk supports it. Two of three are covered; “diastole refills the heart” was never retrieved, so the generator could not have used it. A missing fact caps the best possible answer — the recall floor from L1/L9, now at the context level.

context-recall by-hand. l11-ragas: groundTruthInContext 2 / groundTruthCount 3 = 0.6667 (gated contextRecall). The un-retrieved "diastole" truth is the recall floor: retrieval caps generation. Precision (ranking) and recall (coverage) are the two sides of retrieval quality.
