Part 05

The BM25 Saga

I can rank with TF-IDF now — but it's crude. My brass sextant, tuned over three decades: not a formula, an engineering history.

BM25 is my brass sextant — the instrument I use to measure how well a document fits a query, refined over decades. This engineering arc is the crown jewel of the lecture: from the probabilistic relevance principle through BM1, the saturation and length-norm fixes, the BM11/BM15 extremes, and finally the b-dial that interpolates them into BM25. My "aha" is that BM25 is not handed down — every term was added, measured, and sometimes removed.
