Exact from the BoW JSON. Three short sentences over an eleven-word vocabulary give a three-by-eleven count matrix. Read a row as a vector: S1 is the cat-mat sentence, S2 the dog-log sentence, S3 the plurals sentence. Two things to flag. First, the most frequent word in S1 and S2 is "the" with count two, and it tells you nothing about topic — that is exactly what IDF will down-weight. Second, "cat" and "cats" are different columns; without stemming the matrix treats them as unrelated, which is the lexical brittleness we keep circling back to.
