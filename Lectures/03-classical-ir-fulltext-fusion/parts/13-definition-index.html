Inverted Indexpostings lists

A map from each term to its postings list — the documents (and often positions / counts) in which that term appears.

"Inverted" because it reverses the natural document → terms map into a terms → documents map. A query term becomes a single dictionary lookup; boolean queries become merges of postings lists. This is the data structure behind Lucene, Elasticsearch, and every classical engine.

Emphasise the inversion. Documents naturally list their words; the index stores the opposite. The term dictionary gives you, in one step, every document containing a word — and only those. Postings often also store term frequency and positions, which BM25 and phrase queries need. Build it once offline; query it millions of times.
