Определение с прошлого слайда — теперь пощупай его: термин указывает прямо на свои документы, никакого сканирования. У каждого термина — свой список постингов, а булев AND — это пересечение двух списков. Заметь, каким хрупким выходит строгое пересечение — здесь оно пустое; этот излом мы будем чинить всю оставшуюся лекцию.The definition from the previous slide — now touch it: a term points straight at its documents, no scanning anywhere. Every term owns a postings list, and boolean AND is the intersection of two of them. Notice how brittle the strict intersection turns out to be — here it comes back empty; the rest of this lecture is about repairing that fracture.

Act 2’s climb, between the key-term slide and the build-the-postings-by-hand slide. The eight documents, both postings lists and the empty intersection are read from data/l3-index.json. Steps: (0) the documents and why scanning is hopeless (callback to act 1); (1) the first term’s postings — the “term points at documents” inversion, DWELL here, it is the whole data structure; (2) the second term’s postings; (3) the AND merge — the intersection is EMPTY, and that emptiness is the act’s cliffhanger: strict boolean has no notion of “almost”. Students stumble on the direction of the inversion (document→terms vs term→documents) — step 1 is where to fix it. The by-hand build on the next slide then feels mechanical, which is the point.
