The Star Catalog

I'm a star-cartographer now — one record in a billion, and I cannot read them all at query time.

I'm a star-cartographer now. The sky is my corpus; each star is a document. A user asks me for one specific star out of a billion — and I cannot visit each one and check. My answer is the search engineer's answer: build a catalog indexed by what you are looking for, so a query lands you on the right page instantly.
