Part 02

The Inverted Index

I've proved brute force is hopeless — now I flip the map: instead of doc → words, store word → which docs.

My reveal. A normal document maps doc to its words. My catalog flips it: for every term, I store the list of documents that contain it — a postings list, one catalogue card per word. Now a query term is a single lookup, not a scan. This is the inverted index, the foundation of every full-text search engine for fifty years. The image is my card-wall metaphor; the text stands alone if it 404s.
