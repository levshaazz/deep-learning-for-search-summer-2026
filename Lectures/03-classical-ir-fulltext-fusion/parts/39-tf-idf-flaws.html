overview
Numbers from the bm25 JSON. With idf of space at 0.6931, plain TF-IDF multiplies by raw term frequency: D0 with one occurrence scores 0.6931, D2 with two scores 1.39, D3 with three scores 2.08. The ranking puts D3 first purely because it repeats the word most. Two flaws are now visible: term frequency never saturates, so the tenth occurrence counts as much as the first, and there is no penalty for a long document that accumulates occurrences just by being long. BM25 fixes exactly these two things — that is the next part.
