Exact from the car/truck JSON, part 1 — the shared words. Both documents share "the", "is", "driven", "on" — they appear in every document, so their idf with log of two over two is exactly zero and they drop out of every score. This is half of why TF-IDF works: it automatically silences the words that cannot help you tell documents apart. The distinguishing words come next.
