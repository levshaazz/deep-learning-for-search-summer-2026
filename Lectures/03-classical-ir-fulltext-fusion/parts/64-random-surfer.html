A surfer follows a random link with prob \(d\), else teleports to a random page. Teleport is "obviously needed": without it, rank leaks into sinks (dangling pages with no out-links) and never returns.

PageRank is the stationary distribution of a random surfer. With probability d, here 0.85, the surfer follows one of the current page's out-links uniformly; with probability one minus d it teleports to a uniformly random page. The teleport term is not cosmetic — without it, a dangling page with no out-links, or a small cycle, acts as a rank sink that absorbs all probability mass and never gives it back, so the iteration never converges to anything useful. Teleport guarantees the chain is ergodic, so a unique stationary distribution exists. That distribution is the rank.
