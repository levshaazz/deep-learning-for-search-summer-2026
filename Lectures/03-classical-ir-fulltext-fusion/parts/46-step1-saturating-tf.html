As \(f\to\infty\), \(\mathcal{F}\to S_1\): a ceiling. Raw tf over-rewards repetition; this cheap rational function mimics the 2-Poisson weight with one knob, \(K_1\) — the picture beside it.

The first engineering step. Rather than evaluate the full two-Poisson weight, approximate it with a simple rational function: f over K1 plus f, scaled by S1. As the count grows the factor approaches S1, giving the bounded, concave shape the theory demands, controlled by a single parameter K1 that sets how quickly it saturates. This replaces raw, linear term frequency and is the heart of why BM25 beats TF-IDF. The choice S1 equals K1 plus 1 is not arbitrary — it is one of the things the authors learned empirically, which we flag shortly.
