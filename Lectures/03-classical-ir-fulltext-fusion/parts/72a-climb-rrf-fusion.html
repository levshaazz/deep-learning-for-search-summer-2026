Steps: (0) the two disagreeing rankings — recall the score-normalization danger slide: their raw scores cannot be added; (1) the reciprocal-rank vote — DWELL on why rank, not score, is the thing being trusted; (2) summing the votes; (3) the fused order — the consensus document rises; (4) the full table with a tie and a compromise — read it aloud, it preempts the “fusion is magic” misconception two slides ahead.
