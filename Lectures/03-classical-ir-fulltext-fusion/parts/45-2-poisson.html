Why saturation: about t vs merely mentions t

the theoretical "why" — no probability prerequisite needed

A document is either about \(t\) (the topic recurs) or merely mentions it (in passing). The best weight that tells the two apart rises with tf but flattens — the principled origin of saturation. (Formally: model tf as a 2-Poisson mixture, one component per regime — Robertson 1981.)

This is the theory the old deck never gave: why saturation. Robertson's eliteness idea says each document is either elite for a term — genuinely about that concept, so the term recurs — or not, where it appears only incidentally. Model the term count as a mixture of two Poisson distributions, one per class, and the optimal weight from the likelihood ratio is an increasing but bounded function of tf. In other words, the saturating shape is not an engineering hack; it falls out of a principled probabilistic model. BM25's tf factor is a cheap, robust approximation to that curve.
