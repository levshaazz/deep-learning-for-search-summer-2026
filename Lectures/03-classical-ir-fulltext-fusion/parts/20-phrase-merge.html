Phrase queries need more than co-occurrence. The positional index stores, per posting, the list of token offsets. To match "new york" you first AND the documents, then walk the two position lists looking for offsets exactly one apart. In D5, new at 3 and york at 4 satisfy the phrase; new at 11 and york at 27 do not, so the document matches once. Generalising the gap to at most k gives proximity search. The cost is a larger index, paid because exact phrase matching is essential for names and quotations.
