Cosine over TF-IDF: the second ranker

normalize, then dot
$$ \operatorname{sim}(q,d) = \frac{\vec{w}_q \cdot \vec{w}_d}{\lVert \vec{w}_q\rVert\,\lVert \vec{w}_d\rVert} = \sum_{t} \frac{w(t,q)}{\lVert \vec{w}_q\rVert}\cdot\frac{w(t,d)}{\lVert \vec{w}_d\rVert} $$
\(\vec{w}_d\)
Doc vector
TF-IDF weights
\(\lVert\cdot\rVert\)
Length
normalizes for size
\(\cos\theta\)
Angle
\(1\) = identical direction

Dividing by the vector lengths removes the document-size effect, so cosine compares direction, not magnitude. This is the cosine ranker — the one RRF will later fuse with BM25.

Build the cosine ranker now, because rank fusion in Part 7 fuses it with BM25. Each document and the query become TF-IDF vectors; cosine similarity is their dot product divided by their lengths, which is the cosine of the angle between them. Normalising by length is what makes a short and a long document comparable — only direction in term space matters. This is the same cosine from Lecture 2, now applied to TF-IDF weighted vectors to produce a full ranking. Keep it in mind: it is the "cosine" list in the RRF example.
