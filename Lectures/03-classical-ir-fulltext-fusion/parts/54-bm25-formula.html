The complete scorer: a sum over query terms of the saturating, length-aware tf factor times the smoothed idf. Read the parameters through their limits. At k1 zero the tf factor is constant, so BM25 collapses to BM1 — presence only. As k1 grows without bound the factor becomes linear in tf, recovering un-saturated term frequency. b at zero or one gives BM15 or BM11. The standard defaults, k1 around 1.5 and b around 0.75, sit deliberately between these poles. Those defaults are what every worked number in this deck uses. One notation point: the saga wrote the saturation constant as capital K1 and a separate scale S1; the standard modern form writes lowercase k1 and folds S1 = k1+1 into the numerator (k1+1)f — it is the same factor, just renamed. The variants footnote replaces a full encyclopedia slide: BM25F normalises each field by its own average length and is what Lucene/Elasticsearch run; BM25+ adds a small floor so a long document does not score essentially zero on a term it genuinely contains; the Lucene and ATIRE forms clamp idf non-negative. Knowing these exist explains why two libraries can give slightly different BM25 scores.
