Objectives · index & score
  • ✓Build an inverted index — postings, compression, skip-merge — and explain why it makes retrieval sublinear in the corpus size.
  • ✓Score documents with TF-IDF and BM25 by hand, on real numbers, including idf smoothing, saturation, and length normalization.
  • ✓Explain why BM25 beats TF-IDF — saturating tf and the \(b\) length-dial — and trace BM25's engineering history from BM1.
These are measurable outcomes — by the end you should be able to do each one on paper, not just recognise it. Part 1: build the index and score with TF-IDF and BM25, the two load-bearing skills of the day. The remaining objectives — authority, fusion, and the lexical gap — are on the next slide.
