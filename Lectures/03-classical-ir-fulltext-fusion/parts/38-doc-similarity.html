The second live application closes the loop with the cosine ranker. TF-IDF plus cosine is a complete retrieval system: weight the query, weight every document, rank by angle. The same setup detects near-duplicates — two texts whose TF-IDF vectors point almost the same way are essentially the same document, which powers dedup and plagiarism detection. Its blind spot is the recurring one: synonyms occupy different axes, so cosine cannot see that "shuttle" and "spacecraft" mean nearly the same thing. That limitation is the whole reason embeddings exist.
