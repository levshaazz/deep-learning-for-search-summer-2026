Intuition: a word that appears 10× is more relevant than once, but not 10× more. So every useful TF is sublinear in \(f\) — the seed of BM25's saturation.

Term frequency comes in flavours, all sharing one principle: more is better, but with diminishing returns. Raw count is linear and over-rewards repetition. Log-tf, one plus log f, is the classic sublinear choice. Augmented tf normalises by the document's max frequency to stop long documents from dominating. The takeaway is that good TF is sublinear in the count — which is precisely the property BM25 formalises with its saturation function. Hold that thread; it returns in Part 5.
