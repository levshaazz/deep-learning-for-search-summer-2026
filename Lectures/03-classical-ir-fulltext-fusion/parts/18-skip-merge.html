The merge is the workhorse of boolean retrieval. Two cursors walk the sorted lists; whenever the two heads are equal you emit a match and advance both, otherwise you advance the smaller. The trick is skip pointers: sparse forward links (about every root-n entries) that let you leap over runs of postings that cannot match, turning the linear scan into roughly root-n work. This is why intersecting two long postings lists is cheap even when each has millions of entries.
