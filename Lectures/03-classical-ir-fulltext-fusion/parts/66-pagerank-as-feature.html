PageRank is one feature, not the ranking

segue to learning-to-rank

Modern engines never rank by PageRank alone. It is one feature among hundreds — combined with BM25, freshness, clicks, and more by a learned ranker. That is learning-to-rank, a later lecture.

Set expectations honestly. PageRank was historically important and is conceptually beautiful, but no serious engine ranks by it alone — it would surface authoritative but irrelevant pages. Instead it becomes one query-independent feature fed, alongside BM25, freshness, click signals and hundreds of others, into a learned ranking model. That supervised combination is learning-to-rank, which we cover later in the course. The point here is to understand the signal and how it is computed; the machinery that weights it comes later.
