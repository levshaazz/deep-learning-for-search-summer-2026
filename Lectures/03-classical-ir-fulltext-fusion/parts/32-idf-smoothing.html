Сглаживание +0,5Add-half smoothing
\(\log\frac{N-df+0{,}5}{df+0{,}5}\): слагаемые \(+0{,}5\) удерживают отношение конечным и хорошо ведущим себя. Это форма RSJ / вероятностная.\(\log\frac{N-df+0.5}{df+0.5}\): the \(+0.5\) terms keep the ratio finite and well-behaved. This is the RSJ / probabilistic form.

03
Может ли стать отрицательным?Can it go negative?
Если \(df>N/2\), отношение падает ниже 1 и логарифм становится отрицательным. Lucene добавляет \(+1\) внутри логарифма, чтобы зажать его: \(\log\!\big(\frac{N-df+0{,}5}{df+0{,}5}+1\big)\).If \(df>N/2\) the ratio drops below 1 and the log goes negative. Lucene adds \(+1\) inside the log to clamp it: \(\log\!\big(\frac{N-df+0.5}{df+0.5}+1\big)\).

Форма, которую мы используем — с подстановкойThe form we use — substituted
\(\operatorname{idf}(t)=\ln\!\big(1+\frac{N-df+0{,}5}{df+0{,}5}\big)\). На нашем корпусе \(N=3\), \(df(\texttt{cat})=3\), \(df(\texttt{dog})=2\):\(\operatorname{idf}(t)=\ln\!\big(1+\frac{N-df+0.5}{df+0.5}\big)\). On our corpus \(N=3\), \(df(\texttt{cat})=3\), \(df(\texttt{dog})=2\):
\(\operatorname{idf}(\texttt{cat})=\ln\!\big(1+\frac{3-3+0.5}{3+0.5}\big)=\ln(1.1429)=0.1335\)
\(\operatorname{idf}(\texttt{dog})=\ln\!\big(1+\frac{3-2+0.5}{2+0.5}\big)=\ln(1.6000)=0.4700\)
cat 0.13 · dog 0.47

The build animation the old deck had. Plain log N over df divides by zero for an unseen term and bottoms out at zero when a term is everywhere. Adding a half to numerator and denominator — the Robertson–Spärck-Jones probabilistic form — keeps it finite. But that form can go negative when more than half the documents contain the term, so Lucene adds one inside the log to clamp it non-negative. The final smoothed idf gives cat 0.1335 and dog 0.4700 on the flagship collection, the exact values we use in the BM25 worked example later.
