Поскольку шансы — монотонное преобразование \(P\), можно ранжировать по шансам — которые удобно факторизуются.Robertson's PRP: ordering documents by probability of relevance is optimal for the user. Because the odds are a monotone transform of \(P\), we may rank by the odds — which factorize nicely.

The theoretical foundation. The Probabilistic Ranking Principle states that if you order documents by their probability of relevance to the query, you maximise the expected usefulness to the user — it is provably optimal under reasonable assumptions. The practical move is to rank by the odds of relevance rather than the probability directly, because the odds factorise into per-term contributions once you apply Bayes' rule and an independence assumption. That factorisation is what produces a sum over query terms, the shape every scorer in this part shares.
