Sort a single document's terms by TF-IDF and the top of the list reads like a human summary: shuttle, crew, lost. (The filler word "maybe" also lands at rank 4 — it appears in just one doc, so it earns a high idf — a reminder that raw TF-IDF has no notion of stopwords; a real keyphrase system would filter it out first.) Note how the weighting works — "shuttle" wins on frequency (three times) despite a lower idf, while "crew" and "lost" tie on a higher idf because they are rarer in the corpus even at lower frequency. No model, no training, no labels: the document's own statistics surface its topic. This is the standard baseline for keyphrase extraction and tag suggestion.
