Part 07

Rank Fusion

I now hold three rankers that disagree — so I convene a council of navigators and combine their votes into one robust list.

My rankers see different things — BM25 reads exact terms, a cosine ranker reads direction in vector space, PageRank reads authority. Each produces its own ranked list on its own incomparable scale. Rather than trust one navigator, I convene a council: a document several navigators rank highly should rise. The robust, parameter-light way I fuse them is Reciprocal Rank Fusion, which I build up to through the whole fusion family.
