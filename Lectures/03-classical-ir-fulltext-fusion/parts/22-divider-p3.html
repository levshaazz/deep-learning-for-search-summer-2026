Bag-of-Words &
the Vector Space Model

Прежде чем ранжировать, я должен превратить документ в нечто, что машина сможет взвеситьBefore I can rank, I have to turn a document into something a machine can weigh — вектор счётчиков.— a vector of counts.

Before I can build any scorer I need a representation. The simplest one that works is the Bag-of-Words: I throw away order and keep counts. Stack those count vectors and I get the document-term matrix; treat each row as a point in term space and I have the vector space model. Naming these explicitly fixes a gap the old deck had — TF-IDF and BM25 are weightings on top of this representation.
