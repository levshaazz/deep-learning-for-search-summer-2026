Why raw counts fail → motivate TF and IDF

frequent words drown the signal

Rank by raw count and every document is "about" the. We need two corrections: TF (a sublinear take on within-document frequency) and IDF (down-weight terms common across the corpus). That is TF-IDF, next.

Motivate the whole of Part 4. If you score documents by raw word counts, the winner is always dominated by stopwords like "the" and "of" — they are frequent everywhere and informative nowhere. Two fixes are needed and they are orthogonal: damp the within-document term frequency so repetition has diminishing value, and down-weight terms that appear in many documents because ubiquity means low specificity. Those are TF and IDF, and their product is the first real ranking weight.
