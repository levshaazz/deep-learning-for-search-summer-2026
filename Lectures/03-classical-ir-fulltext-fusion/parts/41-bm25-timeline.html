    The definitive derivation from the probabilistic relevance framework.

The provenance the old deck lacked. Maron and Kuhns in 1960 propose ranking by probability of relevance. Robertson and Spärck Jones in 1976 formalise it as the Binary Independence Model, yielding the relevance weight we will call BM1. Robertson's 1981 two-Poisson model explains why the optimal term-frequency weight should saturate. In 1994 Robertson and Walker's Okapi system at City University wins TREC and BM25 enters the world. Singhal's 1996 pivoted length normalisation is the parallel insight that the b parameter captures. Robertson and Zaragoza's 2009 monograph is the definitive treatment. Two pivots: 1994 birth, and the theory that explains it.
