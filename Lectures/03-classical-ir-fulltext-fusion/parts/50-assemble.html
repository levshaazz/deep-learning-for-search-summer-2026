All three multiply the same BM1 idf weight. The only axis that varies is how term frequency and length are handled — from "presence only" up to "saturating + fully length-normalized".

Lay the family out, as the old deck did. Every member multiplies the same BM1 idf weight; what differs is the term-frequency treatment. BM1 ignores tf entirely. BM15 adds the saturating tf factor but no length normalisation. BM11 adds full length normalisation on top. So the progression is monotone: each step keeps the previous idf and improves how it counts repetition and length. The obvious question — does a document collection want BM15 or BM11? — has the answer "it depends", which is exactly what motivates a dial between them.
