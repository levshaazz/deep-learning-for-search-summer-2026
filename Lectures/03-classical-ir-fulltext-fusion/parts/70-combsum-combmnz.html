Классика Fox & Shaw. CombSUM складывает (нормализованные) оценки; CombMNZ умножает сумму на то, сколько ранкеров вообще извлекли \(d\) — документ, найденный большим числом систем, получает буст. Оба по-прежнему зависят от нормализации оценок.Fox & Shaw's classics. CombSUM adds the (normalized) scores; CombMNZ multiplies the sum by how many rankers retrieved \(d\) at all — a document found by more systems is boosted. Both still depend on score normalization.

Fox and Shaw's combination methods, the classic score-based fusion. CombSUM simply adds the normalised scores across rankers. CombMNZ multiplies that sum by the number of rankers that retrieved the document at all, so consensus is explicitly rewarded — a document found by three systems beats one found by one even if their summed scores match. CombMNZ often edges out CombSUM in practice precisely because of that agreement bonus. But both inherit the normalisation fragility from the previous slide, which is why the field largely moved to rank-based methods.
