Spärck Jones's insight: a term's value is its rarity. A word in every document (\(df=N\)) scores \(\log 1 = 0\); a word in one document scores \(\log N\) — it pinpoints.

Karen Spärck Jones, 1972: the weight of a term should reflect how specific it is, and specificity is rarity. Inverse document frequency is the log of the corpus size over the document frequency. A term that appears everywhere has idf zero and contributes nothing; a term in a single document has the maximum idf and is a near-perfect locator. This single idea is why search ignores stopwords without ever maintaining a stopword list — they simply earn zero weight.
