Прозрение Spärck Jones: ценность терма — это его редкость. Формула выше — современная учебниковая запись этой идеи; исходную форму 1972 года покажет лекция 4. Слово в каждом документе (\(df=N\)) получает \(\log 1 = 0\); слово в одном документе — \(\log N\): оно точно указывает.Spärck Jones's insight: a term's value is its rarity. The formula above is the modern textbook form of that idea; Lecture 4 shows her original 1972 form. A word in every document (\(df=N\)) scores \(\log 1 = 0\); a word in one document scores \(\log N\): it pinpoints.

Karen Spärck Jones, 1972: the weight of a term should reflect how specific it is, and specificity is rarity. That is her idea; the formula on the slide is the modern textbook form of it (her 1972 original was an integer bucketed logarithm, shown in Lecture 4): inverse document frequency is the log of the corpus size over the document frequency. A term that appears everywhere has idf zero and contributes nothing; a term in a single document has the maximum idf and is a near-perfect locator. This single idea is why search ignores stopwords without ever maintaining a stopword list — they simply earn zero weight.
