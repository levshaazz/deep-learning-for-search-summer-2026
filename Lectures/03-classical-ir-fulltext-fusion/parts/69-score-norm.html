Можно перешкалировать оценки в общий диапазон и сложить их — но это хрупко: один аномальный результат запроса искажает min-max, а нормализацию приходится переоценивать для каждого запроса. Эта хрупкость — довод в пользу слияния на основе рангов.You can rescale scores to a common range and add them — but it is fragile: one outlier query result distorts min-max, and the normalization must be re-estimated per query. This fragility is the case for rank-based fusion.

The first family of answers normalises the scores onto a common scale and then combines them. Min-max rescales each ranker's scores into zero-to-one; z-score centres them to mean zero, standard deviation one. With scores comparable you can add them. The danger is that score distributions vary per query and are easily distorted: a single outlier blows up the min-max range, and z-score assumes a roughly stable spread that retrieval scores rarely have. So score normalisation works but is brittle and needs per-query re-estimation. That fragility is the argument for throwing scores away entirely and fusing on rank.
