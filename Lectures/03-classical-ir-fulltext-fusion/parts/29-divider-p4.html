My plan: term-frequency variants and why they are sublinear; inverse document frequency and Spärck Jones's specificity argument; the smoothing fix; the boxed product and its SMART variants; a worked example where common words vanish; the cosine ranker I will later fuse; two live applications; and finally the flaws that push me to BM25.
