Part 04

TF-IDF

My counts alone get fooled by "the" — so I re-weight: frequent in the document, rare in the corpus, the first real ranking weight.

TF-IDF is where my ranked retrieval truly begins. My plan: term-frequency variants and why they are sublinear; inverse document frequency and Spärck Jones's specificity argument; the smoothing fix; the boxed product and its SMART variants; a worked example where common words vanish; the cosine ranker I will later fuse; two live applications; and finally the flaws that push me to BM25. Every number is computed on real data.
