Случайный сёрфер с прошлого слайда превращается в вычисление: гоняй вектор рангов по ссылкам, пока он не перестанет меняться. Страница выигрывает не своим текстом, а тем, кто на неё ссылается, — и итоговый порядок вообще не зависит от запроса. Такой сигнал считают один раз и потом просто подставляют признаком в ранкер.The random surfer from the previous slide becomes a computation: push the rank vector along the links until it stops moving. A page wins not by its text but by who links to it — and the final order does not depend on the query at all. A signal like that is computed once and then simply plugged into the ranker as a feature.

Act 6’s climb, between the random-surfer slide and the by-hand power-iteration walkthrough. The graph, every iterate and the limit vector are read from data/l3-pagerank.json. Steps: (0) three pages, four links, the uniform start; (1–2) ONE update, for one node, in full — DWELL: this is the only arithmetic in the whole method, everything after is repetition; (3) the oscillation dying down; (4) the converged vector; (5) the final order — the page with two in-links wins. Students stumble on the teleport/damping term (why the update is not just the link sum) — handle it at step 1 with the “bored surfer” callback.
