The Star Catalog. Serega, now a star-cartographer, faces a galaxy of records: one match hides among a billion. You cannot fly past every star to find one — you need a catalog. Today we build the first end-to-end, find-it-fast search engine: the inverted index that turns a query into a tiny candidate set, the BM25 scorer (tuned over three decades) that orders those candidates, PageRank's query-independent authority, and rank fusion that combines several rankers into one robust list. This is the classical baseline every neural method later in the course must beat.
