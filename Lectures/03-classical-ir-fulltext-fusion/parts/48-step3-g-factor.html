An additive correction favoring shorter documents, scaled by query length. Tried during the Okapi TREC experiments — and, as we'll see, eventually dropped.

A piece of the history most modern accounts skip. During the Okapi TREC experiments, the authors added a separate additive term G that gives shorter documents a small bonus proportional to query length. It looked principled and was carried in the candidate formula. The reason to show it is the engineering lesson on the next slide: they tried it, measured it, and found its best setting was to switch it off. Keeping the dead branch visible makes the point that BM25 is the survivor of many experiments, not a clean derivation.
