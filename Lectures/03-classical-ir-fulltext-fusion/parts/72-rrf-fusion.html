Cormack, Clarke and Büttcher 2009; numbers exact from the RRF JSON. Throw away raw scores, keep only positions. A document at rank one contributes one over sixty-one, about 0.016393. D6 is rank one in both lists, summing to 0.032787, the maximum. D3 is rank two in both, summing to 0.032258. The instructive case is D2 versus D7: the two rankers swap them at ranks three and four. D2 is rank four in BM25 and three in cosine, contributing 1/64 + 1/63 = 0.015625 + 0.015873 = 0.031498; D7 is rank three then four, contributing 1/63 + 1/64 — the same two reciprocals in the other order, so both fuse to exactly 0.031498. Fusion gracefully averages the disagreement. The constant k, conventionally sixty, tunes the discount: larger k is more democratic, smaller k rewards the very top harder. Weighted RRF scales each ranker's contribution by trust.
