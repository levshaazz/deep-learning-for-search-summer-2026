Part 06

PageRank

My sextant scores text, but not trust — so I ask a new question: which stars do other stars point to?

My BM25 scores how well a document's text matches a query, but it says nothing about how trustworthy or important the document is. PageRank, Page, Brin, Motwani & Winograd 1998, supplies the missing query-independent signal from the link graph: a page is important if important pages link to it. In my star metaphor, stars vote for each other along their links, and the most-endorsed star shines brightest regardless of any particular query.
