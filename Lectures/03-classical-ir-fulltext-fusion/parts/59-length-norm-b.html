Регулятор The \(b\): длинные против коротких документов dial: long vs short documents

\(b=0\) игнорирует длину · ignores length · \(b=1\) штрафует полностью penalizes fully

При \(b=0\) вклад терма не зависит от длины. При \(b\to1\) документ длиннее среднего штрафуется сильнее, а более короткий усиливается — всё поворачивается в точке \(\mathrm{len}=\mathrm{avgdl}\). Значение по умолчанию \(0.75\) — сильный, но не полный штраф.At \(b=0\) a term's contribution is independent of length. As \(b\to1\), a document longer than average is penalized more and a shorter one boosted — all pivoting at \(\mathrm{len}=\mathrm{avgdl}\). The default \(0.75\) is a strong-but-not-total penalty.

The companion picture to saturation. Hold term frequency fixed and vary document length. At b zero the line is flat — length is irrelevant. As b rises toward one, documents longer than the average lose score and shorter ones gain, and all the lines pivot at exactly the average length, where the bracket equals one regardless of b. The default 0.75 is a deliberate three-quarters penalty: enough to stop long documents winning by sheer accumulation, not so much that a legitimately thorough long document is crushed.
