Diminishing returns on term frequency

TF-IDF is linear · BM25 saturates

From \(f=1\) to \(f=2\) adds a lot; from \(f=9\) to \(f=10\) almost nothing. TF-IDF climbs linearly forever; BM25 is concave toward \(k_1+1\). Smaller \(k_1\) saturates sooner — a whole family of curves. Length normalization moves the curve right (more occurrences needed to climb), but the asymptotic ceiling stays \(k_1+1\) regardless of document length.

A picture of saturation. TF-IDF is a straight line through the origin — the hundredth occurrence is worth the same as the first. BM25 is concave and bounded, rising steeply for the first few occurrences then flattening toward a ceiling proportional to k1 plus one. The parameter k1 selects a curve from a family: smaller k1 saturates sooner, larger k1 stays linear longer. This matches intuition — once a document is clearly about a topic, more repetitions tell you little — and it is the single most important difference between the two scorers.
