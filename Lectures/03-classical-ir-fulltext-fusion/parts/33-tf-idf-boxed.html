The SMART triple is ddd.qqq — one weighting for documents, one for queries. lnc.ltc (log-tf docs, full-idf queries) is the classic recipe.

The product is the whole point: a term scores high in a document only if it is both frequent there and rare in the corpus. The SMART notation, from Salton's system, is a three-letter code per side — term-frequency scheme, document-frequency scheme, normalisation — written as document-triple dot query-triple. The famous lnc.ltc recipe uses log-tf with cosine normalisation on documents and adds idf on the query side. You will see these codes in IR papers; they are just a compact way to name a weighting.
