Part 2 — the distinguishing words. What remains after the shared words vanish is precisely the words that distinguish the two documents: car and road for A, truck and highway for B, each with idf log 2 = 0.6931 and tfidf 0.099. This is the other half of why TF-IDF works: it amplifies the words that can tell documents apart. The cleanest possible demonstration — silence the shared words, score the distinguishing ones.
