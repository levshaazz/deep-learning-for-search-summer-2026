Перевод символьных границ в токенные, и только он.Lines 5–13: accumulate \(o_{\text{chunk}} \mathrel{+}= o_i\) until the character length reaches \(|c_j|\), then close the chunk and reset. Character boundaries translated into token boundaries, nothing else.

Algorithm 1 read out properly, which the deck never did. The load-bearing detail is o_i: it is NOT an offset, it is the LENGTH OF EACH TOKEN IN CHARACTERS, and lines 5–13 accumulate it until it reaches |c_j| — that is the entire mechanism by which a chunker that cut characters tells a model that lives in tokens where to pool. The pooled mean is over [cue_start, cue_end] inclusive, hence the (cue_end + 1) − cue_start denominator. Next slide turns this into ten lines of real code.
