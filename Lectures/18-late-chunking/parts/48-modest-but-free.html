Честная формулировка вместо прежнего «включать почти всегда»: ≈ 90 % пользы за ≈ 0 % предельной цены — при выполнении условий справа. Проверить условия дешевле, чем откатывать индекс. The honest form, replacing the old “turn it on almost always”: ~90 % of the benefit at ~0 % of the marginal cost — provided the conditions on the right hold. Checking them is cheaper than rebuilding an index.

The act's ledger, rewritten. What you buy: +1.4–1.9 nDCG on BeIR and 33 of 36 cells; training-free; query cost unchanged. Under what conditions: a bidirectional, long-context, mean-pooling encoder, and long, topically coherent documents. Outside those conditions the outcome ranges from nothing to the BGE-M3 collapse (0.246 → 0.070) the independent replication measured. The old kicker ("if your model is long-context with mean pooling, turn it on almost always") was the vendor's conclusion, not the evidence's; the replacement is conditional and still positive.
