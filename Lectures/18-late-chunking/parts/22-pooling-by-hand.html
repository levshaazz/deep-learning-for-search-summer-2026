Worked-1, on the toy from data/l16-chunk.json → pool. ONE rule applied twice: a token's contextual vector is the mean of the tokens it may read. Naive (chunk B alone) → (0,2), cos = 0. Late (whole document) → (1,1), cos = 1/√2 = 0.7071. Nothing in the model changed, the boundary did not move, the output is still one vector — only the ORDER of {cut, attend} moved. Say the second half out loud too: the vector's length falls from 2 to √2, i.e. attention over more text dilutes; that same dilution is the Needle-in-a-Haystack failure mode in act 7.
