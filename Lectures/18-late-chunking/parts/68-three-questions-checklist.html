Живой пример цены ошибки. BGE-M3 на NFCorpus при переходе на позднее чанкование падает с 0,246 до 0,070 NDCG@5 — это не «не помогло», это в три с половиной раза хуже. Правдоподобная причина — несовместимая голова пулинга; в статье механизм не разобран, так что назови это гипотезой, а не диагнозом. What the mistake actually costs. BGE-M3 on NFCorpus drops from 0.246 to 0.070 NDCG@5 when switched to late chunking — not “no help”, three and a half times worse. The plausible culprit is an incompatible pooling head; nobody has published the diagnosis, so call it a hypothesis, not a finding.

Порядок вопросов не случаен: первый отсекает API-чёрные ящики, второй — декодерные эмбеддеры, третий — ровно тот случай, на котором репликация и обожглась. The order is deliberate: the first question rules out black-box APIs, the second rules out decoder-based embedders, the third is exactly the case the replication got burned on.

The actionable checklist, ordered by how cheaply each question is answered and how expensive the mistake is. (1) token-level outputs — a pooled black-box API cannot do this at all; (2) ≥8192 context AND bidirectional attention — a decoder embedder under a causal mask cannot let the first half of a document see the second, which is the structural argument from the "conditioned on previous chunks" slide; (3) a mean-pooling head — CLS and max are called out as incompatible by Jina's blog, and last-token pooling is simply undocumented anywhere. Then the price tag: BGE-M3 / NFCorpus, 0.246 → 0.070 NDCG@5 in the independent replication. Label the pooling-head explanation a hypothesis: no one has published the diagnosis.
