Отсрочка бесплатна ровно тогда, когда отложенный оператор коммутирует со счётом. Усреднение коммутирует — позднее чанкование берёт контекст даром и платит один раз, при индексации; отсюда и обещание предыдущего слайда про цену запроса. MaxSim — нет: все векторы обязаны дожить до запроса, и платит он с каждого. A deferral is free exactly when the deferred operator commutes with scoring. The mean commutes — late chunking gets context for nothing and pays once, at index time; hence the previous slide’s promise about query cost. MaxSim does not — every vector must survive until the query, and every query pays.

The act's mirror — index-time bill versus query-time bill — was shown as a table and as gigabytes, but never derived. Here it is, and it is one property: linearity. Mean pooling commutes with the inner product, so ⟨q, mean_j θ_j⟩ = mean_j ⟨q, θ_j⟩ and the sum can be collapsed before the query exists — that is exactly why late chunking's context is free at query time and why the drop-in claim on the previous slide holds. MaxSim's max does not commute with anything of the sort: the argmax over document tokens depends on the query token, so no per-document precomputation can stand in for it and every vector has to survive until the query arrives. Both operators start from the SAME contextual token vectors of a single encoder pass — the fork is not in the encoder but in which operator you postpone. A good closing question for the room: which other retrieval-time operator commutes with scoring, and what would postponing it buy?
