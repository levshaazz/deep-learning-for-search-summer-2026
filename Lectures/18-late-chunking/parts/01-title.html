Lecture 18 — "Late Chunking" (Günther et al. 2024, arXiv:2409.04701). Contextual chunk embeddings for long documents. It follows L17 (query rewriting — the other end of the same RAG pipeline) and hands off to L19 (the same pipeline on a non-English corpus). Séréga narrates: naive chunking tears the pages out first, then reads each alone; late chunking reads the whole book, THEN marks the boundaries, so every chunk remembers its subject. Chunk Norris (L15) chops scrolls; late chunking corrects his over-eager slicing. Bilingual RU/EN — toggle with L.
