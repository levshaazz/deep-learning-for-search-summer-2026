(да/нет), и четыре ячейки — это TP (вернули и релевантен), FP (вернули, не релевантен), FN (релевантен, не вернули), TN (правильно не вернули).Build the grid from scratch: cross did we return it? (yes/no) with is it actually relevant? (yes/no), and the four cells are TP (returned & relevant), FP (returned, not relevant), FN (relevant, not returned), TN (correctly left out).

$$ \mathrm{Precision} = \frac{\mathrm{TP}}{\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FP}} = \frac{\text{relevant retrieved}}{\text{retrieved}}, \qquad \mathrm{Recall} = \frac{\mathrm{TP}}{\mathrm{TP}+\mathrm{FN}} = \frac{\text{relevant retrieved}}{\text{relevant}} $$

В ранжировании мы применяем их на отсечке \(k\) — «извлечённые» = топ-\(k\) — получая \(\mathrm{Precision}@k\) и \(\mathrm{Recall}@k\).In ranking we apply these at a cutoff \(k\) — "retrieved" = the top \(k\) — giving \(\mathrm{Precision}@k\) and \(\mathrm{Recall}@k\).

Precision and recall are not IR inventions — they are the confusion matrix from classification, read through a retrieval lens. Precision is "of what I returned, how much was right"; recall is "of all the right answers, how many did I return". In ranking there is no single "returned" set, so we impose a cutoff k: the top k is the retrieved set. That turns both into functions of k, which is where the real story begins on the next slide.
