Gaming in practice

where Goodhart bites for real

Adversaries & artifacts

  • ·SEO spam — keyword-stuffing games lexical relevance signals.
  • ·Clickbait — maximises CTR while tanking dwell & trust.
  • ·Feedback loops — train on biased clicks → reinforce position bias → train on it again.

Defences

  • ·Held-out human judgments the optimiser never sees.
  • ·Guardrail + satisfaction metrics, not just the target.
  • ·Debias clicks (IPS) before training on them.
  • ·Confirm offline wins with an online A/B / interleaving test.

Every metric in this lecture is a proxy. The discipline is to keep one honest signal the optimiser can't touch.

Goodhart isn't abstract; it's the daily reality of running a search engine. SEO spam games lexical signals; clickbait games CTR; and the nastiest case is the feedback loop — you train a ranker on click logs, which are biased toward whatever the old ranker showed at the top, so the model learns to reinforce position bias rather than relevance, and the next round of logs is even more biased. The defences echo the whole lecture: keep held-out judgments the optimiser never sees, watch guardrails and satisfaction not just the target, debias clicks with IPS before learning from them, and always confirm an offline win online. One honest, untouchable signal is what keeps the rest from rotting.
