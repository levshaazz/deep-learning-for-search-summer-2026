The cost wall

!
You cannot judge everything

\(N\) docs \(\times\) \(|Q|\) queries explodes. A 1M-doc corpus with 50 queries = 50M pairs — impossible to label exhaustively.

So we judge only a sample of documents per query. Which sample? That is the pooling problem on the next slide.

Judgments are expensive human labour. A guideline document pins down what the query means and what counts as relevant, so different assessors agree. The fundamental problem is scale: you cannot label every document for every query — the product is astronomical. Real corpora are judged on a sample, and the sampling strategy matters enormously, because a metric only sees the docs that were judged. That sets up pooling.
