I'm Serega, and today we're at the proving grounds. Two search systems, same query — which one is better? "Looks good to me" is not an answer. Today we learn to put an honest, comparable number on a ranking, and then to ask the harder question: is the difference real, or did we just get lucky? Offline metrics, statistical significance, and the whole online stack — A/B tests, interleaving, position bias. Every later claim ("neural beats BM25", "reranking helps") can then be checked rather than asserted.
