The practical close to the offline half, part 1 — the binary-relevance tasks. One-answer tasks like QA or navigation → MRR. Exhaustive tasks like legal discovery → recall. Snippet quality → precision at the visible cutoff. General ranked retrieval with binary labels → MAP. The graded-relevance tasks and the closing rule are on the next slide.
