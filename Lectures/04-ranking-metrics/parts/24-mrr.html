Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)

how soon does the first hit arrive?
$$ \mathrm{MRR} = \frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_{q\in Q} \frac{1}{\mathrm{rank}_1(q)} $$
\(\mathrm{rank}_1(q)\)
First hit
rank of the first relevant doc for query \(q\)
\(\tfrac{1}{\mathrm{rank}_1}\)
Reciprocal rank
\(1\) if first, \(0.5\) if second, \(\ldots\)
\(|Q|\)
Query count
average over the query set

Ideal for known-item / QA search: one good answer, found fast. Ignores everything after the first hit.

MRR cares about one thing only: how far you must scroll before the first correct answer. The reciprocal makes rank 1 worth 1.0, rank 2 worth 0.5, rank 10 worth 0.1 — it rewards getting something right immediately and ignores everything after the first hit. Perfect for "I need one good answer fast" tasks like question answering or navigational queries. Next slide computes it over two queries so the mean does real work — MRR is not just one query's RR.
