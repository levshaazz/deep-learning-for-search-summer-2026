Here is my promise for the next ninety minutes. By the end you can collect relevance judgments, compute every offline metric on this list — Recall@k, P@k, MRR, MAP, nDCG — by hand, prove that a difference between two systems is real rather than lucky, and read an online A/B result with its position-bias caveats. And you'll know when a metric has stopped measuring what you care about. Each of these maps directly onto a Part of the agenda you just saw.
