Жеребьёвка. В каждом раунде монета выбирает, кто выбирает первым, затем оба ранкера добавляют свой лучший оставшийся документ. B выигрывал первый выбор в каждом раунде, поэтому ведёт в каждой паре — но шесть слотов всё равно делятся поровну: 3 от B \((b_1,b_2,b_3)\) и 3 от A \((a_1,a_2,a_3)\). B выигрывает этот запрос по кликам, а не за счёт большего числа слотов.Draft. Each round a coin picks who drafts first, then both rankers add their top remaining doc. B won the first pick every round, so it leads each pair — but the six slots still split evenly: 3 from B \((b_1,b_2,b_3)\) and 3 from A \((a_1,a_2,a_3)\). B wins this query on the clicks, not by taking more slots.

$$5\text{ of 6 slots clicked} \Rightarrow \mathrm{credit}_A=2,\ \mathrm{credit}_B=3$$

Кредит. Клики по слотам с тегом A \((a_1,a_3)\) идут в зачёт A; три слота B \((b_1,b_2,b_3)\) все идут в зачёт B; слот 4 \((a_2)\) остаётся некликнутым — это строка q1.Credit. Clicks on A-tagged slots \((a_1,a_3)\) score A; B's three slots \((b_1,b_2,b_3)\) all score B; slot 4 \((a_2)\) goes unclicked — this is the q1 row.

overview
Interleaving is the sharpest tool in online evaluation. Instead of showing A to one group and B to another, you blend both rankers into a single list the user sees — team-draft alternates picks like captains choosing a team, tagging each slot with its source. Every click credits the ranker that placed that result. Because both rankers compete within the same list for the same user, position bias largely cancels: each occupies high and low slots equally often. Each round both teams pick, so the slots split evenly; B wins on the clicks. Over five queries A earns 9 credits, B earns 16, preference for B is 16/25 = 0.64, B preferred in four of five queries. Joachims showed interleaving needs far less traffic than A/B to reach the same confidence. Two caveats. First, you don't declare a winner from the bare 0.64 — you run a binomial/sign test on the per-query preferences (the per-query pairing is exactly why interleaving is more sensitive than A/B at equal traffic). Second, team-draft can mis-credit documents the two rankings share, and it has a known tie bias; probabilistic and optimised interleaving (Radlinski & Craswell; Chapelle et al. 2012, already cited on slide 60) fix these.
