No single metric is "best" — match the metric to the user's task. Report a primary metric plus one or two diagnostics.

Part 2 — the graded-relevance tasks. Graded web search → nDCG@10, the de facto standard. Cascade behaviour → ERR or RBP. The rule: pick one primary metric tied to the task, and keep a couple of diagnostics alongside. Then — because offline labels are still a proxy — confirm online, which is Part 6.
