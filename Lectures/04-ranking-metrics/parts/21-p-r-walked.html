4/4
Walk it one row at a time, reading numerator/denominator aloud. Precision rises 0 → 0.333 → 0.4 → 0.5 — it never gets high because the relevant docs are diluted by hockey docs at every odd rank. Recall marches 0 → 0.25 → 0.5 → 1.0: by rank 8 we have found all four relevant docs. The two metrics disagree on purpose; that disagreement is exactly why we report both at chosen cutoffs — and why the tradeoff curve on the next slide matters.
