Relevance
judgments

Before I can score a ranking, I need to know which results are actually correct — so first I appoint the referee.

✓ You can now justify why one number is unavoidable. Next: where the ground truth comes from.

Every offline metric I compute compares the system's ranking against a set of relevance judgments — qrels. My referee decides, for each (query, document) pair, whether it is relevant. Without this ground truth I have nothing to measure against. This part is where that ground truth comes from and how much I should trust it.
