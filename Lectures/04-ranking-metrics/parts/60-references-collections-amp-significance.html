Appendix references, part 2 — collections and significance. Sanderson is the survey of test-collection-based evaluation. Smucker, Allan and Carterette is the definitive comparison of significance tests for IR — it argues for the randomization/permutation test, which is why we started there. Chapelle et al. is the large-scale validation of interleaved evaluation; Joachims, Swaminathan and Schnabel is the IPS/unbiased learning-to-rank paper.
