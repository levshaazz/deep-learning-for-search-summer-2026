✓ Теперь вы умеете собирать оценки и читать их согласованность. Далее: превратим их в балл.You can now collect judgments and read their agreement. Next: turn them into a score.

With my ground truth fixed, I can count. These metrics all treat relevance as yes/no. Precision and Recall answer "how clean / how much" at a cutoff; F combines them; MRR asks "how soon the first hit"; MAP rolls precision over every hit into one number. I compute them all on the running ranking — and MRR and MAP get a second query so you see the averaging actually do something.
