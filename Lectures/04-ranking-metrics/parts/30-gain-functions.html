Экспоненциальный выигрыш \(2^{\mathrm{rel}}-1\) — отраслевой стандарт (веб-поиск): он позволяет документам высшей градации доминировать. Линейный мягче. Оба питают одну и ту же машинерию DCG.The exponential gain \(2^{\mathrm{rel}}-1\) is the industry default (web search): it makes top-grade documents dominate. Linear is gentler. Both feed the same DCG machinery.

A gain function maps a relevance grade to a reward. Linear is the obvious choice — the gain is the grade. Exponential, 2 to the rel minus 1, is what Google-scale web search uses, because it makes the difference between "perfect" and "okay" enormous: with our 0/1/3 grades, linear gives 0/1/3 but exponential gives 0/1/7, so a grade-3 doc is worth seven marginal ones rather than three. The choice changes the numbers but not the procedure; the next E2E computes nDCG under both.
