overview
The headline graded computation, all exact, linear gain. Discounts: 1/log2 3 = 0.6309, 1/log2 5 = 0.4307, 1/log2 7 = 0.3562, 1/log2 9 = 0.3155. Contributions: grade 3 at rank 2 → 1.8928, grade 3 at rank 4 → 1.2920, grade 1 at rank 6 → 0.3562, grade 1 at rank 8 → 0.3155. DCG = 3.8565. The ideal ordering is grades 3,3,1,1 at ranks 1–4, giving IDCG = 5.8235. nDCG = 3.8565 / 5.8235 ≈ 0.6622.
