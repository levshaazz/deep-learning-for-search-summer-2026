Притянуть запрос к его позитиву \(d^+\); оттолкнуть его от негативов \(d^-\).Pull the query toward its positive \(d^+\); push it away from the negatives \(d^-\).
  • ·Убери отталкивание — и модель жульничает: схлопывает всё пространство в одну точку, где каждый пассаж одинаково близок, а потеря выглядит прекрасно.Drop the push and the model cheats — collapse the whole space to one point, where every passage is equally close and the loss looks great.
  • ·Негативы — единственная сила, останавливающая это схлопывание.Negatives are the only force stopping that collapse.

InfoNCE: выбери позитив из NInfoNCE: pick the positive out of N

поиск как вопрос с выбором ответаretrieval as a multiple-choice question
Определение · контрастная потеряDefinition · the contrastive loss
$$ \mathcal{L} = -\log P^+,\quad P^+ = \frac{e^{\,\cos(q,d^+)/\tau}}{e^{\,\cos(q,d^+)/\tau} + \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} e^{\,\cos(q,d_i^-)/\tau}} $$

где ℒ = функция потерь, q = запрос, d+ = позитив, di− = i-й негатив, cos = косинусная близость, τ = температура, N = кандидаты (1 позитив + N−1 негативов), P+ = вероятность на позитиве.where ℒ = the loss, q = the query, d+ = the positive, di− = the i-th negative, cos = cosine similarity, τ = temperature, N = candidates (1 positive + N−1 negatives), P+ = probability on the positive.

  • ·Вопрос с выбором ответа среди \(N\) кандидатов — 1 позитив + \(N-1\) негативов; оцени каждого по сходству, софтмакс при \(\tau\), вся вероятность на \(d^+\).A multiple-choice question over \(N\) candidates — 1 positive + \(N-1\) negatives; score each by similarity, softmax at \(\tau\), all probability on \(d^+\).
  • ·Лучшие негативы делают вопрос труднее и информативнее.Better negatives make the question harder and more informative.
  • ·\(\tau\) управляет тем, насколько резко модель вынуждают выбирать.\(\tau\) controls how sharply the model is forced to choose.
InfoNCE = softmax over N candidates = 1 positive + (N−1) negatives, at temperature τ; loss is -log P+. (N is the number of CANDIDATES the softmax chooses among — the same N as the log N ceiling on S12.) Read once and the field clicks: harder negatives = harder, more informative question; τ sets choice sharpness. Right plate: the in-batch arena. Next: read the loss off a real lineup by hand.

Hardness is an angle

Негатив труден, когда теснит запрос по углу — и он стоит ровно того градиента, что несёт.A negative is hard when it crowds the query in angle — and it is worth exactly the gradient it carries.

Sir Cosine returns to measure the angle. The central lens (Wang & Liu, 2021): the negative gradient is a Boltzmann softmax of sim/τ; τ→0 ⇒ nearest-neighbour hard-max. FaceNet warning: the very hardest negatives can collapse training (an optimisation failure of a genuine negative).

InfoNCE знает лишь «позитив ближе всех» и толкает прочь любой негатив. The act named the third cure and drew an arrow — margin_teacher → bi-encoder — but never wrote the objective, so "the teacher unmasks the impostor" stayed a slogan. Margin-MSE (Hofstätter et al. 2020) is a squared error between two MARGINS: the student's s(q,d+) − s(q,d−) against the teacher's. Say the contrast aloud: InfoNCE has no notion of relevance, only "the positive must win", so it pushes every mined negative away with the Boltzmann weight from slide 18 — which is exactly what makes an unlabelled positive toxic. Margin-MSE replaces the direction with a target. When the mined negative is really a positive, the cross-encoder scores both passages alike, the target margin collapses to about zero, and the student is penalised for separating them at all. A genuinely worthy negative keeps a large teacher margin and is still pushed. That is the whole mechanism of the impostor act in one line, and it is also why the modern recipe on the next slides can afford to mine aggressively.

The mechanism under the act's headline claim — "uniformity from in-batch, the boundary from a few clean hard negatives" — which slides 41-43 assert but never derive. Write InfoNCE as two terms: an alignment term A that pulls the positive in, and a log-sum-exp term U over every candidate in the batch. Only U sees the negatives, and U is a sampled estimate of how crowded the corpus is around the query. That single observation explains the whole two-stage recipe. Tens of thousands of cheap in-batch negatives make that estimate broad and nearly unbiased, so the repulsion is spread across the sphere — that is what "uniformity" actually is, not a slogan. A handful of mined hard negatives does the opposite: as slide eighteen showed by hand, the softmax weights collapse onto the nearest few, so those few dominate the direction of the push and set the decision boundary. So the two stages are not two ideas glued together — they are two samplers feeding the same sum. It also answers the question the recipe leaves open, namely why the hard set stays small: push the sample too far toward the edge and the estimate stops being about the corpus at all, and the model only tidies the neighbourhood of the boundary. The impostor from act four is the same bias with the sign of the error flipped. Symbolic on purpose — batch sizes and hard-negative counts are recipe-specific.
