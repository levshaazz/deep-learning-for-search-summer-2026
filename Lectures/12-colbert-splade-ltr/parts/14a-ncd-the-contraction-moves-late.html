Позднее взаимодействие на схемеLate interaction, drawn

шаг (←/→): би-энкодер → кросс-энкодер → ColBERT — смотри не на прямоугольники, а на ячейкиstep (←/→): bi-encoder → cross-encoder → ColBERT — don’t count the boxes, count the cells

Считай не прямоугольники, а ячейки. У ColBERT кодировщик снова один, как у разведчика — переехало стягивание: оно ушло в самый конец, в MaxSim, токен против токена. И серые векторы вернулись не рядом, а сеткой — по вектору на каждый токен документа. Вот и всё позднее взаимодействие: деталь токенов без \(N\) прогонов BERT — а счёт за эту сетку выставит следующий слайд.Don’t count the boxes here — count the cells. ColBERT is back to one encoder, exactly like the Scout; what moved is the contraction: it slid to the very end, into MaxSim, token against token. And the grey vectors came back not as a row but as a grid — one vector per document token. That is late interaction, whole: token detail without \(N\) BERT passes — and the bill for that grid arrives on the next slide.

The same circuit L10 used to count encoder boxes, now stepped one further — and this time the thing to watch is not the boxes but the cells. Step zero is the bi-encoder, for calibration: one encoder box, on the query; a row of grey pre-encoded cells on each document card; a cheap cup per document. Step one is the cross-encoder, and the grey cells vanish — an empty dashed slot where the cache would have been, a full encoder box on every row, N of them. That is the wall Lecture 10 hit. Now step two, and read it as a diff against both. Against the cross-encoder: the vectors are BACK, so the corpus is precomputable again and the encoder count drops from N to one. Against the bi-encoder: the vectors have multiplied — each card now carries a GRID of grey cells, not a row, one per document token, and the query is encoded once into a grid of token vectors too. Nothing has been pooled into a single vector before the query meets the document. That is what late interaction actually means, in ink: the contraction did not disappear, it MOVED — from the moment of encoding to the moment of scoring, where it becomes MaxSim, each query token against its best document token. So the encoder count is the bi-encoder's, and the detail is the cross-encoder's. What did we pay? Look at the grid again: a vector per token, not per document. That is the storage bill, and it is exactly what the next slide prices — two hundred and eighty-six gibibytes, naive, on MS MARCO.
