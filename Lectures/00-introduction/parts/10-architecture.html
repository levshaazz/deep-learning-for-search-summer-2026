Each block is roughly 1–2 weeks of the course.

This is the map of the whole journey. We climb left to right: classical IR gives us the baseline and the vocabulary; embeddings give us meaning; neural retrieval makes it learnable; ANN and vector databases make it fast at scale; RAG grounds language models; and we close on agentic and multimodal retrieval. The doodle strip restates the climb as stepping-stones — the same arc, friendlier.
