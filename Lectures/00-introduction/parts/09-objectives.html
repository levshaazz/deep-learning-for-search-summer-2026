What you will be able to do

Objectives
  • ✓ Build an end-to-end neural search / RAG pipeline, from corpus to answer.
  • ✓ Reason about classical IR versus neural retrieval, and when each wins.
  • ✓ Evaluate retrieval and ranking with proper metrics — nDCG, MAP, MRR, \(\text{Precision@}k\), \(\text{Recall@}k\).
  • ✓ Design a search system for production: latency, scale, and feedback loops.
  • ✓ Read and implement key papers behind modern retrieval.
These are the course-level outcomes — measurable skills, not topics. By the final defense you should be able to ship a small neural search system and defend your design choices with metrics.
