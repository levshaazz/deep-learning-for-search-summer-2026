IMPORTANT mechanism: this is NOT cosine retrieval. llava:7b is a generative VLM — for each image it was shown the five candidate captions and prompted "Which ONE caption best describes it? So it GENERATES a forced multiple-choice answer grounded in the pixels; it does not produce a CLIP-style image embedding or an argmax over cosine. Reserve the cosine/argmax-diagonal framing for the toy CLIP matrix (slides 21–22) and CLIP proper.
