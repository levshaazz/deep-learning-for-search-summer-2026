Горстки пассажей хватает, чтобы диктовать ответ.An attacker plants documents built to be retrieved: close to the query for the retriever, persuasive for the generator. A handful of passages is enough to dictate the answer.
  • ·Почему дёшево — побеждает не большинство корпуса, а топ-k выдачи: одно точное попадание в нишевый запрос вытесняет честные пассажи из окна контекста.Why it is cheap — the winner is not the corpus majority but the top-k: one precise hit on a niche query pushes honest passages out of the context window.
  • ·Где входит — открытые вики, пользовательский контент, автокраулинг, плагины: каждый канал записи в корпус — канал атаки.Where it enters — open wikis, user content, auto-crawling, plugins: every write path into the corpus is an attack path.
  • ✓Мера — контроль записи в индекс; уровни доверия источников; дедуп и аномалия-детект на вливе; происхождение пассажа рядом с ответом.Mitigation — write control into the index; source trust tiers; dedup and anomaly checks at ingestion; passage provenance next to the answer.
Attack 2 — poisoning the retrieval supply chain (PoisonedRAG, Zou et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.07867: a few adversarial passages per target question suffice against million-document corpora). The asymmetry: retrieval is argmax, not vote — you only need to win the top-k for the queries you target, so niche queries are cheapest. Every ingestion channel (UGC, crawls, plugins) is a write path; a poisoned passage often doubles as the injection carrier from the previous slide. Mitigations are supply-chain practice transplanted: gate the writes, tier the sources by trust, scan at ingestion, and show provenance so a poisoned answer is at least attributable.
