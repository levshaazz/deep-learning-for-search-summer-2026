Scouts and Judges

Два вида команды.Two kinds of crew. Разведчики сметают быстро и вширь; судьи совещаются медленно над немногими. Галактику не пересудить.Scouts sweep fast and wide; Judges deliberate slowly over a handful. You can’t judge a galaxy.

Cold open. Picture a search frontier split in two. On the left, a loose band of Scouts sweeps across the whole field — fast, fanned out, covering everything cheaply. On the right, a few Judges sit on a raised bench and deliberate, slowly and carefully, over a single case at a time. That is the whole lecture in one image: you cannot run the careful Judge on a galaxy of documents, so you send the Scouts first to bring back a shortlist. Bi-encoders are the Scouts; cross-encoders are the Judges. The catchphrase — Scouts and Judges — opens here and closes the lecture.
