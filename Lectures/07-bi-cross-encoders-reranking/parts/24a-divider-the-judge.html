Выходит СудьяEnter the Judge

Разведчик быстр, потому что читает запрос и документ порознь. Судья медлителен ровно по той же причине наоборот: он читает пару вместе — и потому видит то, что двум отдельным векторам не видно в принципе.The Scout is fast because it reads query and document apart. The Judge is slow for exactly the mirrored reason: it reads the pair together — and therefore sees what two separate vectors cannot see at all.

The missing curtain. Every other agenda item of this lecture opens on its own divider; the cross-encoder — the second protagonist of a lecture literally titled "bi- and cross-encoders" — used to start mid-act, and the agenda link had nowhere honest to land. Say the mirrored trade out loud before any architecture appears: the Scout's speed comes from reading apart (precompute everything, compare cheaply), the Judge's quality comes from reading together (full token-level interaction between query and document inside one forward pass) — and reading together is exactly what cannot be precomputed. The next slide opens the architecture.
