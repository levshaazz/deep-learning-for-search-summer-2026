The newest branch of the Judge family. A listwise LLM reranker reads a numbered window of passages and outputs a full reordering, sliding a window of twenty with stride ten over the top hundred. The numbers are from the RankGPT paper. Two lessons. First, capability is not automatic: zero-shot GPT-3.5 scores below a supervised two-hundred-twenty-million-parameter monoT5, so a big model is not free quality. Second, distillation closes the gap cheaply — RankZephyr, an open seven-billion model distilled from GPT-4-reranked lists, matches or beats GPT-4 itself. These are powerful but slow, so they sit at the very tip of the cascade.
