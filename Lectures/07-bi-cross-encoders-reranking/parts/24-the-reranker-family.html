The reranker lineage. monoBERT, Nogueira and Cho 2019, is the canonical cross-encoder reranker: feed the joint input to BERT, read CLS through a linear layer, get a score. monoT5 does the same with a T5 that emits the token true or false. These are pointwise — they score one document at a time; pairwise and listwise variants compare documents instead. In practice the small ms-marco-MiniLM is the workhorse Judge, fast enough to rerank about a hundred candidates inside a tight latency budget.
