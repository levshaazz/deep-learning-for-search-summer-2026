RankNet \(\to\) LambdaRank \(\to\) LambdaMART — рабочая лошадка классического Learning-to-Rank. Это мост в L8.RankNet \(\to\) LambdaRank \(\to\) LambdaMART is the workhorse of classic Learning-to-Rank. That is the bridge to L8.

Three ways to train a ranker, and the bridge to the next lecture. Pointwise scores each document independently and sorts — binary cross-entropy, which is precisely what a cross-encoder does. Pairwise learns the relative order of two documents with the RankNet logistic loss; duoT5 is the pairwise cross-encoder. Listwise optimizes the entire ordering toward the ranking metric directly — LambdaRank weights the RankNet gradients by the nDCG change from a swap, and LambdaMART puts those gradients inside gradient-boosted trees. That RankNet-to-LambdaMART line is the spine of classic Learning-to-Rank, which Lecture 8 takes up.
