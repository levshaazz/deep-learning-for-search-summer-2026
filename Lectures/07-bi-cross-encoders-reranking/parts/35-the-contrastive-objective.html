Один позитив \(d^+\) против многих негативов: софтмакс с температурой \(\tau\) (меньше \(\tau\) — резче, сильнее давит на трудные негативы). DPR’s top-20 climbs from \(69.1\) as the in-batch negatives grow \(7\!\to\!127\); E5 trains at batch \(32{,}768\).

The loss behind every modern retriever, and a callback to Lecture 6. InfoNCE is a softmax over similarities: the numerator is the positive pair, the denominator sums the positive and all the negatives, divided by a temperature. Minimizing it pulls the query toward its true document and pushes it away from the rest — exactly DPR's negative log-likelihood of the positive. Two knobs matter. Temperature sharpens the distribution; a small tau makes hard negatives dominate. And batch size is a lever, because the negatives are largely the other documents in the batch — DPR's accuracy climbs with batch size, and E5 trains at a batch of thirty-two thousand to drown the positive in negatives.
