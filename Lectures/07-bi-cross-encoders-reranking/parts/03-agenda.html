Six moves, about ninety minutes. One: the bi-encoder, the fast Scout, and the real-world embedder zoo with its tricks — prefixes, Matryoshka, quantization. Two: the cross-encoder, the careful Judge, and the reranker family from monoBERT to LLM rerankers. Three: how these models are actually trained — the contrastive objective, in-batch and hard negatives, and cross-encoder distillation. Four: the offline stage — preparing data and building the ANN index, and the build-time versus query-time split. Five: staging them into the neural cascade, the full pipeline, vector databases, hybrid search and RAG. Six: the engineering limits — the recall floor, rerank depth against a latency budget, and the honest twist that a single dense vector does not always beat BM25. Click any item to jump.
