Four moves. One: the bi-encoder, the fast Scout — two towers into one space, the score is a cosine, and precomputing the document vectors is its whole speed argument. Two: the cross-encoder, the careful Judge — joint reading buys accuracy and costs the offline cache. Three: stage them into the neural cascade, the L1 funnel made neural. Four: the engineering — the recall floor that caps the pipeline, how deep to rerank against a latency budget, and the honest twist that a single dense vector does not always beat BM25.
