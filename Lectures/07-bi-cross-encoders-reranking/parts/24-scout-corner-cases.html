Не обязательно. Энкодер, обученный на MS MARCO, деградирует вне домена: на BEIR (zero-shot) BM25 даёт nDCG@10 \(0.43\) и часто обходит одиночный плотный вектор \(0.38\). Длинные документы упираются в лимит токенов; иной язык — в одноязычную модель.Not necessarily. An encoder trained on MS MARCO degrades out of domain: on BEIR (zero-shot) BM25 reaches nDCG@10 \(0.43\) and often beats a single dense vector’s \(0.38\). Long documents hit the token limit; a different language hits an English-only model.

Урок.Lesson. Обобщение не бесплатно: мерь на своём домене, адаптируй (дообучение, GPL) или гибридизуй с BM25 (см. дальше).Generalization is not free: measure on your domain, adapt (fine-tune, GPL), or hybridize with BM25 (coming up).

The corner case that bites every team. The MTEB leaderboard is an average over generic benchmarks; a model that wins there can lose on your corpus, because retrievers trained on MS MARCO degrade out of domain — that is the central BEIR finding, where BM25's zero-shot nDCG of 0.43 frequently beats a single dense vector's 0.38. Add the other traps: documents longer than the token limit get silently truncated, and an English-only model fails on other languages. The fixes are the throughline of the rest of the lecture — measure on your own domain, adapt with fine-tuning or generative pseudo-labeling, or hybridize with the lexical signal of BM25.
