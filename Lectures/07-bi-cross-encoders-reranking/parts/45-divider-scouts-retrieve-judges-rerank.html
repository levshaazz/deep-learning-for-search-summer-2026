Не выбирай — выстрой стадии. Дёшево-и-широко сначала, дорого-и-узко потом. Это воронка из L1 — теперь нейронная.Don’t choose — stage them. Cheap-and-wide first, dear-and-narrow last. The L1 funnel — now neural.

The synthesis. We refuse to choose between fast and careful. Stage them: the Scouts sweep cheap and wide first, then the Judges deliberate dear and narrow last. This is exactly the retrieve-then-rerank funnel from Lecture 1, The Lost Record — only now both stages are neural. Cheap-and-wide protects recall; dear-and-narrow protects precision where the user looks. This one funnel is the architecture of modern neural search.
