The cross-encoder forward pass, mounted and driven by the deck. Step zero: the joint input row, CLS, query, SEP, document, SEP. Step one: full attention connects query tokens to document tokens across the separator. Step two: the query-by-document attention heatmap — each query token, a row, spreads its attention over the document tokens, and each row sums to one. Step three: read the CLS vector through the linear head, w dot CLS plus b gives the logit two-point-four, and the sigmoid gives zero-point-nine-two. One logit per pair — a property of the pair, so it cannot be cached. Step four: the same head on a keyword-sharing distractor returns sigmoid of minus-one-point-one, zero-point-two-five — far below the relevant pair's zero-point-nine-two. The Judge discriminates where the Scout would blur.
