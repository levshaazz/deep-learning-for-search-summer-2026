Разведчик ставит отвлекателю обманчиво высокие \(0{,}6875\) (общее слово); судья, читая совместно, режет до \(0{,}1159\). Совместное чтение различает там, где раздельное размывает — и режет в обе стороны: оно не только давит не-ответы с общим словом, но и поднимает верные ответы, не делящие с запросом ни одного слова.The Scout gives the distractor a deceptively high \(0.6875\) (shared word); the Judge, reading jointly, cuts it to \(0.1159\). Joint reading discriminates where separate encoding blurs — and it cuts both ways: it not only suppresses keyword-sharing non-answers but also promotes true answers that share no word with the query.

What the real models actually do, the StatQuest BAM on real numbers. Query: what is the capital of Australia. The true answer, Canberra is the capital of Australia, and a tempting distractor, Australia is a large country with many cities, both contain the word Australia. The bi-encoder Scout rates them zero-point-eight-four versus zero-point-six-nine — a narrow gap, nearly fooled by the shared keyword. The cross-encoder Judge, reading query and document together, rates them zero-point-nine-nine-nine-eight versus zero-point-one-two — it sharply rejects the distractor that does not answer. Matching words is not matching meaning; joint reading discriminates where separate encoding blurs.
