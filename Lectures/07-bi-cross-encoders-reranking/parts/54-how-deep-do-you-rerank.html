The depth dial. Raising k lets the Judges see more of the Scouts' output, so they can recover answers buried deeper — but each extra candidate is another cross-encoder pass, and latency rises roughly linearly with k. The answer is B. There is no free k: pick the shallowest depth that still captures the recall your retriever provides, and trade the rest against your latency budget. When the retriever already saturates recall at a shallow depth, going deeper buys nothing but cost.
