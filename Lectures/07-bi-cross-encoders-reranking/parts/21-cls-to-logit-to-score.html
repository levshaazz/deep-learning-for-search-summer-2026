Read the head out by hand. With weights point-five and one and bias point-four, a relevant CLS vector of one and one-point-five gives a logit of two-point-four, and the sigmoid of that is zero-point-nine-two. A constructed negative CLS, one and minus-two, gives a logit of minus one-point-one and a sigmoid of zero-point-two-five. This is a constructed example to show the mechanism: a joint reader can penalise a term interaction that separate encoding would miss.
