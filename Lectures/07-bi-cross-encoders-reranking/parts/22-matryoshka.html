Matryoshka-обучение (Kusupati 2022) кладёт грубую информацию в первые измерения. Усечёшь \(768\!\to\!256\) — индекс втрое меньше, качество падает на ~1 пункт. OpenAI text-embedding-3-large на 256 измерениях обходит ada-002 на 1536.Matryoshka training (Kusupati 2022) packs coarse information into the first dimensions. Truncate \(768\!\to\!256\) and the index is 3× smaller for about one point of quality. OpenAI text-embedding-3-large at 256 dims beats ada-002 at 1536.

Matryoshka representation learning, the trick that decouples dimension from quality. You train so that the first k coordinates are themselves a valid embedding — coarse-to-fine, like nested dolls. Then at deploy time you truncate to the budget you can afford and re-normalize, with no retraining. nomic at 512 dims loses a quarter of a point versus 768, and at 256 about one point — for a three-times-smaller index. OpenAI exposes this as the dimensions parameter, and their large model truncated to 256 still beats the old ada-002 at 1536. This is the dimension-versus-cost dial from the previous slide, made real.
