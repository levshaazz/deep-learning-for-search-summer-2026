Нейронный каскадThe Neural Cascade

корпус → разведчики извлекают → судьи переранжируютcorpus → Scouts retrieve → Judges rerank
The payoff architecture, mounted live. Step zero: the whole corpus, about a million documents — no Judge can read them all. Step one: the Scouts, the bi-encoder, sweep the corpus and keep the top thousand — cheap and high-recall; the answer must survive this cut. Step two: the Judges, the cross-encoder, rerank the last ten — slow and precise, optimising precision where the user looks. Cheap-and-wide first, dear-and-narrow last. This is the L1 funnel made neural, and it is the same widget the Book uses.
