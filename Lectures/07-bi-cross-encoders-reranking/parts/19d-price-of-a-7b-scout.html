Несколько пунктов MTEB стоят кратного роста индекса и латентности кодирования. Окупается на дорогих запросах (юр-поиск, медицина, кросс-язычный корпус) — и не окупается там, где хватает MiniLM + реранкера.A few MTEB points cost a multiple in index size and encoding latency. It pays on expensive queries (legal, medical, cross-lingual corpora) — and does not where MiniLM + a reranker already clears the bar.

Смягчители на следующих слайдах: Matryoshka режет размерность, квантизация — байты; запрос кодируется один раз, корпус — офлайн.The softeners are on the next slides: Matryoshka cuts the dimension, quantization the bytes; the query is encoded once, the corpus offline.

The trade-off slide, deliberately qualitative. A 7B decoder embedder buys roughly five-plus MTEB points over the strong BERT class, but the bill arrives three ways: 2048-4096-dim vectors make the ANN index an order larger per million chunks, corpus encoding needs GPUs, and per-query encoding latency grows. The decision rule mirrors the cascade logic of this whole lecture: spend where a miss is expensive (legal, medical, cross-lingual), keep MiniLM-class + reranker where it clears the bar. Matryoshka truncation and quantization (next slides) recover much of the index cost — and remember the asymmetry: the corpus is encoded offline once, only the query is encoded per request.
