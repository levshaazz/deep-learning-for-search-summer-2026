The close, where the catchphrase opened. Scouts and Judges win the day together: a fast bi-encoder retriever feeds an accurate cross-encoder reranker, the neural cascade, the core architecture of modern neural search. But the Scouts still sweep linearly over the whole corpus — at a billion documents even cheap dot products add up. The next quests fix exactly that: approximate nearest-neighbour search makes the sweep sub-linear, jump-gates for the linear scan in Lecture 9, and ColBERT and hybrid retrieval give the Scout a richer, multi-vector read of each document in Lecture 8. Scouts and Judges — that is where we started, and that is the team that won the day.
