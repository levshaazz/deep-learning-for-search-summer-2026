Walk the cascade with a latency budget. Start at a million-document corpus. The bi-encoder Scouts retrieve, in roughly thirteen milliseconds for a query encode plus an indexed search, down to a thousand. The cross-encoder Judges then rerank those, at roughly twelve milliseconds per pair, about a hundred and twenty milliseconds for the shortlist, down to ten. The representative total is about a hundred and thirty milliseconds — these latencies are cited, hardware-dependent, never measured in our CI; the per-stage counts are the exact funnel shape.
