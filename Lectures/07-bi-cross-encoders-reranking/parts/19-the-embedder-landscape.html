MTEB — это лидерборд, а не истина. Числа не сравнимы между версиями бенчмарка и часто заявлены самими авторами. Выбирай под свой домен, язык и SLA, а не по верхней строчке.MTEB is a leaderboard, not gospel. Scores are not comparable across benchmark versions and are often self-reported. Choose for your domain, language and SLA — not for the top row.

The market, on one slide. all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is still the most-downloaded retriever for its speed and 384 dimensions, even though modern large encoders beat it by eight-plus MTEB points. E5 introduced the query and passage prefixes; BGE a query instruction; GTE and Nomic and Jina push context to eight thousand tokens; OpenAI's text-embedding-3 lets you truncate the dimension. The warning matters: MTEB averages are computed on different benchmark versions and many vendor numbers are self-reported, so treat the leaderboard as a starting shortlist, then evaluate the finalists on your own domain.
