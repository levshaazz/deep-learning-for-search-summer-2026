A turn outward, from the idea to the market. The bi-encoder is not one model but a whole family. DPR and SBERT were the openers; the MTEB leaderboard now ranks dozens — E5, BGE, GTE, Nomic, Jina, mxbai, Arctic, plus the API models from OpenAI, Cohere and Voyage.
