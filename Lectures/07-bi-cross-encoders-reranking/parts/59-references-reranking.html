Appendix, part two — the Judge lineage. Nogueira and Cho 2019 is monoBERT, the canonical cross-encoder reranker. Nogueira and colleagues 2020 is monoT5, the sequence-to-sequence variant. Nguyen and colleagues 2016 is MS MARCO, the large real-query passage dataset our cascade numbers run on. Thakur and colleagues 2021 is BEIR, the zero-shot benchmark behind the honest twist that BM25 still beats single-vector dense out of domain. Distillation links them: a Judge teaches a Scout.
