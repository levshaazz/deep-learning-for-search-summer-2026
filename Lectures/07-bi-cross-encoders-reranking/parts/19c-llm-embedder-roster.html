Та же оговорка, что и для всего зоопарка: числа лидерборда — ориентир для шорт-листа, финалистов меряй на своих данных.Same caveat as the whole zoo: leaderboard numbers build a shortlist — measure the finalists on your data.

The roster, one row per idea. RepLLaMA (Ma et al. 2023) opened the road: fine-tune LLaMA-2 with a last-token embedding and beat the BERT-class retrievers. E5-mistral added LLM-synthesized training pairs and instructions. LLM2Vec is a portable recipe (un-mask, adapt, contrast) that turns any decoder into an encoder. NV-Embed's latent-attention pooling plus two-stage instruction tuning held the MTEB top for months (score around 72.3). Qwen3-Embedding ships a 0.6-to-8B ladder with 100+ languages and Matryoshka dims; gemini-embedding is the API-only frontier on multilingual MTEB. All approximate leaderboard readings — shortlist, then evaluate on your own domain.
