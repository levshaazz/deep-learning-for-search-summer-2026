Асимметричные башни — для поиска вопрос→пассаж; общая башня — для близости предложений. Оба — контрастно обучены (InfoNCE, L6).Asymmetric towers suit question→passage retrieval; a shared tower suits sentence similarity. Both are contrastively trained (InfoNCE, L6).

Two landmark Scouts. DPR uses two separate encoders, one for questions and one for passages, pools with the CLS token, and trains with in-batch negatives plus one BM25 hard negative per question — the contrastive trick from Lecture 6. It is asymmetric: query and document are different kinds of text. SBERT is a single siamese encoder, mean-pools the token vectors, and trains on NLI and STS — symmetric. Asymmetric towers fit question-to-passage retrieval; a shared tower fits sentence similarity.
