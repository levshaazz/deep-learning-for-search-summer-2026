How a state-of-the-art embedder is actually built — three stages. First, self-supervised pretraining with no labels: the Inverse Cloze Task treats a sentence as a query and its context as the answer; Contriever crops random spans and uses a momentum queue of negatives; RetroMAE is a masked-autoencoder objective. Second, weakly-supervised contrastive learning on hundreds of millions of mined pairs at enormous batch size — E5 uses about two hundred seventy million pairs at a batch of thirty-two thousand. Third, supervised fine-tuning on labeled data with the two tricks from the last slides: mined hard negatives and cross-encoder distillation. The payoff: the unsupervised, pretrain-only E5 was the first model to beat BM25 zero-shot on BEIR without using any labeled data. And when you have a new domain with no labels at all, GPL generates synthetic queries and pseudo-labels them with a cross-encoder, lifting nDCG by over nine points.
