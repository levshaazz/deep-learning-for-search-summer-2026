Построение индексаBuilding the index

Каскад — это онлайн. Но до первого запроса есть тяжёлая офлайн-стадия: подготовить данные, заэмбедить весь корпус и собрать ANN-индекс. Сделал один раз — окупается на каждом запросе.The cascade is the online story. But before the first query there is a heavy offline stage: prepare the data, embed the whole corpus, and build the ANN index. Done once, it pays off on every query.

A turn from query-time to build-time. We have spent the lecture on what happens when a query arrives, but most of the work — and most of the cost — happens before any query exists. The offline stage prepares the corpus, runs the bi-encoder over every document, and stores the vectors in an approximate-nearest-neighbour index. It is expensive, but it is paid once and amortized over every future query. The next three slides walk it: data preparation, the index build, and the clean line between build-time and query-time.
