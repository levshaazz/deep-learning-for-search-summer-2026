Запрос и документ кодируются порознь в одно пространство; оценка — косинус. Башня документа работает офлайн, её векторы кэшируются (callback L2: косинус — линейка сэра Косинуса; L5: слова имеют координаты).Query and document are encoded separately into one space; the score is a cosine. The document tower runs offline and its vectors are cached (callback L2: cosine is Sir Cosine’s ruler; L5: words have coordinates).

The architecture. Two towers, the query encoder and the document encoder, both map their input into one shared space — the map of meaning from Lecture 5. The score is the cosine between the two output vectors, Sir Cosine's ruler from Lecture 2. The crucial asymmetry: the document tower never sees the query, so it runs offline, once, and its vectors are cached. The next slides mount the live widget and then compute the score by hand.
