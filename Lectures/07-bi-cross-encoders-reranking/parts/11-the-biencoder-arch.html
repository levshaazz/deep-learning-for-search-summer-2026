Би-энкодер (Разведчики)The Bi-encoder (Scouts)

две башни → одно пространство → косинус; документы — заранееtwo towers → one space → cosine; docs precomputed

Запрос и документ кодируются порознь в одно пространство; оценка — косинус. Обе башни обучены в одно пространство, поэтому \(q\) и \(d\) сравнимы; башня документа не видит запрос и работает офлайн — её векторы кэшируются (callback L2: косинус — линейка сэра Косинуса; L5: слова имеют координаты).Query and document are encoded separately into one space; the score is a cosine. Both towers are trained into one space, so \(q\) and \(d\) are comparable; the document tower never sees the query and runs offline — its vectors are cached (callback L2: cosine is Sir Cosine’s ruler; L5: words have coordinates).

The architecture, redrawn. Two towers, the query encoder and the document encoder, map their inputs into one shared embedding space — and because they are trained into that one space (a single siamese encoder for SBERT, or two separately-weighted ones for DPR), a query vector and a document vector are directly comparable. The score is the cosine between the two output vectors, Sir Cosine's ruler from Lecture 2. The crucial asymmetry: the document tower never sees the query, so it runs offline, once, and its vectors are cached — that is what the label d-cached marks.
