“The demo worked — so we’re done.”

The notebook that finds neighbours on your laptop is the easy 10% above the waterline. The 90% below it keeps the lanes open in production: serving under the SLA, monitoring recall and latency, rebuilding the index as documents change, re-training the IVF centroids and PQ codebook as the data drifts, and the cost of the RAM that holds it all. “The demo worked, so we’re done” is the most expensive sentence in search infrastructure.

The ANN structures are the easy part; the operations are the warning.
