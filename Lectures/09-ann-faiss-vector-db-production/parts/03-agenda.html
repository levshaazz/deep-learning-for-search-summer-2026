The route through hyperspace

approximate nearest neighbour, then ship it
  1. The death of linear scan — \(O(N\cdot d)\) cost and high-dimensional distance concentration (the Wraith). Why exact kNN can't fly the galaxy.
  2. Metrics — L2 vs cosine vs inner product on the same vectors; the same pair, three different top-1. The metric choice is a modeling choice.
  3. HNSW — a navigable small-world graph you walk to the query (greedy descent; the \(O(\log N)\) win; the ef / M knobs).
  4. IVF — carve space into Voronoi cells; probe only the nearest (recall vs nprobe).
  5. PQ — fold each vector to a handful of codebook bytes (32 → 4; ADC; the memory↔recall dial).
  6. The comparison — flat / HNSW / IVF / IVF-PQ / HNSW-PQ side by side, and a decision tree for picking one.
  7. Production — FAISS vs vector DBs, the index-factory grammar, the latency budget against a \(p_{99}\) SLA, caching, and the Iceberg below the demo.
Seven stops: linear-scan death → metrics → the three ANN lanes (HNSW / IVF / PQ) → the comparison → production. Each lane fronted by a light turn. Closes by bridging to L10 (find → answer).
