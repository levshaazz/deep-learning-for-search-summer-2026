When to reach for PQ

the memory wall, and what you give up
Applicability
PQcompression for RAM-bound, billion-scale corpora

Reach for it when: memory, not compute, is the wall — a billion full vectors won't fit in RAM. PQ folds each vector to a few bytes (32×–64×), and ADC keeps search to cheap table lookups.

The cost: distances are approximate (quantization error), so recall drops — a representative PQ-m4 recall@1 is \(\approx 0.70\). You must also train codebooks offline and retrain as the data drifts.

PQ is rarely used alone: it is the compression layer inside IVF-PQ or HNSW-PQ. The partition or graph gives speed; PQ gives the RAM. The comparison matrix next lays all five options side by side.
