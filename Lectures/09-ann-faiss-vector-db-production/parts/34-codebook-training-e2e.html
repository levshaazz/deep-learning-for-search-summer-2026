$$ \text{init } c_0=(1,1),\ c_1=(2,1):\quad \text{assign }[0,1,0,1,1,1],\quad \text{inertia }=\textstyle\sum\lVert x-c\rVert^2=\mathbf{284.0} $$ $$ \text{recompute } c_0=(1.0,1.5),\ c_1=(6.75,6.5):\quad \text{reassign }[0,0,0,1,1,1],\quad \text{inertia}=\mathbf{20.6875} $$ $$ \text{recompute } c_0=(1.3333,1.3333),\ c_1=(8.3333,8.3333):\quad \text{inertia}=\mathbf{2.6667} $$

Each Lloyd iteration assigns points to the nearest centroid, then recomputes each centroid as its cluster mean. Inertia (total squared error) is monotone non-increasing: \(284.0 \to 20.6875 \to 2.6667\). This runs once per subspace, offline — the training cost PQ pays for its compression.
