The two knobs: M and ef

build-time connectivity vs search-time breadth
Knob When Turn it up → Turn it down →
M — edges per node build time (fixed in the index) denser graph, higher recall, more robust less memory & faster build, lower recall
efConstruction build time better-quality graph, higher recall ceiling faster build, sloppier neighbour lists
efSearch (ef) query time (free to retune) wider candidate list, escapes local-min traps faster but can trap (ef = 1 → recall 0.0)
memory — grows with M (edges) — the graph overhead ef costs nothing in memory, only time

\(M\) and \(\textrm{efConstruction}\) are baked in at build; efSearch is the live dial you slide to the recall knee per query. We just saw \(\textrm{ef}=1\to\) recall \(0.0\), \(\textrm{ef}\ge2\to 1.0\).

ef/M cmp-table. M = per-node degree (build-time, sets graph density → recall & memory); efConstruction = build-time graph quality; efSearch = the query-time breadth dial (retunable, costs time not memory). The ef=1→0.0 / ef≥2→1.0 contrast references the recall-curve slide (gated l9-hnsw efSweep). Knobs list matches data/l9-bench.json hnsw.knobs [M, efConstruction, efSearch].
