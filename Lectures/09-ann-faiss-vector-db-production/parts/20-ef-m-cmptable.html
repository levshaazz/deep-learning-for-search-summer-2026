\(M\) и \(\textrm{efConstruction}\) запекаются при построении; efSearch — живой регулятор, который ты сдвигаешь к колену полноты для каждого запроса. Мы только что видели \(\textrm{ef}=1\to\) полнота \(0.0\), \(\textrm{ef}\ge2\to 1.0\).\(M\) and \(\textrm{efConstruction}\) are baked in at build; efSearch is the live dial you slide to the recall knee per query. We just saw \(\textrm{ef}=1\to\) recall \(0.0\), \(\textrm{ef}\ge2\to 1.0\).

ef/M cmp-table. M = per-node degree (build-time, sets graph density → recall & memory); efConstruction = build-time graph quality; efSearch = the query-time breadth dial (retunable, costs time not memory). The ef=1→0.0 / ef≥2→1.0 contrast references the recall-curve slide (gated l9-hnsw efSweep). Knobs list matches data/l9-bench.json hnsw.knobs [M, efConstruction, efSearch].
