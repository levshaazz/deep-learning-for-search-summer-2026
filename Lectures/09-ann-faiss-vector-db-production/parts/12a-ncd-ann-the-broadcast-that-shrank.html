Дивайдер обещал сделку — схема (нотация из L6) её показывает и выставляет счёт. Точный поиск рассылает запрос по всем \(N\) документам: \(N\) чаш, \(N\cdot d\) умножений, полнота \(1.0\) по построению. Приближённый — по кандидатам \(C\ll N\), до которых доходит обход графа: чаш столько же, сколько кандидатов, и счёт следует за \(|C|\cdot d\). Цена нарисована там же: настоящий сосед за границей раскрытых вершин чаши не получает — а неоценённое невозможно вернуть.The divider promised the bargain — the circuit (the L6 notation) draws it and bills it. Exact search broadcasts the query over every one of the \(N\) documents: \(N\) cups, \(N\cdot d\) multiplications, recall \(1.0\) by construction. Approximate search broadcasts over the candidates \(C\ll N\) that a graph walk reaches: one cup per candidate, and the bill follows \(|C|\cdot d\). The price is drawn on the same picture: a true neighbour behind the frontier never gets a cup — and what is never scored can never be returned.

ncd-ann, three steps — the hook of the HNSW lane, in the circuit notation we taught in L6. The divider just promised a bargain: a sliver of recall for a mountain of speed. This slide is that sentence turned into a checkable picture, and then it charges for it. Step zero, exact search: the query wire enters a dashed broadcast region, and that region encloses every single document in the index. Each one carries a contraction cup — one dot product, q dot d-i — so the ledger says N cups, N times d multiplications, recall one point zero. And note WHY recall is one: not because brute force is clever, but because the candidate set IS the corpus. C equals N. You looked at everything, so you cannot have missed anything. Step one, approximate search: nothing in the circuit changes. Same wire, same cup, same dot product. The only thing that changed is the SIZE of the axis you broadcast over — the region physically contracts onto a candidate set C, reached by an orange walk over a proximity graph from an entry point. Now make them look at which documents actually get a cup: not just the four the walk expanded. d5 and d6 get cups too and the walk never steps on them, because to decide where to hop next you must score every neighbour of every node you open. Scored is not the same as expanded. So the candidate set is the walk PLUS its frontier, the bill is roughly the size of C times d, with the size of C about hops times degree — still vanishingly small next to N. The documents left outside have no cup in front of them at all: the ABSENCE of the glyph is the saving. Step two is the price, and it is the whole reason this slide exists. The query has two true neighbours in the index. One, d4, the walk reached and scored. The other, d10, sits two hops from every node the search opened — behind the frontier, at a genuine local optimum, which is the only kind of miss a graph walk can actually make. Every route into it runs through a node that was scored but never expanded, so the two red dashed edges were never crossed. No cup ever forms in front of d10, and what is never scored can never be returned. Recall falls below one. That is the deal in one picture: you did not buy the speed with cleverness, you bought it by exchanging a guarantee for a budget. Everything left in this lecture — the layers, the ef knob, nprobe, the codebooks — is machinery for choosing how big that budget is. Symbolic on purpose: N, C much less than N, cost N·d versus roughly |C|·d, recall one versus under one. We have no grounded ANN recall measurement in data/, so we quote none.
