Frontier — TurboQuant

data-oblivious, online, no codebook

PQ's biggest production pain is training the codebook. TurboQuant (Zandieh et al., 2025) removes it: it is data-oblivious and online — randomly rotate the vector (concentrating its coordinates into a Beta distribution), then apply a per-coordinate optimal scalar quantizer; a two-stage MSE + 1-bit QJL residual gives an unbiased inner-product estimate.

  • Lands within a small (\(\approx 2.7\times\)) constant of the information-theoretic distortion bound.
  • Reports better recall with near-zero indexing time, outperforming existing PQ techniques.
  • We keep FAISS PQ as the taught baseline; TurboQuant is where the field is going.
Frontier nod folded into the PQ climb. Verified claims: data-oblivious/online/no codebook; random-rotation → Beta → scalar quantizer; ≈2.7× from the bound; MSE + 1-bit QJL residual → unbiased inner product. NOT a FAISS-kernel head-to-head. arXiv:2504.19874.
