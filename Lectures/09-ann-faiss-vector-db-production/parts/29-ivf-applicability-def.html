When to reach for IVF

cheap training, the boundary-neighbour catch
Applicability
IVFpartition for large, cheaply-trained corpora

Reach for it when: \(N\) is large, training must be cheap (just \(k\)-means for the centroids), and inserts are easy (assign to a cell, append to its list). nprobe retunes recall live without rebuilding.

The catch: the boundary neighbour — a true neighbour just across a cell border is missed until you probe that cell. Raising nprobe recovers it but scans more.

IVF still stores full vectors, so it does not solve the memory wall on its own — which is why production stacks it with PQ as IVF-PQ: the cells for speed, the codes for RAM. That is the third lane.

IVF applicability def-card. Use when: large N, cheap training (k-means only), easy inserts, nprobe retunable. Catch: boundary neighbours stranded in unprobed cells. Still full vectors → no memory win alone → IVF-PQ in production. Bridges to the PQ lane.
