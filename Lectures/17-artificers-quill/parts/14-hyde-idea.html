The HyDE idea (Gao ACL 2023): a question and its answer live in different registers, so generate a hypothetical answer and retrieve with that instead of the raw query. The draft may be factually wrong — what matters is it lands in document-space, inventing "under-extraction" (the term RM3 could not reach). Toy: gold 4→1, RR 0.25→1.00.
