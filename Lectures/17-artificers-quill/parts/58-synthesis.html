Золото сидело в корпусе всё это время. Каждый приём здесь переформировывал вопрос, пока фиксированный ретривер не смог его найти.The gold sat in the corpus the whole time. Every move here reshaped the question until the fixed retriever could find it.

Synthesis: the four moves each reshape the QUERY to close a specific gap — vocabulary→HyDE/expand, specificity→step-back, compositionality→decompose — while multi-query+RRF hedges the vocabulary gap via consensus. Land the thesis sentence: "Change the query, not the retriever." The retriever never moved; the question did.
