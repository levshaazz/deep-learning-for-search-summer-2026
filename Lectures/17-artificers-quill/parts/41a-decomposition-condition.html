Диагностика проверяема: разбивай, когда стороны расходятся. При \(n = 1\) они совпадают — разбивать нечего, лишний переход добавит только риск.The diagnosis is checkable: split when the sides genuinely differ. At \(n = 1\) they coincide — nothing to split, and every extra hop adds only risk.

The decomposition act had no mechanism slide: the compound trap was shown by recall counts, the flavours tabulated, the price computed — but the move itself was never stated structurally. Here it is in one line: the joint query asks for a single document satisfying the CONJUNCTION of all sub-questions; decomposition moves the conjunction OUTSIDE the existential, asking instead that each sub-question have some document of its own. The implication holds left to right only, so the split condition is strictly weaker — which is exactly why it is satisfiable when the joint one is not, and why the compound trap's recall goes from nothing to everything. The one-way gap is also the bill: evidence now lives in n documents and the reading step must recompose it, which is where per-hop success and its powers enter on the next slide. Corollary to say out loud: with one sub-question the two sides coincide, so decomposing a single-fact lookup buys nothing and costs hops.
