Шаг 1 — закрепи всё, кроме запросаStep 1 — nail down everything but the query
Корпус \(D\), золотой пассаж \(g\) и ретривер не меняются. Только так подъём можно будет приписать переписыванию, а не индексу.The corpus \(D\), the gold passage \(g\) and the retriever stay put. Only then can a lift be attributed to the rewrite instead of the index.

Шаг 2 — прогони технику AStep 2 — run technique A
Техника даёт список \(L_A\). Найди в нём позицию \(g\) — это \(\mathrm{rank}_A\), и обратная величина есть \(\mathrm{RR}_A\). Полнота отвечает на «попал ли в топ-\(k\)», RR — на «насколько высоко».The technique returns a list \(L_A\). Find where \(g\) sits — that is \(\mathrm{rank}_A\), and its reciprocal is \(\mathrm{RR}_A\). Recall answers "inside the top \(k\)?", RR answers "how high?".
\(\mathrm{RR}_A = 1/\mathrm{rank}_A\)

Шаг 3 — та же процедура для B, затем разностьStep 3 — same procedure for B, then the difference
Получаешь \(L_B\), \(\mathrm{rank}_B\), \(\mathrm{RR}_B\) — и \(\Delta = \mathrm{RR}_B - \mathrm{RR}_A\). Рядом со счётом качества веди счётчик вызовов: у каждой техники своя цена.You get \(L_B\), \(\mathrm{rank}_B\), \(\mathrm{RR}_B\) — and \(\Delta = \mathrm{RR}_B - \mathrm{RR}_A\). Keep a call meter beside the quality column: each technique has its own price.
\(\Delta = \mathrm{RR}_B - \mathrm{RR}_A\)

Итог — читай знак, а не величинуResult — read the sign, not the size
\(\Delta\) на одном запросе — наблюдение, а не вывод: RR скачет от запроса к запросу. Утверждать можно лишь знак \(\Delta\), устойчивый по набору запросов, — ровно ту долю прогонов, которую покажет слайд «игрушка против литературы».A single query's \(\Delta\) is an observation, not a finding: RR jumps from query to query. What you may claim is the sign of \(\Delta\), holding across a query set — the very share of runs the toy-vs-literature slide reports.

The workshop act frames the experiment and then tabulates results; the PROCEDURE in between — how you actually put two rewriters on one ruler — was never walked. On letters, no numbers. Step 1: hold D, g and the retriever fixed, because attribution is the whole point of the frame ("only the query moves"). Step 2: run A, locate the gold in the returned list, read rank, invert it for RR — and name the division of labour, recall = "did it make the cut", RR = "how high". Step 3: repeat for B and take the difference, keeping the LLM-call meter alongside so quality is never read without its price. Step 4 is the honesty beat and the one students skip: RR is a per-query quantity, so one Δ is an anecdote; the claim a practitioner may make is the SIGN of Δ holding over a query set — which is exactly why the next-but-one slide reports the share of seeds on which each ordering holds rather than a magnitude.
