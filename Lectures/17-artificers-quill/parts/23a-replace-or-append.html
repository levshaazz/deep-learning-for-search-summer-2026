Итог — это один и тот же выборResult — it is one and the same choice
Обе ветки решают, держать ли нить к исходному вопросу. В плотном пространстве нить — слагаемое \(+f(q)\), в разреженном — сама конкатенация. У HyDE якорь опционален, у Query2doc он встроен в конструкцию.Both branches decide whether to keep a thread back to the original question. In dense space that thread is a summand, \(+f(q)\); in sparse space it is the concatenation itself. HyDE's anchor is optional, Query2doc's is structural.

Hand-walk on letters, no numbers: the act names replace-vs-append twice (HyDE, GAR/Query2doc) but never puts the two substitutions side by side. Step 1: one draft d̂ serves both branches — the LLM call is shared. Step 2 (dense): HyDE substitutes the ENCODER'S INPUT, e = f(d̂); the query's terms never enter retrieval. Step 3 (sparse): Query2doc substitutes the QUERY STRING, q' = q ‖ d̂; the query's terms survive and the draft's terms are added. Step 4 is the unification to say out loud: both branches are answering the same question — keep an anchor to the original q or not — and the anchor is a vector summand +f(q) in one space and the concatenation itself in the other. HyDE makes it optional; Query2doc builds it in. This is why drift is HyDE's boundary and not Query2doc's.
