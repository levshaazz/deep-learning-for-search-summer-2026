Подъём снимает конъюнкты — ограничения уходят из условия поискаThe climb drops conjuncts — the constraints leave the retrieval condition

что step-back делает с областью, из которой вообще может прийти ответwhat step-back does to the region an answer can possibly come from
$$ q = t \wedge c_1 \wedge \dots \wedge c_m \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; R(q) = \{\, d \in D \;:\; d \models t \wedge c_1 \wedge \dots \wedge c_m \,\} $$ $$ q^{\uparrow} = t \;\;\Longrightarrow\;\; R(q^{\uparrow}) = \{\, d \in D \;:\; d \models t \,\} \;\supseteq\; R(q) $$

где t = тема-принцип, cj = ограничения частного случая, D = корпус, ⊨ = «документ покрывает», R = область, из которой ответ может прийти, q↑ = поднятый запрос.where t = the principle-level topic, cj = the case's constraints, D = the corpus, ⊨ = "the document covers", R = the region an answer can come from, q↑ = the lifted query.

\(R(q) = \varnothing\)

все конъюнкты вместе не покрыты ни одним чанком — область пуста, и ранжировать попросту нечего.no chunk covers all the conjuncts at once — the region is empty, so there is nothing to rank.
\(R(q^{\uparrow}) \supseteq R(q)\)

Ограничения переезжают в чтениеThe constraints move to reading
спуск прикладывает их к найденному принципу. Они не выброшены — они перестали быть ключами поиска и стали условиями ответа.the descent applies them to the retrieved principle. They are not discarded — they stopped being search keys and became answer conditions.

Обе границы акта читаются прямо отсюда: чем больше конъюнктов снято, тем шире \(R(q^{\uparrow})\) — и тем больше в нём нерелевантного, вот цена переабстрагирования. А если ограничений и не было, снимать нечего: \(q^{\uparrow} = q\), и вызов потрачен впустую.Both boundaries of the act read straight off this: the more conjuncts you drop, the wider \(R(q^{\uparrow})\) grows — and the more irrelevance it holds, which is the price of over-abstraction. And with no constraints to drop, \(q^{\uparrow} = q\): the call bought nothing.

The step-back act had no mechanism slide at all — the move was named and worked numerically, never shown structurally. Model the query as a conjunction: a topic t plus the case constraints c_1..c_m. Retrieval can only answer from R(q), the documents covering the whole conjunction; over-specific means R(q) is empty, which is exactly what cos = 0 against the principle passage reports. Step-back drops conjuncts, so R(q↑) is a SUPERSET — abstraction is monotone, it can never shrink the region, which is why the climb is safe on recall and costly on precision. The descent is not decoration: it re-imposes c_1..c_m at reading time, i.e. the constraints migrate from the retrieval condition to the answer condition. Both boundaries now follow mechanically: over-abstraction widens R until precision drops, and an already-generic query has no conjuncts to drop so q↑ = q and the LLM call is wasted.
