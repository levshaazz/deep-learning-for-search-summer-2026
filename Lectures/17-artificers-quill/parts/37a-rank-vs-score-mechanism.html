Весь аргумент устойчивости: \(r \mapsto 1/(k+r)\) ограничено сверху и убывает, поэтому сбойный парафраз портит лишь свою долю. У балла такого свойства нет.The whole robustness argument: \(r \mapsto 1/(k+r)\) is bounded above and decreasing, so a broken paraphrase spoils only its own share. A score has no such property.

The act asserts "rank-fusion is robust, score-fusion needs calibration" in a comparison table but never shows why. Two lines do it. (1) Bounded influence: every list's contribution to the RRF sum lies in (0, 1/(k+1)], so no single list — however inflated its similarities — can dominate; a weighted sum of raw scores has no such cap, because the range of σ is chosen by the encoder, not by the merge rule. (2) Bounded sensitivity: moving a document one rank changes its contribution by 1/((k+r)(k+r+1)), which shrinks quadratically with depth, so noise in the tail of any one paraphrase's list barely perturbs the total while a genuine top hit still counts. Robustness is therefore a structural property of the map r ↦ 1/(k+r) — bounded above, monotonically decreasing — not an empirical accident. Keep this distinct from the L3 hybrid case: there the problem is incomparable UNITS, here it is uncontrolled RANGE.
