Почему RM3 не может изобрести слово — носитель обратной связиWhy RM3 cannot invent a word — the support of the feedback

потолок акта, символьно: перезапрос не выходит за словарь собственной выдачиthe act's ceiling, symbolically: a re-query cannot leave its own results' vocabulary
$$ \operatorname{supp} P_{\mathrm{RM}} \;\subseteq\; \bigcup_{d \,\in\, D_k} \operatorname{supp} d $$ $$ w \notin q \cup \textstyle\bigcup_{d \in D_k} d \quad\Longrightarrow\quad P_{\mathrm{RM3}}(w) = (1-\lambda)\underbrace{P(w\mid q)}_{=\,0} + \lambda\underbrace{P_{\mathrm{RM}}(w)}_{=\,0} = 0 $$

где supp = носитель распределения (термы с ненулевой вероятностью), Dk = top-k первого прохода, w = терм-кандидат, λ = вес доверия к обратной связи из слайда о семействе B.where supp = the support of a distribution (terms with non-zero probability), Dk = the first pass's top-k, w = a candidate term, λ = the feedback-trust weight from the family-B slide.

Mechanism slide for the vocabulary act, which named the PRF ceiling but never showed it. The relevance model is built ONLY from the feedback documents, so its support is contained in the union of their term sets; any term outside that union has probability zero in both mixture components, hence zero in RM3 — for every λ. This is the formal statement of "RM3 can only re-copy what it already read": channeling is in D_k so it can be up-weighted, under-extraction is not so it can never appear. λ trades mass between the query model and the relevance model but never widens the support — turning it up only accelerates drift toward the trap's terms. Sets up the LLM turn: world knowledge is a source whose support is not bounded by the first pass.
