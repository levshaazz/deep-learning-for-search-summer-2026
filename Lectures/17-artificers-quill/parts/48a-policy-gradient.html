Как награда доходит до \(\theta\) сквозь замороженные чёрные ящикиHow the reward reaches \(\theta\) through frozen black boxes

«шаг policy-gradient подталкивает \(\theta\)» — вот чем именно подталкивает"a policy-gradient step nudges \(\theta\)" — here is what does the nudging
$$ \nabla_\theta\, \mathbb{E}_{\hat q \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot\,\mid q)}\big[\,R(\hat q)\,\big] \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{\hat q \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot\,\mid q)}\big[\, R(\hat q)\; \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\hat q \mid q) \,\big] $$ $$ R(\hat q) = R\big(\mathrm{read}(\mathrm{retrieve}(\hat q))\big), \qquad \mathbb{E}_{\hat q \sim \pi_\theta}\big[\nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\hat q \mid q)\big] = 0 $$

где θ — веса переписчика, πθ — его политика над переписываниями, q̂ — сэмплированное переписывание, R — награда за ответ. Награда входит множителем, а не аргументом производной.where θ = the rewriter's weights, πθ = its policy — a distribution over rewrites, q̂ = a sampled rewrite, R = the downstream answer reward, The reward enters as a multiplier, not as an argument of the derivative; the second identity is what makes a baseline subtraction harmless.

А подмена «правильного переписывания» на «верный ответ» — та, за которой следит Гудхарт.Sample rewrites → weight their log-gradients by the reward → step. Retriever and reader are frozen not out of modesty: they never need differentiating. And swapping “a good-looking rewrite” for “a correct answer” is the substitution Goodhart watches.

The RRR act states the objective and then says a policy-gradient step "nudges θ" — that verb was the whole mechanism, unshown. This is the score-function (REINFORCE) identity: the gradient of the expected reward equals the expectation of reward times the gradient of the log-probability of the sampled rewrite. Two consequences to say out loud. (1) The chain never passes through retrieve or read: the reward is a scalar MULTIPLIER, so both may be non-differentiable black boxes — a hosted API, an inverted index — which is what makes "keep them frozen" a design consequence rather than a modesty claim. (2) The standard baseline subtraction leaves the estimator unbiased, because the expectation of the log-gradient is zero, while cutting variance. Close on the Goodhart hook the act already carries: the learned signal is answer correctness, so whatever the reward actually measures is what the quill will learn to write toward.
