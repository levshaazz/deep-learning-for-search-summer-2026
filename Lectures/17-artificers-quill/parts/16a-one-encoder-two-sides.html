Mechanism under "two spaces", which was purely conceptual. Two points, both load-bearing. (1) HyDE changes the ARGUMENT of the encoder, not the space: the same f that embedded the corpus embeds the draft, so the retrieval score becomes a document-vs-document comparison — that is the whole reason a hypothetical answer is commensurable with real passages. (2) Because the inner product is linear, averaging k draft vectors and then scoring is identical to averaging the k per-draft similarities; and cos(e, f(d)) divides by ‖e‖, which is the same constant for every candidate, so it cannot reorder anything — it only measures inter-draft agreement. Ties directly to the k-averaging slide: the ranking is a MEAN, so independent wobble cancels while a shared bias survives, and the norm gives no warning.
