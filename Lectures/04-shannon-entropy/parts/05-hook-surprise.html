Неожиданно · стрелка подскакивает Surprising · the needle spikes

А букву, которую нельзя предугадать — редкую q, символ случайного шума — ты бы не назвал. Она несёт много: сразу несколько бит. Стрелка подскакивает. Одна и та же буква стоит по-разному — всё решает удивление.But a letter you cannot predict — a rare q, a symbol of random noise — you could not have guessed. It carries a lot: several bits at once. The needle spikes. The very same letter is worth different amounts — surprise decides.

Итак, сколько стоит буква? Ровно столько удивления, сколько она несёт — в битах. Усреднённое по всему языку, это число — энтропия, и у него есть пол (красная черта). Дальше строим стрелку. So how much is a letter worth? Exactly as much surprise as it carries — in bits. Averaged over the whole language, that number is entropy — and it has a floor (the red line). Next, we build the meter.

Cold-open hook. The metaphor is set: Séréga the 1948 telegraph operator reads a "surprise meter" (a gauge in bits). PROVENANCE: 'e' ≈ 12.7 % is the standard 26-letter CORPUS table (Lewand 2000), NOT a figure printed by Shannon 1951 — his alphabet has 27 symbols and with p(space) ≈ 0.186 the share of E falls to ≈10.3 %. A predictable letter — the "e" after "th" — barely twitches the needle (self-information ≈ 0 bits); an unpredictable letter spikes it (many bits). Same symbol, different worth: information is surprise, not content. "How much is a letter worth, in bits?" is the question the whole lecture answers. Averaged over the source, that surprise is entropy H — and the recurring red "entropy floor" line reminds us it is a hard limit, not a target.
