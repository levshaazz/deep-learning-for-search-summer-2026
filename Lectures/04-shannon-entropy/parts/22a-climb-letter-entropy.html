Термометр справа — это и есть «пол». Равномерные 27 символов держат его на потолке 4,76; частоты роняют его; контекст роняет ещё. У русского потолок выше (33 буквы, 5,044) и \(H\) выше — но доля снятого сюрприза почти та же. Сравнивать языки в битах нельзя, в избыточности — можно.The thermometer on the right is the floor. Uniform 27 symbols hold it at the 4.76 ceiling; frequencies pull it down; context pulls it further. Russian has a higher ceiling (33 letters, 5.044) and a higher \(H\) — yet the fraction of surprise removed is almost identical. You cannot compare languages in bits; you can compare them in redundancy.

Act 3's climb. Every number is read from data/l17-entropy.json (letterFreq.en26 / letterFreq.ru33) and data/l17-bench.json (fn, humanBounds) — nothing is computed in the browser. Steps: (0) uniform 27 symbols at the 4.76 ceiling; (1) English letter frequencies fan the bars out and the thermometer drops to 4.1758 against 4.7004; (2) digrams drop it again (F2 = 3.32 / 3.56); (3) Russian — 33 bars, a HIGHER ceiling of 5.0444 and H = 4.4626, i.e. more bits but the same 11–12 % redundancy; (4) the whole staircase F0→F1→F2→F3→F100 descending toward the 0.6–1.3 band. Only the top-8 bars carry labels — 26 or 33 labels in a row would collide (the viz-probe gate HARD-fails ≥50 % label overlap).
