Отрывок — 75 символов из того же «Jefferson the Virginian», ставки 27:1, ~5 часов на человека. Brown et al. 1992 give an upper bound ≈1.75 bpc from a word-trigram model — bits-per-char IS Shannon's Fn measured by a machine. Huffman 1952 is the optimal prefix code that makes the compression floor concrete (dyadic source hits H exactly). Tribus & McIrvine 1971 is the source of the von Neumann "nobody knows what entropy really is, so call it entropy" anecdote — treat as legend. All names/dates hand-verified.
