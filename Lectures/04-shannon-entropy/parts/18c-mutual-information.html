Это тот же счёт, которым в поиске меряют, сколько информации о релевантности несёт признак: отбор признаков, query clarity и рескоринг сидят на этой формуле. ⚠ And the approximation sign is not a typo: subtract the rounded numbers as printed and you get 0.2248. The correct 0.2247 comes from subtracting the unrounded entropies, and the two differ in the last digit. Every chain of roundings does this; better to show the gap than to fudge it.
The very same 0.2247 bit as the last slide, renamed: not “how much surprise the context removed” but how many bits the neighbouring letter tells you about the next one.
