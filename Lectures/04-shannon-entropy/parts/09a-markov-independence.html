Not one formula: we just counted pairs. A Russian text puts a vowel after a vowel three and a half times less often than a coin with the same bias would. A few slides from now we will measure that “less often than chance” in bits — and it will come to 0.2247 bit per letter. reported (Markov 1913, the counts) · computed by hand (the expectation).

Hayes 2013's sharpest observation, and the strongest single line of act 1: if letters were independent, the 19 999 adjacent pairs would contain 0.4319² × 19 999 ≈ 3731 vowel-vowel pairs. Markov counted 1104 — 3.4× fewer. Dependence is visible in the raw tallies, with no entropy in sight.
