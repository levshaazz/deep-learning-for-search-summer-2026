Спросите, где в блоке параметры, — ответят «во внимании». Сводка отвечает иначе: \(4d^2\) против \(8d^2\) — FFN вдвое больше. \(12d^2\) на блок — это 7.08M, и отсюда растёт вся модель. Шаг 3 перерезает остаток: формы сходятся, проход считается — умирает единственный identity-путь, и обратный провод гаснет.Ask where a block keeps its parameters and you hear “attention”. The ledger disagrees: \(4d^2\) against \(8d^2\) — the FFN is twice as much. \(12d^2\) per block is 7.08M, and the whole model grows from there. Step 3 cuts the residual: shapes still line up, the forward pass still runs — what dies is the only identity path, and the backward wire fades to nothing.

ncd-block, four steps, and it answers a question the parameter walkthrough only did arithmetic for: where do the parameters actually LIVE. Step zero is the first sublayer. The token wire arrives, the residual wire takes a copy and goes AROUND attention — over the top — attention mixes information between tokens, the results are added back to the untouched copy at the circled plus, and the bold circle, LayerNorm, re-centres and re-scales each vector. Shape in, shape out: n by m, unchanged. Step one is the second sublayer, and it is the same recipe: take a residual, run a small MLP that widens each vector from m to 4m, applies a non-linearity and projects back to m — this is the per-token thinking, where attention was the per-token gathering — add the residual, LayerNorm again. Still n by m. That is why blocks stack with no glue. Step two is where the surprise is. Read the ledger, not the picture: attention is four d squared, the feed-forward network is EIGHT d squared. The FFN is twice attention. Two thirds of every transformer block is not attention at all — and if you asked the room, not one hand would have said "the MLP". Twelve d squared per block is seven point zero eight million; twelve blocks plus the token embeddings is where the famous hundred-and-ten-million comes from — and the ledger is honest that the round number also pays for positions, segments, biases, the LayerNorm gains and the pooler, which the diagram does not draw. Step three is the counterfactual, and it is the reason the residual is a wire and not a decoration. Cut it. Both rows are the SAME circuit; every shape still lines up; the forward pass still runs. The difference is exactly one wire — and with it gone, the only path from x to the output runs THROUGH self-attention and THROUGH the FFN, so the gradient has nothing to hold on to and the backward wire fades to nothing, block after block. The network does not break. It simply stops learning, and it does so silently — which is the failure mode the notation is for.
