Головы делят ширину, а не добавляют еёHeads split the width — they do not add it

шаг (←/→): одна голова → h копий → concat возвращает осьstep (←/→): one head → h copies → concat puts the axis back

Соседний разбор утверждал, что головы бесплатны, — схема это считает. Проекции ведут не в \(m\), а в \(m/h\): при \(m=768,\ h=12\) голова видит полосу шириной 64, а не всю модель. Шестиугольник concat — точная обратная операция к разрезанию: \(12\times 64 = 768\), ровно; дальше \(L_O\) возвращает \(n\times m\). Если бы головы добавляли ширину, ось голов стоила бы \(h\cdot m = 9216\) — красная строка в сводке.The worked example asserted that heads are free — the circuit computes it. The projections map not into \(m\) but into \(m/h\): at \(m=768,\ h=12\) a head sees a 64-wide strip, never the whole model. The concat hexagon is the exact inverse of the split: \(12\times 64 = 768\), precisely; then \(L_O\) hands back \(n\times m\). Had heads added width, the head axis would cost \(h\cdot m = 9216\) — the red row in the ledger.

ncd-multihead, three steps, and it exists to kill the commonest multi-head misreading: that h heads mean h times the model. Step zero draws ONE head honestly. The token matrix goes into three learned projections — the chipped rectangles L_Q, L_K, L_V — and look at where they land: not in 768 dimensions, but in 64. d_head = m/h = 768/12 = 64. Attention runs entirely inside that 64-wide strip and hands back n by 64. A head is not a copy of the model; it is a twelfth of it. Say that number out loud, because everything else follows from it. Step one makes the broadcast concrete. Instead of waving at "a head axis", the diagram draws h literal COPIES of the circuit from step zero — same recipe, own weights, own n-by-64 slice — inside a dashed region, and the dashed region IS the head axis. The vertical ellipsis is the nine heads we did not draw. Notice what does NOT change: the same x is broadcast into every copy; heads differ only in what they learned to look for. Step two is the payoff. The concat hexagon is a reindex, not a computation — it lays the twelve slices side by side, and 12 times 64 is 768, exactly m, which is the whole argument: concat is the precise inverse of the split, so heads PARTITION the width, they do not ADD to it. The ledger carries the counterfactual: had each head kept all 768 dimensions, the head axis would have cost h times m — nine thousand two hundred and sixteen — twelve times the work, for a model that would no longer stack. Then L_O mixes the reassembled 768 back into the model width and the block hands on exactly the shape it received. The table on the next slide says "cost: the same". This is why.
