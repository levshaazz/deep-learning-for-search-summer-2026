Пропущенный шестиугольникThe missing hexagon

шаг (←/→): без маски → шестиугольник перед \(\sigma\) → матрица \(n\times n\)step (←/→): no mask → the hexagon before \(\sigma\) → the \(n\times n\) grid

Разбор напротив посчитал утечку: без маски \(\mathrm{softmax}(0,2,3)\) отдаёт 0.705 внимания токену из будущего — модель читает ответ. Это не баг обучения, а утечка, и в нотации она видна как дыра: шестиугольник-маска перед треугольником \(\sigma\) не поставлен. Поставили — остаётся (0.119, 0.881). Шаг 2: верхний треугольник выброшен, а память всё равно \(O(n^2)\) — вы платите за весь квадрат и пользуетесь половиной.The worked example computed the leak: with no mask \(\mathrm{softmax}(0,2,3)\) puts 0.705 of the attention on the FUTURE token — the model is reading the answer. Not a training bug but a leak, and in this notation it shows as a hole: the mask hexagon before the \(\sigma\) triangle was never placed. Place it, and (0.119, 0.881) is what remains. Step 2: the upper triangle is thrown away — and the memory is still \(O(n^2)\). You pay for the whole square and use half.

ncd-causal, three steps, and it turns the mask from a line of code into a glyph you can miss. Step zero: no mask. The query at position t2 scores against all three keys — including t3, the very token it is being trained to predict — and softmax of zero, two, three hands t3 nought point seven zero five of the attention. Read that again: seven tenths of the model attention is spent looking at the answer. This is not a slow bug you find in a loss curve; it is a leak, and the loss curve will look GREAT, because the task has become copying. In this notation the defect has a shape: there is a hole in the circuit where a hexagon should be. Step one places it. The hexagon — the reindex-slash-mask glyph — goes between the scores and the softmax triangle, and that ORDER is the whole content of the rule "mask before softmax": it keeps j less-than-or-equal-to i and sends everything ahead of the query to minus infinity, so exp of minus infinity is zero and the future gets exactly zero weight. Softmax then renormalises over the allowed keys alone: nought point one one nine and nought point eight eight one. Nothing else in the diagram changed — same q, same K, same triangle. The entire difference between a language model and a cheat is one glyph. Step two steps back from one row to the whole n-by-n score matrix: each row a query, each column a key, and the mask kills the strictly upper triangle, about half the square, including the red cell where our seven-tenths went. Now look at what you still pay for: the tensor is allocated n by n BEFORE the mask touches it, and a minus infinity occupies exactly as many bytes as any other number. The mask changes what softmax SEES; it does not change what memory ALLOCATES. Causal attention is not cheaper than full attention — you buy the whole square and use half of it. That is the honest bill, and it is why FlashAttention and the sparse family exist at all.
