The Crucible of Negatives

Same code, a different blade: a dense retriever is forged by the opponents it trains against. Easy negatives teach nothing, the hardest are often impostors — unlabelled positives that corrupt the edge — and effectiveness lives in a narrow hard-but-honest band. Hard negative mining in contrastive learning for dense retrieval · the 15-year search for good opponents.
