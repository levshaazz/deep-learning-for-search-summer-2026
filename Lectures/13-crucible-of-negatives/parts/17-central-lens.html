A negative is worth its gradient

a softmax push; easy negatives are wasted
The central lens (Wang–Liu)
$$ w_i = \frac{e^{\cos(q,\,d_i^-)/\tau}}{\sum_j e^{\cos(q,\,d_j^-)/\tau}} \;\;\text{(Boltzmann weight)} $$
  • ·Each negative's push is a Boltzmann weight — a softmax of its similarity over \(\tau\).
  • ·An easy negative gets almost no weight → almost no gradient → training it is wasted effort.
  • ·Every mining strategy is just a different way to spend the gradient budget on negatives worth it.
