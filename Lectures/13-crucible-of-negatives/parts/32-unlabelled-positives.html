What if the opponent is a friend?

the hardest ‘negative’ may be a positive
Definition · the unlabelled positive
Real datasets label barely one correct passage per query — against a corpus of millions.
  • ·Many other passages are also relevant — just never labelled.
  • ×Mine the hardest candidates and you scoop up these unlabelled positives, then train against them as negatives.
  • ·A false negative: an ally in an enemy’s mask — push it away and you corrupt the blade.
Turn-impostor. Datasets label ~1 positive/query vs millions; many other passages are relevant but unlabelled. Hard mining scoops these unlabelled positives and trains against them as negatives — a false negative that corrupts the blade. The hardest 'negative' may be a positive nobody labelled.
