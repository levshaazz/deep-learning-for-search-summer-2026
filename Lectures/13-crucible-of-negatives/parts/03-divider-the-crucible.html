Part 0 · The paradox

The Crucible of Negatives

Two teams run the same retriever code, on the same data, with the same architecture. One ends up finding the right passage almost every time; the other barely beats keyword search. The only thing they chose differently was the negatives — the wrong answers each query is taught to push away. You cannot sharpen a blade against air; it is the opponents that give it an edge.

Cold open / paradox. The team behind one of the first strong dense retrievers said it plainly: how you select negatives is often overlooked but can be decisive. Forge metaphor: Séréga is a blade; training is the crucible; negatives are sparring opponents.
