Random negatives

the first rung — easiest to climb past
Strategy 1 · random
Sample any passage that isn’t the positive, and call it a negative.
  • ·Mechanism — draw \(d^-\) uniformly from the corpus. Free, trivial, no extra model calls.
  • ×Flaw — a random passage is almost always easy: \(\cos(q, d^-) \approx 0\) (our \(n_1 = 0.05\)). The push barely fires — near-zero gradient, wasted compute.

Cheap, and the model learns almost nothing.
