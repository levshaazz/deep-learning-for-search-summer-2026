The impostor & denoising — the second axis

step (←/→): the cos(·,d⁺) axis → pushing n₅ drags d⁺ → why → denoise recovers

n₄ and n₅ are equally hard to the query, but on the cos(·,d⁺) axis they split: n₄ is safe to push, n₅ sits in d⁺’s neighbourhood. Pushing the impostor drags the positive down; a denoiser that filters it restores recall.
