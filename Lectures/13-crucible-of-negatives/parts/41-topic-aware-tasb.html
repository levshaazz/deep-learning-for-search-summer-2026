Manufacture hard negatives for free (TAS-B)

topic clustering makes hard negatives cheap
Strategy 6 · topic-aware sampling (Hofstätter 2021)
Cluster the queries by topic; build each batch from a single cluster, so every in-batch negative is automatically on-topic — and hard.
  • ·Mechanism — same-topic queries crowd in angle, so their reused positives land near \(d^+\). No index to refresh, no extra forward pass.
  • ·Fixes — gets hardness cheaply (no mining pass) and balances the batch across topics.
  • ·Caveat — topic clustering alone is not the whole recipe: TAS-B pairs it with margin-MSE dual-teacher distillation and a balanced pairwise sampler. And it is an in-domain gain — on BEIR zero-shot it still sits below BM25.

The free in-batch negatives stop being random luck: manufacture the hardness instead of mining for it.

TAS-B (Hofstätter 2021). Cluster queries by topic, sample each batch from one cluster: in-batch negatives are on-topic and hard for free — no giant index to refresh. Balanced sampler keeps topics even. Caveat: TAS-B needs margin-MSE dual-teacher distillation + a balanced sampler too (topic clustering alone is not enough), and it remains below BM25 on BEIR zero-shot (in-domain win, not out-of-domain). Hardness manufactured, not mined.
