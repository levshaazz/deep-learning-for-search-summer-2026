Strategy 4 · ANCE (Xiong 2021)
Index the corpus with the current model; train on the negatives it actually confuses.
  • ·Mechanism — encode the whole corpus, retrieve each query’s nearest non-positives, train against those (our \(n_4 = 0.75\)). As the model sharpens, so do its negatives.
  • ✓Fixes — BM25’s blindness: the negatives are now model-mined and adapt as it learns. This turn pushed dense retrievers past strong lexical baselines.
