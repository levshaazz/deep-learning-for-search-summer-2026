Decision guide. Small data/fast baseline → in-batch + BM25. SOTA dense → ANCE mining + denoising filter. Tight compute → TAS-B topic sampling + GradCache. Noisy labels → cross-encoder distillation. The negative strategy follows your budget and your labels.
