  • ·Поэтому FaceNet с самого начала брал выборку в полутрудной полосе — достаточно трудно, чтобы учить, но не настолько, чтобы дестабилизировать.So FaceNet sampled in a semi-hard band from the outset — hard enough to teach, not so hard it destabilises.

Toxic hardest: FaceNet (Schroff 2015). Only the very hardest negatives → gradients huge/unstable → triplet collapse, the model folds. FaceNet sampled in a semi-hard band from the outset, citing the collapse risk as the motivation: hard enough to teach, not so hard it destabilises. An optimisation failure of a GENUINE negative; the next act is a darker failure (L13-05 shattered blade).
