Toxic hardest: FaceNet (Schroff 2015). Only the very hardest negatives → gradients huge/unstable → triplet collapse, the model folds. FaceNet sampled in a semi-hard band from the outset, citing the collapse risk as the motivation: hard enough to teach, not so hard it destabilises.
