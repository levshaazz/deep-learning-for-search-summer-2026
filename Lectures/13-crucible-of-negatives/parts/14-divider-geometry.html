Part 2 · The geometry of hardness

Hardness is an angle

A negative is hard when it crowds the query in angle — and it is worth exactly the gradient it carries.

Sir Cosine returns to measure the angle. The central lens (Wang–Liu): the negative gradient is a Boltzmann softmax of sim/τ; τ→0 ⇒ nearest-neighbour hard-max. FaceNet warning: the very hardest negatives can collapse training (an optimisation failure of a genuine negative).
