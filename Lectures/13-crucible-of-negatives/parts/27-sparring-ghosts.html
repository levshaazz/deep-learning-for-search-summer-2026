The flaw in Strategy 4 · staleness
The index is refreshed only now and then — so the negatives fall behind the model.
  • ·Mechanism — re-indexing millions of passages is slow, so it happens rarely. Between refreshes the model keeps improving while its negatives stay frozen.
  • ×Flaw — frozen negatives drift hard → easy — like ghosts shadow-boxing a position the fighter has long left.
The flaw in ANCE: re-indexing is expensive, refreshed only periodically. Between refreshes the model improves but negatives are frozen → they go hard→easy (the Sparring Ghosts mascot). Motivates per-step methods (STAR/ADORE).
