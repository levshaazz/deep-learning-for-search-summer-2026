The killer ablation — by the numbers

reported (DPR / RocketQA) vs measured on our toy — the same SHAPE, never the same y-axis

DPR (NQ top-20, reported): gold-7 in-batch 69.1 → one BM25 hard negative 78.0. It was the negative selection, not the architecture, that carried it.

RocketQA (MS MARCO MRR@10, reported): baseline in-batch 32.39 → undenoised hard negatives 26.03 (drops below in-batch!) → cross-encoder denoising 36.38 → data augmentation 37.02. The inversion — and its cure.

Our toy (recall@10, measured over 20 seeds): toy in-batch 0.645 → toy +undenoised 0.411 → toy +denoised 0.783. The same in-batch → drop → recover shape — the ordering reproduces, not the magnitude.

The toy corpus is constructed to be dense in unlabelled positives, so the impostor’s damage is demonstrable by design — not discovered by luck. The real numbers belong to the papers; the shape is what you can rerun yourself.
