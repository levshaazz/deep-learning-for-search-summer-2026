the negatives you already paid for
Strategy 2 · in-batch (Karpukhin 2020)
Reuse the other queries’ positives already in the batch as this query’s negatives.
  • ·Mechanism — the batch’s positives are already encoded; cross-pair them for free — no extra forward pass. A bigger batch quietly buys more (and slightly harder) negatives.
  • ×Flaw — they’re whatever happened to share the batch: local, and mostly easy (our \(n_2 = 0.41\)).
  • +DPR’s best — in-batch plus one BM25 hard negative: NQ top-20 climbs \(69.1 \to 78.0\). A 2nd BM25 negative doesn’t help.
