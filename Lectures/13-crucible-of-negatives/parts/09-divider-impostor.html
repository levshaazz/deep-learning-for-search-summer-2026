Part 4 · The Impostor

What if the opponent is a friend?

Real datasets label barely one correct passage per query against a corpus of millions — yet many other passages are also relevant, just never labelled. Mine the hardest candidates and you scoop up these unlabelled positives and hand them over as negatives. Hardness \(\cos(q,\cdot)\) cannot see the danger; it lives on a second axis, \(\cos(\cdot,d^+)\).

The Impostor (NEW mascot): an ally wearing an enemy's mask. Two orthogonal axes — geometric hardness vs collateral danger. The next two climbs make it visceral: the inversion (mining-comparator) and the second axis + denoising (impostor-denoise). Cures: filter (RocketQA) · sample (SimANS) · co-train (AR2) · distil (Margin-MSE).
