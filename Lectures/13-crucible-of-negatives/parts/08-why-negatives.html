Two forces · pull and push
Pull the query toward its positive \(d^+\); push it away from the negatives \(d^-\).
  • ·Drop the push and the model cheats — collapse the whole space to one point, where every passage is equally close and the loss looks great.
  • ·Negatives are the only force stopping that collapse.
  • ·They are not a detail of training — they are the signal that gives the space its shape.
Without the push term the model collapses every embedding to one point: loss is minimal, retrieval is useless. Negatives are the sole anti-collapse force — the training signal that shapes the space. Sets up InfoNCE as the formal pull-vs-push objective.
