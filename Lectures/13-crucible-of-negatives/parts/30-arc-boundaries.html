Dynamic mining wins on hardness — but the gains are not free.
  • ·Cost & complexity — re-indexing the corpus and the engineering to keep it current are real overhead the static rungs never paid.
  • ·The cliffhanger — harder negatives raise the risk of scooping up false negatives: unlabelled positives mistaken for enemies.
  • ·The per-step bargain — STAR / ADORE trade a little final quality for a lot of freshness.
