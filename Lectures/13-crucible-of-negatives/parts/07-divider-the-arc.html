Part 3 · The historical arc

The search for good opponents

For fifteen years the field has asked one question in better and better ways: where do good negatives come from? Each answer fixes a specific flaw in the one before it. Walk the path — random, in-batch, BM25-static, model-mined, denoised, distilled, modern two-stage — one anvil at a time.

The arc divider. Each step = mechanism + the failure it fixes. Random (useless) → in-batch (DPR, free but easy/local) → BM25 (lexical, model-independent) → ANCE (mine your own index) → STAR/ADORE (per-step, fixes staleness) → false-negative cures → distillation → modern two-stage.
