«Ортогонально размеру» — вот оно: заземление и масштаб сидят в разных сомножителях. Чанкование, трансформация запроса, маршрутизация правят \(p_{\eta}\); промпт и верность — \(p_{\theta}\).That is what “orthogonal to size” means: grounding and scale hold different factors. Chunking, query translation and routing edit \(p_{\eta}\); the prompt and faithfulness edit \(p_{\theta}\).

The mechanism behind the act's thesis. The act asserted "grounding, not scale" and "RAG adds non-parametric memory alongside the parametric model" — this shows where in the equation that memory actually sits. Write the answer distribution as a marginal over a latent retrieved passage z: the retriever p_eta picks z out of the index, the generator p_theta reads x and z. The bare model is the degenerate right-hand side — one term, no z, every fact obliged to live in theta. That makes the orthogonality mechanical rather than rhetorical: the two levers are two different factors of the same product. Growing theta sharpens p_theta but cannot conjure the sum, and a private or fresh corpus was never inside theta to be sharpened. Two properties fall out for free: freshness, because you rebuild the index instead of retraining, and citability, because z is a pointer to a document rather than a memory. It also frames the deck — everything in the chunking, indexing, query-translation and routing acts is an edit to p_eta, everything in the generation act is an edit to p_theta. Say out loud the honest simplification: production RAG replaces the full marginal with a top-k set, which is exactly the R_k the next act defines.
