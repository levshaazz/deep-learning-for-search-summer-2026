Тот же смысл, ×5,8 токенов. Это налог: окно контекста, латентность и цена API растут на ровном месте. Same meaning, ×5.8 the tokens. That is the tax: context window, latency and API cost all rise for nothing.

Worked-by-hand on our measured toy (data/l20-ru.json, gen_l20.py): a character BPE trained on a 9:1 English:Russian mix, then applied to a parallel sentence. English "search" stays one token; Russian "запроса" falls all the way back to its seven characters because no merges were learned for it. Totals: EN 8 words → 8 tokens (1.0/word), RU 4 words → 23 tokens (5.8/word) — a ×5.8 tax on the same content. The direction and order are the point, not the exact ratio; production tokenizers show a smaller but real premium (next slide). This is not a bug — it is what an English-trained tokenizer does to a morphologically rich language.
