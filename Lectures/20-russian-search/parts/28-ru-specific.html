  • ·Когда брать ru-специфичную? Узкий русский домен (юр., мед., госязык), где мультиязычная модель «размазана» по языкам. Иначе сильная мультиязычная часто не хуже.When to pick ru-specific? A narrow Russian domain (legal, medical, officialese) where a multilingual model is spread thin. Otherwise a strong multilingual is often just as good.

Правило то же, что в L7: не верь лидерборду вслепую — шорт-лист по языку и домену, финал меряй на своих русских данных.Same rule as L7: don’t trust the leaderboard blindly — shortlist by language and domain, evaluate the finalists on your own Russian data.

The ru-specific route. Base encoders: ruBERT / ruRoBERTa (DeepPavlov), pre-trained on Russian, a foundation to fine-tune a retriever on. Sentence encoders: sbert_large_nlu_ru and ru-en-RoSBERTa (Snegirev et al. 2024, arXiv:2408.12503, bilingual ru+en, introduced with ruMTEB). The decision rule mirrors L7's choosing-an-embedder slide: pick ru-specific for a narrow Russian domain where a multilingual model is spread thin across 100 languages; otherwise a strong multilingual encoder is frequently competitive. And always evaluate finalists on your own Russian corpus rather than the leaderboard average.
