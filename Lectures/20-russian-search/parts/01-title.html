Lecture 20 — "Search in Russian", catchphrase "Morphology strikes back". A supplementary deep-dive: what breaks when the corpus stops being English. Three spines: the token tax (BPE fragments Russian into many more pieces), lemmatization vs sub-words for BM25 (a fusional language means query and answer can share zero surface forms), and Russian / multilingual embedders (E5-multilingual, BGE-M3, ruMTEB).
