The full paradigm of one inanimate masculine noun, straight from data/l20-ru.json (gen_l20.py): 6 cases × 2 numbers = 12 slots, but nominative equals accusative in both numbers, so 10 distinct surface strings. The punchline: a keyword index that does not normalize sees ten unrelated terms for one word — inverse document frequency splinters, and a query in one case cannot match a document in another.
