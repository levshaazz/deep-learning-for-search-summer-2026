Все они детерминированы и дёшевы (правила/словарь, не нейросеть). Ставь нормализацию в оба плеча — аналайзер индекса и препроцессинг запроса — одним и тем же инструментом.All are deterministic and cheap (rules/dictionary, not a neural net). Put normalization in both arms — the index analyzer and the query preprocessor — with the same tool.

The practical toolbox, all rule/dictionary-based and CPU-cheap. Snowball/Porter (stemmer, in every IR stack, the fast default); pymorphy2 (Korobov 2015, OpenCorpora-dictionary lemmatizer in Python, returns a full parse); Mystem (Segalovich 2003, fast with disambiguation heuristics); spaCy/Stanza Russian pipelines (lemma+POS+parse); and the built-in Elasticsearch/OpenSearch russian analyzer + hunspell for index-time normalization. The engineering rule from the worked example: apply the identical normalization to the index analyzer and the query preprocessor, or the query and corpus drift apart again.
