Правило: стемминг — быстрый дефолт для BM25; лемматизация — когда важна точность и надо снять омонимию («стали»: сталь? стать?). Обе применяют симметрично к запросу и корпусу.Rule of thumb: stemming is the fast default for BM25; lemmatization when precision matters and homonymy must be resolved (“стали”: steel? became?). Apply either symmetrically to query and corpus.

The two normalization tools contrasted. Stemming applies rules to chop an ending, producing a stem that need not be a real word (Snowball/Porter for Russian); cheap, context-blind, prone to over- or under-stemming. Lemmatization maps to the dictionary form using a morphological dictionary and can disambiguate homonyms (стали = steel-genitive or became); more accurate, pricier. For BM25, stemming is the fast default and lemmatization the precision option. The one non-negotiable: apply the same normalization to both the query and the corpus, or you reintroduce the mismatch you were trying to remove.
