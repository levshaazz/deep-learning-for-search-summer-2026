Folding ё→е in the INDEX ONLY while the query keeps its ё: back to 0.000 — asymmetry is strictly worse than doing nothing, the single most important row on the slide. Folding in both arms: 0.333. Adding Snowball on top, both arms: 0.667 (d1, d2, d5, d6). Snowball folds ё→е in its own prelude, which is why stemmed fields are cured for free while keyword, phrase, autocomplete and facet fields are not.
