Флективность — не «сложность», а способ упаковки.Taught in Innopolis, with a trilingual book (en/ru/tt): Tatar is not exotica here but the language next door — and it breaks differently. Fusionality is not “difficulty”, it is a packing scheme.

The typology slide, and the one place the course's own trilingual setting pays off. Fusional (Russian): one affix fuses several grammatical values, so you cannot peel them apart — the whole ending has to be removed at once, which is why lemmatization beats naive suffix stripping. Agglutinative (Tatar, and the Turkic family generally): morphemes are strung in a fixed order, one meaning each, so a rule-based stripper works far better even though the form count is higher. Analytic (English): grammar sits in separate function words, which is exactly why lexical search "just works" in English. The 12.5 forms per lexeme is the OpenCorpora dictionary average — a lexeme's df is smeared across roughly a dozen index terms.
