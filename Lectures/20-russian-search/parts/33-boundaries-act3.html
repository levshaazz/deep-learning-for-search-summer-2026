Итог акта: плотный поиск снимает промах по формам, но не отменяет ни точных термов, ни налога. Лучший русский стек — гибрид, а не одна модель.Act takeaway: dense retrieval removes the surface-form miss but abolishes neither exact terms nor the tax. The best Russian stack is hybrid, not a single model.

Boundaries of Act 3, keeping dense honest. A multilingual model divides its capacity across ~100 languages, so on a narrow Russian domain a specialized or fine-tuned model can win. Dense vectors blur exact terms — names, product codes, rare entities — which is exactly what the sparse/BM25 arm preserves, so hybrid (Act 2 + Act 3) remains the strongest baseline. And the token tax from Act 1 persists inside the encoder: Russian text is still longer, raising index-time cost and bumping length limits. Bridge to Act 4: even with the right model and normalization, a thousand small orthographic details quietly cut recall.
