Тренд: то, что сегодня чинят препроцессингом (лемма, транслит, ё/е), завтра всё чаще берёт на себя сама модель — но принцип симметрии остаётся.The trend: what we patch with preprocessing today (lemma, translit, ё/е) the model increasingly absorbs tomorrow — but the symmetry principle stays.

The frontier. Three directions. Fairer tokenizers: language-balanced training mixes and morpheme-aware vocabularies reduce the token tax at the source rather than patching it. Byte/character-level and token-free models (ByT5, Canine) skip the sub-word vocabulary entirely, trading sequence length for the absence of a language skew. And stronger multilingual retrievers (BGE-M3, multilingual-E5 and successors) keep narrowing the Russian/English quality gap, making explicit lemmatization less necessary over time. The through-line: preprocessing we do by hand today (lemmatization, transliteration, ё/е) is increasingly absorbed by the model — but the symmetry principle (same treatment for query and corpus) survives every generation.
