The three Russian retrieval benchmarks, with metrics inherited from L4/L7 (nDCG@10, MRR@10, recall@k). MIRACL (Zhang et al. 2023, arXiv:2210.09984): open-retrieval over 18 languages including Russian, human-annotated on native in-language Wikipedia corpora — the gold standard. mMARCO (Bonifacio et al. 2021, arXiv:2108.13897): MS MARCO machine-translated into 13 languages including Russian — convenient but carries translationese. ruMTEB (Snegirev et al. 2024, arXiv:2408.12503): the Russian MTEB branch, 23 tasks spanning retrieval, STS, classification. The rule: compare models within a single benchmark; scores are not comparable across them. Next slide unpacks the native-vs-translationese distinction.
