Метрики те же, что в L4/L7: nDCG@10, MRR@10, recall@k. Сравнивай модели внутри одного бенчмарка — числа не переносятся между MIRACL и mMARCO: у них разные корпуса, разная длина документа и разное происхождение текста.Metrics are the same as L4/L7: nDCG@10, MRR@10, recall@k. Compare models within one benchmark — numbers don’t carry between MIRACL and mMARCO: different corpora, different document lengths, different provenance of the text.

The three Russian retrieval benchmarks, now with their sizes. MIRACL's Russian dev split (Zhang et al., TACL 2023): 1252 queries against 9,543,918 passages drawn from 1,476,045 Wikipedia articles of the 2019-02-01 dump, judged by 31 hired native speakers rather than crowdworkers — that is why it is the selection benchmark. mMARCO's Russian split (Bonifacio et al. 2021): 8,841,823 passages and 6980 dev queries, produced by machine translation with Helsinki OPUS-MT and Google Translate — big and convenient, but not written by Russian speakers. ruMTEB (Snegirev et al., NAACL 2025): 23 tasks, only 3 of them retrieval, in the version MTEB(rus, v1.1). Metrics inherit from L4/L7. The rule stands: compare within one benchmark, never across.
