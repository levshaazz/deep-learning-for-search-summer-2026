Gotcha — homoglyphs. Per UTS #39 confusables.txt 17.0.0 (2025-07-22), 141 Cyrillic characters carry a confusable mapping; exactly 7 lowercase letters (а е о р с у х) and 12 uppercase (А В Е К М Н О Р С Т У Х) are visually identical to Latin. Note the asymmetry: В→B and Н→H are confusables while lowercase в and н are not, so capitals are the higher risk. Demo: "сосна" (U+0441 U+043E U+0441 U+043D U+0430) versus "cocнa" (U+0063 U+006F U+0063 U+043D U+0061) — four letters swapped, indistinguishable by eye, BM25 exactly zero. The token cost compounds it: Москва is 1 token in o200k, Mocквa is 4. Historical anchor: Xudong Zheng's 2017 IDN attack, xn--80ak6aa92e.com rendering as аpple.com, which defeated mixed-script detection by being single-script; fixed in Chrome 58. Cure: confusables normalization applied symmetrically.
