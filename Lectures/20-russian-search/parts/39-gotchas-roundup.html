Roundup of Act 4 as one reference table: ё/е (normalize ё→е), case (lowercase plus an entity field), transliteration (variant expansion or a multilingual vector), keyboard layout (ЙЦУКЕН↔QWERTY map), and hyphen/space normalization (по-русски = по русски). Every cure is one line of preprocessing, and the unifying principle is the whole lecture's: fold query and corpus to one form, with one code path, applied symmetrically in both arms. Each is trivial alone; together they are a measurable recall difference in production.
