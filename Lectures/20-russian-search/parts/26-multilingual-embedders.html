Все видели русский. multilingual-E5 — безопасный дефолт; BGE-M3 — когда нужны длинный контекст и гибрид в одной модели; LaBSE — для поиска переводов, а не обычного ранжирования.All saw Russian. multilingual-E5 is the safe default; BGE-M3 when you need long context and hybrid in one model; LaBSE for translation retrieval, not ad-hoc ranking.

The multilingual roster, all covering Russian. multilingual-E5 (Wang et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.05672): same query:/passage: prefixes as English E5, ~100 languages, the safe default. BGE-M3 (Chen et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.03216): 100+ languages, up to 8192 tokens, and dense+sparse+multi-vector in one model. LaBSE (Feng et al. 2022): 109 languages, trained on parallel pairs — a bitext/translation-retrieval specialist that underperforms on ad-hoc search. Plus a small fast paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM and the API frontier (gemini-embedding, Cohere multilingual). Same caveat as the L7 zoo: shortlist by language coverage, then evaluate on your own Russian data.
