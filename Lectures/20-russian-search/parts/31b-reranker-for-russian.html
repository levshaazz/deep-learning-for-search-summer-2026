Средний выигрыш около 9 пунктов nDCG@10 авторы называют уже по расширенному набору из 27: две цифры из разных наборов, рядом их не вычитают.Russian numbers barely exist. Not one multilingual reranker publishes a Russian result. The only citable one is RusBEIR’s: BM25 52.16 → 59.87 with a reranker, BGE-M3 61.13 → 65.85 — that is the 17-dataset collection. The average gain of about 9 nDCG@10 points is quoted on the larger 27-dataset one: two figures from two collections, not to be subtracted side by side.
  • ·Отсюда правило. Реранкер для русского сегодня берут вслепую — значит, замер на своих данных обязателен, а лицензию читаешь до прода. Глубина переранжирования и цена за пару — те же, что в L7: платит кросс-энкодер, а не индекс.Hence the rule. A Russian reranker is picked blind today — so measuring on your own data is mandatory, and you read the licence before production. Rerank depth and cost per pair are exactly as in L7: the cross-encoder pays, not the index.
The floor of the L7 cascade this deck never built for Russian. Four candidates: bge-reranker-v2-m3 (0.6 B, Apache-2.0), jina-reranker-v2-base-multilingual (278 M, CC-BY-NC — flag this aloud, it forbids commercial use), Qwen3-Reranker (Apache-2.0, 32k context), and DiTy/cross-encoder-russian-msmarco, the only Russian-only cross-encoder, trained on machine-translated mmarco-ru with no published evaluation. The honest state of the field: none of the multilingual rerankers reports a Russian number, so the single citable Russian result is RusBEIR's — BM25 52.16 rises to 59.87 with a reranker, BGE-M3 61.13 rises to 65.85, an average of roughly 9 nDCG@10 points. That is a large, reliable gain and a strong argument for the cascade; it is also an argument for evaluating the reranker yourself, because you are choosing one on model-card prose.
