Как выбрать русский ретривер Picking a Russian retriever

решение по домену, длине, бюджету и приватности decide on domain, length, budget and privacy
Правило The rule
  • ·Дефолт. Начни с multilingual-E5 или BGE-M3 — сильные русские baseline из коробки.Default. Start with multilingual-E5 or BGE-M3 — strong Russian baselines out of the box.
  • ·Домен. Узкий русский корпус → попробуй ru-специфичную модель или дообучение; меряй на своих данных, а не на среднем MIRACL.Domain. A narrow Russian corpus → try a ru-specific model or fine-tuning; evaluate on your data, not the MIRACL average.
  • ·Гибрид не отменяется. Плотный + BM25 с лемматизацией (RRF, L8) — лучший русский baseline: смысл плюс точные термины.Hybrid still wins. Dense + lemmatized BM25 (RRF, L8) is the best Russian baseline: meaning plus exact terms.
  • ·Токенизатор и лицензия. Проверь токены/слово (Акт 1) и open-weights vs API — приватность и цена.Tokenizer & licence. Check tokens-per-word (Act 1) and open-weights vs API — privacy and cost.
The decision rule, mirroring L7's choosing-an-embedder. Default to multilingual-E5 or BGE-M3 (strong Russian baselines). For a narrow Russian domain, try a ru-specific model or fine-tune, and always evaluate on your own data rather than the MIRACL average. Crucially, hybrid is not superseded: dense retrieval plus lemmatized BM25 fused with RRF (L8) is the strongest Russian baseline — the dense arm carries meaning past morphology, the lexical arm keeps exact terms (names, codes, quotes) the dense vector blurs. And weigh the tokenizer (Act 1 tokens-per-word) and licence (open-weights vs API) for cost and privacy. This is the whole lecture's engineering advice on one slide.
