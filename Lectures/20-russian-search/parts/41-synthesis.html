Тезис часа The thesis of the hour
Приведи запрос и корпус к одной форме — в обоих плечах. Fold query and corpus to one form — in both arms.

Флективность бьёт трижды: длиннее токены, разные строки, английский энкодер не переносится. Лечит один принцип — симметричная нормализация плюс модель, видевшая русский. Наша игрушка показала перелом ранга: 0,0 → 1,3884, #2 → #1.Fusionality strikes three times: longer tokens, different strings, an English encoder that won’t transfer. One principle cures it — symmetric normalization plus a model that saw Russian. Our toy showed the rank flip: 0.0 → 1.3884, #2 → #1.

Synthesis in one view. Three bills from morphology, each mapped to a fix: the token tax (L2 → multilingual tokenizer, measure tokens/word), the surface-form miss (L3 → lemma/stem symmetrically), and the model choice (L7 → multilingual-E5/BGE-M3/ru model, hybrid with lemmatized BM25), plus the thousand small orthographic cuts. The thesis: fold query and corpus to one form, in both arms — symmetric normalization plus a Russian-aware model. The measured payoff from our toy: the gold document's BM25 goes 0.0 → 1.3884 and rank 2 → 1 once forms are matched. No new architecture — the familiar pipeline, honestly tuned for Russian.
