Практический вывод: при выборе LLM/эмбеддера для русского токенизатор — такой же критерий, как качество: он задаёт цену каждого документа.Practical takeaway: when picking an LLM/embedder for Russian, the tokenizer is as much a criterion as quality — it sets the price of every document.

Grounding the toy in reality. Modern multilingual tokenizers (tiktoken for the GPT-4o family, Gemini's vocabulary, multilingual BPEs) carry enough Cyrillic merges to keep Russian near two tokens per word — far better than our English-only toy's 5.8, but still above English's ~1. Older English-centric vocabularies are much worse. The engineering habit: tokenize your own corpus and read tokens-per-word rather than assuming — it is a one-liner and it makes the token budget honest. The tokenizer is a first-class selection criterion for Russian, not an afterthought.
