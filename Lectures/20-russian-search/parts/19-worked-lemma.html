Worked-by-hand part 2 (lemmatized) — the inversion, the whole act in one number. Query lemmas "котёнок играть"; the gold document now shares both, so BM25 = idf(котёнок)·… + idf(играть)·… = 0.8594 + 0.5291 = 1.3884, rank 1 (up from rank 2). The shop document shares only "котёнок" and scores 0.8594 (rank 2); the surface-winner distractor "дети играть" shares only "играть" and falls behind.
