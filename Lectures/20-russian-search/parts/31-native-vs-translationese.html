The translationese caveat, and a Goodhart callback to L4. Machine-translated Russian (mMARCO) syntactically mirrors its English source — word order, phrasing, lexical choices that a native speaker would not use. A model with strong English priors can exploit that calque, so a high mMARCO score can reflect the model's grip on translation artifacts rather than genuine Russian, and won't transfer to real users' queries. Practical guidance: select models on MIRACL/ruMTEB (native, human-annotated) and use mMARCO for scale/training while discounting the bias. It is the L4 Goodhart lesson in a new coat: optimizing a proxy on the wrong distribution measures the wrong thing.
