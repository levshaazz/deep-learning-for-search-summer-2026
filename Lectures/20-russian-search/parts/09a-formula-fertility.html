Слово ты уже встречал: в L2 «фертильность» ввели как число токенов на слово и намеряли «привет» = 12 байтовых токенов. Дальше в лекции меряем только ей. Тонкость: символов русскому нужно всего в 1,089 раза больше, а байтов — в 1,984.You have met the word before: L2 introduced “fertility” as tokens per word and measured “привет” = 12 byte-level tokens. From here on this is our only ruler. The subtlety: Russian needs only 1.089× the characters, but 1.984× the bytes.

The terminological bridge to L2, which the deck previously skipped even though the course catalogue advertises "tokenizer fertility" for this lecture. Fertility is tokens per word — one number, comparable across tokenizers and languages, and the thing you should actually measure on your own corpus. The byte premium is the second, independent ruler: measured on FLORES, Russian carries the same meaning in 1.089× the characters but 1.984× the bytes, because Cyrillic averages 1.824 UTF-8 bytes per character. That gap is where a byte-level BPE starts, before a single merge is learned — the next slides make both numbers concrete.
