The vocabulary gap

why the right doc can rank low
Definition
A query and its true answer often use different words for the same idea — so a retriever scoring by word/embedding similarity ranks the right document below worse matches.
  • ·Example: "How does the heart pump blood?" vs a doc on "the cardiac cycle, ventricular systole" — same topic, almost no shared vocabulary.
  • ·Callback (L3): Furnas et al. found two people pick the same word for a concept under 20% of the time. Embeddings narrow the gap; they don't close it.
  • ·The move: don't build a better retriever — give it a better query, closer in vocabulary to the answer. That is query translation.
