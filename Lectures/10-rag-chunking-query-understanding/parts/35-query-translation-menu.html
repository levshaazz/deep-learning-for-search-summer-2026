The query-translation menu

five moves, one retriever · what each one buys
move what it does buys extra LLM calls
multi-queryparaphrase, retrieve each, take the unionrecall (coverage)1 (generate paraphrases)
HyDEembed a hypothetical answer instead of the questioncloses the vocabulary gap1 (write hypothetical)
RAG-Fusionmulti-query + RRF over the paraphrase rankingsconsensus-weighted recall1 (paraphrases) + RRF
decompositionfan a compound question into atomic sub-questionsmulti-fact answers1 + one retrieval per sub
step-backabstract up to a generic question, retrieve the principleanswers over-specific queries1 (abstract the query)

All five share the same trick: change the query, not the retriever. They differ in what they buy — coverage, vocabulary, consensus, multi-fact, or abstraction — and all cost roughly one extra LLM call. Reach for the one your failure mode needs.
