"A bigger model won't hallucinate"

grounding, not scale — the lecture's thesis
Misconception
"Hallucination is a capacity problem — a larger model trained on more tokens will simply know the answer."
  • ·✗ Scale sharpens fluency, not provenance — a bigger model is a more convincing guesser, still confident when it is wrong.
  • ·✗ Parametric memory is frozen at training time and unciteable — a private, fresh, or proprietary corpus is something no pre-training can hold.
  • ·✓ The fix is orthogonal to size: retrieve the evidence and make the model read it. RAG adds non-parametric memory (the index) alongside the parametric model — Lewis et al., 2020.
