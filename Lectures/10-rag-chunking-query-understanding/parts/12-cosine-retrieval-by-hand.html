Where the retrieval scores come from

\(R_k\) is just cosine — by hand on the anchor trace
$$ \cos(\mathbf{e}_q,\mathbf{e}_d) = \frac{\mathbf{e}_q\cdot\mathbf{e}_d}{\lVert\mathbf{e}_q\rVert\,\lVert\mathbf{e}_d\rVert}, \qquad \mathbf{e}_q = (3,2,1,1) $$ $$ \text{cardiac cycle } (1,2,1,2):\ \frac{10}{\lVert\mathbf{e}_q\rVert\,\lVert\mathbf{e}_d\rVert} = \mathbf{0.8165} $$ $$ \text{circulatory } (1,2,2,2):\ \frac{11}{\lVert\mathbf{e}_q\rVert\,\lVert\mathbf{e}_d\rVert} = \mathbf{0.7877} \qquad \text{valves } (1,5,0,2):\ \frac{15}{\lVert\mathbf{e}_q\rVert\,\lVert\mathbf{e}_d\rVert} = \mathbf{0.7071} $$

The four dimensions are pump · anatomy · flow · valves. Higher dot product is not enough — valves has the biggest dot (15) yet the lowest cosine, because cosine divides out length. These are exactly the scores the pipeline showed: ranking by cosine is the whole of \(R_k\).
