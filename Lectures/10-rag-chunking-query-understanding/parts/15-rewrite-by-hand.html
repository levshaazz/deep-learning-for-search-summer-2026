HyDE & multi-query, by hand

two gold-sets, never blended
$$ \text{original: true doc rank } 8 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{recall@5} = 0, \quad \text{RR} = 1/8 = \mathbf{0.125} $$ $$ \text{HyDE: rank } 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{recall@5} = 1, \quad \text{RR} = 1/2 = \mathbf{0.5} $$ $$ \text{multi-query (separate 5-relevant gold-set):}\quad 2/5 \Rightarrow \mathbf{0.4} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{union } 4/5 \Rightarrow \mathbf{0.8} $$

HyDE closes the vocabulary gap by searching with answer-shaped text; multi-query widens coverage by asking several ways. Both lift recall without touching the retriever — they just feed it a better query.
