Self-RAG, formally

the model learns to decide when & whether to retrieve
Definition · arXiv:2310.11511
Train the LM to emit reflection tokens — Retrieve · IsRel · IsSup · IsUse — that gate retrieval and critique its own output as it generates.
  • ·Retrieve — decide per segment whether retrieval is needed at all (skip what the model already knows).
  • ·IsRel / IsSup — is the passage relevant, and each claim supported by it? The seed of faithfulness (metric in L11).
  • ·IsUse — is the response useful? CRAG bolts a grader on; self-RAG bakes critique in — Asai et al. (ICLR 2024).
