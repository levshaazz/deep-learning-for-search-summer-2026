Self-RAG, formally

the model learns to decide when & whether to retrieve
Definition · arXiv:2310.11511
Train the LM to emit reflection tokens — Retrieve · IsRel · IsSup · IsUse — that gate retrieval and critique its own output as it generates.
  • ·Retrieve — decide per segment whether retrieval is even needed (skip it for things the model already knows).
  • ·IsRel / IsSup — is the retrieved passage relevant, and is each generated claim supported by it? This is the seed of faithfulness (the metric is L11).
  • ·IsUse — is the overall response useful? CRAG bolts a grader on; self-RAG bakes the critique into the model — Asai et al. (ICLR 2024).
self-RAG definition. Cite Asai et al., "Self-RAG", ICLR 2024 (arXiv:2310.11511). Reflection tokens Retrieve/IsRel/IsSup/IsUse. IsSup is the seed of faithfulness — defer the METRIC to L11. Contrast: CRAG bolts on, self-RAG bakes in.
