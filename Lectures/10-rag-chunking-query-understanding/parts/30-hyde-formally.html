HyDE, formally

embed a hypothetical answer, not the question
Definition · arXiv:2212.10496
Ask an LLM to write a hypothetical answer to the query, embed that document, and retrieve with its vector — even though the hypothetical may be partly wrong.
  • ·Why it works: an answer-shaped paragraph lives in the same region of embedding space as the real answers. Searching with it closes the vocabulary gap — the hypothetical's shape matters more than its factual accuracy.
  • ·Precise Zero-shot Dense Retrieval: Gao, Ma, Lin & Callan (ACL 2023) showed it lifts retrieval with no relevance labels — the LLM supplies the missing query-document bridge.
  • ·Cost & risk: one extra LLM call per query, and a confidently-wrong hypothetical can mislead retrieval — pair it with a retriever that still ranks the true doc highly.
  • ·When it fails: the lift assumes the LLM can place the answer in roughly the right topical region. For rare entities, fresh facts, or domains the model never saw, the hypothetical is topically off — not just factually wrong — and HyDE can rank the true doc lower than the bare query. Shape helps only when the model can guess the right neighbourhood.
HyDE-formally definition. Cite Gao, Ma, Lin & Callan, "Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels", ACL 2023 (arXiv:2212.10496). Shape > accuracy; closes the vocabulary gap; costs one LLM call.
