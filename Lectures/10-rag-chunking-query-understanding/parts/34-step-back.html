Step-back, formally

climb to retrieve, descend to answer
Definition · arXiv:2310.06117
Abstract a too-specific question into a generic one, retrieve the underlying principle, then use it to answer the specific question.
  • ·The trap it escapes: "Why did my heart rate hit 180 bpm running up the stairs?" is so specific that no chunk states exactly that — the literal query retrieves nothing useful.
  • ·The move: step back to "What mechanism regulates heart rate under exertion?" — which does match a chunk (the sympathetic nervous system raises heart rate). Retrieve the principle, then reason down to the specific.
  • ·Decomposition vs step-back: decomposition fans out (more, narrower questions); step-back abstracts up (one broader question). Both are query translation — Zheng et al. (2023).
