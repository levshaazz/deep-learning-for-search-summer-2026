nDCG = DCG / IDCG · two graded docs: the answer (gain 3) and a partially-relevant doc (gain 1)
$$ \text{DCG} = \sum_i \frac{\text{gain}_i}{\log_2(i+1)}, \qquad \text{nDCG} = \frac{\text{DCG}}{\text{IDCG}} $$ $$ \text{before (gain-3 answer at rank 4): } \text{nDCG@5} = \frac{1/\log_2(4)+3/\log_2(5)}{3/\log_2(2)+1/\log_2(3)} \;<\; 0.5 $$ $$ \text{after (relevant doc at rank 1): } \text{nDCG@5} = \frac{\text{IDCG}}{\text{IDCG}} = \mathbf{1.0} $$
