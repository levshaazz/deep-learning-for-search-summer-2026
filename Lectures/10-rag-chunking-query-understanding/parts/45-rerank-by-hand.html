Why the metric jumps — by hand

nDCG = DCG / IDCG · the only relevant doc has gain 3
$$ \text{DCG} = \sum_i \frac{\text{gain}_i}{\log_2(i+1)}, \qquad \text{nDCG} = \frac{\text{DCG}}{\text{IDCG}} $$ $$ \text{before (relevant doc at rank 4): } \text{nDCG@5} = \frac{3/\log_2(5)}{3/\log_2(2)+1/\log_2(3)} \;<\; 0.5 $$ $$ \text{after (relevant doc at rank 1): } \text{nDCG@5} = \frac{\text{IDCG}}{\text{IDCG}} = \mathbf{1.0} $$

Discounted gain falls fast with rank, so burying the gain-3 doc at rank 4 wastes most of it (nDCG below half). Lift it to rank 1 and the order is ideal — nDCG@5 = 1.0. The cross-encoder didn't find a new doc; it just put the right one on top.
