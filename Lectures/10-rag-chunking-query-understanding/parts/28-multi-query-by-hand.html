Multi-query union recall, by hand

a separate 5-relevant gold-set
$$ \text{recall@5} = \frac{|\text{gold} \cap \text{found}|}{|\text{gold}|}, \qquad |\text{gold}| = 5 $$ $$ \text{single query: } 2/5 \Rightarrow \mathbf{0.4} \qquad \text{union of 3 paraphrases: } 4/5 \Rightarrow \mathbf{0.8} $$

The bare query finds 2 of the 5 relevant docs; three paraphrases together cover 4 of 5 — the fifth gold doc is found by none of the three phrasings, so even their union tops out at 4/5. The union lifts recall without touching the retriever — it just asks the same question several ways. The lift is diversity-dependent with fast diminishing returns, and the extra candidates compete for the token budget, so this 0.4→0.8 is a toy, not a guaranteed doubling. (This is its own gold-set, never blended with HyDE's single-true-doc count.)
