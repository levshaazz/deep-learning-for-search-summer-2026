Closes gap G1. The whole third act (Fn, "context removes surprise") IS conditional entropy, and the deck never wrote the definition. Three things on one slide: the definition (an average of per-context entropies, weighted by how often that context occurs), the chain rule H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y|X) — which is why a message's cost adds up letter by letter — and the inequality H(Y|X) ≤ H(Y) with equality iff independence, i.e. "conditioning reduces entropy".
