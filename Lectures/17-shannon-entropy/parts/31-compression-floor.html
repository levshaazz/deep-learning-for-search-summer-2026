The unifying punchline. English's entropy ≈1 bit/letter (reported, Shannon 1951) is simultaneously (a) the hard floor of any lossless compressor and (b) the irreducible loss a perfect language model would still pay — literally the same number, because both measure the source's residual surprise. From the uniform F0 ceiling ≈4.76 bits/letter down to ~1 gives roughly 4:1 compressibility, consistent with Shannon's ≈75% long-range redundancy of English. The red floor line on the surprise meter is one line for both trades.
