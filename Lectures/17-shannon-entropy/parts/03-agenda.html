Six acts tracing one quantity from the telegraph room to the language model. (1) Pre-history: information becomes a QUANTITY — Morse & Vail count movable type, Nyquist 1924 W=K log m, Hartley 1928 H=n log S, Markov 1913 on Eugene Onegin, Boltzmann/Gibbs' matching functional. (2) Shannon 1948: the bit, self-information −log₂p, entropy H = expected surprise (coin, die, biased coin). (3) Shannon 1951: Fₙ n-gram entropy of English, the guessing game (79/102), ≈1 bit/letter, 50–75% redundancy. (4) Source coding: H is the hard compression floor; the dyadic code hits 1.75 bits exactly. (5) The modern bridge: cross-entropy, KL, perplexity, bits-per-char. (6) Search tie: IDF is Shannon self-information. Anchors approximate until final numbering.
