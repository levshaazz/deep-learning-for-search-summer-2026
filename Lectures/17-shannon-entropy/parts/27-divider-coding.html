Стрелка «измерителя удивления» Серёги никогда не опускается ниже красной черты. Эта черта — энтропия H: предел, к которому сжатие подходит вплотную, но пробить не может. The needle on Séréga's “surprise meter” never drops below the red line. That line is the entropy H: the limit compression approaches but can never break.

Divider into Part 04. The recurring image: a red “entropy floor” line on the surprise meter. The next slides prove that H is a hard lower bound on the average length of any lossless code — no coder beats it, good coders approach it — and that this is the same number a perfect language model would still pay.
