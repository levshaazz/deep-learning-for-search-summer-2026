Третье требование — убийственное: единственная непрерывная функция, превращающая умножение вероятностей в сложение информации, — логарифм (уравнение Коши). Дальше остаётся выбрать только знак и основание: минус — чтобы величина была положительной, основание 2 — чтобы единицей был честный вопрос «да/нет». Shannon 1948, приложение 2. The third demand is the killer: the only continuous function that turns a product of probabilities into a sum of information is the logarithm (Cauchy's equation). All that is left is a sign and a base: the minus makes the quantity positive, base 2 makes the unit one fair yes/no question. Shannon 1948, Appendix 2.

Removes the "they just made the formula up" feeling that the old deck left. Three properties — I(1)=0, monotone decreasing in p, and additivity over independent events I(pq)=I(p)+I(q) — pin the functional form to I(p) = −c log p by Cauchy's functional equation (Shannon 1948, Appendix 2 proves the entropy version with a continuity/refinement axiom). The base is a unit choice, not content: 2 → bits, e → nats, 10 → hartleys. Say out loud: additivity is what a search engine relies on when it ADDS the IDFs of two query terms — and the IDF-almost slide in act 6 is exactly where that assumption gets challenged.
