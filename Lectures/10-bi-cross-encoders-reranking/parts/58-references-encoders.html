Appendix, part one — the Scout lineage. Karpukhin and colleagues 2020 is DPR, dense passage retrieval with two encoders and in-batch negatives. Reimers and Gurevych 2019 is Sentence-BERT, the siamese mean-pooled encoder that made sentence embeddings practical. Xiong and colleagues 2021 is ANCE, which mines hard negatives from the index itself. Hofstätter and colleagues 2021 is TAS-B, topic-aware sampling with dual-teacher distillation — teaching a Scout to think like a Judge.
