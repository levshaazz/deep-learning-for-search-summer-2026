The 2024–2026 shift in the embedder zoo. The top of MTEB is now decoder-LLM-based: NV-Embed (arXiv:2405.17428), E5-mistral (2401.00368), Qwen3-Embedding (2506.05176), gemini-embedding (2503.07891), RepLLaMA (2310.08319) — 0.6–8B decoders instead of 0.1–0.3B BERT-class encoders. They win because scale buys semantics before contrastive tuning starts, and instruction prompts make one model multi-task. Crucially the bi-encoder contract is unchanged — one vector per text, cosine score — so everything else in this lecture (caching, ANN, the cascade) applies verbatim. Scores are leaderboard-approximate (~70+ vs ~65), not gospel — the MTEB warning from the previous slide stands.
