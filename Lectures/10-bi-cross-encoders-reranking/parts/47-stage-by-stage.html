Walk the cascade with a latency budget. Start at a million-document corpus. The bi-encoder Scouts retrieve, in roughly thirteen milliseconds for a query encode plus an indexed search, down to a thousand. The cross-encoder Judges then score the top hundred of that shortlist — the same depth-100 the MS MARCO rerank numbers on the next slide are measured at — and hand back the top ten. Be careful with the arithmetic here: a hundred pairs cost about a hundred and twenty milliseconds on a GPU because they are scored in batches of around thirty-two, not one after another; sequentially on a CPU one pair alone costs about twelve milliseconds, so a sequential top-hundred would be over a second. The representative total is about a hundred and thirty milliseconds — these latencies are cited, hardware-dependent, never measured in our CI; the per-stage counts are the exact funnel shape.
