The pivot from using models to making them. Everything so far assumed a trained encoder and a trained reranker. This section opens the forge: the contrastive objective that pulls a query toward its answer and away from everything else; the negatives that make or break it, from free in-batch ones to mined near-twins; the distillation that lets a fast Scout inherit a slow Judge's taste; and the multi-stage recipe behind E5, BGE and the rest. Every number here is sourced to a real paper.
