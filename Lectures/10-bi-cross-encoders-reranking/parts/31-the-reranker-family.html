L20 даёт почти то же, что L7 (\(39{,}02\) против \(39{,}01\)) за ~2× цены — L7 — прод-дефолт. Нужна сырая скорость — TinyBERT-L2 даёт 9000 док/с.L20 buys almost nothing over L7 (\(39.02\) vs \(39.01\)) for ~2× the cost — L7 is the production default. Need raw speed? TinyBERT-L2 does 9000 docs/sec.

The reranker lineage and the production sweet spot. monoBERT, Nogueira and Cho 2019, is the canonical cross-encoder: joint input to BERT, read CLS through a linear layer, binary cross-entropy. monoT5 reframes it as text-to-text. The practical workhorses are the sentence-transformers ms-marco-MiniLM cross-encoders, and they teach a deployment lesson: the twelve-layer model scores thirty-nine point oh-two MRR at ten, versus thirty-nine point oh-one for the six-layer — essentially identical for nearly double the cost. So L7 is the default, and TinyBERT at nine thousand documents per second is there when latency dominates. BGE, Cohere, mxbai and Jina are the modern multilingual options.
