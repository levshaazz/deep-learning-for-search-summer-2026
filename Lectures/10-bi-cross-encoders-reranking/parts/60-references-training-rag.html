Appendix, part three — training, indexing and RAG. van den Oord and colleagues 2018 is the InfoNCE objective behind every modern retriever. Hofstätter and colleagues 2020 is Margin-MSE, distilling a cross-encoder into a bi-encoder. Wang and colleagues 2022 is E5 and the weakly-supervised multi-stage recipe. Malkov and Yashunin is HNSW, the graph index behind sub-linear search, picked up in Lecture 13. Cormack and colleagues 2009 is Reciprocal Rank Fusion, the hybrid combiner. And Lewis and colleagues 2020 is the original Retrieval-Augmented Generation paper — the cascade feeding a generator.
