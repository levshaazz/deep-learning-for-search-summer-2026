A practical decision rule. The single most common mistake is to pick the top of the MTEB leaderboard and ship it — but the leaderboard is averaged over generic benchmarks, and a model that wins there can lose on your domain zero-shot. So evaluate finalists on your own data. Then constrain by language, by document length (chunk or pick an 8K-context model), by the dimension you can afford to index and search, and by latency and licence. Matryoshka models let you trade dimension for cost after training, which we see next.
