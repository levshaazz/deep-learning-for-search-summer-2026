Measurable outcomes, part one. First: draw the bi-encoder — two towers, one shared space, the score is a dot product or cosine — and compute it by hand on toy vectors. Second: draw the cross-encoder — the query and document concatenated into one joint input, full attention, one logit out — and say what joint reading buys and what it costs. Third: assemble the neural cascade from the two, a fast wide retriever feeding a slow narrow reranker. The reasoning objectives — depth, the recall floor, dense versus BM25 — are on the next slide.
