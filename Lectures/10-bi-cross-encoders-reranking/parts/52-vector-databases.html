Выбор по масштабу, фильтрам и тому, нужен ли гибрид «из коробки». FAISS — если хочется контроля; pgvector — если данные уже в Postgres; Vespa/Qdrant/Weaviate — родной гибрид и фильтры.Pick by scale, filtering, and whether you need hybrid out of the box. FAISS for control; pgvector to keep vectors beside your relational data; Vespa/Qdrant/Weaviate for native hybrid and filters.

Where the index actually lives. FAISS is a library, not a server — HNSW and IVF-PQ with full control, the choice when you want to own the index and run at billion scale. Qdrant is a real-time Rust database with rich payload filtering, quantization, and server-side RRF hybrid. Weaviate and Vespa lead on native hybrid composition, and Vespa's multi-phase ranking is what powers Perplexity. Milvus is the GPU-accelerated large-scale option. pgvector keeps your vectors inside Postgres, ideal up to tens of millions when you want one database. Elasticsearch and OpenSearch add kNN to mature text search with RRF as the default hybrid. Choose by scale, by whether you need metadata filtering, and by whether you want hybrid built in.
