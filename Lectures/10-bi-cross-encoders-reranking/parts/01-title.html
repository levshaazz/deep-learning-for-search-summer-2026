Scouts and Judges

Быстрые разведчики (би-энкодеры) сметают вширь; дотошные судьи (кросс-энкодеры) совещаются над немногими. Так рождается нейронный каскад: би-энкодеры, кросс-энкодеры и переранжирование, многостадийные пайплайны.Fast scouts (bi-encoders) sweep wide; careful judges (cross-encoders) deliberate on the few. The neural cascade is born: bi-encoders, cross-encoders & reranking, multi-stage pipelines.

L6 gave words coordinates; L7 gave the encoder a contrastively-trained space and attention. Today we put that encoder to work two ways. A search system can be fast or careful, but not both everywhere — so it splits the crew. Scouts (bi-encoders) encode the query and every document separately, precompute the doc vectors offline, and sweep the whole corpus with cheap dot-products. Judges (cross-encoders) read the query and a document together, with full attention, and score the pair — accurate, but uncacheable, so they only ever see a shortlist. Stage them and you get the neural cascade, the core architecture of modern neural search. Catchphrase: Scouts and Judges.
