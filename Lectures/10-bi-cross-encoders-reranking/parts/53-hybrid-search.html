Hybrid retrieval, and the callback to Lecture 3. A single dense vector is strong on meaning but blurs exact tokens — names, product codes, rare words, typos — which is exactly where lexical BM25 shines. So run both and fuse. You cannot add their scores, because the scales are incompatible, so Reciprocal Rank Fusion combines them by rank: sum one over k plus the rank in each list, with k conventionally sixty. It is the default hybrid in Elasticsearch, OpenSearch and Qdrant. The motivation is the honest twist from Lecture 3 — zero-shot, BM25's nDCG of 0.43 often beats DPR's 0.23 on BEIR — so keeping the lexical signal makes retrieval robust. Anthropic reports their contextual hybrid cut retrieval failures by about half, and adding a reranker pushed that to about two-thirds.
