Судья выбирает только из короткого списка Разведчиков. На наборе L5 полнота растёт с глубиной: \(0 \to 0{,}25 \to 0{,}5 \to 1{,}0\). Извлечёшь слишком мелко — и упрёшься потолком во весь пайплайн. Сначала полнота, потом точность. On the L5 set recall climbs with depth: \(0 \to 0.25 \to 0.5 \to 1.0\). Retrieve too shallow and you cap the whole pipeline. Recall first, precision second. The cascade on slide 48 reranks the full top-8 (recall@8 = 1.0), so it sits at the ceiling — the floor only bites when you retrieve shallower than full recall.

The hard floor. A Judge can only rank a document the Scouts actually returned, so the whole pipeline is capped by the retriever's recall at k. On Lecture 5's eight-document set, recall climbs with depth: zero at one, a quarter at three, a half at five, and one at eight. Retrieve too shallow and you cap the Judges below their potential, no matter how good they are. Recall first, precision second — never the other way round. A strong dense retriever can saturate recall on an easy subset, but on web-scale corpora the depth genuinely matters, which sets up the depth dial.
