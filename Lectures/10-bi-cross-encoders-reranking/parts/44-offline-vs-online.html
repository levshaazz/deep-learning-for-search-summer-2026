Офлайн · время сборкиOffline · build-time

  • ·приём → чистка/дедуп → чанкование → эмбеддинг корпуса → ANN-индексingest → clean/dedup → chunk → embed corpus → ANN index
  • ·обучение/дообучение моделей; переиндексация при дрейфеtrain/fine-tune models; re-index on drift

  • ·оценка онлайн: A/B по кликам/конверсии (callback L5)online eval: A/B on clicks/conversion (callback L5)
  • ·дёшево на запрос — потому что индекс готовcheap per query — because the index is ready

Знай, на каких часах считается каждая стоимость. Тяжёлое (эмбеддинг корпуса, обучение, ANN) — офлайн, один раз. Лёгкое (один эмбед запроса + поиск + реранк коротыша) — онлайн, на запрос. Офлайн меряем BEIR/MTEB, онлайн — A/B.Know which clock each cost runs on. The heavy work (embedding the corpus, training, ANN) is offline, once. The light work (one query encode + search + rerank a shortlist) is online, per query. Offline you measure on BEIR/MTEB; online you A/B test.

The single most clarifying distinction in production retrieval: which clock a cost runs on. Build-time, offline, you ingest, clean, chunk, embed the whole corpus, build the ANN index, and train or fine-tune the models — expensive, but paid once and amortized over every query, and evaluated offline on BEIR and MTEB. Query-time, online, you embed the single query, retrieve with the Scouts, rerank with the Judges, and assemble — that is the cascade, and the latency budget lives entirely here, evaluated online by A/B testing clicks and conversions, the callback to Lecture 5. Confuse the two and you will either blow your latency budget or rebuild your index on every request.
