Training the reranker is simpler than training the retriever, because a cross-encoder is just a pair classifier. The monoBERT recipe is pointwise binary cross-entropy on the CLS token over labeled query-document pairs, and it remains a very strong baseline. The one subtlety that matters: mine hard negatives with a fast bi-encoder first, because a reranker only learns to discriminate if it is trained on candidates that are genuinely confusable, not random easy ones. When you have richer signal you can move to pairwise RankNet or listwise KL distillation from a stronger teacher, as on slide thirty-two. And in practice the sentence-transformers version-four trainer makes fine-tuning a domain reranker an evening's work.
