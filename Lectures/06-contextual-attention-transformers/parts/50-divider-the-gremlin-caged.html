The Lexical Gremlin, Caged

Обратный вызов из L1 — главный:The big callback to L1: «couch» и «sofa» наконец схлопываются в одну точку. Гремлин пойман.“couch” and “sofa” finally collapse together. The Gremlin is caged.

The biggest payoff in the whole arc, and now it lands last — the final content beat before the bridge. The Lexical Gremlin has haunted us since Lecture 1, where it personified the lexical gap; it cameoed in Lecture 3 as BM25's blind spot; in Lecture 5 we cornered it but admitted it was not caged, because polysemy survived. Today we cage it. With contextual encoders, couch and sofa are mapped to nearly the same point, so a query for one retrieves the other — synonymy solved. And because the vectors are contextual, bank by a river and bank with money finally separate — polysemy solved too. Both halves of the lexical gap close. This lands as a callback, the resolution of a long thread, not a new idea.
