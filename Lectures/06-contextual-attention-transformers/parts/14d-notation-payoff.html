Баг, сохраняющий формуThe shape-preserving bug\(n\times n \to n\times n\)

softmax, нормированный не по той оси, форму сохраняет: вошло \(n\times n\) — вышло \(n\times n\). Каждая проверка формы во всём стеке пройдёт. Ничего не упадёт. Модель просто выучит бессмыслицу — молча, за тысячи GPU-часов.A softmax normalised over the wrong axis is shape-preserving: \(n\times n\) in, \(n\times n\) out. Every shape assertion in your stack passes. Nothing crashes. The model simply learns nonsense — silently, over thousands of GPU-hours.

Единственное, что вам об этом скажет, — ориентация треугольника: раскрыт поперёк оси ключей (в единицу суммируется строка) или поперёк оси запросов (в единицу суммируется столбец). В формуле этого нет. На схеме это видно за секунду.The only thing that can tell you is the orientation of the triangle: does it open across the key axis (a row sums to one) or across the query axis (a column sums to one)? The formula does not record it. The diagram shows it in a second.

Отсюда правило: отказ — это недостающий или повёрнутый глиф, а не загадка. Нотация выучена; дальше мы ею просто пользуемся.Hence the rule: a failure is a missing or a turned glyph, not a mystery. The notation is learned; from here we simply use it.

Close the notation interlude by paying it off, because a tool has to earn its keep. Three ways to break attention. Forget to transpose K, and the contraction cup tries to join an axis of size four to an axis of size three — the wires do not meet, and any shape check anywhere catches it in a second. Forget to merge the heads back, and the block emits h by n by sixty-four where the next block expects n by seven sixty-eight — it crashes, just one floor later than you would like. But the second one is the reason we are here. Normalise the softmax over the query axis instead of the key axis, and the tensor is n by n going in and n by n coming out. Nothing changes shape. Every assertion in your stack passes, nothing crashes, no stack trace appears — and the model quietly learns nonsense, for as many GPU-hours as you are willing to pay for. The formula does not record which axis was normalised. The einsum string does not either. The only thing in the entire pipeline that can tell you is the orientation of that triangle: opened across the keys, a row sums to one; turned a quarter turn, a column sums to one. That is the trade we just made. Three slides of glyphs, in exchange for bugs that are visible instead of silent. From here on, we use the notation and do not talk about it again.
