Train the Space for Search

Готовые векторы не заточены под поиск:Off-the-shelf vectors aren’t tuned for search: тяни релевантное вместе, отталкивай нерелевантное. Сэр Косинус — линейка потерь.pull relevant together, push the rest apart. Sir Cosine rules the loss.

The second turn of the lecture. We have a contextual encoder, but its raw vectors are not organised for retrieval. Contrastive learning fixes that by training directly on the thing we care about: relative similarity. For an anchor — say a query — we define a positive that should be close, and negatives that should be far, and we adjust the encoder to pull the positive in and push the negatives out. The ruler inside this objective is cosine similarity, so Sir Cosine, who has ridden every lecture since L2, now powers the loss itself. This is the step that finally makes the space a search space.
