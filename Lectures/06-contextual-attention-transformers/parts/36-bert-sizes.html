Это тот же блок, просто \(N\) раз: BERT-base — \(12\) слоёв, \(d=768\), \(12\) голов, \(110\)M параметров. Глубже = богаче контекст, но дороже. Deeper = richer context but costlier. The width \(d\) splits over \(h\): \(768/12=64\) — that is one head’s \(d_k\).

Concrete numbers so the abstraction has a body. BERT-base stacks twelve of the block we just built, with a model width of seven hundred sixty-eight, twelve attention heads, and about a hundred and ten million parameters. BERT-large doubles the depth to twenty-four layers, widens to one thousand twenty-four, uses sixteen heads, and reaches three hundred forty million parameters. Notice the relationship we predicted earlier: the model width divides over the heads, so seven hundred sixty-eight over twelve gives sixty-four — that sixty-four is the key dimension d k of a single head, and root sixty-four equals eight is the scaling divisor at real scale. Depth buys richer context at higher cost; the unit being repeated never changes.

The dynamic payoff: connect the stack back to retrieval, using a real run of DistilBERT — six blocks. Take the same word, bank, in two sentences: a river sentence and a money sentence. Step zero: at the embedding layer the two bank tokens are essentially the same static vector — cross-sense cosine zero-point-nine-six, the two points sit almost on top of each other in the PCA plane. Then watch them split, one block at a time. Block one drops the cosine to zero-point-seven-one; the river-bank point arcs up, the money-bank point arcs down. Through blocks two to six the two trajectories fan apart and settle, ending at cross-sense cosine zero-point-six-five — exactly the number from the contextual-vectors data file. That is the whole point of the stack: one word, but two different contextual vectors, decided entirely by context — something a single static embedding can never do. Step seven: to search, pool each side, with the CLS token or a mean, into one sentence vector. Untuned mean-pooling is weak for similarity, which is what the contrastive training coming next fixes.
