Many Heads & Position

Один советник видит одну связь:One councillor sees one relation: соберём совет из голов — а потом вернём порядок слов.convene a council of heads — then give word order back.

Two upgrades in this part. First, one attention head can only express one kind of relation at a time — maybe syntactic agreement, maybe coreference, but not both at once. So we run several heads in parallel, each in its own subspace, and concatenate. Second, attention as defined is completely blind to order — it sees a bag of tokens, not a sequence — so we have to inject position explicitly with the sinusoidal positional encoding. Heads first, then position.
