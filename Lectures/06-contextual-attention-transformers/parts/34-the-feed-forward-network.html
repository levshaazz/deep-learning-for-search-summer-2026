После внимания (смешивания между токенами) каждый токен проходит один и тот же MLP независимо: расширение до \(4d\), нелинейность (ReLU/GELU), сжатие назад до \(d\). Здесь токен «обдумывает» собранную информацию.After attention (mixing across tokens) each token passes through the same MLP independently: expand to \(4d\), a nonlinearity (ReLU/GELU), contract back to \(d\). Here a token “digests” the information it gathered.

Разделение труда: внимание перемешивает токены, FFN преобразует каждый. Большая часть параметров блока живёт именно в FFN (скрытый слой \(\approx 4d\)).Division of labour: attention mixes tokens, the FFN transforms each. Most of a block’s parameters live in the FFN (hidden width \(\approx 4d\)).

The feed-forward network, the block's other half. Attention's job is to move information between tokens; the FFN's job is to transform each token on its own. It is a small two-layer perceptron applied identically and independently at every position: a linear layer that expands the dimension by a factor of about four, a nonlinearity such as ReLU or GELU, then a linear layer back down to d. Position-wise means the same weights are reused at every token but each token is processed separately, so there is no mixing here. Think of it as the moment each councillor, having listened, privately digests what it heard. Most of a block's parameters actually sit in this FFN because of that four-times-wider hidden layer.

Step one: subtract the mean — the dashed baseline slides from zero point nine eight down to zero and the bars recentre, negatives now hanging below in warm colour; the variance is unchanged at zero point three eight. Step two: divide by the standard deviation, zero point six one — the variance collapses to one, and the arrow lands exactly on the unit circle (length two, which is root d). That is the whole normalisation: recentre to mean zero, rescale to unit variance, which is the same as putting the vector on a sphere. Step three: the learned gamma scales and beta shifts each dimension, bending the bars a little, so the network is not forced to stay perfectly normalised — it buys back expressive freedom. That is LayerNorm, applied to the very vector we built with attention.
