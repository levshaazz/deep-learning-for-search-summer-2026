Assemble the Transformer Block

Внимание — лишь одна деталь:Attention is just one piece: соберём повторяющийся блок и сложим его в стек.build the repeating unit, then stack it deep.

The headline of the lecture. Attention is the star, but it is one component of a small, repeating unit — the Transformer block — and depth comes from stacking that unit. The block wraps multi-head attention with two more ideas we have not yet justified: a residual connection followed by layer normalisation, and a position-wise feed-forward network, also wrapped in residual and norm. We will look at each piece, then assemble the whole thing as a stepped architecture you can walk through, and finally stack it N deep. This is the engine that produces contextual embeddings.
