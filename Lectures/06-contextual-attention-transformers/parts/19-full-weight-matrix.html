Look at the whole attention matrix, because the structure is the point. Each row is one token's distribution over who it heeds, and each row sums to one. The row for the is zero point four two two, zero point one five five, zero point four two two — fairly spread. The row for cat is zero point zero nine, zero point six six five, zero point two four five — the sharpest of the three, cat strongly attending to itself. The row for sat is zero point two one two, zero point two one two, zero point five seven six. These are the exact rows of the weights matrix in the data file. Multiplying this matrix A by the value matrix V produces all three contextual output vectors at once. Attention is just this matrix, read as who heeds whom.
