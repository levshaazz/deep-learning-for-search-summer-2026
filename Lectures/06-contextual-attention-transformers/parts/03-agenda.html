Seven movements. Part 1: why one static vector cannot hold two senses — polysemy, the bank problem. Part 2: the attention idea — each token a councillor blending its neighbours. Part 3: the climb — scaled dot-product attention, built exactly on a tiny worked example. Part 4: many heads in parallel subspaces, and how we inject word order with sinusoidal positional encoding. Part 5: the headline — assembling the whole Transformer block, residual and LayerNorm and the feed-forward network, stacked N deep. Part 6: contrastive learning — InfoNCE and triplet, tuning the space for retrieval. Part 7: the anisotropy trap, then the big payoff — the Lexical Gremlin caged — and the bridge to Lecture 7.
