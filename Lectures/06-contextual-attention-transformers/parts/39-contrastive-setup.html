Откуда берутся пары? Из данных: (вопрос, релевантный ответ) — позитив; всё прочее — негатив. Размечать смысл не нужно — пары и есть обучающий сигнал.Where do pairs come from? From data: (question, relevant answer) is a positive; everything else is a negative. No need to label meaning — the pairs are the supervision.

The contrastive setup in three roles. Pick an anchor — here the word cat. A positive is something that should sit near it, like kitten. Negatives are things that should be far, like airplane, computer, france. The objective then moves the encoder so the anchor's vector aligns with the positive and separates from the negatives, all measured by cosine. The beautiful part is where supervision comes from: in retrieval, a positive is just a known relevant pair — a question and its answer, a query and a clicked document — and everything else is a negative. We never label what a sentence means; the pairs themselves are the training signal. Next, the two classic loss forms.
