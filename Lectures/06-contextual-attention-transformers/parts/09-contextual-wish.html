Чего мы хотим: вектор на контекстWhat we want: a vector per context

не на слово, а на употреблениеnot per word, per use
ЦельGoal
Контекстный эмбеддингContextual embedding(word, sentence) \(\to\) \(\mathbb{R}^{d}\)

where (word, sentence) = a token together with its surrounding context, → = maps to, ℝd = a d-dimensional real vector (d = the embedding size).

Вектор слова должен зависеть от предложения. Тогда «bank» в «river bank» и в «bank account» получит две разные точки — каждая собрана из своих соседей.A word’s vector should depend on the sentence. Then “bank” in “river bank” and in “bank account” gets two different points — each built from its own neighbours.

Статический эмбеддинг — это функция слова: \(w\mapsto v_w\). Контекстный — функция слова и его контекста: \((w, \text{ctx})\mapsto v\). Соседи «river» и «account» тянут «bank» в разные стороны.A static embedding is a function of the word: \(w\mapsto v_w\). A contextual one is a function of the word and its context: \((w, \text{ctx})\mapsto v\). The neighbours “river” and “account” pull “bank” in different directions.

The wish, stated as a definition. We want to upgrade the embedding from a function of the word alone to a function of the word and its context. Formally, instead of w maps to v sub w, we want the pair of word and context to map to a vector. Then the same surface word bank produces two distinct vectors when its neighbours differ — river pulls it one way, account the other. The question is how a model can let neighbours influence a token's vector, in a way that is learnable and parallel. The answer is the attention mechanism, and we build it next.
