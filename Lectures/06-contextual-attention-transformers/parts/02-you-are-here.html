Get Data → Measure → Rank

L5 дал словам координаты; сегодня мы даём вектор каждому контексту — и учим пространство для поиска. Это вторая половина нейронного поворота и мост к ранжированию.L5 gave words coordinates; today we give a vector to each context — and train the space for search. This is the second half of the neural turn and the bridge into ranking.

Anchor on the spine. Lecture 5 gave each word a position, but a static one — one vector per word forever. Today the representation upgrades from static to contextual, and crucially we learn it for search, not just as a by-product of language modelling. That is why we straddle Get Data and Measure: the Transformer is the engine that produces contextual vectors, and contrastive learning is the objective that shapes those vectors so retrieval works. Everything after this — bi-encoders, cross-encoders, the neural cascade — stands on today.
