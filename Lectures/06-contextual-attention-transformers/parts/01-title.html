The Council of Attention

От одного вектора на слово — к вектору на контекст: внимание, Transformer целиком, и контрастное обучение, которое наконец затачивает пространство под поиск.From one vector per word to one vector per context: attention, the full Transformer block, and the contrastive training that finally tunes the space for search.

Week 2 · Fri Jun 12
Last lecture we drew the Map of Meaning, but left one thread loose: a word still had exactly one vector, so bank — river bank versus financial bank — was forced into a single averaged point. Today we cut that thread. The crew has too many voices; who do you listen to? Attention is the answer — every token a councillor deciding who to heed. We build scaled dot-product attention exactly, then the whole Transformer block — positional encoding, multi-head, residual and LayerNorm, the feed-forward network, stacked. Then contrastive learning shapes the space for retrieval, and the Lexical Gremlin — our antagonist since Lecture 1 — is finally caged: couch and sofa collapse together.
