Self-attention: \(Q\), \(K\), \(V\) берутся из одной последовательности — каждый токен запрашивает каждый токен, включая себя. Именно поэтому cat мог положить вес на cat. Это вариант внутри энкодера (BERT).Self-attention: \(Q\), \(K\), \(V\) all come from the same sequence — every token queries every token, itself included. That is exactly why cat could put weight on cat. This is the variant inside an encoder (BERT).

МеханикаMechanics
Линейные проекцииLinear projections\(Q=XW_Q,\ K=XW_K,\ V=XW_V\)

Вход \(X\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times d}\) (T токенов). Три обучаемые матрицы \(W_Q,W_K,W_V\) проецируют каждый токен в роли запроса, ключа и значения. Это единственные параметры self-attention — сам механизм без весов.Input \(X\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times d}\) (T tokens). Three learned matrices \(W_Q,W_K,W_V\) project each token into its query, key and value role. These are the only parameters of self-attention — the mechanism itself is weight-free.

Обучение настраивает \(W_Q,W_K,W_V\) так, чтобы «полезные» соседи получали высокий скор. В нашем примере \(Q,K,V\) уже даны — мы пропустили проекцию ради чистых чисел. (Позже встретится и cross-attention: \(Q\) — из одной последовательности, \(K,V\) — из другой; арифметика та же.)Training tunes \(W_Q,W_K,W_V\) so that “useful” neighbours score high. In our example \(Q,K,V\) were given — we skipped the projection for clean numbers. (Later you also meet cross-attention: \(Q\) from one sequence, \(K,V\) from another — the arithmetic is identical.)

A precise naming, then the source of the three matrices — placed before the theorem and the cost wall, both of which already manipulate Q, K and V. First, self-attention: in our worked example the queries, keys and values all came from the same three tokens, so every token both asks and answers and attends to every token including itself — which is exactly why cat could put weight on cat. This is the encoder variant inside BERT. Now where do Q, K and V come from? The layer's input is X, a matrix of T token vectors each of dimension d, and self-attention multiplies X by three learned weight matrices — W Q, W K and W V — to produce the queries, keys and values. Those three projection matrices are the only learnable parameters in attention; the softmax-and-weighted-sum machine itself has no weights. In our worked example we handed you Q, K and V directly and skipped this projection purely to keep the arithmetic clean. Later, in encoder-decoder models, you also meet cross-attention, where the queries come from one sequence and the keys and values from another — but the arithmetic is identical.
