Одно правило на всю нотацию: следите за осью. Родилась — умерла — оплетена. Всё остальное — грамматика.One rule for the whole notation: follow the axis. Born — killed — wrapped. Everything else is grammar.

The whole vocabulary on one card — six glyphs, and you never need a seventh. A wire is an axis of a tensor, and its name is a letter: n for tokens, d for the head dimension, h for heads. Everything else in the notation describes what is being done to a wire. A cup — the little u-shape where two wires meet and end — is a contraction: a sum over that axis, which is to say the axis dies there. That is the only glyph that kills an axis, which is why your eye should go to it first. A triangle marked sigma is a softmax: it normalises across an axis but does not consume it, so the shape that goes in is the shape that comes out — remember that, it is the trap on the fourth slide. A chipped rectangle is a learned linear map; the chipped corner tells you which side is the input, so the diagram never becomes ambiguous under reflection. A hexagon is a reindex — a slice, a mask, a top-k — the axis survives, but its entries have been permuted or cut. And a dashed region is a broadcast: an axis that wraps around everything drawn inside it, which is how heads and batches appear. Note the word wraps, and remember it costs: whatever is inside gets multiplied by the size of that axis.
