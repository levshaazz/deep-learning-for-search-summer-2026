Triplet тянет одну пару; InfoNCE превращает «ближе негатива» в softmax-классификацию позитива против всего батча. Температура \(\tau=0.1\) делит косинусы (Сэр Косинус) и заостряет распределение; минимизировать \(-\log p^{+}\) = поднять \(p^{+}\to 1\). Реальные числа дают потерю \(\mathbf{0.1191}\) — её мы и посчитаем целиком дальше.Triplet tunes one pair; InfoNCE turns “beat the negative” into a softmax classification of the positive against the whole batch. Temperature \(\tau=0.1\) divides the cosines (Sir Cosine) and sharpens the distribution; minimising \(-\log p^{+}\) pushes \(p^{+}\to 1\). The real numbers give a loss of \(\mathbf{0.1191}\) — which we compute end-to-end next.

The contrastive punchline, derived as one moving frame instead of two static cards, and it sets up the live InfoNCE climb that follows. Step zero: the triplet loss — the hinge that wants the anchor's similarity to its positive to beat its similarity to one negative by a margin m. Step one: one negative is a weak signal, so we score the positive against many candidates at once — the positive itself plus k negatives. Step two: divide every similarity by the temperature tau to get logits, then take a softmax — that gives p-plus, the probability mass the model puts on the true positive. Step three: InfoNCE is simply minus the log of that probability — read it as a softmax classification, which candidate is the true positive, and minimising it drives p-plus toward one. On our real cosines this lands at zero point one one nine one, the exact number we compute step by step on the next slide. Use the arrows to step the algebra.
