Все три принимают одну истину: при равной трудности только нечто, знающее о релевантности — фильтр, учитель, метка — может отличить достойный негатив от друга.All three accept one truth: at equal hardness, only something that knows about relevance — a filter, a teacher, a label — can tell a worthy negative from a friend.

Three cures. Filter (RocketQA): cross-encoder drops suspected positives. Sample (SimANS): ambiguous negatives near a sweet spot, not the absolute hardest. Co-train/distil (AR2, Margin-MSE): a relevance-aware teacher shapes the negatives. Only relevance knowledge separates friend from foe at equal hardness.
