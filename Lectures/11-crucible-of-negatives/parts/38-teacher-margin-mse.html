Turn-teacher. A cross-encoder reads (q,d) together and scores true relevance; distil its margins into the bi-encoder via Margin-MSE. When a mined negative is really an unlabelled positive, the teacher's score is high, the target margin shrinks — the student keeps n₄ and drops n₅.
