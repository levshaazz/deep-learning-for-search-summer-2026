Сама функция потерь: ученик копирует не метку, а зазорThe loss itself: the student copies a margin, not a label

строка, в которой релевантность наконец входит в обучениеthe line where relevance finally enters training
$$ \mathcal{L} \;=\; \big(m_\theta - m_T\big)^{2}, \qquad m(q,d^+,d^-) \;=\; s(q,d^+) - s(q,d^-) $$

InfoNCE знает лишь «позитив ближе всех» и толкает прочь любой негатив. Здесь цель приходит извне: у самозванца \(m_T\approx 0\) — значит и \(m_\theta\) обязан быть около нуля, ученик просто не разводит эту пару. Достойный негатив с большим \(m_T\) отталкивается по-прежнему. Релевантность вошла в обучение ровно одним числом — чужим зазором.InfoNCE knows only “the positive is nearest” and pushes every negative away. Here the target comes from outside: for an impostor \(m_T\approx 0\) — so \(m_\theta\) must be near zero and the student never separates that pair. A worthy negative with a large \(m_T\) is still pushed. Relevance entered training as exactly one number — somebody else’s margin.

The act named the third cure and drew an arrow — margin_teacher → bi-encoder — but never wrote the objective, so "the teacher unmasks the impostor" stayed a slogan. Margin-MSE (Hofstätter et al. 2020) is a squared error between two MARGINS: the student's s(q,d+) − s(q,d−) against the teacher's. Say the contrast aloud: InfoNCE has no notion of relevance, only "the positive must win", so it pushes every mined negative away with the Boltzmann weight from slide 18 — which is exactly what makes an unlabelled positive toxic. Margin-MSE replaces the direction with a target. When the mined negative is really a positive, the cross-encoder scores both passages alike, the target margin collapses to about zero, and the student is penalised for separating them at all. A genuinely worthy negative keeps a large teacher margin and is still pushed. That is the whole mechanism of the impostor act in one line, and it is also why the modern recipe on the next slides can afford to mine aggressively.
