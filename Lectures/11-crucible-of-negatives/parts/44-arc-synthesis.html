Каждый шаг покупал более трудного, более честного противника.Every step bought a harder, more honest opponent.

The ladder in one table: random (too easy) → in-batch (local) → BM25 (model-blind) → ANCE (dynamic) → STAR/ADORE (fresh) → denoise (no impostors) → two-stage (uniformity+boundary). Each row names the flaw the step fixed. The whole arc at a glance.
