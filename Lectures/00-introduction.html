The stakes slide — motivates the whole course before the objectives. Two order-of-magnitude figures (illustrative, not precise): the web ≈ 10^12 pages, and Google has long quoted ≈15% of daily queries as brand-new (never seen). Plan A — pre-compute every answer — dies on the unseen 15% (tomorrow's questions don't exist yet). Plan B — scan the whole corpus per query — dies on the clock: even at 1 µs/doc, 10^12 docs is over a week of compute, far past the ~250 ms a user will wait. Neither brute force works; that tension — too much data, too little time — is the entire course in one breath. Mirrors the Book stakes-scale beat.
