Six steps on four cities, exact 2×2 arithmetic from data/l18-geometry.json → whitenToy. The question it answers is the one students actually ask: I already centred, why whiten? Because centring only kills the shared shift; Σ = [[5,3],[3,5]] still has an off-diagonal 3 (λ₁ = 8, λ₂ = 2, condition number 4), and that shear is what keeps the two strangers at 0.60.
