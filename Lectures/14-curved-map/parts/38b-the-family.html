Сравнение 12 методов и 2 бейзлайнов на 50 датасетах: DisSimLocal лучше всех по самой метрике хабности, Local Scaling — по kNN-классификации (единственный, кто значимо обходит оба бейзлайна). Реальный \(S_{N_{10}}\) лежит от \(-0{,}1156\) до \(15{,}5188\) — два порядка разброса. Отсюда операционное правило: выигрыш появляется только выше \(S_{N_{10}} \approx 1{,}4\). Сначала измерь — потом лечи. И меряй при том же \(k\): индекс здесь \(10\), а все скосы этой лекции — при \(k=5\), в лоб их не сравнить. Взаимный kNN-граф — прямая операция над индексом L9.A comparison of 12 methods and 2 baselines on 50 datasets: DisSimLocal wins on the hubness metric itself, Local Scaling wins on kNN classification (the only method significantly beating both baselines). Real \(S_{N_{10}}\) runs from \(-0.1156\) to \(15.5188\) — two orders of magnitude. Hence the operating rule: the gain only appears above \(S_{N_{10}} \approx 1.4\). Measure first, treat second. And measure at the same \(k\): the subscript here is \(10\), while every skew in this lecture is at \(k=5\) — the two do not compare. The mutual-kNN graph is a direct operation on the L13 index.

Plan N5, F10, R3 — this closes a promise L2 made and L14 had kept only one third of (L2's beat 25 promised "mutual proximity, local/global scaling, mutual-kNN graphs"; Mutual Proximity got one line on old slide 44, local/global scaling one line on 38, and the mutual-kNN graph was never mentioned at all — even though it is the most L9-relevant cure, being a single symmetrisation pass over a neighbour graph you have already built). Feldbauer & Flexer 2019, Knowledge and Information Systems 59(1):137–166 ("A comprehensive empirical comparison of hubness reduction in high-dimensional spaces" — note the venue and title, both often miscited): 12 methods + 2 baselines on 50 datasets; DisSimLocal leads on the hubness metric, Local Scaling on kNN classification; observed S_{N₁₀} spans −0.1156 … 15.5188; Mutual Proximity is cubic and unusable at scale while LS/NICDM/DSL are quadratic. The operating threshold S_{N₁₀} ≈ 1.4 is the single most actionable number in act 04 and reappears in the 2026 checklist — and this is where the k-mismatch warning belongs (late review §5): the subscript is 10, whereas every skew this deck shows (our toy's 2.5904, Radovanović's 0.121/1.541/5.445, QB-Norm's 0.939) is at k = 5, so a student who measures S_{N₅} and compares it to 1.4 is comparing two different statistics.
