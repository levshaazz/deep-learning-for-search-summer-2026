Как Меркатор сплющивает глобус, конус сплющивает все города в один клин. Like Mercator flattening a globe, the cone squashes every town into one wedge.

Внутри клина всё смотрит в одну сторону, поэтому угол между любыми двумя точками мал — далёкие по смыслу слова выглядят соседями. Изотропия (равномерно по сфере) дала бы средний угол ≈90°. Inside the wedge everything faces one way, so the angle between any two points is small — semantically distant words look like neighbours. Isotropy (uniform on the sphere) would give an average angle ≈90°.

The map metaphor for anisotropy: the cone is a Mercator-style projection that squashes every "town" into one wedge, so every vector faces roughly one way and the angle between any two points is small — semantically distant words read as neighbours. Isotropy (uniform on the sphere) would give an average pairwise angle ≈90° (cosine ≈0), which separates points; the wedge collapses that separation.
