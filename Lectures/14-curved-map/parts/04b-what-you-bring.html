Continuity slide (plan N4/N5/N6, P4). L14 does not introduce a new model: it repays four earlier promises and audits the ruler L13 has just been using. L2 named all three effects (concentration, hubness, anisotropy) as teasers and promised the cures (mutual proximity, local/global scaling, mutual-kNN graphs). L13 built the neighbour graph and measured the concentration of EUCLIDEAN distances (cv 0.4784 → 0.0187, data/l2-highd.json) — a distance statistic, not a cosine one, which is the correction the late review §1 forced here and on slide 09d. The tension students actually carry — "everything is nearly orthogonal" vs "random words read 0.99" — is settled in act 01 by measuring the cosine on L13's own uniform [0,1]^d cloud (0.8312 → 0.7496: a cone) against a gaussian N(0,I) (within 0.0065 of zero at every d).
