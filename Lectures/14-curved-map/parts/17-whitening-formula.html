Итоговая ковариация \(\tilde{x}\) — единичная матрица: каждое направление используется одинаково, корреляции между осями обнулены. Полная изотропия — та самая равновеликая карта.The output covariance of \(\tilde{x}\) is the identity: every direction is used equally and the cross-axis correlations are zeroed. Full isotropy — the equal-area map itself.

Whitening: x̃ = (x − μ)W with W = U Λ^(−1/2) from the eigendecomposition Σ = U Λ Uᵀ. Two steps in one: U rotates onto the principal axes, Λ^(−1/2) rescales each to unit variance. The transformed covariance is the identity, so all directions are equally used and cross-axis correlation is removed — full isotropy, which standardization (per-axis rescale) alone cannot achieve because it leaves the off-diagonal correlations.
