Читай сверху вниз как лестницу риска: чем шире операция, тем больше она чинит и тем больше может сломать. Первая строка почти бесплатна и почти всегда полезна; третья — самая эффектная в 2021-м и самая опасная в 2026-м; четвёртая просто дороже всех. Row one is nearly free and nearly always helpful; row three was the showpiece of 2021 and the hazard of 2026; row four is simply the most expensive. The slide “When whitening hurts” shows exactly where row three went negative.

Synthesis of act 02 (plan NEW-14). The four repairs are usually taught as one blurry idea ("normalise your embeddings"); they are four operations with four risk profiles. Mean-direction removal touches the first moment only. ABT removes a handful of named axes and takes whatever else those axes carried (GloVe RG65 76.96 → 74.36). Full whitening rescales every axis and, per the previous slide, spends alignment to buy uniformity. Contrastive training is the only one that improves both, at the price of a training run. The ladder framing is what makes the 2026 checklist in act 05 memorable: as you go down, the blast radius grows.
