REWRITTEN — this slide carried the lecture's single largest factual error (plan §1.0 F0). 42.6 → 66.1 is Conneau et al. 2018 TABLE 3: cross-lingual SENTENCE retrieval on Europarl, en→it, 2000 query sentences against 200,000 target sentences, idf-weighted bag of words, supervised Procrustes, NN vs CSLS. It is not word-translation P@1, and there is no Procrustes-NN row for en-it in the word-translation tables at all; the likely source of the splice is Table 2's best unsupervised Adv-Refine-CSLS 66.2 ≈ 66.1. The genuine NN→CSLS word-translation pairs (Table 1, fastText/Wikipedia, 1500 queries, 200k vocabulary, K = 10, stable for K ∈ {5,10,50}) are en-es 77.4 → 81.4, en-de 68.4 → 73.5, en-ru 47.0 → 51.7, en-zh 40.6 → 42.7 — now shown, and note how much smaller those gains are than the retrieval row. The reverse direction it→en is 53.5 → 69.5. The authors' own framing of CSLS as a hubness fix is quoted because it ties act 04 back to act 03 in their words, not ours.
