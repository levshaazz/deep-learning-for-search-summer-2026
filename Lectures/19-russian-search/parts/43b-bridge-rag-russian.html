The outgoing bridge: back through L15's chunking and L18's late chunking, then forward into L20 (advanced RAG, multimodal, ethics), the next and last lecture. Three consequences of Act 1's token tax: a chunk holds less Russian meaning at the same token budget, so chunks shrink or context bills grow; retrieval pays the tax twice, in the query and again in the pasted context; and quotation-based grounding breaks on surface forms, because a lemmatized index will not return a quote searched as a literal string. Then the table that overturns "dense always wins" (RusBEIR, arXiv:2511.05079, Table 5): on sentence-length chunks averaging 17.6 words BM25 scores 13.58 against FRIDA's 20.79, but on full articles averaging 2059 words BM25 reaches 74.95 and beats mE5-large's 60.62. Same content, different cut, arms reversed — which arm wins is a function of document length.
