Токенизатор o200k_base даёт 1,915 токена на слово на русском и 1,220 на английском. Что из этого следует? The o200k_base tokenizer gives 1.915 tokens per word on Russian and 1.220 on English. What follows from that?
A Русский ищется хуже: раз токенов больше, качество выдачи будет ниже.Russian retrieves worse: more tokens means lower result quality.

C. Фертильность — это счёт, а не оценка: 1,915 / 1,220 ≈ 1,6, значит тот же текст в словах стоит в 1,6 раза дороже по токенам. Самый липкий дистрактор — A: он подменяет цену качеством, а качество меряется на выдаче (Акт 3), не на счётчике токенов. C. Fertility is a bill, not a grade: 1.915 / 1.220 ≈ 1.6, so the same text in words costs 1.6× in tokens. The stickiest distractor is A: it swaps price for quality, and quality is measured on the result list (Act 3), never on a token counter.
Quiz closing Act 1. Correct answer C: 1.915 / 1.220 is about 1.6, so a document of the same word count costs roughly 1.6× the tokens — that is context and money, nothing else. Distractor A (Russian retrieves worse) is the sticky one and is exactly the confusion this act exists to kill; B over-reads one number into a tooling mandate — a native tokenizer sometimes helps, but this measurement does not imply it; D reads a billing ratio as a comprehension claim. Note for the lecturer: 1.6 is the per-word ratio, not the ×1.42 content premium from the fertility table — Russian says the same thing in fewer words, and the two numbers answer two different questions.
