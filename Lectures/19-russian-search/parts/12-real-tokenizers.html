The table that replaces the deck's old unsourced "about a couple of tokens per word". Fertility measured on FLORES-200, the same parallel corpus Petrov et al. used; our reproduction matched their published values to within two tokens. Read it three ways. First, OpenAI's own arc — ×5.74 on GPT-2's r50k, ×2.49 on cl100k, ×1.42 on o200k — proves the premium is a vocabulary-budget decision, not a property of Russian. Second, BGE-M3 and multilingual-E5 both inherit XLM-R's 250k vocabulary, so ×1.17 is simultaneously an Act 1 number and an Act 3 number — the encoders you will pick later are also the cheapest tokenizers here. Third, ruBERT inverts the tax outright at ×0.67, at the cost of being useless for English. The Claude caveat matters on a commercial course: no public tokenizer, and tiktoken under-counts, so this table does not transfer.
