The ru-specific route, corrected. ruBERT and ruRoBERTa (DeepPavlov) are masked-LM base encoders: Russian pre-training, no contrastive query-passage objective, so they are the starting point for fine-tuning a retriever (the L11 story), not retrievers themselves. The correction that matters: this deck used to recommend sbert_large_nlu_ru as a Russian sentence encoder, and it scores 11.00 on MTEB(rus, v1.1) Retrieval; rubert-tiny2 scores 11.21 and LaBSE 31.53, against bge-m3's 75.20. All three optimise sentence similarity, which is a different task from ad-hoc retrieval — a near-zero retrieval score on a model that is fine at STS is not a paradox, it is the objective showing.
