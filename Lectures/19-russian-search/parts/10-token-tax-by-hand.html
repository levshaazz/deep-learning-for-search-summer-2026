Тот же смысл — ×2,9 токенов (23 против 8). На слово выходит ×5,8, но русских слов вдвое меньше: за содержание платишь меньше, чем говорит фертильность. Same meaning — ×2.9 the tokens (23 against 8). Per word it is ×5.8, but Russian needs half the words: the content premium is smaller than the fertility.

Start from the number the class already owns: in L2 we trained byte-level BPE at vocabulary 1,000, 4,000 and 16,000 and the Russian word "привет" cost 12 tokens at every size — a sixteen-fold vocabulary bought exactly nothing. This slide supplies the missing "why". Our own toy (data/l20-ru.json, gen_l20.py) trains a character BPE on a 9:1 English:Russian mix: English "search" stays one token, Russian "запроса" falls all the way back to its seven characters, because every merge the algorithm could afford went to an English pair. Totals: EN 8 words → 8 tokens (1.0/word), RU 4 words → 23 tokens (5.8/word). The direction is the lesson, not the exact ratio — production tokenizers show a smaller but real premium, measured two slides on.
