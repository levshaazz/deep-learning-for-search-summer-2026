The multilingual roster with numbers, from the MTEB(rus, v1.1) leaderboard restricted to models with full coverage of all 23 tasks. Two corrections over the old qualitative version. First, language counts are quoted from the model cards, not from a derivation: multilingual-E5 is built on XLM-RoBERTa and its card lists 100 languages, BGE-M3 lists 100+, LaBSE 109 — and the count is deliberately NOT used as an argument, because it predicts nothing about retrieval quality. Second, LaBSE: 109 languages, but a Retrieval score of 31.53 against BGE-M3's 75.20, because it was trained on parallel sentence pairs for bitext mining, not ad-hoc search. The proprietary APIs (gemini-embedding, Cohere multilingual) simply do not have a full ruMTEB run, so listing them beside scored models would be dishonest.
