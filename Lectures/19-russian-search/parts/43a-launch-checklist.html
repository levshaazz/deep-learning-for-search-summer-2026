The production launch checklist for Russian, ten items, each cheap. Measure your own corpus's fertility rather than trusting a published table. Normalize in both arms with one analyzer and one code path. Keep не/ни/без out of the stop list so negation survives indexing. Put the ё→е char_filter before the stop filter, since the Lucene list has no ё forms. Normalize to NFC on input, because macOS serves NFD. Keep a case- and ё-preserving keyword field for entities. Restore keyboard layout in the query preprocessor. Choose an embedder by its ruMTEB Retrieval score, never the 23-task average. Apply query:/passage: prefixes wherever the model requires them. And evaluate on your own Russian data — a public benchmark is not your production traffic.
