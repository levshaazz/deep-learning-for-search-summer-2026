Bill 3 — here it is. The hybrid adds +0.198 nDCG@10 over BM25 and +0.125 over dense. Until this slide all of Act 3 ran on words.

Ни одно плечо в одиночку не догоняет их сумму. Полнота на глубине 100 растёт с 0,661 до 0,874 — гибрид приносит кандидатов, которых лексика не видит, а плотное плечо не находит. Neither arm alone catches their sum. Recall at depth 100 climbs from 0.661 to 0.874 — the hybrid brings in candidates the lexical arm cannot see and the dense arm does not find.

The missing third bill, cited from MIRACL (Zhang et al., TACL 2023) on the Russian dev split — 1252 queries judged by native speakers. BM25 scores 0.334 nDCG@10, the dense mDPR arm 0.407, and their fusion 0.532: +0.198 over the lexical arm and +0.125 over the dense one. Recall@100 tells the same story more starkly, 0.661 / 0.797 / 0.874. Say out loud that this is a REPORTED number, not one we measured, and that it is the only published Russian evidence for the deck's "hybrid is the production baseline" claim — which is why the claim is made here and not earlier. It also closes the lecture's three-bills frame: token tax, rank inversion, and now the retrieval score itself.
