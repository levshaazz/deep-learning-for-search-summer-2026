План лекции The shape of the hour

четыре акта · один флективный язык four acts · one fusional language
01 Морфология и токен-налог Morphology & the token tax BPE рвёт русское слово на много кусков — счёт в токенах BPE shreds a Russian word into many pieces — the bill is in tokens 02 Нормализация до индекса Normalize before you index лемматизация/стемминг против подслов — BM25 и совпадение форм lemmatization/stemming vs sub-words — BM25 and matching forms 03 Плотный поиск по-русски Dense retrieval speaks Russian E5-multilingual, BGE-M3, ru-модели · MIRACL / mMARCO / ruMTEB E5-multilingual, BGE-M3, ru models · MIRACL / mMARCO / ruMTEB 04 Тысяча мелких порезов The thousand small cuts ё/е, регистр, транслит, раскладка клавиатуры yo/ye, case, transliteration, keyboard layout
Four acts. (1) The token tax — a BPE tokenizer trained on English-heavy data fragments Russian words into several times more sub-word pieces; the bill is context length and cost. (2) Normalize before you index — Russian is fusional, so query and answer can share zero surface forms; lemmatization/stemming vs the sub-word tokenization dense encoders already do. (3) Dense retrieval in Russian — multilingual and ru-specific embedders, and the MIRACL/mMARCO/ruMTEB benchmarks. (4) Practical gotchas — ё/е, case, transliteration, keyboard-layout typos. Anchors jump to each act divider.
