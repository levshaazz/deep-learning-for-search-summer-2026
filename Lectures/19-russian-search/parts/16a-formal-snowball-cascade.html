Границы RV/R1/R2 — это вся «лингвистика» стеммера: он не знает морфем, он знает, где кончается зона, в которой резать не страшно. Отсюда и его повадки — и хорошие, и дурные.The RV/R1/R2 marks are the whole “linguistics” of the stemmer: it knows no morphemes, only where the safe-to-cut zone ends. That is where both its virtues and its vices come from.

The algorithm, opened up. Snowball Russian first folds ё to е (a prelude added in 2018), then computes three regions: RV starts after the first vowel, R1 after the first non-vowel that follows a vowel, R2 the same rule applied inside R1. Everything else is four steps of "longest matching ending inside a region": step 1 removes a perfective gerund, or else a reflexive плюс one adjectival/verbal/nominal ending; step 2 a trailing и; step 3 a derivational ост/ость but only inside R2; step 4 undoubles нн, strips a superlative, or drops a soft sign. There are no morphemes anywhere in it. Our generator ports this algorithm in stdlib and asserts it against a 36-word control list at generation time, so every stem on the following slides is the real algorithm's output, not an illustration.
