Одно и то же содержание, меняется только длина куска. На предложениях BM25 позади всех плотных; на окне 6 он обходит mE5-large и BGE-M3 (31,54 против 31,42 и 31,15), но не FRIDA; на полных статьях — 74,95 против 60,62. Значит, «плотный лучше» — не свойство языка, а свойство режима длины. Прямой мост к чанкованию из L15/L18.The same content, only the chunk length changes. On sentences BM25 trails every dense model; at window 6 it passes mE5-large and BGE-M3 (31.54 against 31.42 and 31.15) but not FRIDA; on full articles it is 74.95 against 60.62. So “dense is better” is not a property of the language but of the length regime. A direct bridge to chunking in L15/L18.

The table that overturns "dense always wins" — RusBEIR (Kovalev et al., arXiv:2511.05079, Table 5). Identical content is re-cut into progressively longer windows and every system is re-scored. On sentence-length chunks (17.6 words) BM25 sits at 13.58 while FRIDA reaches 20.79, a seven-point gap in the dense arm's favour. The crossover happens around window 6 (105.8 words), where BM25's 31.54 already edges past mE5-large and BGE-M3. On full articles (2059 words) BM25 scores 74.95 against mE5-large's 60.62 — a fourteen-point win for the lexical arm. The mechanism: long documents give BM25 many term matches and length normalization to work with, while a single dense vector must summarize 2000 words into one point. The engineering consequence is the chunking decision from L15/L18, and one more argument for hybrid over choosing a side.
