Gotcha 1 — the ё/е collapse, now with numbers. Measured on FLORES-ru (231,992 Cyrillic letters): ё is 0.163 % of letters and only 1.93 % of all (ё+е) occurrences, and the corpus contradicts itself — «все» 60 vs «всё» 29, «еще» 27 vs «ещё» 23, «чем» 57 vs «чём» 0. On cl100k the letter also doubles the token cost of the word (1 → 2). The nuance students must carry away: Snowball folds ё→е in its own prelude step (2018-03-16), so a stemmed field is cured for free — but keyword fields, exact phrases, autocomplete and facets never reach the stemmer, and that is exactly where ё/е costs recall.
