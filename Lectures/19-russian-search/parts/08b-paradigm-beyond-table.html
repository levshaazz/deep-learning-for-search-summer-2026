Хорошая новость: лечится это одним и тем же приёмом — свести к лемме.So the ten terms from the table are a lower bound on what one lexeme becomes in the index, not the total. The good news: one cure covers all of it — reduce to the lemma.

Honesty patch on the six-case simplification. Russian keeps residual forms outside the six-by-two grid: the locative/second prepositional (в лесу vs о лесе), the partitive genitive (чаю vs чая), and a productive colloquial vocative (Саш, мам) that shows up in real query logs. And nouns are the small case: the widget's own lists give 16 distinct forms for the verb «играть» and 18 for the adjective «новый». The takeaway is not "Russian is hard" but "ten was a floor" — which is why the fix has to be lemmatization rather than an enumeration of endings. A linguistically literate viewer will catch the six-case claim if you do not say this out loud.
