  • ·Арабский. Клитики приклеиваются к слову, а огласовка на письме опускается — одна цепочка букв читается несколькими способами. Здесь единица нормализации не лемма, а снятие клитик.Arabic. Clitics attach to the word and the vowels are simply not written — one letter string reads several ways. The normalization unit here is not the lemma but clitic stripping.
  • ·Турецкий и татарский. Агглютинация нанизывает суффиксы, пока слово не станет предложением. Парадигма русского в десяток форм тут выглядит скромно — и токен-налог растёт соответственно.Turkish and Tatar. Agglutination strings suffix onto suffix until a word is a sentence. Russian’s dozen forms look modest by comparison — and the token tax scales accordingly.
  • ·Финский — 15 падежей против шести русских: та же болезнь, крупнее доза. Китайский ломает шаг раньше всех: пробелов нет, и токенизация начинается с сегментации, а не с нормализации.Finnish — 15 cases against Russian’s six: the same disease at a larger dose. Chinese breaks an earlier step: there are no spaces, so tokenization starts with segmentation, not normalization.
Closing the promise that Russian was an example, not the subject. The four bills and the symmetry principle transfer directly. Arabic: clitics attach to the word and short vowels are unwritten, so the normalization unit is clitic stripping rather than lemmatization. Turkish and Tatar: agglutination stacks suffixes until a word is effectively a sentence, so the paradigm explosion and the token tax are both larger than Russian's. Finnish: 15 cases against Russian's six — same disease, bigger dose. Chinese breaks an earlier step entirely: no spaces, so the pipeline starts with segmentation before any normalization question arises. The checklist stays: measure fertility, choose the normalization unit your language actually needs, apply it symmetrically in both arms, evaluate on your own data.
