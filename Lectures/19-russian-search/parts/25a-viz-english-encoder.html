Через английский словарь русское слово рассыпается почти на байты: 7,773 токена на слово против 1,227 у английского — премия ×5,74. Через мультиязычный словарь XLM-R (его делят BGE-M3 и mE5) — 1,804 против 1,392, премия ×1,17. Причина видна в словаре: у r50k 17 кириллических токенов и ни одного длиной в два кириллических символа.Through an English vocabulary a Russian word crumbles into near-bytes: 7.773 tokens per word against 1.227 for English — a ×5.74 premium. Through XLM-R’s multilingual vocabulary (shared by BGE-M3 and mE5) it is 1.804 against 1.392, a ×1.17 premium. The cause is in the vocabulary: r50k holds 17 Cyrillic tokens and not one of them is two Cyrillic characters long.

The picture behind the previous slide's claim, measured on FLORES-200 (the same parallel corpus Petrov et al. used). GPT-2's r50k vocabulary spends 7.773 tokens on an average Russian word versus 1.227 on an English one — a 5.74x premium — because its vocabulary contains exactly 17 Cyrillic tokens, none of which spans two Cyrillic characters; Russian is therefore encoded essentially byte by byte. XLM-R's 250k vocabulary, which BGE-M3 and multilingual-E5 both inherit, spends 1.804 versus 1.392 — a 1.17x premium, roughly two pieces per word. Note o200k reaches 14,211 Cyrillic tokens: the tax is a vocabulary budget decision, not a property of the language. This is why an encoder that never allocated vocabulary to Russian cannot represent it well.
