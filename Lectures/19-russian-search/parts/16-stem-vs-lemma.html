Никакой из двух не «лучше» по определению: на слайде «Три прохода рядом» мы посчитаем оба на одном корпусе и увидим, что стемминг вытаскивает ответ из небытия, но ставит его позади щенка. Одно правило без исключений: тот инструмент, что стоит на индексе, стоит и на запросе.Neither is “better” by definition: on the slide “Three passes side by side” we measure both on one corpus and watch stemming rescue the answer from nothing — and still rank it behind a puppy. One rule with no exceptions: whichever tool sits on the index sits on the query.

The Russian-specific contrast — the generic stemming-vs-lemmatization table was L2's job (L2-16), so this slide does not repeat it. Two corrections over the previous version. First, the "context-aware: yes" cell for lemmatization was wrong as a blanket claim: pymorphy2/pymorphy3 are context-FREE by design (parse() returns a ranked list for an isolated word; [0] is the most probable parse, not a disambiguation). Context is used by Mystem's heuristics, spaCy ru_core_news, Stanza and Natasha — so the honest cell is "depends on the tool", and slide 20 splits the tools by that column. Second, the Russian failure mode of stemming is not a vague "conflation" but stem alternation: котёнок/котята/котят produce three different stems, which slide 18a then measures in BM25 points.
