Steps: the bare lemma, the unfolded 6x2 paradigm (12 slots, 10 distinct strings because nominative equals accusative in both numbers), the ten posting lists an un-normalized index creates, a query in a single form lighting exactly one of them (recall 1/10), lemmatization merging the ten lists into one with df = 10, and the same query reaching all ten (recall 10/10). The part-of-speech control is the sting: the verb has 16 forms and the adjective 18, so the ten in the table is a floor. This is the mechanism behind the whole of Act 2.
