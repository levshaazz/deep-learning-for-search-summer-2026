Yandex’s study of spelling errors in search queries, H1 2012: over 150M queries a day, roughly one in ten carries an error, roughly half of the errors are typos — layout slips included. Live numbers from the same study: “Таиланд” is misspelt with “й” 85 % of the time, “агенство” loses its “т” in about 25 % of attempts.
  • ·Лечение — и ловушка внутри лечения. Детерминированная карта ЙЦУКЕН↔QWERTY возвращает буквы. Но ё живёт на клавише `: «котёнок» набирается как rjn`yjr, а типичная опечатка даёт rjntyjr → «котенок». Раскладочная опечатка всегда теряет ё — после карты нужен ещё шаг ё→е.The cure — and the trap inside it. A deterministic ЙЦУКЕН↔QWERTY map restores the letters. But ё lives on the ` key: “котёнок” is typed rjn`yjr, while the ordinary slip gives rjntyjr → “котенок”. A layout typo always loses the ё — after the map you still owe a ё→е step.
Gotcha 4 — the keyboard-layout typo, a bilingual-keyboard failure mode. Typing Russian without switching layouts yields Latin gibberish on the same physical keys: ghbdtn → привет, rjntyjr buhftn → котенок играет. Unfixed, these queries return nothing. Scale, with a source at last: Yandex's H1-2012 study of spelling errors in search queries — over 150 million queries a day, roughly one in ten containing an error, roughly half of those errors typos, layout slips among them; "Таиланд" is misspelt with й 85 % of the time and "агенство" drops its т in about 25 % of attempts. Cure: a deterministic ЙЦУКЕН↔QWERTY key-position map. The trap inside the cure: ё sits on the backtick key, so a layout typo always strips it — котёнок is rjn`yjr but the slip produces rjntyjr → котенок, and a ё→е fold must follow the map.
