Дефолт для русского прод-поиска: лемматизированный BM25 + мультиязычный плотный. Каким способом их сливать — по скорам (так измерено на MIRACL-ru) или RRF из L12 (по-русски никем не замерено) — решай сам и меряй у себя.The default for Russian production search: lemmatized BM25 + a multilingual dense model. How to fuse them — by score (that is what MIRACL-ru measured) or by L12’s RRF (never measured in Russian) — is your call, and your measurement.

The practitioner's decision table, tying every act together. Keyword/exact-term search → BM25 + lemmatization (cheap, transparent, fixes forms). Semantic/paraphrase → multilingual-E5/BGE-M3 (meaning past morphology, no lemmatizer). Production best baseline → a hybrid of the lexical and the dense arm — exact terms and meaning together. The fusion RULE is deliberately not prescribed here: slide "Hybrid in Russian by the numbers" shows that MIRACL-ru's 0.532 comes from fusing BM25 and mDPR SCORES, and that L12's Reciprocal Rank Fusion has no published Russian evaluation at all — so recommending RRF by name would be claiming a number nobody has. Narrow Russian domain → a ru-specific model or fine-tuning. Cross-lingual ru↔en → a multilingual vector (BGE-M3 / mE5); LaBSE is a bitext-mining specialist and scores 31.53 on MTEB(rus, v1.1) Retrieval, so it is not the cross-lingual retriever to reach for.
