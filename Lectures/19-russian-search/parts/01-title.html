Lecture 19 — "Search in Russian", catchphrase "Morphology strikes back". What breaks when the corpus stops being English. It follows L18 (late chunking) — the chunk that now remembers its context still holds less Russian meaning per token — and hands off to L20 (advanced RAG, multimodal, ethics). Three spines: the token tax (BPE fragments Russian into many more pieces), lemmatization vs sub-words for BM25 (a fusional language means query and answer can share zero surface forms), and Russian / multilingual embedders (E5-multilingual, BGE-M3, ruMTEB). Séréga narrates with Tokenosaurus, who chokes on Russian words. Bilingual RU/EN — toggle with L.
