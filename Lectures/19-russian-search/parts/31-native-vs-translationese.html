Гипотеза поля, а не измерение A field hypothesis, not a measurement
  • ·Переводной язык (translationese) — русский, полученный переводом: порядок слов, обороты и лексика калькируют английский оригинал. Модель с сильным английским ловит кальку легче живого русского.Translationese is Russian produced by translation: word order, phrasing and lexicon echo the English source. A model with strong English priors catches the calque more easily than genuine Russian.
  • ·Честно: контролируемого исследования нет. Никто не сравнил «носитель против машинного перевода» на русском поиске при прочих равных. Косвенно: MMTEB (ICLR 2025) исключил машинно-переведённые наборы; MIRACL построен носителями по замыслу; а BM25 на переведённом mMARCO-ru даёт 15,25 — один из худших на RusBEIR (ниже него на RusBEIR только rus-SCIDOCS).Honestly: there is no controlled study. Nobody has compared native versus machine-translated Russian retrieval all else equal. Indirectly: MMTEB (ICLR 2025) excluded machine-translated sets; MIRACL was built by native speakers by design; and BM25 on translated mMARCO-ru scores 15.25 — its worst result across all of RusBEIR.
  • ·Оговорка и вывод. Это разные коллекции с разной длиной документа — сигнал наводящий, не причинный. Сами авторы mMARCO меряли связь BLEU с качеством поиска: R² ≈ 0,33. Выбирай модель на MIRACL/ruMTEB, а mMARCO держи для масштаба и обучения.The caveat and the takeaway. These are different collections with different document lengths — the signal is suggestive, not causal. mMARCO’s own authors measured how BLEU tracks retrieval quality: R² ≈ 0.33. Select models on MIRACL/ruMTEB; keep mMARCO for scale and training.

Урок Гудхарта из L5 в новом костюме: высокий балл на неверном распределении — не то, что ты хотел измерить.The L5 Goodhart lesson in a new coat: a high score on the wrong distribution is not what you meant to measure.

The translationese caveat, stated at the honesty level the evidence supports. Machine-translated Russian mirrors its English source syntactically, so an English-strong model can exploit the calque — that is the mechanism, and it is plausible. But there is no controlled native-vs-MT retrieval study for Russian, so present this as a field hypothesis, not a measurement. The three indirect supports: MMTEB (ICLR 2025) deliberately excluded machine-translated datasets and warned that even human translations bias toward English-trained models; MIRACL was built by native speakers on native corpora; and RusBEIR's BM25 baseline scores 15.25 on mMARCO-ru, its lowest across the whole benchmark. State the caveat aloud: those are different collections with different document lengths. mMARCO's own authors report R² ≈ 0.33 between BLEU and retrieval quality, i.e. translation fidelity explains a third of the variance. Practical rule unchanged: select on MIRACL/ruMTEB, train on mMARCO.
