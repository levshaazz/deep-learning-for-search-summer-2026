Счёт 3 мы уже посчитали: на MIRACL-ru гибрид обходит и BM25, и плотное плечо. Это единственное опубликованное русское число, которым можно оправдать фразу «гибрид — прод-база» — и потому важно, чего в нём НЕТ.

Bill 3 is already counted: on MIRACL-ru the hybrid beats both BM25 and the dense arm. It is the only published Russian number that can justify the phrase “hybrid is the production baseline” — which is why what it does NOT contain matters.

Но приписывать выигрыш RRF нельзя. MIRACL сливает скоры BM25 и mDPR; RusBEIR ставит второй проход кросс-энкодером. Ни то ни другое — не RRF из L12. Русского RRF-замера в литературе просто нет: незанятая клетка — вот тебе задача.

But the gain may not be attributed to RRF. MIRACL fuses BM25 and mDPR scores; RusBEIR adds a cross-encoder second pass. Neither is L12’s RRF. No Russian RRF measurement exists in the literature: an empty cell — there is your assignment.

Разница не педантская: слияние скоров требует калибровки шкал, RRF её не требует. Утверждать про русский можно только то, что кто-то измерил. The distinction is not pedantry: score fusion needs the two scales calibrated, RRF does not. About Russian you may claim only what somebody measured.

The evidence behind "hybrid wins in Russian", and its exact limits. MIRACL's Russian split reports 0.532 nDCG@10 for the fusion against 0.334 for BM25 and 0.407 for mDPR — that is the number the deck's recommendation rests on. The correction this slide exists for: the deck used to credit RRF, and RRF is not what was measured. MIRACL fuses raw scores from the two arms, which requires their scales to be comparable; RusBEIR's gain comes from a cross-encoder second pass, a different mechanism again. Reciprocal Rank Fusion from L12 has no published Russian evaluation at all. Offer that as a real assignment: run BM25-with-lemmatization plus a dense arm through RRF on MIRACL-ru and you have produced a number nobody has.
