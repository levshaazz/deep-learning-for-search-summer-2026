Кто платит и чем Who pays, and with what
  • ·Окно контекста. Больше токенов на слово → в тот же лимит влезает меньше смысла: русский документ тратит контекст быстрее английского.Context window. More tokens per word → less meaning fits the same limit: a Russian document spends the budget faster than an English one.
  • ·Цена и латентность. API берут за токены, а внимание растёт как \(O(n^2)\). Тот же ответ по-русски дороже и медленнее.Cost and latency. APIs bill per token, and attention grows as \(O(n^2)\). The same answer in Russian costs more and runs slower.
  • ·×15 — это не про русский. У Petrov et al. 2023 ×15 — максимум по всем языкам (шанский); русская строка живёт в приложении C. Измеренная премия русского — от ×5,74 (GPT-2) до ×1,42 (GPT-4o).×15 is not the Russian number. In Petrov et al. 2023 ×15 is the maximum across all languages (Shan); the Russian row lives in appendix C. Russia’s measured premium runs from ×5.74 (GPT-2) down to ×1.42 (GPT-4o).

То есть налог у русского реален, но конечен — и зависит не от языка, а от того, чей словарь ты взял. The fairness framing, told honestly. Petrov et al. 2023 ("Language Model Tokenizers Introduce Unfairness Between Languages", arXiv:2305.15425) report a worst case of about fifteen times English — but that maximum belongs to Shan, not to Russian; the Russian row is in the paper's appendix C and its companion repository. Stating "×15" next to a Russian lecture invites the student to read it as Russia's number, which is wrong by a factor of three or more. Russia's own measured premium spans ×5.74 on the GPT-2 vocabulary down to ×1.42 on GPT-4o's. The three costs are unchanged: less meaning per context window, per-token billing, quadratic attention. The cure is the vocabulary, not the language.
