The Map of Meaning is Drawn

Слова получили координаты, «близко» стало значить «похоже»,Words have coordinates, “near” now means “related”, и Сэр Косинус едет по этой карте.and Sir Cosine rides this map.

The payoff. We set out to give the First-Contact alien a place on the map, and we did — every word now has coordinates, learned from nothing but the company it keeps, and on those coordinates nearness means related meaning. Sir Cosine rides this map as its ruler. We earned the coordinates with word2vec, watched meaning become arithmetic, and folded three hundred dimensions down to a picture we can read. One thread remains loose: a word still has only one vector. Giving that map context is the next lecture.
