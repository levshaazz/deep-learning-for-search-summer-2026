Один вектор слова — это \(50\)–\(300\) вещественных координат. Близость, кластеры, направления — всё это есть в данных, но человеческий глаз привязан к 2–3 осям. Нам нужно свернуть карту, сохранив форму.A single word vector is \(50\)–\(300\) real coordinates. Nearness, clusters, directions — all of it is there in the data, but the human eye is stuck with 2–3 axes. We must fold the map while keeping its shape.

Распечатать вектор king — это 300 чисел в строку. Глазами в этом порядка не увидеть. Отсюда — задача снижения размерности.Printing the king vector is 300 numbers in a row. The eye sees no structure in that. Hence: dimensionality reduction.

Zoom out to the stakes. A production embedding gives each word fifty to three hundred real numbers. All the structure we just celebrated — the clusters, the gender and capital directions, the cosine nearness — genuinely lives in that high-dimensional space. But a human being can perceive at most three spatial dimensions, and on a slide really only two. So we are in the strange position of owning a perfect map we cannot look at. The rest of the lecture is about folding that map down to two dimensions while preserving as much of its shape as possible. That is dimensionality reduction.
