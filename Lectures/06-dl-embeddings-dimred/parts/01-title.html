The Map of Meaning

От символов к геометрии: плотные векторы слов (word2vec, GloVe), аналогии как арифметика, и инструменты, чтобы увидеть 300-мерное пространство — PCA и t-SNE.From symbols to geometry: dense word vectors (word2vec, GloVe), analogies as arithmetic, and the tools to see a 300-D space — PCA and t-SNE.

Albert Nasybullin

Week 4 · Session 2
Remember the alien from First Contact back in Lecture 2? Today it finally gets a place on the map. Up to now our words still lived as symbols — strings the engine matched but did not understand. Today we make the leap from symbols to geometry: every word becomes a point in a few-hundred-dimensional space, learned so that nearness equals relatedness. We will earn those coordinates with the word2vec training objective, watch meaning turn into arithmetic with king minus man plus woman, and then learn to fold a 300-D map down to a picture we can read — PCA and t-SNE. Sir Cosine, the ruler of nearness from L2, becomes the ruler of this whole map.
