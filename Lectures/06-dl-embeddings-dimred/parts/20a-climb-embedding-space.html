Steps: (0) words as points, with interpretable directions on this toy map; (1) the king − man + woman walk drawn as arrows — DWELL: the MOVE is the meaning, the destination is just where it lands; (2) the nearest-word reveal; (3) the same cosine ruler on ordinary pairs — near, far, unrelated. Do not oversell the magic: the caveat and where-it-fails slides come three positions later and will temper it — better to flag “this works embarrassingly often, and soon you’ll see where it doesn’t” yourself at step 2.
