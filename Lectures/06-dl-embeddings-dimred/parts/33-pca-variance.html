The exact PCA climb on our own data. We have forty-four words living in fifty dimensions, in seven semantic groups. The first principal component — the single direction of greatest spread — captures nineteen point six percent of the total variance. The second component, orthogonal to it, adds another eighteen point one percent. So a flat two-dimensional PCA picture retains thirty-seven point seven percent of the shape, the sum of those two, straight from the L6 dimred file. That is a lot for two axes out of fifty, but notice the flip side: nearly sixty-two percent of the structure is folded away. That residual is exactly why we will want a non-linear method next.

Коррелированное облако: оси экрана \(x,y,z\) не совпадают с направлениями разброса. Жми →.A correlated cloud: the screen axes \(x,y,z\) do not line up with how the data spreads. Press →.

2 · центрируем → ковариация C2 · centre → covariance C
\(\displaystyle C=\tfrac1n\sum_i (x_i-\bar x)(x_i-\bar x)^{\top}=\begin{bmatrix}6.1326 & 3.2196 & 1.0354\\ 3.2196 & 4.2826 & 1.2191\\ 1.0354 & 1.2191 & 0.6846\end{bmatrix}\)

Симметричная \(3\times3\): большие недиагональные члены = оси \(x,y,z\) коррелированы.Symmetric \(3\times3\): the large off-diagonal terms mean the \(x,y,z\) axes are correlated.

3 · собственные векторы C = оси3 · eigenvectors of C = axes
\(C v_k=\lambda_k v_k,\quad \lambda_1=8.8561\gt\lambda_2=1.934\gt\lambda_3=0.3097\)

Собственные векторы — главные направления; \(\lambda_k\) — дисперсия вдоль оси. Доли: \(\tfrac{8{,}8561}{11{,}0998}=79{,}79\%,\ \tfrac{1{,}934}{11{,}0998}=17{,}42\%,\ \tfrac{0{,}3097}{11{,}0998}=2{,}79\%\).The eigenvectors are the principal directions; each \(\lambda_k\) is the variance along that axis. Shares: \(\tfrac{8.8561}{11.0998}=79.79\%,\ \tfrac{1.934}{11.0998}=17.42\%,\ \tfrac{0.3097}{11.0998}=2.79\%\).

4 · поворот в базис собственных векторов4 · rotate into the eigenbasis
\(y=R^{\top}(x-\bar x),\quad R=[\,v_1\ v_2\ v_3\,]\)

\(R\) ортогональна (\(R^{\top}R=I\)) ⇒ это жёсткий поворот: длины и углы сохраняются, общая дисперсия не меняется \((\sum\lambda_k=11{,}0998)\).\(R\) is orthogonal \((R^{\top}R=I)\) ⇒ a rigid rotation: lengths and angles are preserved, total variance is unchanged \((\sum\lambda_k=11.0998)\).

5 · дисперсия по выровненным осям5 · variance on the aligned axes
\(\mathrm{Var}(\text{PC}_k)=\lambda_k:\ \ 79.79\%\ \gt\ 17.42\%\ \gt\ 2.79\%\)

Оси теперь лежат на экране; PC3 несёт лишь \(2{,}79\%\) разброса.The axes now lie on the screen; PC3 carries only \(2.79\%\) of the spread.

6 · проекция на топ-2 → сохранено6 · project onto top-2 → kept
\(\displaystyle \frac{\lambda_1+\lambda_2}{\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3}=\frac{8.8561+1.934}{11.0998}=\frac{10.7901}{11.0998}=\mathbf{97.21\%}\)

Отбрасываем PC3, оставляем \((\text{PC1},\text{PC2})\): сохранено \(97{,}21\%\) — это 3-D демо, не 50-мерные слова \((\to 37{,}7\%)\).Drop PC3, keep \((\text{PC1},\text{PC2})\): \(97.21\%\) is retained — this is the 3-D demo, not the 50-D words \((\to 37.7\%)\).

This is the geometry of PCA made visible, now stepped through the full pipeline on the real thirty-point demo cloud from the L6 data file. Step zero: the raw correlated three-D cloud, where the screen axes do not align with how the data spreads. Step one: we centre the cloud by subtracting the mean and form the three-by-three covariance matrix C — its large off-diagonal terms, three point two two and one point oh four, are exactly the correlation. Step two: the eigenvectors of C give three principal axes — PC1 along the greatest spread carrying seventy-nine point eight percent, PC2 seventeen point four, PC3 a thin two point eight. Step three: a rigid rotation — the whole cloud and its axes turn together, nothing stretched or sheared, total variance unchanged at eleven point one. Step four: fully aligned, the principal axes now lying on the screen, per-axis variance labelled. Step five: we drop the thin PC3 and land in two dimensions, keeping ninety-seven point two one percent of the spread. Keep this distinct from the thirty-seven point seven percent earlier — that was the fifty-dimensional GloVe words; this is the three-D demo where two axes capture almost everything. Every number is from the L6 PCA-rotate data file.
