Appendix, part two — the geometry and the folding tools. Levy and Goldberg 2014 prove word2vec is implicit matrix factorisation, unifying the predict and count families from Part 4. van der Maaten and Hinton 2008 is the t-SNE paper; the Distill article by Wattenberg, Viégas and Johnson 2016 is the canonical demonstration of the over-reading traps in Part 7; and McInnes and colleagues 2018 is UMAP. Karen Spärck Jones's idf from Lecture 3 is the count-based ancestor of all of this.
