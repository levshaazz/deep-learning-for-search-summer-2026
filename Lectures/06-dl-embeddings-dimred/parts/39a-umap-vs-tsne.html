Последняя строка — вся суть. UMAP быстрее и врёт о глобальной геометрии чуть меньше, но всё равно врёт: меняется цена, а не правило чтения.The last row is the point. UMAP is faster and lies about global geometry a little less — but it still lies: the cost changes, the reading rule does not.

UMAP was named on the act divider and in the references but never actually taught, which left students with the impression that it is the "fixed" t-SNE. It is not: it is a different trade-off. McInnes et al. 2018 build the map from a fuzzy topological representation rather than a t-distribution over pairwise affinities, which buys real speed, an out-of-sample transform, and somewhat better global arrangement — but the three traps from the previous slide survive almost intact. Cluster sizes still mean nothing, between-cluster distance is still not a ruler, and n_neighbors moves the picture the way perplexity does. Deliver the last row as the takeaway: swapping the algorithm changes what you pay, never what you are allowed to conclude.
