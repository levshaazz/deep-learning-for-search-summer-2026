Sir Cosine, ruler of the map · symbols → geometry
My catchphrase for the day, and Sir Cosine speaks it. Until now words were symbols: the engine could check if two strings were equal, but had no notion of one word being close to another. The entire project of this lecture is to give each word a position in space, learned from data, so that the geometric notion of nearness lines up with the human notion of related meaning. Cosine similarity is the ruler we use to read that nearness — that is why Sir Cosine, from Lecture 2, rules this map.
