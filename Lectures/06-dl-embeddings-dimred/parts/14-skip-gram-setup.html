Для каждого центрального слова \(w\) предсказываем слова контекста \(c\) в окне радиуса \(m\). У каждого слова две роли: вектор-центр \(v_w\) и вектор-контекст \(u_c\).For each centre word \(w\), predict the context words \(c\) within a window of radius \(m\). Each word has two roles: a centre vector \(v_w\) and a context vector \(u_c\).

«the quick brown fox jumps» с \(m=2\): центр brown, контекст {the, quick, fox, jumps}. Это четыре обучающих пары из одного шага окна.“the quick brown fox jumps” with \(m=2\): centre brown, context {the, quick, fox, jumps}. Four training pairs from one window step.

Set up the skip-gram model before the formula. We slide a window over the corpus. At each position one word is the centre and the words around it, within radius m, are its contexts. The task is to predict those context words from the centre. Crucially every word carries two vectors: one used when it is the centre, written v sub w, and one used when it is a context word, written u sub c. Keeping them separate makes the math clean and the dot products meaningful. One window position over brown yields four centre-context training pairs.
