Гремлин загнан, но не пойман.Cornered, not caged. Одно слово — много смыслов. Чтобы дать слову контекст и развести смыслы, нужны контекстные эмбеддинги → это L7.One word, many meanings. To give a word context and split the senses, we need contextual embeddings → that is L7.

The Gremlin callback, and the honest limit. Big win first: the lexical gap that beat BM25 is largely closed. Couch and sofa now have nearly the same vector, because they share contexts, so a query for one retrieves the other. The Gremlin's wall from Lecture 3 has cracked — synonyms land together. But do not cage him yet. Every word still gets exactly one vector, so a polysemous word like bank — river bank versus financial bank — is forced into a single point that blends both senses. The fix is to make a word's vector depend on its sentence, which is contextual embeddings, and that is precisely the climb of Lecture 7. Cornered, not caged.
