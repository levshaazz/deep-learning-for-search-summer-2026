Интуиция: тень 3-D объекта на стену. PCA выбирает тот угол освещения, при котором тень теряет меньше всего информации о форме.Intuition: the shadow of a 3-D object on a wall. PCA picks the lighting angle whose shadow loses the least information about the shape.

Introduce PCA conceptually before the algebra. Principal Component Analysis looks for the orthogonal directions along which the data varies the most, calls them principal components, and projects everything onto the first few. The shadow analogy is the one to hold: a three-dimensional object casts a two-dimensional shadow, and the informativeness of that shadow depends entirely on the angle of the light. PCA chooses the angle that throws the most faithful shadow — the projection that preserves the most variance. It is linear, fast, and the workhorse first look at any high-dimensional dataset.
