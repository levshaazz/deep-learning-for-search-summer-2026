Текст, изображение, звук и белок режутся на токены каждый по-своему, но дальше рецепт общий: закодировать каждый кусок и положить точку в одно пространство. Именно поэтому подпись и картинка, которую она описывает, могут оказаться соседями, а кроссмодальный поиск вообще возможен.The table from the previous slide — now as one picture. Text, an image, audio and a protein are each cut into tokens in their own way, but from there the recipe is shared: embed every piece and drop it as a point into one space. That is exactly why a caption and the picture it describes can end up as neighbours, and why cross-modal search is possible at all.

The climb of the “same trick everywhere” act, mounted right after the fields table so the mechanism follows the claim instead of preceding it. Steps: (0) four inputs that could not look less alike — ask the room what they could possibly share; (1) the cut into tokens, one row per modality (word / patch / spectrogram frame / residue) — this is the step that carries the lecture’s thesis, DWELL; (2) the vectors land in ONE plane, on shared axes, measured by the same ruler; (3) axes + legend, and the payoff: image↔text retrieval is not a separate technology, it is this picture. The toy coordinates are illustrative — say so; the claim being made is about the RECIPE, not about any particular position. Bridges straight into the closing act.
