PCA как поворот: покрути облако самPCA as a rotation: turn the cloud yourself

шаг (←/→): облако в 3-D → собственные оси → поворот → оси совпали → сплющитьstep (←/→): the 3-D cloud → its own axes → the turn → axes aligned → flatten

Ковариация и собственные векторы с прошлого слайда — теперь буквально: возьми облако точек и поверни его так, чтобы его собственные оси легли на оси экрана. Поворот жёсткий — ни одно расстояние между точками не растянуто и не сжато. Потом сплющи самую тонкую ось и посмотри, какая доля разброса переживёт падение размерности; следующий слайд посчитает её на наших словах.The covariance and eigenvectors from the previous slide — now literally: take the point cloud and turn it until its own axes lie along the screen’s. The turn is rigid — not one distance between points is stretched or squeezed. Then flatten the thinnest axis and see what share of the spread survives the drop in dimension; the next slide computes that share on our words.

The climb of the cartographer act, between the PCA-math slide and the variance-on-our-words slide. The cloud, eigenvectors, rotation frames and variance shares are all read from data/l5-pca-rotate.json — the browser replays a precomputed rotation, it does not diagonalise anything. Steps: (0) the correlated 3-D cloud; (1) its own axes drawn in — the eigenvectors from the previous slide made visible; (2) the rigid rotation — DWELL: rotation preserves every pairwise distance, this kills the “PCA distorts the data” misconception before it forms; (3) axes aligned, variance neatly split; (4) dropping the thin axis — the flatten. Students stumble on rotation-then-projection being all PCA is; the physicality of steps 2–4 is the cure. Hand over to the next slide with “so how much survives on real words?”.
