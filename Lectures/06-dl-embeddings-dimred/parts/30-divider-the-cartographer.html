Enter the Cartographer

A new minor character joins the cast: the Cartographer, whose one job is to fold the high-dimensional map down to something we can carry and read. The tell is a cartographer creasing a vast star-map along careful lines. The art is in which creases to make. A good fold keeps the overall shape — which points are near which — while collapsing the directions that do not matter. We will meet two folding styles: PCA, which keeps the directions of greatest spread, and t-SNE, which instead protects each point's local neighbourhood.
