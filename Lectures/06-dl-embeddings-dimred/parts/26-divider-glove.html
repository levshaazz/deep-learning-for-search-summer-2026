GloVe & the Curse of 300-D

Вторая дорога к той же карте,A second road to the same map, а потом — отрезвление: карта живёт в 300 измерениях, которые нельзя увидеть.and then the sobering fact: the map lives in 300 dimensions you cannot see.

Two short ideas in this part. First, GloVe — a second, count-based route to the same geometry, which reassures us the map is real and not an artefact of one algorithm. Second, the catch that motivates the rest of the lecture: these vectors live in fifty to three hundred dimensions, and humans can only see two or three. We have a beautiful map we cannot look at. That tension is what brings the Cartographer on stage to fold it.
