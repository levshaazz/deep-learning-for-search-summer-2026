t-SNE в движении: точки расходятся по островамt-SNE in motion: the points drift apart into islands

шаг (←/→): старт с PCA → дрейф → кластеры стягиваются → карта устояласьstep (←/→): the PCA start → the drift → the clusters tighten → the map settles

Градиент с прошлого слайда — не формула на бумаге, а движение. Смотри, как одно и то же облако, стартовав с PCA-раскладки, само расходится на острова: каждая точка тянется к своим настоящим соседям. А промежутки между островами растут просто потому, что так удобнее уложиться на плоскости, — и ровно поэтому мерить их линейкой нельзя.The gradient from the previous slide is not a formula on paper — it is motion. Watch the same cloud, started from the PCA layout, pull itself apart into islands: every point drifts toward its true neighbours. The gaps between islands widen only because that is a convenient way to fit on a plane — and that is exactly why you must not measure them with a ruler.

The curved-map act’s second climb, mounted straight after the t-SNE mechanics (affinities → Student-t tail → KL gap → gradient) so the arrows they just met are shown DOING something. Steps: (0) the PCA init — nothing has moved yet, the colours are jumbled, say out loud that this is the STARTING picture, not a result; (1) the first drift — DWELL here, the trails are the point: each segment is one point walking toward its own neighbours; (2) the clusters tighten and the gaps open; (3) settled — and land the caveat verbatim: the gaps and the cluster sizes are NOT distances. That sentence is the seed of the very next act (the t-SNE mirage), and this is the slide where students first SEE why: the gaps were produced by the layout, not by the data. The finished 2-D word map on the following slide is now read as “the end of this migration”, not as a photograph of the space.
