The Map of Meaning

Помнишь пришельца из Первого контакта?Remember the alien from First Contact? Сегодня он наконец получает место на карте.Today it finally gets a place on the map.

Cold open, callback to Lecture 2. In First Contact we met an alien language with no shared dictionary — symbols we could match but not understand. The whole lecture is the answer to that: we are going to draw a map where every word is a point, and where being near on the map means being near in meaning. By the end the alien — and every word — has a position. This is the Map of Meaning, and Sir Cosine is its ruler: nearness on this map is cosine similarity.
