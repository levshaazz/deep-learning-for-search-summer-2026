«Эти два кластера далеко на графике ⇒ они далеки по смыслу».“These two clusters are far apart on the plot ⇒ they are far apart in meaning.”

Нет. t-SNE сохраняет локальное соседство, а не глобальные расстояния. Три ловушки: (1) размер кластера не отражает разброс; (2) расстояние между кластерами почти ничего не значит; (3) перплексия всё меняет — при \(5\) и при \(30\) картинки разные. Правило.Rule. Читай t-SNE как «кто рядом с кем», и никогда — как линейку расстояний.Read t-SNE as “who is near whom”, never as a ruler of distances.

The specific traps, the P7 anti-over-reading beat. Trap one: cluster sizes are meaningless — t-SNE expands dense regions and contracts sparse ones, so a big blob is not a high-variance group. Trap two: the distance between two clusters is not reliable — two clusters drawn far apart can be closer in the original space than two drawn near. Trap three, the big one: perplexity changes the picture qualitatively; at perplexity five you may see many tiny islands, at thirty they may merge, and neither is the truth on its own — and perplexity must stay below the point count, so fifty would not even run on our forty-four words. The discipline is simple — read it as a neighbourhood claim, run several perplexities, and never quote a distance off a t-SNE plot.
