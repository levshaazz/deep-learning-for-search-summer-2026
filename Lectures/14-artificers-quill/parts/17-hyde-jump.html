HyDE by hand — the cosine jump

step (←/→): the raw query (trap beats gold) → the quill drafts an answer → embed it → gold jumps to rank 1

Raw query: the lexical trap (cos 0.45) outranks the gold passage (cos 0.22). HyDE embeds a hypothetical answer written in the documents' vocabulary — the gold jumps to cos 0.63 (rank 1) and the trap drops to 0.16.

hyde-embed (l14-rewrite): the cosine jump is the lecture's central visual. Two number lines (0 far → 1 identical). Raw query — trap 0.45 > gold 0.22 (gold buried). HyDE pseudo-doc — gold 0.63 > trap 0.16 (gold rank 1). WHY: the pseudo-answer lives in document-space (shares the docs' register); the raw question lives in question-space. Cosines are set-overlap on the toy — ordering, not magnitude.
