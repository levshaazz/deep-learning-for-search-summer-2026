A chain is as weak as its weakest hop

worked numeric climb ⊗ · the price of feed-forward
Definition · compose success
$$ P_{\text{compose}} = p^{\,n} $$

Least-to-most feeds each hop's answer into the next. If any hop is wrong, the error propagates — every downstream hop inherits it. Per-hop success \(p\), \(n\) hops, and the successes must all land.

Worked ⊗ · three hops at p = 0.90

$$ P_{\text{compose}} = 0.90^{\,3} = 0.90 \times 0.90 \times 0.90 = 0.73 $$

  • ·Each hop looks reliable — 90%.
  • ·Chained three deep, the plan succeeds only 73% of the time.
  • ·Depth is not free: more hops → \(p^{\,n}\) decays.

The same feed-forward dependency that recovers recall on compound queries is what propagates a single wrong hop. Decompose only when the depth pays.
