Common misconception

Bust the myth · diversity > count
What students think

“More paraphrases always help — so throw ten at every query and recall keeps climbing.”

What lifts recall is diversity, not count. Redundant paraphrases — the same facet in near-identical words — add cost, not coverage: they retrieve the passages you already had.

The bill.
(a) Latency: N paraphrases means N× retrieval — the slowest path gates the response.
(b) $ / calls: one LLM generation for the paraphrases, then N retrievals; redundant variants pay this for zero new hits.
(c) Design rule: spend your N on different facets and vocabulary, then stop — extra copies are pure overhead.
