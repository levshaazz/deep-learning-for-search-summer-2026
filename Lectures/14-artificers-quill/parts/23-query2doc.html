Query2doc (Wang EMNLP 2023) = HyDE's sparse twin: instead of replacing q, concatenate the LLM pseudo-doc to q and feed q' to BM25 or a dense retriever — reusing the same draft. Reported +3–15%, LARGEST on sparse BM25 / zero-shot (new matchable terms), small on a strong fine-tuned dense retriever. Same boundary, mirrored; cost 1 LLM call.
