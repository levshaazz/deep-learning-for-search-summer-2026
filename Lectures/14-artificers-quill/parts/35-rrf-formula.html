RRF: score a document by its ranks alone

rank-based, score-agnostic · Cormack, SIGIR 2009
Definition · reciprocal rank fusion
$$ s(d) = \sum_{i} \frac{1}{k + r_i(d)},\qquad k = 60 $$

\(r_i(d)\) = the rank of \(d\) in list \(i\); \(k=60\) damps the influence of any one top hit. Sum over all lists; sort by \(s(d)\).

  • ✓Only ranks enter — raw similarity scores never appear.
  • ✓Here (same-retriever paraphrases): the win is no per-query score calibration + robustness to any one paraphrase's noise.
  • ·The “incomparable score scales” argument is the L3 hybrid case (BM25 vs cosine) — not this one.
