RRF by hand — consensus beats a single love

worked numeric climb ⊗ · k = 60, three paraphrase rankings
$$ \text{gold ranks across the 3 paraphrases:}\quad [\,3,\ 1,\ 2\,] $$ $$ s(\text{gold}) = \frac{1}{60+3} + \frac{1}{60+1} + \frac{1}{60+2} = \frac{1}{63} + \frac{1}{61} + \frac{1}{62} \approx 0.0484 $$ $$ \text{one paraphrase that loves it (rank 1 only):}\quad s = \frac{1}{60+1} = \frac{1}{61} \approx 0.0164 $$

0.0484 > 0.0164. Three phrasings that agree on the gold (ranks 3, 1, 2) beat one phrasing that ranks it first alone. Consensus across paraphrases wins — and \(k=60\) keeps any single rank-1 hit from dominating.

Worked RRF, k=60. Gold sits at ranks [3,1,2] across three paraphrases → 1/63+1/61+1/62 ≈ 0.0484, which beats a lone rank-1 hit 1/61 ≈ 0.0164. The lesson: agreement across diverse phrasings outweighs one enthusiastic list; k=60 damps any single top hit so consensus can accumulate.
