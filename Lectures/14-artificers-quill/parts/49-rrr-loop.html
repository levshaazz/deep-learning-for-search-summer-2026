The apprenticeship loop
  • 1Rewrite. The small rewriter\(_\theta\) samples \(\hat q \sim \pi_\theta(q)\).
  • 2Retrieve. The frozen retriever fetches passages for \(\hat q\).
  • 3Read. The frozen reader produces an answer.
  • 4Reward. Score the answer — \(R = 1\) if correct.
  • 5Update. A policy-gradient step nudges \(\theta\) toward rewrites that earned reward. The retriever and reader never change.
