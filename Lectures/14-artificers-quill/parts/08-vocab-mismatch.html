Definition · vocabulary mismatch
A relevant document need not contain any query term — and colloquial phrasing under-matches even a dense encoder.

Formally, relevance lives in meaning, but the score reads surface form:

$$ q = \{\text{sour, watery, espresso}\},\quad d_{\text{gold}} = \{\text{under-extraction, channeling}\} $$ $$ q \cap d_{\text{gold}} = \varnothing \;\Rightarrow\; \text{no lexical bridge} $$

where q = the set of query terms, dgold = the terms of the truly-relevant passage, ∩ = shared terms, ∅ = the empty set (no overlap).
