Step-back: climb to retrieve, descend to answer

Zheng et al., ICLR 2024 · abstract up to the principle, then reason back down
Definition · step-back prompting
specific case \(\;\rightarrow\;\) generic question \(\;\rightarrow\;\) retrieve principle \(\;\rightarrow\;\) answer
  • ·Climb. Prompt the LLM: “what governs espresso extraction and sourness?” — drop the 18g / 58mm / 22s constraints.
  • ·Retrieve. The generic question matches the principle the corpus actually states.
  • ·Descend. Reason the retrieved principle back onto the specific case.
The move · change altitude, not words

HyDE and RM3 stay at the query's level — they swap or add vocabulary. Step-back generalizes: it trades a case the corpus never wrote for a rule it did.

Cost: 1 LLM call to write the generic question.

Step-back (Zheng, ICLR 2024): prompt the LLM to abstract the over-specific query UP to a generic principle-level question, retrieve the principle, then reason back DOWN to the specific case. "Climb to retrieve, descend to answer." Distinct from HyDE/RM3, which stay at the query's altitude and change vocabulary. Cost: 1 call.
