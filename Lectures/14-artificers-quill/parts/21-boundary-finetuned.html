Boundary #2 · HyDE is a low-resource move

when NOT to reach for the quill
The tempting belief

“HyDE lifts my unsupervised baseline, so bolting it onto my strong fine-tuned retriever must help too.”

HyDE is a zero-shot / low-resource move. Against a strong fine-tuned dense retriever — one that already learned the vocabulary from labels — HyDE often underperforms: it adds a hallucinated middle-man where the retriever no longer needs one.

When NOT to HyDE. · you have relevance labels and a fine-tuned retriever · entity-centric queries (a name, an ID) — the draft drifts off the entity · and always at a cost of 1 LLM call per query (latency + $). Reach for it when you have no labels, not to top up a supervised model.
