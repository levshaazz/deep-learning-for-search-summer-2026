Up is not sideways

abstraction generalizes · decomposition atomizes — two orthogonal moves
UP · abstraction (step-back)
  • ·Make the question more generic — drop constraints to reach the principle.
  • ·Cures the specificity gap: a case no chunk holds becomes a rule one does.
SIDEWAYS · decomposition (Act 4)
  • ·Split into more atomic sub-questions — same altitude, several aims.
  • ·Cures compositionality: no single chunk answers the whole.
Boundary · when the climb hurts
  • ·Over-abstraction drops the constraint. Climb too high and the specific answer (18g, 22s) is lost — you must descend to reattach it.
  • ·Already-generic query? The step-back call is wasted — there is no altitude left to climb.

Diagnose the gap first: climb for a missing rule, split for a missing composition.

Abstraction (UP, more generic) ≠ decomposition (SIDEWAYS, more atomic) — orthogonal, cure different gaps (specificity vs compositionality). Boundaries: over-abstraction drops the specific constraint, so you must descend to reattach it; and an already-generic query wastes the step-back call. Diagnose the gap before choosing the move.
