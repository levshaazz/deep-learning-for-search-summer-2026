Two places to intervene — before or after the retriever

L10's aiming stage · today we sharpen the aim, not the archive
Pre-retrieval · fix the query (THIS lecture)
Change what we ask before the index ever runs.
  • ·rewrite — replace the query with a better-aimed one.
  • ·expand — add terms the query was missing.
  • ·decompose — split a compound question into parts.
  • ·step-back — generalize a too-specific query.
Post-retrieval · fix the results (L7 / L10)
Change what we keep after candidates come back.
  • ·rerank — reorder the top-k with a stronger scorer.
  • ·compress — distil passages to what the answer needs.

L10 called query→retrieve the aiming stage. A better reranker cannot fix an aim that never found the target.

The RAG loop has two intervention points. PRE-retrieval (today): rewrite/expand/decompose/step-back — change the query before the index runs. POST-retrieval (L7/L10): rerank/compress — reorder or shrink what came back. Callback to L10's aiming stage: a query problem cannot be fixed downstream.
