RAG-Fusion = multi-query + RRF

rank-fusion vs score-fusion · where each genuinely bites
The recipe

RAG-Fusion: paraphrase the query into N variants, retrieve each, then fuse the lists with RRF. Diverse queries in, one ranking out.

fusion by…when it wins
rank (RRF)robustness to a noisy paraphrase; no score calibration
score (weighted sum)needs comparable scales — breaks on BM25 vs cosine

The L3 hybrid (BM25 vs cosine) is where incomparable score scales genuinely bite. Here, over same-retriever paraphrases, the payoff is robustness.

Name the recipe: RAG-Fusion = multi-query + RRF. Contrast rank-fusion (RRF) with score-fusion (weighted sum): score-fusion needs comparable scales, which is exactly why the L3 BM25-vs-cosine hybrid is the case where incomparable scales bite. For same-retriever paraphrases the RRF win is robustness, not scale-mismatch.
