Rule of the tree: spend the smallest move that closes the gap you actually have. Don't decompose a single-fact query; don't step-back an already-generic one.

The takeaway artifact: a diagnosis-to-move tree on a cost/label axis. Vocabulary → HyDE/expand (1 call); too-specific → step-back (1 call); multi-facet → multi-query+RRF (N calls); multi-hop → decompose (1+hops); if you have labels and a latency budget, train a rewriter (Rewrite-Retrieve-Read). Match the cheapest move to the gap you actually diagnosed.
