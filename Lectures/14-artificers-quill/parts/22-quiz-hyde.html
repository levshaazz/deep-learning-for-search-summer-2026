Reveal explanation
B. A fine-tuned retriever has already learned the vocabulary from labels — the gap HyDE fills is mostly closed. Bolted on, HyDE tends to underperform here and risks hallucination drift, while adding a flat cost of 1 LLM call per query. HyDE is a zero-shot / low-resource move; rank 2 from a supervised model is better improved by reranking or better negatives (L13), not by a pseudo-doc.
