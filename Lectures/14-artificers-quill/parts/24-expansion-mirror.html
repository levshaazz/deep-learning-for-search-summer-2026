Two faces of prompted expansion. Query side (Jagerman 2023): prompt-variant expansion — Q2D (HyDE-style), chain-of-thought, few-shot — all growing q at query time. Doc side mirror (Doc2Query, Nogueira 2019): predict the queries a doc answers and append them at INDEX time, so there's zero query-time latency. Same vocabulary bridge, built from opposite ends.
