Why it works — the draft lands in document-space

Sir Cosine · question-space vs document-space
Question-space
The raw query \(q\) — interrogative, colloquial, thin.
  • ·“why does my espresso taste sour and watery?”
  • ×Nearest neighbours are look-alikes: the sour candy trap.
Document-space
The pseudo-doc — declarative, shares the docs’ register & vocabulary.
  • ·Speaks under-extraction, channeling — the archive’s words.
  • ✓So its nearest neighbour is the gold passage, not the trap.

The quill doesn’t make the query smarter — it moves the query into the space where the answers live.

Sir Cosine's conceptual why: the raw question sits in question-space (interrogative, colloquial) whose neighbours are surface look-alikes like the sour-candy trap; the pseudo-doc sits in document-space, sharing the docs' register and vocabulary, so its nearest neighbour is the gold passage.
