Перо не делает запрос умнее — оно переносит запрос в пространство, где живут ответы.The quill doesn’t make the query smarter — it moves the query into the space where the answers live.

Sir Cosine's conceptual why: the raw question sits in question-space (interrogative, colloquial) whose neighbours are surface look-alikes like the sour-candy trap; the pseudo-doc sits in document-space, sharing the docs' register and vocabulary, so its nearest neighbour is the gold passage.
