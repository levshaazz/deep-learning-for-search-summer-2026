The cost/quality frontier: three quills

who pays, and when — training-time vs inference-time
rewriter flexibility inference cost needs where it wins
classical PRF (RM3) low — corpus terms only free · 0 LLM calls nothing cheap lexical lift; can’t invent new vocabulary
prompted LLM (HyDE…) high — world knowledge $$ · latency, 1 LLM call each an instruction-tuned generator zero-shot, no labels; but frozen & generic
trained small rewriter (RRR) medium — tuned to your pipeline cheap at inference (small model) training data + a reward + RL high volume; recoups training over many queries

No free lunch: PRF is free but weak; the prompted LLM is flexible but pays per query; the trained rewriter is cheap at inference but pays up front in labels and training.

The cost/quality frontier: classical PRF (free, weak, corpus-bound), prompted LLM (flexible via world knowledge but $$ and latency per query, needs an instruction-tuned generator), trained small rewriter (cheap at inference, tuned to your pipeline, but needs training data + reward + RL up front). The axis is where the cost lands — per-query vs up-front.
