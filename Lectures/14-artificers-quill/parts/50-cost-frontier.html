Бесплатных обедов нет: PRF бесплатен, но слаб; промптованная LLM гибка, но платит за каждый запрос; обученный переписыватель дёшев на инференсе, но платит заранее метками и обучением.No free lunch: PRF is free but weak; the prompted LLM is flexible but pays per query; the trained rewriter is cheap at inference but pays up front in labels and training.

The cost/quality frontier: classical PRF (free, weak, corpus-bound), prompted LLM (flexible via world knowledge but $$ and latency per query, needs an instruction-tuned generator), trained small rewriter (cheap at inference, tuned to your pipeline, but needs training data + reward + RL up front). The axis is where the cost lands — per-query vs up-front.
