Measurable outcomes, part one. First: state precisely why a static embedding cannot represent a polysemous word — one point must average both senses. Second: compute scaled dot-product attention on a small matrix by hand, and explain the scaling by root d k as a fix for softmax saturation. Third: assemble the full encoder block from its pieces and justify each — residual connections for gradient flow, LayerNorm for stable activations, the position-wise feed-forward network for per-token transformation.
