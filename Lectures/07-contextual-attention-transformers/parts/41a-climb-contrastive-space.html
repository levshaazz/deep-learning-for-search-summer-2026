Косинусы с прошлого слайда — теперь как движение. Функция потерь не «оценивает» пространство со стороны, а тянет его: позитив едет к якорю, негативы разъезжаются к краю. После шага «близко» наконец начинает значить «релевантно» — и негативы для этого шага уже лежат рядом, в том же батче.The cosines from the previous slide — now as motion. The loss does not “score” the space from outside, it pulls on it: the positive travels toward the anchor while the negatives slide out to the rim. After the step, “near” finally starts to mean “relevant” — and the negatives for that step are already lying next door, in the same batch.

The climb of the retrieval-training act, mounted right after the cosine table so the same pairs are re-used as motion rather than as new material. The anchor, every cosine, the InfoNCE readout and the triplet readout come from data/l6-contrastive.json. Steps: (0) the off-the-shelf space, positives and negatives already mixed; (1) Sir Cosine measures each pair — the ordering is nearly right already, which is exactly why students think nothing needs fixing; (2) the forces, drawn but not yet applied — DWELL: the loss IS a gradient, an arrow per point, and the arrows are what training does; (3) the step, the dots landing at their trained positions; (4) both losses read out of the same cosines, so triplet and InfoNCE are visibly two readings of one picture. Close on the last line of step 4 — the negatives came from the batch for free — and walk straight into the next slide.
