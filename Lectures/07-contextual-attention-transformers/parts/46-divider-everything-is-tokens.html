Тот же блок — за пределами текста:The same block — beyond text: пиксели, картинки, аудио, белки, графы. Внимание оказалось универсальным.pixels, images, audio, proteins, graphs. Attention turned out to be universal.

A short cross-domain coda — placed just before the payoff so the Gremlin is the last content beat. The remarkable thing about the encoder block we just built is how little of it is about language. Tokenise anything into a sequence of vectors and the same machinery — attention, residuals, LayerNorm, the feed-forward network — applies. The next three slides make that concrete: images as patches in a Vision Transformer, image-and-text aligned by the very same InfoNCE loss in CLIP, and finally the unifying view that a Transformer is just a graph neural network on a fully-connected graph. The point for this course: contextual encoders and contrastive training are a general recipe for turning any modality into a searchable vector space — which brings us all the way home to the creature this whole journey was about.
