Формула с прошлого слайда — теперь как картинка. Строка сетки и есть подпись позиции: двух одинаковых строк нет, поэтому порядок слов перестаёт теряться. А по столбцам видно разделение труда: левые колонки дрожат быстро и различают соседей, правые еле шевелятся и держат общий план.The formula from the previous slide — now as a picture. A row of the grid IS the signature of a position: no two rows are alike, so word order stops getting lost. And across the columns you can see the division of labour: the left columns wobble fast and tell neighbours apart, the right ones barely move and hold the coarse view.

The climb of the position act, between the sinusoidal formula and the by-hand grid reading. Every cell comes from data/l6-positional.json — the same grid the next slide walks through by hand, so this is the picture that makes that walkthrough feel like confirmation. Steps: (0) the grid as a whole, one row per position, one column per dimension; (1) read it ROW-wise — each position gets a unique signature, which is the entire point of the construction; (2) read it COLUMN-wise — DWELL: this is the idea students miss, that the dimensions are a ladder of wavelengths from fine to coarse, not an undifferentiated blob of sines; (3) closed form, bounded values, extends past the training lengths. The stumbling block is “why sines at all” — answer it at step 2 with the ladder, not with the formula. The next slide reads a couple of rows out loud; the one after weighs fixed against learned.
