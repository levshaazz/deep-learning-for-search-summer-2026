Кривая слева утверждала \(O(n^2)\) — схема её считает. Чаша стягивает \(d\) и тем самым рождает ось \(n\times n\); её оплетают головы; сводка выставляет счёт: 12 голов при \(n=512\) — 6,3 МБ, при 4096 — 403 МБ, при 32768 — 25,8 ГБ. Форма схемы не менялась — менялось только \(n\).The curve asserted \(O(n^2)\) — the circuit computes it. The cup contracts \(d\) and thereby births the \(n\times n\) axis; the head broadcast wraps it; the ledger bills it: 12 heads at \(n=512\) is 6.3 MB, at 4096 it is 403 MB, at 32768 it is 25.8 GB. The picture never changed shape — only \(n\) did.

Step zero: the token wire is projected into Q, K and V by three learned boxes, and Q and K meet at a cup. Nothing in the formula warns you about it, and that axis is the entire quadratic cost. Step one: the softmax triangle, opened across the key axis, and note what does not happen — the shape does not change, n by n in, n by n out. Softmax is free; the damage was done at the cup. Step two: V arrives, a second cup contracts the key axis, and the big matrix collapses back to a compact context. The whole life of the expensive axis is on one picture — born at the first cup, killed at the second. Step three is the bill. The head and batch axes are broadcasts, drawn as wires woven around the whole expression, and a weave multiplies whatever it wraps — which is the n by n axis. Twelve heads at five hundred and twelve tokens is six point three megabytes; at four thousand ninety-six it is four hundred and three megabytes; at thirty-two thousand seven hundred and sixty-eight it is twenty-five point eight gigabytes. The diagram never changed shape; only n did. That is what the wall on the previous slide actually feels like.
