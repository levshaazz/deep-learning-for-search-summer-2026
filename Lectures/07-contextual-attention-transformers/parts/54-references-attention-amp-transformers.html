Appendix, part one — the architecture lineage. Vaswani and colleagues 2017 is the Transformer paper itself, the source of scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention and the sinusoidal positional encoding. Devlin and colleagues 2019 is BERT, the bidirectional encoder that made contextual embeddings mainstream and gave us [CLS]. Ba, Kiros and Hinton 2016 is layer normalization, the Norm in every Add and Norm. He and colleagues 2016 is ResNet, the residual connection that lets a deep stack train at all.
