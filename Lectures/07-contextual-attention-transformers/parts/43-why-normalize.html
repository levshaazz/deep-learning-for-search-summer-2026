«Можно просто брать скалярное произведение сырых векторов».“Just use the raw dot product of the vectors.”

Сырое скалярное произведение путает направление (смысл) с длиной (часто — частотность). Если нормировать векторы на единичную длину, скалярное произведение превращается в косинус — чистый угол. Контрастная цель с косинусом раскладывает векторы по сфере, где близость = смысл.A raw dot product confuses direction (meaning) with length (often frequency). Normalising vectors to unit length turns the dot product into cosine — pure angle. A cosine contrastive objective lays vectors on a sphere where nearness = meaning.

Урок.Lesson. Нормализация + косинус = та же линейка, что в L6. Сэр Косинус правит и здесь.Normalise + cosine = the same ruler as L6. Sir Cosine rules here too.

A short conceptual beat that ties the loss back to the ruler. Why not just use the raw dot product of two contextual vectors? Because the dot product mixes two things: the angle between the vectors, which carries meaning, and their magnitudes, which often just track word frequency or sentence length. Normalising every vector to unit length removes the magnitude, and the dot product becomes pure cosine — the angle alone. Training contrastively on cosine arranges all the embeddings on the surface of a unit sphere, where being near means being similar in meaning, which is exactly what retrieval wants. It is the same Sir Cosine ruler from Lecture 6, now baked into the training objective rather than only used at query time.
