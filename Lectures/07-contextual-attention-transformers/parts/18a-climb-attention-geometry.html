Тот же разобранный пример — только увиденный глазами. Веса внимания здесь не абстрактные числа, а сила притяжения: запрос сходит со своей точки и садится во взвешенное среднее точек-значений. Куда он сядет, решают те, кому он внимает сильнее; на кого он почти не смотрит, тот почти не тянет.The same worked example — now seen with the eyes. The attention weights are not abstract numbers here but a pull: the query leaves its own point and settles at the weighted average of the value points. Where it lands is decided by whom it heeds most; whoever it barely looks at barely tugs.

The climb of the scaled-dot-product act, mounted right after the end-to-end walkthrough so the numbers are already on the table — this slide’s job is to turn them into a picture. The value points, the weight row and the blended point are read from data/l6-attention-geo.json (which reuses the very weights and V of data/l6-attention.json the previous slide computed). Steps: (0) every token’s Value as a point in the plane — attention has not decided anything yet; (1) the query’s weights drawn as edges whose thickness IS the weight — DWELL, this is where “attend” stops being a metaphor and becomes a measurable pull; (2) the slide to the weighted average — the ONE move the whole formula makes, everything before it only decides the weights; (3) the check — the blended point is the output row they just computed by hand. Land the takeaway verbatim: attention never invents a new vector, it averages the vectors already in the room. The next slide zooms back out to the full weight matrix; after this climb that reads as bookkeeping, not as new material.
