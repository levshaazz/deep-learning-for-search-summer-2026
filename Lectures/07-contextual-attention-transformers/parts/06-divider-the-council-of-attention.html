The Council of Attention

У команды слишком много голосов.The crew has too many voices. Кого слушать? Смысл слова решает его окружение.Who do you listen to? A word’s meaning is decided by its neighbours.

Cold open. Picture a round council table. Each seat is a token in the sentence, and each councillor must decide, for every other seat, how much to heed it. That weighted listening is attention, and it is the single mechanism behind everything modern in this course. The tell for our new minor character — the Council, the Attention-Head — is exactly this: a round table where each seat leans toward whom it heeds. The whole lecture answers the opening question — the crew has too many voices, who do you listen to? — by making each token compute its own answer.
