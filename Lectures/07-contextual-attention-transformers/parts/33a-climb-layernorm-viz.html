LayerNorm по шагам: вычесть, поделить, вернуть свободуLayerNorm step by step: subtract, divide, hand the freedom back

шаг (←/→): сырой вектор → вычесть среднее → поделить на разброс → масштаб γ и сдвиг βstep (←/→): the raw vector → subtract the mean → divide by the spread → scale γ and shift β

Формула с прошлого слайда — на живом векторе, том самом, который внимание только что собрало для «cat». Первые два шага чинят не смысл, а масштаб: вектор перецентрируется и садится на единичную сферу, направление при этом не трогают. И только третий шаг возвращает модели свободу — обучаемые множитель и сдвиг умеют частично отменить нормализацию там, где она мешает.The formula from the previous slide — on a live vector, the very one attention just assembled for “cat”. The first two moves fix the scale, not the meaning: the vector is recentred and lands on the unit sphere while its direction is left alone. Only the third move hands the freedom back — a learned multiplier and offset that can partly undo the normalisation wherever it gets in the way.

The block act’s second climb, mounted straight after the LayerNorm formula and deliberately separated from the residual climb by that formula slide. The vector, its mean and variance, the normalised values and the learned scale/shift are all read from data/l6-layernorm.json — and the input is the cat attention-output row from earlier in this very deck, so the callback is real, not rhetorical. Steps: (0) the raw vector sitting off-centre; (1) subtract the mean — shape untouched, only the baseline moves; (2) divide by the spread — DWELL: this is the geometric claim, the vector is pulled onto the unit sphere, same direction, fixed length, and THAT is what “normalise” means here; (3) the learned scale and shift — the part students forget exists, and the reason LayerNorm does not cost the model any expressiveness. Stress that the normalisation is per-token across features, not across the batch — the question always comes.
