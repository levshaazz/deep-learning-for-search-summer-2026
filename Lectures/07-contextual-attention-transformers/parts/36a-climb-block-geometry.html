Что один блок делает с облаком токеновWhat one block does to the cloud of tokens

шаг (←/→): облако точек → внимание стягивает → Add & Norm на кольцо → FFN гнёт → снова на кольцоstep (←/→): the cloud of points → attention contracts it → Add & Norm onto the ring → the FFN bends it → back onto the ring

Стопку из многих слоёв проще понять, если увидеть, что делает один. Внимание сближает точки, Add & Norm возвращает облаку размер, FFN гнёт каждую точку по отдельности, не спрашивая соседей, — и всё сначала. Смешать, укротить, преобразовать, укротить: глубина — это повтор одного и того же цикла, а не новая идея на каждом этаже.A stack of many layers is easier to grasp once you have watched one. Attention draws the points together, Add & Norm gives the cloud its size back, the FFN bends each point on its own without asking its neighbours — and then it all starts again. Blend, tame, reshape, tame: depth is one cycle repeated, not a new idea on every floor.

The block act’s closing climb, mounted after the size table on purpose: they have just been told how MANY blocks are stacked, so now show what ONE of them does to the geometry — and the cloud it leaves behind is exactly what the next slide pools into a single vector. Every stage’s point cloud comes from data/l6-block-geo.json. Steps: (0) five token points spread over the plane; (1) attention contracts the cloud — blending neighbours IS a pull, the same claim as the attention-geometry climb, now for all tokens at once; (2) Add & Norm puts them on the unit ring — DWELL: shape preserved, scale tamed, which is the whole job of the norm; (3) the FFN reshapes each point in isolation — name the contrast out loud, this stage mixes NOTHING across positions, attention is the only mixer in the block; (4) the ring again, and the four-verb summary. Then pooling collapses this cloud to one vector, and the act is closed.
