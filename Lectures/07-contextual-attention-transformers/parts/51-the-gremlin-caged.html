«Контекстные эмбеддинги — это просто эмбеддинги получше».“Contextual embeddings are just slightly better embeddings.”

Это качественный скачок. Синонимия: couch и sofa схлопываются в почти одну точку — стена Гремлина из L1/L3 рушится (контрастное обучение тянет их вместе). Полисемия: «river bank» и «bank account» расходятся, ведь вектор теперь зависит от контекста. Обе беды словарного разрыва закрыты.It is a qualitative leap. Synonymy: couch and sofa collapse to nearly one point — the Gremlin’s L1/L3 wall falls (contrastive learning pulls them together). Polysemy: “river bank” and “bank account” separate, because the vector now depends on context. Both failures of the lexical gap are closed.

Гремлин пойман.The Gremlin is caged. Контекст развёл смыслы; контраст собрал синонимы. То, чего не смог one-hot, BM25 и даже статический word2vec.Context split the senses; contrast gathered the synonyms. What one-hot, BM25, and even static word2vec could not do.

Name the win precisely so it does not sound like a small upgrade. Two distinct failures of the lexical gap are closed at once. Synonymy: couch and sofa, which a string matcher and even a one-hot vector treated as orthogonal, now collapse to nearly the same point, because contrastive training pulled co-relevant texts together — a query for one retrieves the other. Polysemy: river bank and bank account, which static word2vec was forced to average into one point, now separate, because the contextual vector depends on the surrounding sentence. Context split the senses; contrast gathered the synonyms. One-hot could not do this, BM25 could not, and even the static map of Lecture 6 could only corner the Gremlin. Here it is finally caged.
