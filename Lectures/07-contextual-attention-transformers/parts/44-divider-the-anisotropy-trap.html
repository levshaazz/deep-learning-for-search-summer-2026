The Anisotropy Trap

Сырые контекстные векторы лгут о близости:Raw contextual vectors lie about nearness: они сбиваются в узкий конус. Обратный вызов из L2.they crowd into a narrow cone. A callback to L2.

The catch beat, P7, and it is a callback to the high-dimensional pathologies of Lecture 2. Here is the surprise: take a raw, untuned BERT and embed a pile of random sentences, and their vectors do not spread out over the space — they bunch into a narrow cone, all pointing roughly the same way. This is called anisotropy, and it means even unrelated sentences have high cosine similarity, so cosine stops discriminating. It is exactly why raw BERT [CLS] underperformed GloVe. The next slide makes the trap explicit, and explains why contrastive training plus normalisation is the fix.
