Appendix, part two — the retrieval and contrastive lineage. Reimers and Gurevych 2019 is Sentence-BERT, which showed raw BERT vectors are poor for similarity and fixed it with siamese contrastive fine-tuning and mean-pooling. Karpukhin and colleagues 2020 is DPR, the dense passage retriever trained with in-batch negatives — the direct bridge to next lecture's bi-encoders. Ethayarajh 2019 is the anisotropy measurement behind our trap slide. And van den Oord and colleagues 2018 introduced InfoNCE, the contrastive loss we computed to zero point one one nine one. Sir Cosine, the ruler since Lecture 2, runs through all of them.
