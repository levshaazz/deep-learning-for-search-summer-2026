Interleaving

Слейте результаты A и B в один список (team-draft); отнесите каждый клик к тому ранжировщику, который его дал. Каждый пользователь видит оба → межпользовательская дисперсия исчезает.Merge results of A and B into one list (team-draft); attribute each click to the ranker that contributed it. Every user sees both → between-user variance vanishes.

Filter with interleaving (days), confirm survivors with A/B. Interleaving answers "is A better than B?" — it does not give absolute lift or guardrail numbers (latency, revenue), which is why A/B stays the confirmation step.

Interleaving is the ranking-specific superpower: because every user sees both rankers in one merged list, you remove the dominant noise source (between-user variance) and need far fewer users for the same statistical power — Netflix reports >100×. Mention novelty/primacy effects: treatment effects are non-stationary, so never read out on day 1. Workflow: interleaving as a fast filter, A/B as the final causal confirmation.
