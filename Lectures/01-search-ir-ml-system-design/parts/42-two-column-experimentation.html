Interleaving

Слейте A и B в один список (team-draft); каждый клик отнесите к его ранжировщику. Каждый пользователь видит оба → межпользовательская дисперсия исчезает.Merge A and B into one list (team-draft); attribute each click to its ranker. Every user sees both → between-user variance vanishes.

Interleaving is the ranking-specific superpower: because every user sees both rankers in one merged list, you remove the dominant noise source (between-user variance) and need far fewer users for the same statistical power — Netflix reports >100×. Mention novelty/primacy effects: treatment effects are non-stationary, so never read out on day 1. Workflow: interleaving as a fast filter, A/B as the final causal confirmation.
