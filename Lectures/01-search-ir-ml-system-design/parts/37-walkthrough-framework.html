Правило Google #1: не бойтесь сначала запуститься без ML.. Google Rule #1: don't be afraid to launch without ML first.

6/6
This is the backbone of the systems half — memorise the six steps. Two Google Rules of ML anchor the early steps: Rule #1 (a heuristic often beats a premature model and de-risks the infra) and Rule #4 (a simple first model proves the pipeline before you add complexity). The chips are deliverables, so students can ask "what artifact have I actually produced?" at each stage. Step 6 loops back to step 2 — the lifecycle is a cycle, not a line.
