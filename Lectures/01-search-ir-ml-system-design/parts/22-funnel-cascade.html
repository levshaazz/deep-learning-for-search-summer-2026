Почему не гонять лучшую модель по каждому документу? BERT-кросс-энкодер при ~десятках мс/пару × \(10^{9}\) документов — это примерно \(10^{8}\times\) сверх бюджета. Поэтому: дёшево-и-широко, затем дорого-и-точно.Why not run the best model on every document? A BERT cross-encoder at ~tens of ms/pair × \(10^{9}\) documents is roughly \(10^{8}\times\) over budget. So: cheap-and-broad, then expensive-and-precise.

The cascade is the central systems idea of search. The arithmetic on the caption sells it: you cannot afford the good model on \(10^{9}\) documents, so retrieval makes a huge first cut (≈\(10^{9}\to10^{3}\)), then each later stage spends ~10× more compute on ~10× fewer candidates. Recall lives early (you can never recover a document you didn't retrieve — next slide); precision lives late. The same funnel reappears as a latency budget in Part 03.
