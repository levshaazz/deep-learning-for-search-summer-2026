Every technique — cascade, smaller embeddings, caching — entered as the fix to a measured pain point, not as a default.

Ported from the old Lecture 1 PCA arc: a real task — 371M short multilingual strings, top-100 matches with a score, 1-second SLA. The naive ledger (768-dim float64 over 371M rows, no GPU) is unservable. The engineered ledger shrinks dimension 768 → 24 (e.g. via PCA / a smaller model), adds an ANN index and a cache, and every pain annotation flips to "(no problem)". The pedagogical pattern to reuse all term: introduce each technique as the answer to a measured bottleneck, never as a hammer looking for a nail.
