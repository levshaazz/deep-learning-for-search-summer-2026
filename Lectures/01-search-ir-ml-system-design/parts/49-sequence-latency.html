Latency is a design budget you spend, and the ranker is the expensive line item — which is precisely why retrieval narrows the field first. Critical detail: monitor \(p_{99}\), not \(p_{50}\) — and if \(p_{99}\) rises while \(p_{50}\) falls, your request queue is backing up.
