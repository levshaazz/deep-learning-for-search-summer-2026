Sample-Ratio Mismatch (SRM) is the first thing to check, before you even look at the primary metric. If you assigned 50/50 but observe a significant deviation, something upstream is broken — bot filtering, a redirect, logging loss — and the experiment is untrustworthy. Microsoft's rule: every A/B passes an SRM chi-square test before any analysis. Then guardrails, then the metric — and always with segment cuts, because of Simpson's paradox (next on the drift slide).
