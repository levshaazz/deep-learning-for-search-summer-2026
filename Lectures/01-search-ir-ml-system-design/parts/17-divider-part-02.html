Anatomy of a
search engine

How does one machine answer a query against a billion documents in under 200 ms — and why is it still so hard to get right?

Part 02 opens the machine. First the anatomy (offline build vs online serve, the inverted index, the cascade), then a tour of why search is hard — the lexical gap, the long tail, intent, and biased feedback. Every "hard" slide plants a seed that the DL half of the course later harvests.
