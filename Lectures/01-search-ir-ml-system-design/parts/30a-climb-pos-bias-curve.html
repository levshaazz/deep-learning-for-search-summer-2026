Разрыв между плоской честной линией и высокими верхними столбиками — и есть смещение, которое отравляет обучение на сырых кликах.The thought experiment from the previous slide, now stepped through. Make every result equally relevant and count the clicks: the bars still pile up at the top, pulled by position, not quality. The gap between the flat honest line and the tall top bars is exactly the bias that poisons training on raw clicks.

The climb that seals the “click logs lie” misconception. The click-through curve comes from data/l1-click-model.json — the widget draws it, the slide asserts nothing numeric of its own. Steps: (0) the setup — ten equally relevant results; (1) the observed clicks per rank; (2) how much the top ranks soak up; (3) the flat line the clicks SHOULD follow if they measured relevance — DWELL on the gap; (4) the Goodhart punchline: train on raw clicks and you reward position. Step 4 deliberately fore-echoes the Goodhart slide later in act 3 — say “remember this bar chart” when you get there.
