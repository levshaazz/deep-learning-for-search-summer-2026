Feedback loops and how to break the bad ones

Bias → degeneracy → fix

The biases

Position bias: clicks reward rank-1 for being rank-1.

Exposure bias: never-shown items get no feedback — missing-not-at-random.

Degenerate loops: the model only ever sees feedback on what it chose to show.

The fixes

Unbiased LTR: inverse-propensity-weight clicks by examination probability (Joachims) — or model examination vs relevance directly. Caveat: \(1/p_r\) is unbiased only if the propensities are right and the variance is controlled — it blows up at low-examination ranks, so clip propensities or jointly learn examination + relevance.

Exploration: \(\varepsilon\)-greedy / bandits to gather feedback on unshown items.

Context features: time, device — not pure interaction history.

This pays off the position-bias slide from Part 02. The bias on the left and the fix on the right line up: position bias ↔ inverse-propensity weighting; exposure bias ↔ exploration; degenerate loops ↔ context features over raw interaction history. The meta-lesson: you must actively counteract the loop your own system creates, or it slowly poisons its own training data.
