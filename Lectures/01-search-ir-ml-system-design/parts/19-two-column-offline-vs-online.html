«Офлайн строит индекс; онлайн его обходит». Асимметрия между двумя таймерами формирует каждое последующее проектное решение."Offline builds the index; online walks it." The asymmetry between the two clocks shapes every design decision that follows.

The mantra: offline builds the index, online walks it. This split is why we can afford an expensive analysis pipeline (it runs once, offline) but must keep retrieval cheap (it runs per request). It also seeds the train/serving-skew discussion in Part 03 — the same feature must be computed identically in both worlds.
