Step through it slowly — this is the concrete payoff of the previous definition. Emphasise step 5: the intersection is a linear merge of two sorted lists, which is why postings are kept sorted. With more terms you intersect shortest-first to minimise work. This worked example is the seed of BM25 scoring in Lecture 03: \(\mathrm{tf}\) and \(\mathrm{df}\) are already in the postings.
