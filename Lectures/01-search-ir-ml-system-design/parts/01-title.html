Welcome. This is the opening pair of Deep Learning for Search. Today is deliberately conceptual, not code-heavy: we build the mental model the whole course hangs on. Two halves: (1) what Information Retrieval is and why search is genuinely hard; (2) the ML System Design framework that turns a model into a system that survives production. Sci-fi framing — call it The Lost Record: imagine a lone operator on a ship who must find the one record that saves the mission among billions — IR is finding it; ML System Design is building the machine that keeps finding it after you have gone to sleep.
