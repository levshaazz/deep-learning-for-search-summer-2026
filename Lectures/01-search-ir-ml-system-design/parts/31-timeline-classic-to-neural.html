The narrative spine of the whole term, drawn as the arc that took us here. BM25 over the inverted index is fast, interpretable, and strong on rare/exact terms (names, IDs, codes) — but it hits the lexical-gap wall. Dense retrieval (SBERT, DPR) bridges synonymy/polysemy but can lose substantial recall on rare exact tokens (names, IDs, codes). Fuse them and recall@1000 climbs to ≈0.98 — hybrid is the production norm. The orange-bead pivots — BM25 (1994) and dense (SBERT 2019 · DPR 2020) — are the two transitions everything else hangs off.
