Применение лекарства от ковариативного сдвига к дрейфу концепта ничего не решает — диагноз определяет лечение. Холодный старт (новый пользователь/запрос/документ, без истории) требует контентных признаков, априорных оценок популярности и исследования.Applying a covariate-shift fix to concept drift solves nothing — the diagnosis determines the cure. Cold start (new user/query/doc, no history) needs content features, popularity priors, and exploration.

The discipline is matching diagnosis to cure. Covariate shift (\(P(x)\) moves, \(P(y\mid x)\) fixed) is caught by watching feature distributions — KL divergence or PSI. Concept drift (\(P(y\mid x)\) itself moves) is invisible in feature distributions; you only see it as rising error against fresh labels. Leakage is the offline-metric inflator — random splits on temporal data leak the future. Simpson's paradox: if the aggregate moves but no cohort moves the same way, suspect a traffic-mix shift and never trust the aggregate alone.
