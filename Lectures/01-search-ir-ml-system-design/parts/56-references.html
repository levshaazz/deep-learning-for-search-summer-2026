  7. Karpukhin, V. et al. · Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain QA (DPR) · EMNLP 2020
  8. Sculley, D. et al. · Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems · NeurIPS 2015

  10. Zinkevich, M. · Rules of Machine Learning (Google) — Rules #1, #4, #29, #32, #37 · Google Developers 2017
Manning et al. is the IR bible — chapters 1, 6, 8 first. Broder and Furnas are the two classics behind the "why search is hard" half. Sculley, Huyen and Google's Rules of ML are the spine of the system-design half. Covington, Nogueira & Cho, and DPR are the bridge into neural ranking and dense retrieval later in the term.
