Business goal → ML objective → proxy metric

The translation spine (step 01)
Business goal ML objective Proxy metric
Users find what they want rank relevant docs at the top \(\text{nDCG}@10\)
Answers feel fast low serving latency \(p_{99}\) latency
Content stays fresh keep the index up to date indexing lag

Every row is a contract: if the metric moves the right way, the goal is served. Choosing the metric is the design decision — and the next slides show how that choice can betray you.

Stage 01 made concrete. The most expensive failures live here: an ML objective only loosely coupled to the business goal. Chasing \(\text{nDCG}\) while latency quietly blows the SLA is a classic. Each row is a contract you must be able to defend. This sets up the metrics taxonomy and Goodhart's law next.
