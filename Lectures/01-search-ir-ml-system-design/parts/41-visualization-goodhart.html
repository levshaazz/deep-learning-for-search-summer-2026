Закон ГудхартаGoodhart's law

Когда мера становится целью…When a measure becomes a target…

Оптимизируй CTR достаточно сильно — и ты оптимизируешь кликбейт, возмущение и случайные касания: прокси растёт, а то, что тебе на самом деле было нужно, падает. Сопровождай каждый прокси защитной метрикой (время просмотра, доля быстрых возвратов).Optimise CTR hard enough and you optimise clickbait, outrage, and accidental taps — the proxy rises while the thing you actually wanted falls. Pair every proxy with a guardrail (dwell time, quick-back rate).

As Strathern (1997) crisply restated Goodhart's 1975 observation: "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." Picture Goodhart the Trickster yanking the CTR line up with a clickbait hook while real satisfaction quietly sinks. A pure CTR objective rewards clickbait, outrage, ambiguity, and accidental taps — none of which is satisfaction. The mitigation is exactly the guardrail discipline from two slides back: pair CTR with dwell time and pogo-sticking/quick-back rate so the degenerate direction is caught. Prefer causal goal metrics over convenient proxies where you can.
