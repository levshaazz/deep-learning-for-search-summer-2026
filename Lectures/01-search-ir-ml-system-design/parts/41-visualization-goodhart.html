Goodhart's law

When a measure becomes a target…

Optimise CTR hard enough and you optimise clickbait, outrage, and accidental taps — the proxy rises while the thing you actually wanted falls. Pair every proxy with a guardrail (dwell time, quick-back rate).

Goodhart: "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." Picture Goodhart the Trickster yanking the CTR line up with a clickbait hook while real satisfaction quietly sinks. A pure CTR objective rewards clickbait, outrage, ambiguity, and accidental taps — none of which is satisfaction. The mitigation is exactly the guardrail discipline from two slides back: pair CTR with dwell time and pogo-sticking/quick-back rate so the degenerate direction is caught. Prefer causal goal metrics over convenient proxies where you can.
