Three families of metrics

Offline · online · guardrail

Offline & online

Offline (on logged labels): \(\text{nDCG}\), MAP, MRR, plus classification proxies AUC and log-loss. Cheap, fast to iterate.

Online (on live users): CTR, conversion, dwell time, session success, time-to-first-click. The behaviour you actually care about.

Guardrail

Must-not-harm metrics that veto a launch even if the primary metric improves:

!
\(p_{99}\) latency · error rate · revenue · retention

A treatment that lifts CTR but breaks the latency guardrail does not ship.

Three families with three jobs. Offline metrics are for fast iteration; online metrics are the ground truth of user behaviour; guardrails are the safety net. The key discipline: every primary metric is paired with guardrails that catch its degenerate direction. Calibration matters when scores feed a cascade or an auction — log-loss, not just AUC.
