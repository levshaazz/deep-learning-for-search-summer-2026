The long tail of queries

~15% of daily queries are brand-new

You cannot memorise the tail — you must generalise. This is the statistical reason search needs machine learning.

Query frequency is Zipfian: a small head dominates volume, but the tail is endless. Google has reaffirmed for years that ~15% of daily queries have never been seen before (25% in 2007, ~15% since ~2013). At ~8.5B queries/day that is ≈1.3B novel queries every single day. You cannot precompute answers for queries you have never seen — so you must generalise, which is exactly what learned representations buy you.
