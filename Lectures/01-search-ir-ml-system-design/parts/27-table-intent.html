То же ранжирование, которое идеально попадает в навигационный запрос, может быть неверным для информационного. И заметь: эти доли зависят от исследования — разметка намерения сама по себе нечёткая, и это отдельный учебный тезис.The same ranking that nails a navigational query can be wrong for an informational one. And note: these shares are study-dependent — labelling intent is itself fuzzy, which is a teaching point in its own right.

Broder's taxonomy (from AltaVista logs) is the classic frame. The practical lesson: optimal ranking depends on intent — top-1 precision for navigational, diversity for ambiguous informational, conversion signals for transactional. Stress the caveat: different studies give different percentages because intent labelling is subjective — that fuzziness is exactly why we eventually learn intent from behaviour rather than hand-labelling it.
