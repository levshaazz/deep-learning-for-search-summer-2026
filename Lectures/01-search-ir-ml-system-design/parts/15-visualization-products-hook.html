Three products, one retrieval problem

Google · Ozon · Yandex Neuro
Product What the user wants What gets retrieved How the answer is delivered
Google (web search) a credible page web documents ten blue links, ordered
Ozon (e-commerce) an item to buy product listings a grid of products, ordered
Yandex Neuro (generative answer) a direct answer passages from retrieved pages one fluent paragraph + citations

Same machinery underneath: a query, a corpus, a ranker. Different surfaces. Yandex Neuro adds an LLM on top of retrieval — when that grounding fails, you get a confident horse with eight legs.

Three very different products — web (Google), e-commerce (Ozon), and a generative answer engine (Yandex Neuro) — are the same retrieval problem underneath: query → corpus → ranker → ordered result. The Neuro example is the hallucination hook ("a horse has 8 legs"): an LLM is fluent but ungrounded; bolt good retrieval in front of it and the answer becomes both fluent and correct. This motivates RAG at the very end of the course.
