The data flywheel — and its evil twin

The same loop that helps, harms

The flywheel ↻  (virtuous)

  • 1more users
  • 2more logs / labels
  • 3a better model
  • 4better results  ↻ back to 1

Scale compounds quality.

The evil twin ↻  (degenerate)

  • 1show a popular item
  • 2more exposure
  • 3more clicks (just from position)
  • 4ranked even higher  ↻ back to 1

The same loop, fed naive clicks, amplifies bias — “rich get richer”.

The flywheel is the dream: scale compounds quality. The evil twin is the nightmare: the same loop, fed naive click data, amplifies whatever bias is in the logs. Popular items get more exposure → more clicks → ranked higher → even more exposure — a degenerate, rich-get-richer spiral that buries the long tail and freezes the catalogue.
