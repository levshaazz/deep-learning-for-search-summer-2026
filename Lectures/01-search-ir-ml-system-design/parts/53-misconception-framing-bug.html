Самый дорогой баг — в постановке задачи, а не в моделировании. Если цель рассогласована с задачей, технически идеальная модель делает хуже — безупречный максимизатор CTR, который тихо обрушивает удержание. Модель можно всегда поменять; но то, что оптимизируешь не то, замечаешь редко — обычно уже после того, как бизнес-метрика сдвинулась против тебя.The most expensive bug is in problem framing, not modeling. If the objective is misaligned with the goal, a technically perfect model makes things worse — a flawless CTR-maximiser that quietly tanks retention. You can always swap the model; you rarely notice you optimised the wrong thing until the business metric has already moved against you.

Same two-tower architecture, same logged data  ⇒  the ranker now prefers videos people finish, not videos they start. Architecture didn't change; the framing of "good" did.

The closing systems lesson and the reason step 01 (framing) leads the framework. A perfect model on the wrong objective is not neutral — it is actively harmful, and it fails silently because the proxy metric looks great. This is why we spend a whole framework step on framing and pair every proxy with a guardrail. It also rhymes with Goodhart: the danger is not a bad model, it is a well-optimised wrong target.
