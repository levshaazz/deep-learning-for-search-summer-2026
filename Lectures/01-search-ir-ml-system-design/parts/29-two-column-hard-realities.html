More hard realities

The world keeps moving

Queries are messy

Short: 2–3 words, almost no context. Misspelled: typos everywhere. Ambiguous: python = language or snake?

!
Zero-result queries

10–20% of on-site e-commerce searches return nothing — usually vocabulary mismatch (joggers vs catalog sweatpants), not missing stock.

Relevance moves

Freshness: after an earthquake, a 1-hour-old report beats a perfect 5-year-old page. Personalization: python means different things to a programmer and a herpetologist; coffee near me depends on where you stand.

i
The label rots.

A "relevant" judgment from last year may be wrong today.

Each item here is a future engineering project: spell correction, query understanding, freshness signals, personalization, zero-result recovery. The zero-result number has teeth: 100k searches/month × 10% = 10,000 high-intent visitors lost monthly — and the fix is usually synonyms and typo tolerance, not more inventory.
