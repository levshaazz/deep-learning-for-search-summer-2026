The needle
in the cosmos

У пользователя есть потребность. Где-то среди миллиардов документов лежит тот единственный, что на неё отвечает. Что вообще значит найти его?A user has a need. Somewhere in billions of documents sits the one that answers it. What does it even mean to find it?

Set the scene. The Project Hail Mary framing: a single operator, a catalogue the size of a galaxy, and one record that matters — Serega cues the catchphrase here, The Lost Record. Part 01 is about the problem — relevance, the lossy gap between need and query, and why the right answer is a ranked list rather than a filter.
