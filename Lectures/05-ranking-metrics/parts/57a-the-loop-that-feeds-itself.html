Вот точная форма закона Гудхарта: в измеряемой величине сидит множитель, которым ранкер управляет сам. Убери его — делением на склонность или отложенной оценкой — и табло снова меряет релевантность.Goodhart's law in exact form: the quantity you score carries a factor the ranker itself controls. Take it out — by the propensity, or by a held-out judgment — and the scoreboard measures relevance again.

The mechanism under the previous slide's third bullet, which asserted the feedback loop — "train on biased clicks, reinforce position bias, train on it again" — as a chain of arrows with nothing under them. Start from the examination hypothesis the position-bias slide already drew: a click happens only if the user looked at the rank AND found the document relevant, so the click probability factors into an examination term that depends only on rank and a relevance term that depends only on the document. Now take the naive estimate every logs-driven pipeline computes: clicks over impressions. It does not estimate relevance — it estimates relevance times the examination probability of whatever rank the OLD ranker chose. That is the loop, stated exactly: the ranker's own past decision is a multiplicative factor inside its next training label, so whatever it put on top gets a higher estimate and stays on top, and the next round of logs is more skewed than the last. Nothing adversarial is required — no SEO spammer, no clickbait — the system games itself. Then the two defences on the previous slide stop being a list and become the same fix twice: IPS divides the term out, and held-out human judgments never contained it.
