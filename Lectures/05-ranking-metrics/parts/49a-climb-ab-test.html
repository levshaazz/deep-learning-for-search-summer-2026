Тот же тест, что и на прошлом слайде, — теперь доведи его до вердикта сам. Прирост на поверхности ещё ничего не значит: сначала спроси, насколько удивителен такой разрыв, если оба ранкера на самом деле равны. Только после этого ответа слово «выкатываем» имеет право прозвучать — а подводные камни ждут на следующем слайде.The same test as the previous slide — now carry it to the verdict yourself. A lift on the surface means nothing yet: first ask how surprising a gap this size would be if the two rankers were actually equal. Only after that answer does the word “ship” earn the right to be said — and the pitfalls are waiting on the next slide.

Act 6’s climb, straight after the A/B definition slide and before the guardrails-and-pitfalls slide. Counts, rates and the test statistic all come from data/l4-online.json. Steps: (0) the traffic split — emphasise randomisation, it is what makes the later inference legal; (1) the surface lift — PAUSE and ask the room “ship it?”, let them commit; (2) the luck check — same logic as the offline act’s significance climb, one act earlier: how surprising is this gap under equality?; (3) the verdict. The deliberate echo between this climb and the significance climb is the lecture’s spine: offline or online, the question is always “real or luck?”. Students stumble on absolute vs relative lift — step 1 shows both, read them aloud carefully.
