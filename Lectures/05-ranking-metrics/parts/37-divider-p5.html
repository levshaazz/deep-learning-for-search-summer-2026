Trial by dice —
real win or luck?

B обошла A в моей таблице на 0,04 nDCG. Прежде чем объявить победителя, я должен исключить кости случая.B beat A on my scoreboard by 0.04 nDCG. Before I declare a winner I have to rule out the dice of chance.

✓ Теперь ты умеешь оценивать любое отдельное ранжирование с помощью nDCG. Дальше — доказать, что разница между двумя оценками реальна.✓ You can now score any single ranking with nDCG. Next: prove a difference between two scores is real.

This is where I see most evaluations go wrong. I compute a metric for System A and System B, B is higher, and the temptation is to just ship B. But every metric is an average over a finite, noisy query sample — my dice of chance. A 0.04 nDCG gap might be a genuine improvement or might vanish on the next 15 queries. This part is the statistics I use to separate signal from noise: paired tests, confidence intervals, power. If the divider image 404s the slide still stands.
