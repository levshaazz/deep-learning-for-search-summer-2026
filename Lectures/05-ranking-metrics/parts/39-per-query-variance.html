Стандартное отклонение разностей по запросам (\(0{,}0676\)) больше, чем средняя разность (\(0{,}0397\)). Одно среднее способно скрыть, действительно ли система стабильно лучше или ей просто повезло на нескольких запросах.The standard deviation of the per-query differences (\(0.0676\)) is larger than the mean difference (\(0.0397\)). A single average can hide whether a system is reliably better or just lucky on a few queries.

This is the picture that should make you cautious. Each bar is one query's B-minus-A difference: most are green (B wins) but four are red (A wins), and the magnitudes scatter widely. The mean improvement, the dashed line, is real but modest — and the spread of differences (sd 0.0676) is bigger than the mean itself. When variance dwarfs the effect, you cannot eyeball significance; you need a test that accounts for how noisy the per-query differences are. That's the next slide.
