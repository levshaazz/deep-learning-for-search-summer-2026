Один и тот же числитель, разный знаменатель: \(\mathrm{Precision}@k\) делит на \(k\), \(\mathrm{Recall}@k\) делит на общее число релевантных.Same numerator, different denominator: \(\mathrm{Precision}@k\) divides by \(k\), \(\mathrm{Recall}@k\) divides by total relevant.

Two sides of the same coin. Both count how many relevant docs sit in the top k; they differ only in what they divide by. Recall measures coverage — of everything relevant, how much did we surface — and rises monotonically toward 1 as k grows. Precision measures purity — of what we showed, how much was good — and tends to fall as k grows because you scrape further down.
