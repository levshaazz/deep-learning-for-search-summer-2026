Офлайн — это фильтр, а не приговор.Across many launches, offline nDCG gains usually correlate with online CTR gains — but not always. The red point is the dangerous case: a change that improved the offline metric and hurt users. Offline is a filter, not a verdict.

The empirical question that ties the lecture together. If offline metrics perfectly predicted online outcomes, we'd never need experiments — but they don't, only correlate. Most of the time a real offline nDCG gain shows up as an online click or conversion gain, which is why offline iteration works at all. The red outlier is the trap: a change that games the offline metric — maybe by exploiting label quirks or the unjudged-document bias — looks like an improvement offline yet degrades the real user experience. That's Goodhart, which is exactly where Part 7 goes. Validate offline winners online before you trust them.
