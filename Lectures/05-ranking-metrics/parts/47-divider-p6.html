Off the test range —
onto live traffic

Мои полигоны используют прокси-метки. Теперь я выставляю победителя перед реальными пользователями — шумно, смещённо, но это единственная правда, которая приносит деньги.My proving grounds use proxy labels. Now I put the winner in front of real users — noisy, biased, but the only truth that pays.

✓ Теперь ты умеешь доказывать, что офлайн-победа реальна (permutation / \(t\) / Wilcoxon + CI). Далее: живые пользователи.✓ You can now prove an offline win is real (permutation / \(t\) / Wilcoxon + CI). Next: live users.

My offline evaluation optimises a proxy: human labels that approximate what users want. But labels drift, intents change, and no qrel captures whether a user was actually satisfied. So I leave the test range and close the loop by measuring real behaviour — clicks, dwell, conversion. The catch: that behaviour is noisy, biased by position, and influenced by the very system that logged it. This part is the full online stack, ending with the question of whether offline ever predicts online.
