L01 Search & Information Retrieval · ML System Design

Information Retrieval
Finding, from a large collection, the documents that satisfy an information need.
Retrieve-then-rank
The two-stage cascade: cheaply fetch many candidates, then carefully order the few.
Relevance
How well a document satisfies the information need behind a user's query.
Corpus
The full collection of documents a search system indexes and searches over.

L02 NLP · Tokenization · Measures of Similarity

Tokenization
Splitting text into the atomic units (tokens) a model or index works with.
Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE)
A subword tokenizer that merges frequent character pairs, balancing vocabulary size and coverage.
Stemming
Chopping words to a crude root (e.g. "running" → "run") so variants match.
Lemmatization
Reducing a word to its dictionary form using morphology (e.g. "better" → "good").
Stop words
Very common words (the, of, and) often dropped because they carry little signal.
Cosine similarity
Closeness of two vectors measured by the angle between them, ignoring length.
Jaccard similarity
Overlap of two sets: the size of their intersection over the size of their union.
Edit distance
The minimum number of insert/delete/substitute edits to turn one string into another.

L03 Classical IR (TF-IDF, BM25, inverted index) · Full-text Ranking & Rank Fusion

Inverted index
A map from each term to the list of documents that contain it — the backbone of full-text search.
TF-IDF
A term weight that rises with frequency in a document and falls with how many documents use it.
BM25
The classic probabilistic ranking function: TF-IDF with term saturation and length normalization.
Inverse document frequency (IDF)
A weight that boosts terms appearing in few documents and discounts ubiquitous ones.
PageRank
Link-based authority: a page is important if important pages link to it.
Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF)
Combining several ranked lists by summing 1/(k + rank) for each item across them.
Postings list
The list of documents (and positions) stored for one term in the inverted index.

L04 Shannon Entropy — the entropy of language

Entropy
The average surprise (in bits) of a source — how unpredictable its symbols are.
Cross-entropy
The average bits needed to encode data from one distribution using another.
Perplexity
A language model's uncertainty — the exponential of its cross-entropy.

L05 Ranking Metrics (Recall@k, Precision@k, MRR, MAP, nDCG)

Precision@k
The fraction of the top-k results that are relevant.
Recall@k
The fraction of all relevant documents that appear within the top-k.
Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
The average of 1/(rank of the first relevant result) over queries.
Mean Average Precision (MAP)
The mean, over queries, of the average precision computed across a query’s relevant hits.
nDCG
Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain — rewards putting highly relevant results near the top.
Discounted Cumulative Gain (DCG)
Sums graded relevance down the ranked list with a discount that grows with position.
Relevance judgment
A human label saying how relevant a document is to a query — the ground truth for metrics.
Goodhart's law
When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.

L06 Intro to DL for Search · Word Embeddings · Dimensionality Reduction

Word embedding
A dense vector for a word whose geometry captures its meaning and similarity to others.
Word2Vec
A method that learns word embeddings by predicting a word from its context (or vice versa).
Dimensionality reduction
Projecting high-dimensional vectors into a few dimensions while preserving structure.
t-SNE
A nonlinear technique that lays out high-dimensional points in 2-D, preserving local neighbourhoods.
PCA
Principal Component Analysis — a linear projection onto the directions of greatest variance.
Embedding space
The vector space where meanings become positions you can measure and travel between.

L07 Dense & Contextual Embeddings · Contrastive Learning · Transformers & Attention

Dense embedding
A compact learned vector representation, as opposed to sparse term counts.
Contextual embedding
A token vector that depends on the surrounding sentence, so "bank" differs by context.
Contrastive learning
Training that pulls matching pairs together and pushes mismatched pairs apart.
Self-attention
A mechanism letting each token weigh and gather information from every other token.
Transformer
The attention-based architecture underlying modern language and retrieval models.
Positional encoding
Signals added to token vectors so the model knows their order.

L08 BERT & other Transformers — from attention to RLHF

Masked language modeling
BERT's objective: predict hidden tokens from their two-sided context.
Fine-tuning
Adapting a pretrained model to a task with further training on task-specific data.
RLHF
Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback — aligning a model to human preferences.
Attention head
One of several parallel attention functions, each learning a different relation.

L10 Bi-encoders (DPR, SBERT) · Cross-encoders & Reranking · Multi-stage Pipelines

Bi-encoder
Encodes query and document separately into vectors — fast, and enables precomputed indexes.
Cross-encoder
Jointly encodes query + document for an accurate but slower relevance score.
Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR)
A bi-encoder trained to retrieve passages by dense vector similarity.
SBERT (Sentence-BERT)
BERT fine-tuned to produce sentence embeddings that are comparable by cosine similarity.
Reranking
Reordering a retrieved candidate set with a stronger, costlier model.
Multi-stage pipeline
A retrieval cascade where each stage narrows and refines the candidate set.

L11 The Crucible of Negatives — hard negative mining in contrastive dense retrieval

Hard negative
A non-relevant document that looks deceptively similar — the most informative to train on.
In-batch negatives
Reusing the other examples in a training batch as negatives for cheap contrast.
Negative sampling
Choosing which non-relevant items to contrast against during training.

L12 Late Interaction (ColBERT) · SPLADE · Hybrid · Learning to Rank

Late interaction
Scoring by comparing per-token vectors of query and document at the end (e.g. MaxSim).
ColBERT
A late-interaction retriever matching each query token to its best document token.
SPLADE
A learned sparse retriever that expands text into weighted vocabulary terms.
Learned sparse retrieval
Neural models that produce sparse, invertible term weights for fast inverted indexes.
Hybrid search
Combining lexical (BM25) and dense retrieval to get the strengths of both.
Learning to Rank (LTR)
Training a model to order results directly from labelled relevance data.
MaxSim
ColBERT's operator: each query token takes its maximum similarity over the document's tokens.

L13 ANN: HNSW, IVF, PQ · FAISS · Vector DBs · Production

Approximate Nearest Neighbour (ANN)
Trading a little recall for huge speed when searching vectors at billion scale.
HNSW
A navigable small-world graph index enabling fast greedy nearest-neighbour search.
IVF (Inverted File)
Cluster vectors into cells, then search only the cells nearest to the query.
Product Quantization (PQ)
Compressing vectors into short codes by quantizing their sub-vectors independently.
FAISS
A library for efficient similarity search and clustering of dense vectors.
Vector database
A store that indexes embeddings for fast similarity search in production.

L14 The Curved Map — geometry of meaning: anisotropy & hubness

Anisotropy
The tendency of embeddings to crowd into a narrow cone, distorting cosine similarity.
Hubness
In high dimensions, a few points become nearest neighbours to disproportionately many others.
Isotropy
The opposite of anisotropy: directions in the space are used evenly, so cosine keeps its resolution.
Whitening
Centring the cloud and rescaling its own axes so the covariance becomes the identity matrix.
All-but-the-top (ABTT)
Subtract the mean, then project out the few top principal components — the cheap half of whitening.
Reverse k-NN count (N_k)
How many other points list a given point among their k nearest — the measure hubness is read from.
CSLS
Cross-domain similarity local scaling: charge every candidate for how dense its own neighbourhood is.
Anti-hub
A point that appears on nobody’s neighbour list — invisible to retrieval, and silently so.
Matryoshka representation
An embedding trained so its first k coordinates are already a usable shorter vector.

L15 RAG Fundamentals · Chunking · Query Understanding & Rewriting

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Grounding a language model's answer in freshly retrieved documents.
Chunking
Splitting documents into passages sized for retrieval and the model's context window.
Query rewriting
Reformulating a user query to retrieve better — expansion, clarification, decomposition.
HyDE
Hypothetical Document Embeddings — embed an LLM-drafted answer to retrieve real passages.
Semantic router
Directing a query to the right tool or index by embedding similarity.

L16 RAG Evaluation (RAGAS, LLM-as-judge) · Agentic RAG (ReAct, Self-RAG, CRAG)

RAGAS
A framework of reference-free metrics for evaluating RAG pipelines.
LLM-as-judge
Using a language model to score or compare outputs in place of human raters.
Faithfulness
Whether a generated answer is actually supported by the retrieved context.
Agentic RAG
RAG where an agent plans, retrieves and acts over multiple steps.
ReAct
An agent pattern interleaving reasoning traces with tool-use actions.
Self-RAG
RAG that decides when to retrieve and critiques its own generations.

L17 The Artificer's Quill — advanced query rewriting & decomposition

Query expansion
Adding related terms to a query to catch more of the relevant documents.
Step-back prompting
Asking a more general question first to retrieve grounding for a specific one.
Query decomposition
Breaking a complex query into simpler sub-queries answered separately.

L18 Late Chunking — contextual chunk embeddings for long documents

Late chunking
Embedding the whole document first, then pooling into chunks so each keeps full context.

L19 Search in Russian — morphology strikes back

Token tax
The extra tokens a non-English word costs because the vocabulary was not budgeted for it.
Fertility
Tokens per word — the number that turns the token tax into something you can measure.
Fusional morphology
One ending carries several grammatical meanings at once — the reason Russian forms multiply.
Paradigm
The full set of forms one lexeme takes — and the number of terms an un-normalized index files.
Snowball stemmer
The published rule cascade for Russian: cheap, dictionary-free, and blind to stem alternation.
The both-arms rule
Whatever normalization sits on the index must sit on the query — one-sided is worse than none.
ё/е folding
Treating ё and е as one letter in both arms — a one-line fix for a whole class of misses.
Homoglyph
A Latin letter that looks exactly like a Cyrillic one — same pixels, different code points, zero score.

L20 Advanced RAG (multi-hop, GraphRAG) · Multimodal (CLIP, ColPali) · Ethics & Safety

Multi-hop retrieval
Answering questions that require chaining evidence across several documents.
GraphRAG
Building a knowledge graph over a corpus to retrieve and reason over connected facts.
CLIP
A model aligning images and text in one embedding space for cross-modal search.
ColPali
A late-interaction retriever that searches document page-images directly, skipping OCR.
RAPTOR
Recursive summary trees that let retrieval pick the right level of abstraction.
